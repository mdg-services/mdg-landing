The PESO licence in Form XIV does not last for ever. It has an end date, and a renewal has a deadline. Figures here are as on 14 November 2026, from the Petroleum Rules 2002 as amended.

## How long does the licence run, and for how long is it renewed?

A licence stays in force until 31 December of the year up to which it is granted or renewed, for at most three years {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 142}}. A renewal is for three calendar years where there was no breach of the Act, the rules or the licence conditions {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 148(2)}}.

## When must I apply, and what does lateness cost?

| When the application reaches PESO | Consequence |
|---|---|
| At least 30 days before expiry | The licence is deemed in force until PESO renews it or says it is refused {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 148(5)}} |
| Later than that, up to 30 days after expiry | Renewed only on twice the usual fee {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 148(7)}} |
| More than one year after expiry | No renewal; submit all papers for a new licence, with the District Authority's NOC {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2018, rule 148(8), (9)}} |

Example, made up: a licence that expires on 31 December 2026 needs its renewal application at PESO by 1 December 2026. For an application between 30 days and one year after expiry, ask PESO what applies.

The renewal fee for each calendar year is the same as for the grant {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 148(7)}}. For Form XIV that is Rs 5,000 for the first 50 kilolitres of each class, plus Rs 50 for each extra kilolitre or part, up to Rs 50,000 a year {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2018, First Schedule}}.

## How is it filed?

Log in to the NSWS portal, then apply on PESO's site under the form of your licence, upload the documents and pay online {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. The application comes with the licence and its approved plans {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 148(4)}}.

## What if the layout or the licensee changes?

- **Layout:** no change before PESO approves the drawing in writing; the scrutiny fee is Rs 2,000 {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, First Schedule}}. After the work, apply to amend the licence {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 146}}; the amendment fee is Rs 2,000 {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, First Schedule}}, plus any amount by which the licence fee for the amended form would exceed the fee first paid {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 147(2)}}.
- **New licensee:** the holder may apply, before the licence expires, to transfer it to another person {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 156}}; the fee is Rs 2,000 {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, First Schedule}}.

For your own case, ask PESO, the oil company or an advocate.
