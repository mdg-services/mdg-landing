## Who may sell petrol and diesel?

The Motor Spirit and High Speed Diesel Control Order 2005 decides it. A dealer is a person appointed by an oil company to buy, store and sell petrol and diesel {{MS and HSD Control Order 2005, cl 2(d)}}. No person other than a dealer or an oil company may be in the business of selling them {{MS and HSD Control Order 2005, cl 3(4)}}, and only those the Central Government authorises may market them to consumers or dealers {{MS and HSD Control Order 2005, cl 4}}. "Oil company" means Indian Oil, Hindustan Petroleum, Bharat Petroleum or a company the Central Government has authorised {{MS and HSD Control Order 2005, cl 2(h)}}.

## What does a company need to be authorised?

| Condition | What the rules say |
|---|---|
| Net worth | At least Rs 250 crore when applying, kept for the whole term {{MoPNG resolution 8 Nov 2019, para 6.1.1}} |
| Application fee | Rs 25 lakh, non-refundable {{MoPNG amendment 10 Aug 2026, para 6.2}} |
| Outlets | At least 100, with 5% in notified remote areas within five years {{MoPNG amendment 10 Aug 2026, para 6.2}} |
| Fuels | Both petrol and diesel at every outlet {{MoPNG amendment 10 Aug 2026, para 6.2}} |
| Alternate fuel | At least one new-generation fuel, such as CNG or EV charging, at every third outlet within three years {{MoPNG amendment 10 Aug 2026, para 6.1.3}} |

The amendment of 10 August 2026 kept the net worth condition and asks for a yearly affidavit before 30 June {{MoPNG amendment 10 Aug 2026, para 6.1.1}}. For both retail and bulk sales the net worth asked is Rs 500 crore {{PIB 4 Aug 2020}}.

## What can a person do?

Become a dealer of an authorised company. The public sector companies pick dealers through [one common process](@dealership-process); private companies such as [Nayara Energy](@dealership-nayara), [Jio-bp](@dealership-jio-bp) and [Shell](@dealership-shell) run their own. The list of companies is on [petrol pump companies in India](@dealership-fuel-companies).

A dealership comes only through the company's own advertisement. The oil companies say they have appointed no agent, and that payments go only through their official portal {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. A website or caller offering a "private petrol pump licence" for a fee is a warning sign.
