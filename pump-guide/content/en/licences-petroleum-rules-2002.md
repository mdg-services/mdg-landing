## What is the latest amendment?

PESO's Petroleum Rules menu lists amendment pages for 2018, 2019, 2021 and 2024 {{PESO Petroleum Rules 2002 page}}. The newest is the Petroleum (Amendment) Rules 2024, GSR 154(E), 4 March 2024. It changed, for example, rule 144, so a Form XIV licence that is part of a CNG station needs no District Authority NOC {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, rule 144}}. PESO also lists a notification of 29 March 2026, an exemption under rule 201 for distributing PDS kerosene, not an amendment {{Gazette notification 29 Mar 2026}}. Ask PESO if anything newer exists.

For your own pump, ask PESO, the oil company or an advocate.
