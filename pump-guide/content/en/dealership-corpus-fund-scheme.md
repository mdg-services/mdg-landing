## What the company provides

1. **The outlet.** A ready retail outlet with the required facilities, built at the company's cost on the land the candidate offers {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.9}}.
2. **Working capital.** A loan for one full operating cycle, equal to 7 days of sales, at first for at most 18 KL of petrol and 18 KL of diesel, worked out separately for each {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.9}}.
3. **Repayment.** The loan and its interest are recovered in 100 monthly instalments starting from the 13th month after the outlet opens {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.9}}.

An additional loan is possible two to four years after opening, if there has been no late repayment and sales are at least 50 percent above the projected second-year volume; it too is capped at 18 KL of each fuel {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.9}}. Where this sits among other loans: [government loan schemes](@money-loan-schemes) and [working capital](@money-working-capital).

## What a CFS row looks like

In Bharat Petroleum's Bihar advertisement of June 2023, a rural SC location in Araria showed site type CFS, draw of lots, and zero for working capital, building money and fixed fee, with a Rs 2 lakh security deposit {{BPCL Bihar advertisement 2023}}. The brochure lists no fixed fee or minimum bid for CFS sites {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}}.

## What the dealer still pays and does

| Item | Regular | Rural |
|---|---|---|
| Application fee (SC/ST) | Rs 3,000 | Rs 2,500 |
| Refundable security deposit (SC/ST) | Rs 3 lakh | Rs 2 lakh |

Fees from {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.12}}, deposits from {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.20}}. The dealer hands over the land developed, with entry, exit and walls {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8}}, and pays a licence fee of Rs 435.83 per KL of petrol and Rs 363.19 per KL of diesel, including GST {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8}}. A leased plot's deed must allow sub-lease to the company {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.

## Limits to know

A person whose family unit already holds a Corpus Fund or company-owned dealership cannot get another such site, under the multiple dealership rule {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.10}}. Who counts as SC or ST, and the certificates, are on [reserved categories](@dealership-reservation). The figures are from the June 2023 brochure as on 26 November 2026; each advertisement and the company's office have the final word. A dealership comes only through the oil companies' advertisements; MDG Services does not sell or arrange one.
