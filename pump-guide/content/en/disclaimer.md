## Who writes these pages

MDG Services writes these pages, in plain words, from official sources. We do not sell, allot, book or arrange petrol pump dealerships, land, licences or approvals. Dealerships are given only by the oil companies, who advertise the locations themselves {{HPCL dealer selection brochure, June 2023}}. The oil companies say they have appointed no agency or person to select dealers, and that no one is authorised to ask candidates for money at any stage; fees and deposits are paid only through their official website {{BPCL notice to petrol pump applicants}}. If someone asks you for money to get you a dealership, do not pay, and tell the oil company or the police.

## How we check facts, and what to do if something is out of date

Every fact names its source: a law, a rule, a standard, a government notice or an oil company's own guideline. We open the source on the day we write and show that date on the page. If we cannot find an official source for a point, we leave the point out and tell you where to look.

Rules, fees and guidelines change, and oil companies and states do not all follow the same rule. If you see something out of date, write to us at the address in the full disclaimer below and we will check it and correct it. These pages are general information, not legal, tax or financial advice.
