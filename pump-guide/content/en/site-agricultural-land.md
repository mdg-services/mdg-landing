Short answer: agricultural land is not used as it is. The oil companies expect it to be converted to non-agricultural use first. This page shows what two oil companies' land notices say, as on 20 November 2026. It is not legal advice; for your plot, ask an advocate and the state revenue office.

## What do the oil companies say?

HPCL's Rajasthan land notice prefers land that already has non-agricultural conversion, particularly commercial conversion. Those offering agricultural land shall convert it for use of petrol pump and allied purposes at their own expense and cost before registration of the sale deed or lease deed {{HPCL land notice Rajasthan 2025, point 8}}. The bidder also arranges clearances such as Non-Agricultural Permission {{HPCL land notice Rajasthan 2025, point 4}}.

BPCL's land notice for retail outlets says the bidder arranges statutory clearances such as Non-Agricultural conversion, and that those offering agricultural land convert it, more particularly commercially, at their own expenses and costs {{BPCL land notice, points d and i}}.

These notices invite offers of land to the company by sale or lease. HPCL's says it is not for dealership {{HPCL land notice Rajasthan 2025, heading}}. BPCL's page has no date, so check its current notice for your state.

## What about a dealer-owned site?

BPCL's June 2023 dealer selection brochure asks for land registered in the applicant's name or leased for the advertised period {{BPCL DSG 2023, land clause c}}, and, when the company asks, a revenue record confirming ownership {{BPCL DSG 2023, land clause e}}. The clauses I read do not say whether conversion is needed. Ask the oil company in writing, and read the advertisement for your location.

## Who decides if conversion is allowed?

The state. Land-use law and conversion fees differ from state to state, and this guide states none. In practice, ask the state revenue office, such as the tehsil, and an advocate, before you pay for land or sign a lease. For land size see [land size](@site-land-size), and for ownership and lease see [land and lease](@site-lease).
