## What the law says

As on 21 November 2026, the Motor Vehicles Act 1988 says every person above four years of age, driving, riding or being carried on a motorcycle, must wear protective headgear in a public place {{Motor Vehicles Act 1988, s.129}}. A Sikh wearing a turban is exempt, and the helmet must be securely fastened {{Motor Vehicles Act 1988, s.129}}. Driving a motorcycle against this is punishable by a fine of one thousand rupees and three months' disqualification from holding a licence {{Motor Vehicles Act 1988, s.194D}}.

The section puts the duty on the rider. It does not mention petrol pumps.

## Where "no petrol" comes from

A refusal at the pump comes from a local order, not from the Motor Vehicles Act. We opened one official example, from November 2024:

| Place | Order | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| Kamrup district, Assam | District magistrate's order under section 163 BNSS, dated 20 November 2024 | No pump sells fuel to a car driver or two-wheeler rider without seatbelt or helmet {{Kamrup DM order 20 Nov 2024}}; it says it stays in force until further order {{Kamrup DM order 20 Nov 2024}} |

## Such orders are temporary

A section 163 BNSS order cannot remain in force for more than two months from the day it is made. The State Government may, by notification, extend it by a further period of at most six months {{BNSS 2023, s.163}}. The Kamrup order is dated 20 November 2024. On these limits it would have run out by about July 2025 unless extended or made again, and we have seen no later order. So this page does not say there is a rule in Kamrup now. Check whether any order is in force in your district today.

We could not open an official order for any other place, so we name no other state or district.

## How to check your own district

1. Look for orders on your district's website (.nic.in or .gov.in).
2. Ask the district magistrate's office or the police.
3. Check the date, any end date and any extension. Orders can be changed or ended.
