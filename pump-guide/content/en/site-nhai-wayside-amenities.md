This page is as on 26 November 2026, from the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) policy of 11 February 2021. It is not legal advice. MDG Services does not sell or arrange highway land, tenders or dealerships.

## What is a wayside amenity?

A rest stop with food, shops and toilets for car or truck travellers, or both {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 4.1}}. The policy aims for one every 40 to 60 km in each direction {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 3.1}}, at least 1 km from a toll plaza {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 3.3}}, on land of preferably about 1 hectare {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 3.4}}. At least 60% of the land stays open {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 4.3}}.

## Highway authority land: the lease

| Site | Longest lease |
|---|---|
| Empty site, the developer builds | 30 years |
| Amenity already built by the authority | 15 years |

Source: {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 5.1}}. The site goes to the highest yearly rent or revenue share; the bid can be rent, revenue share, or rent with a fixed share. A bid is accepted only if the authority's yearly income is at least 5% of the land cost {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 5.1}}. The rent rises each year with an index of 70% wholesale and 30% consumer prices {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 5.1}}. At the end the site returns to the authority; the old developer may keep it by matching a higher bid that is within 10% {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 5.1}}.

The developer pays for, builds and runs the amenity, and gets six months from the award for approvals before the lease starts {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 7}}.

## Private land beside a highway

A landowner can run a wayside amenity on a co-branding basis, with no access fee charged by the highway authority {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 5.2}}. A pump on your own land is a different matter: see [access permission](@site-highway-access).

## Where a fuel station fits

A fuel station (petrol, diesel, CNG or other) and EV charging are permitted, not mandatory, facilities {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, Annexure-2}}. The authority may help with clearances, but the developer is responsible for them, including the oil companies' requirements {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 6.5}}. Selling fuel still needs an oil company and a PESO licence; see [licences](@licences-peso).

## Is the 2021 policy still in force?

As on 26 November 2026, MoRTH's compendium of circulars lists it, dated 11 February 2021, and lists no later wayside amenities policy {{MoRTH circulars compendium}}. It did not apply to tenders already under way in 2021 {{MoRTH WSA policy 11.02.2021, para 3}}. Each tender sets its own terms, so read it in full.
