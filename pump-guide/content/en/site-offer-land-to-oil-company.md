This page is for landowners, as on 26 November 2026. It is not legal advice; ask an advocate before you sign a sale or lease deed. MDG Services does not buy, sell or broker land.

## A land notice is not a dealership

HPCL's notice says plainly that it is not for appointing retail outlet dealers, and that land owners have no right to partnership, employment or commission in running the pump {{HPCL land notice NZ 2025, note 1}}. BPCL's terms say the outlet will be run only by the company, though it may later change the outlet to a dealership {{BPCL land notice, point s}}. If you want to run a pump yourself, read [company-owned or dealer-owned](@dealership-company-owned-vs-dealer-owned) instead.

## Sale or lease

| | HPCL, Delhi, Haryana, Uttarakhand, Sep 2025 | BPCL land terms (undated) |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer | Outright sale or lease | Outright sale or lease |
| Lease period | At least 19 years 11 months | 30 years preferred |
| What you quote | Monthly rent per sq m and its increase, or sale price per sq m | Sale price, or monthly rent and lease period |
| Plot size | Set per location: 400 to 4,080 sq m in this notice | Set per location |

Sources: {{HPCL land notice NZ 2025, heading and financial bid}} {{BPCL land notice, heading and financial bid}}. BPCL pays by cheque or NEFT at registration of a sale deed, or monthly for a lease {{BPCL land notice, point m}}.

## What the companies check

- A clear, marketable title; HPCL asks for title search documents of the last 30 years {{HPCL land notice NZ 2025, notes 11, 12}}.
- You arrange clearances such as urban land ceiling, non-agricultural permission, income tax clearance and nil encumbrance {{HPCL land notice NZ 2025, note 13}} {{BPCL land notice, point d}}. Agricultural land is converted at your cost; see [agricultural land](@site-agricultural-land).
- No high-tension line over the plot: HPCL rejects a plot with a line above 11 kV {{HPCL land notice NZ 2025, note 22}}.
- On a national highway, the plot must meet the road ministry's access norms {{HPCL land notice NZ 2025, note 4}}; see [highway access](@site-highway-access).
- Green belt or forest land is not considered {{HPCL land notice NZ 2025, note 19}}.
- The cost of filling the plot to road level and shifting lines is added when offers are compared {{HPCL land notice NZ 2025, note 18}} {{BPCL land notice, point l}}.
- Neighbours with joining plots can offer together through one registered power of attorney {{HPCL land notice NZ 2025, note 14}}.

## Where to find the notices

HPCL lists its land notices state by state on its website {{HPCL land page}}. Each notice has its own last date. Brokers and property dealers need not apply {{HPCL land notice NZ 2025, note 2}} {{BPCL land notice, point n}}.
