## What makes an outlet rural

The oil companies identify each location themselves {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.1}}. A regular outlet is on a national or state highway, or in an urban or semi-urban area within municipal limits {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.1}}. A rural outlet is in a rural area, not on a highway, outside municipal limits {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.1}}. In a Lok Sabha reply the petroleum minister called Kisan Seva Kendras (also Gramin pumps) low-cost rural outlets for farmers, with 12,183 across the country on 1 January 2017, the latest count we found, which is nearly ten years old {{PIB 6 Feb 2017}}. The brochure does not use the name Kisan Seva Kendra. The advertisement for each location says which type it is.

## What is the same

One brochure covers both types {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.1}}. Age, education, citizenship and PAN conditions, the reserved shares and the disqualifications do not depend on the type {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.2, cl.4}}. Selection is by draw of lots or bidding according to the site type {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14}}. See [who can apply](@dealership-eligibility) and the [selection process](@dealership-process). Land rules also read the same: the advertisement states the minimum frontage, area and depth {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14}}.

## What differs: the money

All figures include GST where the brochure says so, as on 21 November 2026.

| Item | Regular | Rural |
|---|---|---|
| Application fee, other locations / OBC / SC/ST | Rs 10,000 / 5,000 / 3,000 {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.12}} | Rs 8,000 / 4,000 / 2,500 {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.12}} |
| Fixed fee, dealer-owned sites | Rs 15 lakh {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}} | Rs 5 lakh {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}} |
| Minimum bid, company-owned sites | Rs 30 lakh {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}} | Rs 10 lakh {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}} |
| Security deposit, Open / OBC / SC/ST | Rs 5 / 4 / 3 lakh {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.20}} | Rs 4 / 3 / 2 lakh {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.20}} |

The application fee is not refunded. The fixed fee and the bid are not refundable {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.12, cl.13}}. The security deposit is interest free and refundable, and is paid before the letter of appointment {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.20}}.

## What decides the rest of the cost

Who pays for land, building and equipment depends on the site type, not on rural or regular: on company-owned sites the dealer provides developed land and a generator, and the company provides tanks and dispensers; on dealer-owned sites the dealer also builds the office, driveway and canopy {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8}}. The brochure gives no total investment figure; it says the approximate investment is shown in each advertisement {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8}}.

Dealerships come only through the oil companies' own advertisements. Paying anyone for one is a sign of fraud.
