## What are the conditions to apply?

The table shows the June 2023 brochure as on 9 November 2026 {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4, cl.5, cl.8(C), cl.24}}. In a partnership firm, each partner must meet the individual conditions {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(viii)}}.

| Condition | Individual | Company, society or trust |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Indian citizen and resident of India (182 days or more in the previous financial year), valid PAN | Registered in India |
| Age | 21 to 60 on the date of application | Registered at least 3 years before applying |
| Education | Passed 10th class | Not asked |
| Money | Finance stated in the advertisement | Net profit in the previous 3 financial years, certified by a chartered accountant |
| Land | Owned, leased for at least 19 years 11 months, or firmly offered (SC/ST locations also take applicants who offer land later); family land counts with an affidavit | In the entity's own name |
| Category | Any you belong to | Open only |

## Which categories are reserved?

In all states except Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Nagaland and Mizoram, which have their own shares {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.2}}:

| Category | Share of locations |
|---|---|
| SC/ST | 22.50% |
| OBC | 27% |
| Open | 50.50% |

These include 8% for defence personnel and some government and public-sector personnel, as the brochure defines them, 1% for sportspersons, freedom fighters and agricultural credit societies, and 3% for physically handicapped applicants, who need at least 40% disability {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.2, cl.4(vii)}}.

## What disqualifies an applicant?

- You or your family unit hold a company-owned ("A" or "CC") dealership, or a letter of intent for one {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.10(A)}}.
- A conviction for an offence involving moral turpitude, or an economic offence {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.10(A)}}.
- Being a signatory to a dealership ended for proven malpractice {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.10(A)}}.
- Applying as a Hindu Undivided Family {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.10(A)}}.
- False information: the application can be rejected {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.22}}.

## What changed from the 2018 guideline?

Age, education, lease period and reserved shares read the same in the 24 November 2018 brochure {{Selection brochure Nov 2018, cl.4}}. The 2023 text adds agricultural credit societies, accepts PAN and Aadhaar cards as proof of age, and says Group 3 (no land offered) applies only to SC/ST locations {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4, cl.5}}.
