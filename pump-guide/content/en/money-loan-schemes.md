## How much money a location asks for

Each advertisement row states the working capital and the building money the applicant must arrange {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.24}}, and the applicant undertakes to arrange them {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8}}. In Bharat Petroleum's Bihar advertisement of June 2023, a regular CC row listed Rs 32 lakh and Rs 12 lakh, and a rural dealer-owned row Rs 16 lakh and Rs 35 lakh {{BPCL Bihar advertisement 2023}}. Fees and deposits come on top: see [what it costs](@money). How to read a row: [reading the advertisement](@dealership-reading-advertisement).

## What each scheme says, as on 26 November 2026

| | Mudra (PMMY) | Stand-Up India | Corpus Fund |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who | Non-corporate, non-farm small units | SC, ST or women, new business | Dealers at SC/ST locations |
| Amount | Up to Rs 20 lakh | Rs 10 lakh to Rs 1 crore | 7 days of sales |
| Given by | Banks, NBFCs, MFIs | Scheduled commercial banks | The oil company |
| Subsidy | None, says Mudra | Not described | Not a subsidy, a loan |

Sources: {{MUDRA FAQ}}, {{Stand-Up India FAQ}}, {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.9}}.

## Mudra

Mudra has four sizes: Shishu up to Rs 50,000, Kishor up to Rs 5 lakh, Tarun up to Rs 10 lakh, and Tarun Plus up to Rs 20 lakh for those who have taken and repaid a Tarun loan {{MUDRA FAQ}}. Mudra itself refinances lenders and does not lend directly {{MUDRA FAQ}}, and it says it uses no agents {{MUDRA home page}}.

## Stand-Up India

The loan covers term loan and working capital together, up to 85 percent of the project cost, and the borrower brings 15 percent as margin money {{Stand-Up India FAQ, Stand-Up India home page}}. Repayment is up to 7 years, with at most 18 months before it starts {{Stand-Up India FAQ}}. A firm qualifies if an SC, ST or woman entrepreneur holds at least 51 percent {{Stand-Up India FAQ}}.

## Corpus Fund

Interest is SBI's one-year MCLR plus 1 percent or 11 percent a year, whichever is lower {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.9}}. Details on [the Corpus Fund page](@dealership-corpus-fund-scheme).

The advertisement itself calls a dealership a business proposition with normal business risks, and promises no profit {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.24}}. Ask your bank and a chartered accountant before borrowing.
