Many drivers ask what they earn when they fuel. Each oil company runs its own scheme, and what its own pages state is below, as on 26 November 2026. Rates and partners can change, so the app is the final word.

## HPCL: HP Pay and PAYBACK

HPCL's HP Pay is an app for paying for fuel and lubes at its pumps by card, wallet, UPI or net banking {{HPCL HP Pay page}}. Its reward programme is powered by PAYBACK, a multi-partner loyalty scheme {{HPCL HP Pay page}}.

| Action | Points stated |
|---|---|
| Recharge using HP Pay | 3 points per Rs 100 {{HPCL HP Pay page}} |
| Sale | 1 point per Rs 100 {{HPCL HP Pay page}} |

Points can be redeemed with partners and at all HPCL pumps {{HPCL HP Pay page}}. HPCL also lists a digital incentive of 0.75% on DriveTrack Plus recharge for fleet owners {{HPCL loyalty page}}.

## BPCL: SmartDrive and Petromiles

SmartDrive sits inside the HelloBPCL app. It pays by dynamic QR code or by mobile number and OTP, and gives cashback and Petromiles, BPCL's loyalty points, that you can redeem later {{BPCL SmartDrive page}}. The page does not state how many points a rupee earns, so we give no figure.

## What the pages leave out

- Expiry, minimum redemption and eligibility are not on the pages we read; the terms sit in each app.
- Neither page says what a dealer must do for these schemes. In practice the points are the oil company's, so a dealer with a question asks the company's sales officer.
- This page covers only HPCL and BPCL, whose own pages we read.

Paying by card or app is explained in [paying by UPI or card](@customers-digital-payments). For fleet cards, see [fleet cards](@money-fleet-cards).
