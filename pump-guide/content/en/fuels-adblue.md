AdBlue is the name many drivers use for diesel exhaust fluid (DEF). This page explains what it is and what the oil companies and the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) publish about it, as on 20 November 2026.

## What is AdBlue?

BIS describes the Indian standard IS 17042 (Part 1) as defining quality for AUS 32, an aqueous urea solution used in diesel engines to reduce nitrogen oxide (NOx) emissions by Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) {{BIS IS 17042 summary}}. In an SCR system the solution is injected into the exhaust stream, where NOx becomes nitrogen and water vapour {{BIS IS 17042 summary}}. So it goes in its own tank on the vehicle, not in the fuel tank. HP Lubricants lists urea content of 31.8 to 33.2 per cent for its DEF {{HP Lubricants DEF page}}.

## Why do BS-VI diesels use it?

BS-VI fuel has at most 10 ppm sulphur, against 50 ppm in BS-IV, and this enabled advanced emission control such as SCR in BS-VI vehicles {{PIB 25 Jul 2022}}. Not every diesel uses SCR. Check your vehicle manual.

## What do the oil companies list?

| Item | What the page says |
|---|---|
| BPCL, MAK AdBlue AUS 32 | For SCR systems in BS-IV and BS-VI diesel vehicles; 10 litre Rs 1000, 20 litre Rs 1850; specification ISO 22241:2019 (Part 1) {{BPCL AdBlue page}} |
| HP DEF | Made as per ISO 22241 and IS 17042 (Part 1) {{HP Lubricants DEF page}} |

BPCL's prices are as shown on its page on 20 November 2026; the page carries no price date, and its footer says it was last updated on 25 November 2025 {{BPCL AdBlue page}}. Prices can change, so ask where you buy.

## Is it sold at the pump?

In practice, some outlets stock packs, but no source says every pump does. Both companies list DEF with their lubricants, and HP's page points to its lubricant distributors {{HP Lubricants DEF page}}. A dealer sells only what the oil company supplies; ask the company in writing.

## How should it be handled?

BPCL advises storing it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, keeping it free of fuel, oil, dust and dirt, and using dedicated handling equipment {{BPCL AdBlue page}}. For diesel itself see [diesel grades](@fuels-diesel-grades).
