## The short answer

Diesel is petroleum Class B {{PESO parking SOP}}. As on 27 November 2026, no licence is needed for Class B stored non-bulk, up to 2,500 litres, in receptacles up to 1,000 litres {{PESO Petroleum Rules SOP}}. This matches [diesel in a drum](@customers-diesel-in-drum). Non-bulk means a container of up to 1,000 litres; above that is bulk {{PESO Petroleum Rules SOP}}.

## Which licence for how much Class B diesel

| Storage of Class B | What is needed | Issued by |
|---|---|---|
| Non-bulk, up to 2,500 litres | No licence | None {{PESO Petroleum Rules SOP}} |
| Non-bulk, above 2,500 up to 25,000 litres | Form XIII | District Authority {{PESO Petroleum Rules SOP}} |
| Non-bulk, above 25,000 litres | Form XVI | PESO Circle office {{PESO Petroleum Rules SOP}} |
| Bulk, in an installation | Form XV | PESO {{PESO Petroleum Rules SOP}} |

An installation is premises specially prepared for storing petroleum in bulk {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 2(1)}}. Tanks at a pump that fuels vehicles use Form XIV; see [consumer pump](@dealership-consumer-pump).

## Factory, farm, generator or building site

The sources we read set limits by quantity and container size, not by user. Read your tank size against the table, then ask the PESO Circle office or an advocate which form fits. For a new PESO licence (a few forms excepted), the District Authority also gives a no-objection certificate for the site {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 144}}.

## Petrol is different

Petrol is Class A. No licence is needed only up to 30 litres not meant for sale; above that, up to 300 litres, is Form XII from the District Authority {{PESO Petroleum Rules SOP}}. See [petroleum classes](@licences-petroleum-classes).

## What to check

Fees and the application route are on the PESO site. A local order may add limits; we have not checked any.
