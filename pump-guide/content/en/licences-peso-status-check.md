Two different people ask this question. A dealer wants to know where his own application stands. A buyer, a lender or an oil company officer wants to know whether a pump's licence is real. PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation) has a page for the first. For the second, it has only a public search form. This page says what is on PESO's site as on 20 November 2026.

## How does an applicant check status?

PESO's Application Status page says applicants and licensees can check the status of their applications, or download issued letters, through four links in their online profile: "Application Status", "Granted/Discrepancy", "Discrepancy/Interview Letter" and "View Letter". They appear after logging in to the right portal {{PESO Application Status page}}.

Applications under the Petroleum Rules 2002 sit in the same portal as Calcium Carbide, Gas Cylinders and SMPV(U) applications. Explosives Rules 2008 and Ammonium Nitrate Rules 2012 applications use a different portal {{PESO Application Status page}}. Pick the one for your rule. Keep your login private; nobody who helps you needs your password.

Steps, in order:

1. Open PESO's Application Status page and choose the Petroleum Rules link.
2. Log in with your own account.
3. Open "Application Status" for the stage, or "View Letter" for an issued letter.
4. If a "Discrepancy" letter is listed, read it first; it says what PESO wants corrected.

PESO also lists "Apply through NSWS", the National Single Window System portal, among its menu pages {{PESO Apply through NSWS page}}. For how a new application is filed, see [the PESO licence page](@licences-peso).

## Can anyone check a licence without logging in?

PESO's site has a public search page, "Public Domain". Its form has a type of query ("General"), a Status box, a Rule list that includes Petroleum Rules 2002, a Form list, a Form Action list (Approval, Licence, Renewal due next 2 months, Others), a Certificate No box and a verification-text box {{PESO Public Domain search}}.

We have not run a search, so we cannot tell you what it shows for a petrol pump. If you are checking a pump, use the licence number marked on the premises {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 133}}, and note that a licensee must produce the licence, or an authenticated copy, to an Inspector on demand {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 159}}.

## What about renewal and expiry?

Most licences run to 31 December of the year they are granted or renewed up to, for at most three years; Form III and Form XVII licences run at most one year {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 142}}. See [validity and renewal](@licences-renewal) for deadlines.

## If something does not match

Call PESO's helpline, 1800-233-9011, shown on its site {{PESO Application Status page}}, or ask the PESO office for your area. For legal questions, ask an advocate.
