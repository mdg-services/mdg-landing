## What can I check at the pump?

As on 17 November 2026, these dealer duties are from the guideline for public sector oil company dealers (MDG 2024). A private company's dealer should ask that company for its own rules.

1. The meter must be back at zero after each delivery, and the zero setting is shown to the customer before the next one {{MDG 2024, clause 1.4.2(f)}}.
2. The dealer checks every nozzle daily before sales and records the quantity used for testing {{MDG 2024, clause 1.4.2(d)}}.
3. The dealer keeps a 5-litre measure verified by Weights & Measures, for pump testing only {{MDG 2024, clause 1.4.2(b)}}.
4. The pump's licence allows filling a standard capacity measure to test the pump's discharge {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 16}}.

You can ask at the pump for the test. See also [the metrology page](@equipment-metrology).

## How much error is allowed?

For deliveries of two litres or more the table figure is 0.5 per cent, which is 25 millilitres on 5 litres (our arithmetic), but a minimum error figure in the same rule can make the limit higher {{LM (General) Rules 2011, Eighth Schedule Part IV}}.

## What should I do if I think I was short-changed?

1. Note the date, time, pump, nozzle, product and amount. This is practice, not a rule.
2. Ask for the complaint book. The dealer must keep it and give it on demand {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(iv)}}.
3. Note the dealer's and oil company's contact numbers shown at the outlet {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(viii)}}.

## Where can I complain?

| Route | Contact |
|---|---|
| Oil company | Number on the outlet board; Bharat Petroleum SmartLine 1800 22 4344 {{BPCL SmartLine page}}; others on their websites |
| Other public sector channels | Websites, apps, email, CPGRAMS {{PIB 23 Jul 2026}} |
| National Consumer Helpline | 1915 or 1800 11 4000, 8 am to 8 pm daily except national holidays {{NCH contact page}} |
| District Commission | May be filed electronically {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.35}}; two-year limit {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.69}} |

In practice, a customer can also tell the state's Legal Metrology department. For a legal question ask an advocate; we cannot say what any complaint will bring.
