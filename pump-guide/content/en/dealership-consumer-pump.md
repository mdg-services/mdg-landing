## The short answer

As on 27 November 2026, HPCL says it commissioned 30 consumer pumps in 2024-25 to meet the bulk diesel needs of institutional customers {{HPCL Annual Report 2024-25}}. In practice a consumer pump serves one customer, not the public: the site holds the tank and dispensing unit, and the oil company supplies the diesel on its own terms.

## Licence

PESO's FAQ lists "retail outlet / consumer pump" together under Form XIV {{PESO FAQ Aug 2024}}, issued by PESO's Circle or Sub Circle office {{PESO Petroleum Rules SOP}}. Documents named include a no-objection certificate from the District Authority under rule 144 and a CPCB-guidelines undertaking that there is no school, hospital of 10 beds or more or residential area (as per local law), and no water body within 50 metres {{PESO FAQ Aug 2024}}. Storage limits are on [diesel storage licence](@licences-diesel-storage).

## Bulk price and retail price

Pricing of bulk diesel was deregulated from 18 January 2013; oil companies decide it from international prices, the exchange rate and market conditions, with discount schemes {{PIB 28 Mar 2022}}. The government gave these all-India prices, per kilolitre, before freight, discounts, duties and taxes {{PIB 28 Mar 2022}}:

| From | Bulk diesel (Rs/KL) | Retail diesel (Rs/KL) |
|---|---|---|
| 16 Jan 2022 | 48,942.38 | 49,333.68 |
| 1 Feb 2022 | 53,488.62 | 49,341.91 |
| 16 Feb 2022 | 56,889.37 | 49,341.91 |

These are 2022 figures, not today's price. On 12 June 2026 the ministry said retail diesel was around 40 rupees a litre cheaper than bulk, and a temporary order told industrial and institutional customers to source diesel through consumer pumps, not retail outlets {{PIB 12 Jun 2026}}. The ministry withdrew that order from 1 July 2026 {{PIB 29 Jun 2026}}. This page gives no current price gap.

## How to approach an oil company

HPCL's Direct Sales unit sells bulk fuels directly to industrial customers through 18 regional offices, and lists guidance on statutory approvals among its services {{HPCL Direct Sales page}}. BPCL has an Industrial and Commercial enquiry form and an office list by state {{BPCL I&C enquiry page}}. Ask each company for its terms.
