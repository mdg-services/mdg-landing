## The three groups

| Group | Your land position on the date of application |
|---|---|
| 1 | Owned, or leased for at least 19 years 11 months, by you or your family; or allotted by a government body for a petrol pump |
| 2 | A firm offer from another owner to sell or lease it to you |
| 3 | No land offered; SC/ST locations only |

Source for the table: {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}. The plot must lie in the advertised stretch and meet its frontage, depth and area {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(I)}}. See [plot size](@site-land-size) and [leasing land](@site-lease).

## Land shared with others

If you or your family own the plot jointly with someone else, it still counts as owned when your share covers the land the company needs, and every co-owner gives consent by affidavit or registered power of attorney {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}. If a co-owner's consent is missing after selection, you get 21 days to bring it {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}. One plot offered by two applicants for the same location gets both applications rejected {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.

## Who goes first

The draw of lots or bid opening is held first among Group 1. Group 2 is reached only if Group 1 has nobody, or its applicants are disqualified or their letter of intent is withdrawn {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}. At SC/ST locations, Group 3 is asked for land, within 90 days, only if nobody from the first two groups is selected {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}. More on that in [petrol pump without your own land](@dealership-without-land).

## Declare the right group

You state your group in the form, and the company checks it. Land found outside the declared group is not considered and you become ineligible (at SC/ST locations you move to Group 3); a Group 2 applicant whose land is really Group 1 stays in Group 2 {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}. The brochure advises having an advocate check your land's group before you apply {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}. A wrong group cannot be corrected later {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.23}}.

The company makes no promise to take your land {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}. A dealership comes only through the oil companies' advertisements; MDG Services does not sell or arrange one.
