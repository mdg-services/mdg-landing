## What do the rules say about wrong fuel?

As on 24 November 2026, we found no petrol pump rule or oil company guideline that tells a customer what to do with the vehicle, or says who pays, when petrol goes into a diesel vehicle or the other way round. We leave that out rather than guess. Ask the vehicle maker, a service centre or your insurer about the engine and the tank.

What the sources do state is how a complaint can be made.

## What can I ask for at the pump?

These dealer duties come from the guideline for public sector oil company dealers (MDG 2024). A private company's dealer should ask that company for its own rules.

1. The dealer must keep a suggestion and complaint book and make it readily available on demand {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(iv)}}.
2. The dealer must display his own name and number and the name and number of the oil company person to contact for complaints {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(viii)}}.

In practice, note the date, time, pump, nozzle, the fuel you asked for and the fuel filled, and keep the bill. This is practice, not a rule.

## Does the consumer law cover it?

The Consumer Protection Act 2019 defines deficiency in a service to include any fault in the manner of performance, and an act of negligence that causes loss or injury to the consumer {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.2(11)}}. Whether a case of wrong fuel fits that is for a District Commission to decide on the facts.

| Step | What the Act says |
|---|---|
| Where to file | A District Commission, by the consumer; may be filed electronically {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.35}} |
| Time limit | Two years from the cause of action, unless sufficient cause is shown {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.69}} |
| What it can order | Among others, removing a deficiency in service, or compensation for loss due to negligence {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.39}} |

We cannot say what any complaint will bring. For a legal question, ask an advocate. See also [quality complaints](@customers-quality-complaint) and [short delivery](@customers-short-delivery).

## Where else can I complain?

The National Consumer Helpline takes calls on 1915 or 1800 11 4000, daily except national holidays, 8 am to 8 pm {{NCH contact page}}.
