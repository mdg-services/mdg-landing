## What can a District Commission order?

As on 18 November 2026, where it is satisfied that goods have a defect named in the complaint, or that a service or unfair trade practice allegation is proved, the Act lets a District Commission direct the opposite party to do one or more of the following {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.39(1)}}:

- remove the defect, or replace the goods;
- return the price paid, with interest as decided;
- pay compensation for loss or injury from negligence;
- stop an unfair trade practice.

The Commission decides what, if anything, to order. We cannot say what any complaint will bring.

## Where and how do I file?

1. File in the District Commission covering where the other side does business, where the cause of action arose, in whole or part, or where you live or work {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.34(2)}}.
2. A complaint may be filed electronically, with a fee as prescribed {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.35}}. The current fee is on the consumer commission's own site; we give no figure.
3. File within two years of the cause of action, unless you show sufficient cause for delay {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.69}}.

## What happens next?

The Commission ordinarily decides admission within twenty-one days, and a complaint left undecided is deemed admitted {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.36}}. It may offer mediation if a settlement seems possible {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.37}}. The other side is asked for its version within thirty days, extendable by up to fifteen {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.38(2)}}.

## Is there a step before court?

The National Consumer Helpline registers grievances on 1915 or 1800 11 4000, 8 am to 8 pm daily except national holidays {{NCH contact page}}. First steps at the pump and with the oil company are on [the short delivery page](@customers-short-delivery). For bad fuel and sample tests see [the quality complaint page](@customers-quality-complaint). In practice, keep the receipt, a photo of the meter or board, and dates. For a legal question ask an advocate.
