This page reports what the public sector oil companies' dealer guideline (MDG 2024) says, as on 23 November 2026. It binds those companies' outlets only; a private company's dealer should ask that company. Licence and statutory records are set by their own laws; we found no further register named in the sources we checked, so none is added here. It is not legal advice.

## Which records does the guideline name?

| Record | What the guideline says |
|---|---|
| Daily stock register | Kept for all petroleum products, provided by the company {{MDG 2024, 1.3}} |
| Meter and dip record | Daily record of pump meter reading and of product and water dips of each tank, taken in the morning {{MDG 2024, 1.3}} |
| Water dip | Recorded daily as part of the stock register {{MDG 2024, 1.4.1}} |
| Density register | Morning density recorded before sales begin {{MDG 2024, 1.4.1}}; density after each decanting entered in the format of Annexure 1 {{MDG 2024, 1.2}} |
| Files at the outlet | Inspection reports, company letters, test reports and copies of invoices, ready for reference {{MDG 2024, 1.5}} |

## The list that inspections check

The guideline lists records whose non-maintenance is an irregularity {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.12}}:

1. Daily sales register of the oil company, and the density register, for the current and previous financial years.
2. Pump maintenance records, current and previous financial years.
3. Inspection records for the previous five years.
4. Purchase invoices and sales bills, current and previous financial years.
5. The selling licence, explosives licence and other licences needed to keep the outlet running.
6. Any other record the oil company specifies in writing.

For stock records, see [density checks](@running-density-check) and [inspections](@running-inspections).

## What happens if records are missing

| Case | Class and action |
|---|---|
| No records since the last inspection | Major: suspension of sales and supplies for 15 days, then 30 days, then termination {{MDG 2024, clause 8.3}} |
| Records since the last inspection kept, older ones missing | Minor: a warning letter first, then fines that rise with each instance {{MDG 2024, clause 8.4}} |
| No reference density at inspection | Major {{MDG 2024, clause 8.3}}; the morning density is to be recorded by 10 AM {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.9}} |

See [marketing discipline](@running-marketing-discipline) for the full penalty scheme. If the company issues a notice, [the dealer's reply and appeal route](@running-dealer-grievance) applies.
