Petrol gives off vapour, and a small static spark can light it. Earthing and bonding are how a pump stops that spark from forming. All as on 15 November 2026.

## What do earthing and bonding mean?

- **Earthing** joins a metal part to the ground, so any charge drains away.
- **Bonding** joins two metal parts to each other, so no spark jumps between them.

## What does the law require?

| Point | What the Petroleum Rules 2002 say |
|---|---|
| Earthing of equipment | All electrical systems, structures and major metal objects where petroleum is stored, loaded or unloaded must be efficiently earthed: not more than 4 ohms for electrical systems and equipment, 10 ohms for major metal objects {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 108}} |
| Joints | Joints in pipelines, valves, tanks and fittings must be electrically continuous, with not more than 1 ohm between joints {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 108}} |
| Storage tanks | A bulk tank must have at least two separate earth connections at opposite ends, with resistance to earth not above 7 ohms {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 127}} |
| Testing | Tank earth connections are tested by a competent person at least once in every twelve months, and the record kept at the premises {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 128}} |

Rule 127 is written for bulk tanks; ask PESO or your oil company how your licence applies it.

## What happens when a tanker unloads?

1. The petroleum enters the tank through a close-coupled, electrically continuous, sound hose {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 9}}.
2. The tanker's engine is stopped, its battery isolated, and its chassis bonded by a cable to the pipe it unloads into {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XI, condition 8}}.
3. The tanker carries a bonding cable at least 5 metres long with a clamp or clip at each end {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XI, condition 4}}.
4. No light capable of igniting vapour, and no smoking, near the tanker during transfer {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 11}}.

For loading a tank vehicle, rule 78 says the bonding and earthing connections stay on until loading is finished and the filling and dip pipes are closed {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 78}}.

The Petroleum Rules 2002 were amended on 4 March 2024; the amendment did not change the rules above {{G.S.R. 154(E), 4 Mar 2024}}. OISD standards for retail outlets are sold by OISD, and we could not open them; check the oil company's checklist for its own limits.
