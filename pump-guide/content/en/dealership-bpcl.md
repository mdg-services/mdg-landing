## Is there any way in except the advertisement?

No. BPCL releases an advertisement in the newspapers for each location, and the dealers page carries these advertisements, sorted by district, state or region {{BPCL dealers page}}. A dealership comes only through that advertisement. A message saying your application "has been approved" and asking for a registration charge is a known fraud {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. The OMCs have appointed no agency or person for selecting dealers {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. Nobody at MDG Services sells or arranges a dealership either.

## Step by step, as BPCL publishes it

| Step | Where it happens |
|---|---|
| See the advertisement for a location | Newspapers; BPCL's dealers page {{BPCL dealers page}} |
| Check eligibility and fee for that location | In the advertisement; eligibility differs by location {{BPCL dealers page}} |
| Apply and pay the fee | Online, on the dealer selection portal {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}} |
| Draw of lots or bid opening | As the brochure sets for the site type; see [the selection process](@dealership-process) |

BPCL's page also offers a specimen application form, and says it is valid only if filled according to the eligibility for that location and sent with the fee {{BPCL dealers page}}. Use the method the advertisement states. For who can apply, see [eligibility](@dealership-eligibility).

## Fees, contact and cost

The fee is Rs 10,000 regular and Rs 8,000 rural at locations not reserved for SC/ST or OBC, including GST, as on 14 November 2026 {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.12}}. This page gives no setup-cost figure. Make payments only on the portal {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. Report suspicious activity to the local police station {{Oil companies fraud notice}}.
