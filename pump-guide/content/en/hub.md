A petrol pump is run by a dealer, the person or firm that holds the dealership from an oil company and sells fuel on that company's terms. Whether you want to open a pump or already run one, start with the question you have. The guide has nine topics.

## What does this guide cover?

- [How do I get a dealership?](@dealership) Who can apply, how an oil company picks a dealer, and the route to a petrol pump.
- [What does it cost, and how does the dealer earn?](@money) What it costs to start, and how the dealer commission works.
- [Which licences does a pump need?](@licences) The licences, NOCs and consents, and who gives each one.
- [How much land, and what kind?](@site) Plot size, location and what gets a site rejected.
- [How is a pump kept safe from fire?](@safety) Fire equipment, earthing, signs and the daily checks.
- [How does a pump work, and who checks the equipment?](@equipment) Tanks, dispensers, nozzles, vapour recovery and measurement.
- [Who does what, and how does GST work?](@running) Staff, daily running and GST for a pump.
- [What are E20, CNG and EV charging?](@fuels) The fuels at the pump, and adding CNG or an EV charger.
- [Where does a customer complain?](@customers) Complaint routes, and how a dealer should answer.

## How are these pages written?

Every fact names its source and the date we checked it. Where oil companies or states differ, we say so and show a small table. If we cannot find an official source for a point, we leave it out and tell you where to look.

This guide is separate from the [MDG 2024 guide](/guides/), which explains one document for dealers. We do not sell, allot or arrange dealerships or licences. Read [who writes these pages and how we check them](@disclaimer).

## Where this comes from

- [Petroleum Rules 2002 (English), PESO](https://peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2019-12/PR-2002-English-full.pdf): the licensing rules behind the licence, safety and equipment pages.
- [Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024, Bharat Petroleum](https://www.bharatpetroleum.in/images/files/MDG-2024-Effective-24-10-2024.pdf): the public sector oil companies' guideline for their dealers.
- [Legal Metrology Acts and Rules, Department of Consumer Affairs](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/legal-metrology-act): the measurement rules behind the equipment page.
