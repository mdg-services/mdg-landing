When a rule says "Class A" or "Class B", it is not about quality or grade. It is a fire-risk label. Petroleum is sorted by its flash point, the lowest temperature at which it gives off a vapour that gives a momentary flash when ignited {{PESO SOP Jan 2025}}. The lower the flash point, the more carefully the law makes you store and move it. As on 19 November 2026.

## What are the three classes?

| Class | Flash point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A | Below 23 degrees C | {{PESO licence list Dec 2024}} |
| B | 23 degrees C and above, but below 65 degrees C | {{PESO licence list Dec 2024}} |
| C | 65 degrees C and above, but below 93 degrees C | {{PESO licence list Dec 2024}} |

The Petroleum Rules use these classes everywhere: container types, transport, storage and licence forms {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rules 4 to 6 and 76 to 81}}.

## Which class does a pump sell?

Your pump's tanks are what the licence covers. The PESO tank licence for a pump is Form XIV, and its wording has one line for tanks of petroleum Class A and another for tanks of Class B or C, each with its kilolitres {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV}}.

Two official hints help. The rules require a container of Class A to carry the words "Petrol" or "Motor Spirit" {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 5(7)}}. PESO calls high speed diesel petroleum Class B {{PESO SOP for bowser parking}}, and kerosene is also Class B in the rules {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 3(4)}}. For the exact class of any product, read its flash point on the supplier's documents.

## How do the classes change the licence?

- **Small quantities.** PESO's FAQ says storing Class A below 30 litres, Class B below 2,500 litres and Class C below 45,000 litres needs no PESO licence, though storing Class C needs a one-time approval from the PESO office {{PESO FAQ on PR 2002, Aug 2024}}.
- **District Authority licences.** Storing Class A up to 300 litres and Class B up to 25,000 litres is licensed by the District Authority {{PESO SOP Jan 2025}}.
- **Pump tanks.** Tanks of any pump outfit for fuelling vehicles need Form XIV from PESO {{PESO SOP Jan 2025}}.
- **Tankers.** The rules bar loading Class A or B into a tank vehicle that is not licensed, except a tank wagon {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 77}}. The licence to carry petroleum by land on a motor vehicle is Form XI {{PESO SOP Jan 2025}}.

Fees and forms change, so the current fee is on the PESO site under licence fees. Ask PESO or an advocate which licence your case needs. See [PESO licences](@licences-peso) and [renewal](@licences-renewal).
