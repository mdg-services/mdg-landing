There is no single number of feet of frontage. Each oil company sets the minimum for a location, in the advertisement for it. This page shows what the companies and the highway authority publish, as on 19 November 2026. It is not advice on your plot; ask the oil company before you buy or lease land.

## What do the oil companies say?

BPCL's dealer selection guidelines list three tests for offered land: the minimum frontage, the minimum area, and the minimum depth perpendicular to the frontage at least at one place after leaving the Right of Way line, all as specified in the advertisement {{BPCL DSG 2023, land evaluation}}. Land is also checked for being inside the advertised area, meeting NHAI norms on a highway, and having no high tension line above 11 KV crossing it; land failing any test is rejected {{BPCL DSG 2023, land evaluation}}.

Hindustan Petroleum says the eligibility criteria, terms and conditions are described in its advertisement {{HPCL dealership page}}. So read the advertisement for your location, not a blog.

## What does Nayara publish?

Nayara's own pages show two different tables on the same day, so do not treat either as final.

| Page | City or town | Highways |
|---|---|---|
| Apply page | 30 m frontage, 30 m depth {{Nayara apply page}} | 45 m, 45 m {{Nayara apply page}} |
| Eligibility page | 25 m frontage, 25 m depth {{Nayara eligibility page}} | 35 m, 35 m {{Nayara eligibility page}} |

The eligibility page also says it hopes for land of more than 800 sq m in a town area or more than 1200 sq m on a highway {{Nayara eligibility page}}. Both pages also say the figures vary from location to location as per norms {{Nayara apply page}} {{Nayara eligibility page}}. Ask Nayara which table applies to your location.

## What if the pump is on a national highway?

The highway access norms set a minimum plot, in metres {{MoRTH circular 26.06.2020, Appendix-I}}:

| Stretch | Frontage x depth |
|---|---|
| Rural, plain or rolling terrain | 35 x 35 |
| Urban, plain or rolling terrain | 30 x 30 |
| Hilly and mountainous | 20 x 20 |

NHAI's manual records a relaxation: for urban plots acquired before 26 June 2020, access permission may be granted for 20 m by 20 m instead of 30 m by 30 m {{NHAI manual ch. 14, MoRTH letter 28.01.2021}}.
