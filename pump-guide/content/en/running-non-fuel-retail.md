## What the oil companies publish

**Hindustan Petroleum (HPCL).** On 19 July 2026 HPCL issued an Expression of Interest for non-fuel business partners at its retail outlets across India, with 21 August 2026 as the last date {{HPCL EOI 19 Jul 2026, cover page}}. It invites reputed firms, agencies, brands and organizations {{HPCL EOI 19 Jul 2026, section 1}}. It groups the activities into three segments {{HPCL EOI 19 Jul 2026, section 2}}:

| Segment | Examples listed |
|---|---|
| Store-based | Convenience stores, restaurants and cafes, vehicle care, PUC centres, ATMs, pharmacies, courier points |
| Non-store (forecourt) | Promotions, direct product sales, insurance, fintech, telecom |
| Advertising | Hoardings and in-store advertising |

**Bharat Petroleum (BPCL).** Its convenience page says it launched convenience retailing in 2001 under the In Out Convenience Store brand, later added food outlets, and offers ATM, money transfer and insurance {{BPCL convenience page}}.

**Jio-bp.** Its page says convenience stores are available at selected mobility stations {{Jio-bp wildbean cafe page}}.

## What comes with it

- HPCL says final terms are settled through an MoU, a Leave and Licence Agreement or another contract under its policy {{HPCL EOI 19 Jul 2026, section 6 clause 7}}.
- Partners for regulated services such as pharmacy, insurance or food must hold the licences the regulator requires {{HPCL EOI 19 Jul 2026, section 3}}.
- Any engagement must follow applicable laws, including safety norms, food safety, environmental, labour and municipal rules {{HPCL EOI 19 Jul 2026, section 6 clause 8}}.
- At a licensed pump, no fire, furnace or other heat or light source capable of igniting vapour is allowed except in places the licensing authority has authorised {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 117(3)}}.

## What we could not source

We found no published rule on how much space a shop may take, who keeps the rent or sales share, or whether a dealer may sign directly. Nayara Energy's position we did not find at all. In practice, ask your oil company in writing before any construction or letting.
