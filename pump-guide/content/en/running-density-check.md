Density is how heavy a litre of fuel is. It is measured at a standard 15 degrees C, because fuel swells when warm. This page follows the public sector oil companies' guideline (MDG 2024); a private company's pump should ask for its own. As on 19 November 2026.

## When does the dealer check density?

| When | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tanker arrives | Match the recorded density at 15 degrees C on the invoice with the actual product | {{MDG 2024, 1.1}} |
| Before unloading | Draw a sample from each compartment after draining about 20 litres; check density and temperature, record it | {{MDG 2024, 1.2(viii)}} |
| After unloading | Dispense at least 50 litres, then sample at the nozzle and record the density at 15 degrees C in the density register | {{MDG 2024, 1.2(xiv)}} |
| Every morning | Draw a nozzle sample and record the opening density at 15 degrees C before sales | {{MDG 2024, 1.4.1(c)}} |

## What does the tanker check allow?

The dealer converts the reading with the ASTM tables (53B) and checks it is within +/- 3.0 kg per cubic metre of the invoice density {{MDG 2024, 1.2(iv)}}. The Control Order, as amended on 15 March 2024, gives the limit as +/- 0.0030, with no unit named in its text, and asks for a record of densities {{MS and HSD Amendment Order 2024}}. In case of doubt about quality, ask the supply location or the area field officer {{MDG 2024, 1.2(ix)}}.

## Which range does BIS give?

BIS shows these ranges at 15 degrees C for BS VI fuel, from 2020: petrol (IS 2796) 720-775 kg per cubic metre, diesel (IS 1460) 810-845 {{BIS petroleum standards deck}}.

## What must the pump keep?

- Calibrated hydrometers and thermometers, and the ASTM conversion table, in working order and available to inspectors {{MDG 2024, 1.5(vi)}}.
- The daily sales register and the density register for the current and previous financial years {{MDG 2024, 5.1.12}}.

## What happens without a morning reading?

An inspector treats it as no reference density. Sales and supplies of all products are suspended, samples go to a lab, and penal action follows, in line with adulteration if the sample fails {{MDG 2024, 5.1.9}}.
