## When a pump may refuse

As on 27 November 2026, a pump's licence forces two refusals. Petroleum, petrol and diesel included, must not go into a motor vehicle while the engine is running {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 15}}. And fuel must not be filled into a container other than one clamped or fitted to a vehicle, apart from narrow exceptions {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 16}}.

- Loose containers: [petrol in a bottle](@customers-petrol-in-bottle).
- Diesel in a drum or can: [diesel in a drum](@customers-diesel-in-drum).
- Local orders: [no helmet, no petrol](@customers-no-helmet-no-petrol) and [no PUC, no fuel](@customers-no-puc-no-fuel).

## What the dealer guideline says

The guideline of the public sector oil companies says a dealer should ensure prompt service and courteous behaviour at all times {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(iii)}}, and should display working hours prominently {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(v)}}. We found no clause on refusing a customer for petrol or diesel, and none that makes a pump sell to every customer. Only kerosene dealers are told not to refuse a consumer on a working day during working hours when they have stock {{MDG 2024, chapter 6}}. This is for those companies' dealers only.

## No stock

We found no rule on a pump with no fuel; see [when a pump runs dry](@running-dry-out).

## Cash and coins

RBI says every banknote it issues is legal tender at any place in India for the amount on it {{RBI FAQ on currency}}. For coins, RBI says one rupee or more is legal tender up to Rs 1,000 in a single transaction, and 50 paise up to Rs 10; nobody can be forced to accept coins beyond that {{RBI FAQ on currency}}. RBI's FAQ does not mention petrol pumps, and its complaint route for refused coins is for banks only {{RBI FAQ on currency}}.

## Where to complain

Ask for the complaint book; at a public sector pump the dealer must keep it ready {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(iv)}}. The dealer must display the oil company's contact {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(viii)}}. Or call the National Consumer Helpline: 1915 or 1800 11 4000 {{NCH contact page}}.
