This page reports what two official texts say, as on 18 November 2026. It is not legal advice; ask an advocate or the oil company about a particular case.

## What does the MS and HSD Order say?

The Order lists these malpractices: adulteration, pilferage, stock variation, unauthorised exchange, unauthorised purchase, unauthorised sale, unauthorised possession, over-charging, sale of off-specification product and short delivery {{MS HSD Order 2005, clause 2(f)}}. No dealer may indulge in any of them {{MS HSD Order 2005, clause 3(6)}}.

- On each tanker the dealer inspects the seal and lock {{MS HSD Order 2005, clause 3(3)}}, and checks that quantity and quality match the delivery documents, with density within plus or minus 0.0030 of the document. The dealer keeps a record of densities and samples signed with the driver {{MS HSD Amendment Order 2024, clause 3}}.
- Authorised officers may enter, search, take samples and inspect records {{MS HSD Order 2005, clause 7(1)}}.
- The officer sends samples to a laboratory within ten days; the report is due within twenty days of receipt; the result is passed on within five days of the report {{MS HSD Order 2005, clause 8(4) to 8(6)}}.
- Stock is judged against norms: beyond plus or minus 4 per cent of tank stock, over and above set handling losses {{MS HSD Order 2005, Schedule I}}.

The Order has been amended since 2005. The ministry's page also lists a separate order of 11 June 2026, G.S.R. 474(E), on temporary regulation of supply through retail outlets {{MS HSD Temporary Order 2026, G.S.R. 474(E)}}; read it there.

## What do the guidelines add?

This part is for public sector oil company pumps (MDG 2024). The guideline groups irregularities into three classes {{MDG 2024, clause 8.1}}.

| Class | Examples | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Adulteration, tampered metering or totaliser seals, unauthorised fittings, unauthorised storage, unauthorised purchase or sale {{MDG 2024, clause 8.2}} | Termination at the first instance {{MDG 2024, clause 8.2}} |
| Major | Refusing a sample or inspection, no reference density, stock variation, missing records, over-charging {{MDG 2024, clause 8.3}} | Sales and supplies suspended 15 days, then 30 days, then termination; two items have their own actions {{MDG 2024, clause 8.3}} |
| Minor | Prices or density not displayed, no complaint book, no uniform or badge {{MDG 2024, clause 8.4}} | Warning, then Rs 10,000, then Rs 25,000 per irregularity {{MDG 2024, clause 8.4}} |

Three points the text states. The dealer records the morning density by 10 AM daily {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.9}}. A tampered seal stops sales from that unit, which is sealed {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.2(b)}}. The actions listed are minimum, and each case must be established before action {{MDG 2024, clauses 8.5.1 and 8.5.2}}.
