A licensed pump is licensed as drawn. Changing it, for example a tank or a dispenser, has two PESO steps: approval first, amendment after. This page is as on 25 November 2026, from the Petroleum Rules 2002 as amended. It is not legal advice.

## Step 1: approval before any work

No alteration may be carried out until PESO has approved the drawing in writing {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 146(1)}}. The application has three copies of a plan showing the proposed alteration in distinct colours with the reasons, and the scrutiny fee {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 146(2)}}. That fee is Rs 2,000 {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, First Schedule}}. If satisfied, PESO returns one signed copy with its sanction, subject to any conditions {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 146(3)}}.

The licence itself says the same for a pump or tank: no change of position, and no tank replacement, without previous written sanction {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 6}}. The rules do not list which jobs are alterations; for a dispenser, ask PESO.

## Step 2: amend the licence after the work

The holder must apply to amend the licence as soon as the sanctioned work is done {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 146(4)}}.

| Item | What the rules say |
|---|---|
| Papers | The licence with approved plans; three copies of the sanctioned plan; the fee {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 147(3)}} |
| Tank test certificate | For a tank installed or after a major repair, tested by water pressure by a competent person {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 126(1), (3)}} |
| Safety certificate | When a tank is installed or its position shifted {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 130(1)}} |
| Fee | Rs 2,000 {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, First Schedule}}, plus any amount the fee for the amended form exceeds the fee first paid {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 147(2)}}. The 2002 text of rule 147(2) still reads Rs 500, so confirm the fee on the PESO portal |

## How is it filed?

Register on NSWS, then choose the form on PESO's site, upload documents and pay online {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. The FAQs list the drawing colours (additions red, deletions yellow) and the papers for each step {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. If land is added, a district authority NOC is also listed {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. For your own case, ask PESO, your oil company or an advocate. Licence dates are in [licence renewal](@licences-renewal).
