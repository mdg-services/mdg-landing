A lease has to satisfy two readers: the oil company that advertises the dealership, and PESO, which licenses the site. Facts are as on 14 November 2026. For land size and distances see [land for a petrol pump](@site).

## What must the lease do for the oil company?

- Land offered against an advertisement must be with you on the application date, owned or leased for at least 19 years 11 months, or as advertised {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.
- If there are several owners, all must execute the lease deed; otherwise it is treated as invalid {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.
- For Corpus Fund Scheme and other company-owned sites, a long-term lease must allow sub-lease; the company gives 21 days to amend it if not {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.

## Which papers does PESO ask for?

With the plan application, PESO asks for relevant land documents confirming legal physical possession, a declaration that the land has clear title and is in legal possession of the applicant, and an undertaking that no court case is pending on it {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. See [the PESO licence](@licences-peso) for the steps.

## Why is a short lease a problem?

A licence stands cancelled if the licensee ceases to have any right to the site for storing petroleum {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 152}}. A lease that ends while the licence is still running can leave the pump without a site.

## Your own land or a company lease?

| Site type | Who holds the land |
|---|---|
| Company-owned sites | The land is taken on lease or bought by the oil company, which develops it {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.3}} |
| Dealer-owned sites | The dealer holds the land and develops the superstructure {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.3}} |

The advertisement says which type each location is. The company makes no commitment to take land you offer {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi), note 1(e)}}.

## What should the agreement cover?

In practice, ask an advocate to check at least: access from the road, the right to build and to dig for tanks, who pays what, and what happens at the end of the term. Registration and stamp duty are usually state matters, so ask the local sub-registrar's office or your advocate. This page gives no draft agreement, no rent figure and no legal advice: ask an advocate and use the oil company's own format.

## Do not pay anyone for land or a dealership

Nobody can book a site or dealership for you. They come only from the oil companies' own advertisements, and a payment to anyone for one is a fraud sign {{Oil companies fraud notice}}.
