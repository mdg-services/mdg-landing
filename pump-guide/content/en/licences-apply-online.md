Which button do you press, and what do you upload? This page covers the online side of the PESO licence, as on 26 November 2026. For the order of the whole process, see the [PESO licence page](@licences-peso).

## How the online application works

1. **Register on NSWS**, the National Single Window System, at nsws.gov.in. It lands you on PESO's website {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}.
2. **Choose the Petroleum Rules** and the form for approval, grant, renewal or amendment {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}.
3. **Upload the documents and pay the fee online** {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. The amount is on the [PESO licence page](@licences-peso).

The licensing authority for Form XIV is the PESO Circle or Sub Circle office {{PESO SOP Petroleum Rules 2002}}.

## What goes in each application

Both applications carry Form IX and a covering letter listing the uploaded documents, with the signer's name and designation. Every document except an original is signed by the applicant, or by a person the applicant authorised in writing {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}.

| | Prior approval of the pump | Grant of the licence |
|---|---|---|
| Land papers showing legal possession | Yes | Yes |
| Letter of intent from the oil company | Yes | Not on PESO's list |
| Layout and site plan with everything protected within 100 metres | Yes | A drawing similar to the approved one |
| No-litigation undertaking, PAN, and the CPCB undertaking on nearby schools, hospitals, homes and water | Yes | Yes |
| District Authority NOC under rule 144, through the LSDA module | No | Yes |
| Declaration that the pump is built as approved, with colour photographs | No | Yes |

All of it is from PESO's list for a retail outlet {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. A consumer pump (a pump run for a firm's own vehicles) also uploads the firm's certificate of incorporation, and PESO's prior-approval list adds a registered partnership deed {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. 

## After you apply

Log in to NSWS to check the status, or search PESO's Public Domain page {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}; see also [checking a licence's status](@licences-peso-status-check). Renewal is online too, with its own list: [validity and renewal](@licences-renewal). Help: support.ol@explosives.gov.in or 1800-233-9011 {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}.

For your own case, ask PESO, the oil company or an advocate.
