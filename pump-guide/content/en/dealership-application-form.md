## Before you start

Read the location's row in the [advertisement](@dealership-reading-advertisement). Keep a photo (jpg or png, up to 2 MB) and a signature (up to 1 MB) ready {{Selection brochure June 2023, Appendix IC}}. Registration details fill the form and cannot be edited later {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}. At a bidding location your bid goes in online with the application {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(G)}}.

## What the form asks

The individual form, Appendix IA, has these parts {{Selection brochure June 2023, Appendix IA}}:

1. The location: serial number, name, district, state, category and sub-category.
2. Site type (CC, DC or CFS), regular or rural, and your land group. See [land groups](@dealership-land-groups).
3. Personal details: citizenship, residence, date of birth with the document that proves it, marital status, any job, and your 10th pass.
4. Land: owner, relationship to you, deed or affidavit date, khasra or survey number, frontage, depth and area.
5. An undertaking to arrange the infrastructure and working capital money named in the advertisement.
6. Questions on family dealerships, and undertakings.

## Filling and paying

Save as you go; a saved form can be edited until you submit {{Selection brochure June 2023, Appendix IC}}. Pay through the portal's gateway by net banking, credit or debit card; an unpaid form is rejected {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E), Appendix IC}}. Then download your PDF copy {{Selection brochure June 2023, Appendix IC}}.

## The two kinds of affidavit

| | Appendix III, land offer | Appendix XA or XB, standard |
|---|---|---|
| Who signs | Each family member, co-owner or landowner offering land | You (XA) or your firm (XB) |
| Date | On or before the application date | After the advertisement date |
| When given | Uploaded when the company asks, after selection | Uploaded after selection; fresh ones at the letter of intent and appointment |

Both are notarised, on non-judicial stamp paper bought in the signer's name {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.11, Appendix III}}. The dates and timing come from {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi), cl.11, cl.16, cl.19}}. Originals are checked at field verification, and photocopies of affidavits are not accepted {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.15, cl.24}}.

Applying is not an offer of a dealership {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.24}}. Pay only on the portal; there are no agents {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. MDG Services does not sell or arrange dealerships.
