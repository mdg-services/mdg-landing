## Which sites go to bidding?

The oil company decides the site type and states it against each location in the advertisement {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14}}. Company-owned ("A" or "CC") sites are selected by bidding, except Corpus Fund sites, which use a [draw of lots](@dealership-draw-result) {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(A), cl.14(B)}}. On these sites the offered land is taken on lease or bought by the company and fully developed as a company-owned site {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.3}}. The difference between the site types is on the [company-owned vs dealer-owned](@dealership-company-owned-vs-dealer-owned) page.

## How the bid works

1. **With the application.** You submit the financial bid online when you apply {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(G)}}, and you sign an undertaking to pay the non-refundable final bid amount if selected {{Selection brochure June 2023, Appendix XA}}.
2. **At least the minimum.** Rs 30 lakh regular, Rs 10 lakh rural, including GST {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}}. A lower bid is a deficiency that cannot be corrected {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.23(r)}}.
3. **Bid opening.** Bids are opened at the District Collector's or District Magistrate's office as far as possible, Group 1 (land held) before Group 2 (a firm offer of land) {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}. A lone eligible applicant's bid is opened the same way and that applicant is declared provisionally selected {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}.
4. **Ties.** A draw of lots among the tied bidders {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(B)}}.

## If the selected bidder falls out

If the selected bidder is found ineligible, the next highest bidder is declared provisionally selected {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}. That bidder is told by email or SMS, and the same goes on down the list after any further rejection or withdrawal {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.17}}.

## Paying, and what follows

| Payment | When |
|---|---|
| Initial security deposit, 10% of the deposit | Within 10 days of the bid opening {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.20}} |
| Total bid amount | Within 15 days of receiving the NOC {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}} |
| Licence fee for the company's investment | Per kilolitre sold, as set from time to time {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8(E)}} |

As on 26 November 2026 the June 2023 brochure lists that licence fee on company-owned sites as Rs 435.83 per kilolitre of petrol and Rs 363.19 per kilolitre of diesel, including GST {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8(E)}}. Application fees and the initial security deposit are paid only on the portal, and no agency or person may ask you for money for selection {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. The next step is the [letter of intent](@dealership-letter-of-intent).
