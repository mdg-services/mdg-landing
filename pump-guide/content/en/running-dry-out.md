This page reports what the public sector oil companies' dealer guideline (MDG 2024) and government releases say, as on 22 November 2026. The guideline binds those companies' outlets only; a private company's dealer should ask that company. It is not legal advice.

## What does the guideline say about stock and closing?

We found no clause on a petrol or diesel pump running dry, and none on minimum stock. Its stock and closure duties are for kerosene dealers: keep adequate stocks at all times, and do not keep premises closed in working hours without the oil company's prior written permission {{MDG 2024, chapter 6}}. Outlets display working hours and holiday notices {{MDG 2024, 1.6}}; see [petrol pump timings](@running-opening-hours).

## When are a pump's sales stopped?

| Cause | What the guideline says |
|---|---|
| Weights and Measures seal or totaliser seal tampered | Sales through that unit are suspended until the seals are replaced {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}} |
| No reference density at inspection | Sales and supplies of all products are suspended immediately {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.9}} |
| Major irregularity (for example stock variation or overcharging) | Suspension of sales and supplies for 15 days, then 30 days, then termination {{MDG 2024, clause 8.3}} |
| Fine not paid within 30 days | Suspension from the 31st day for 15 days, extended if still unpaid, then termination {{MDG 2024, clause 8.7}} |

The penalties are explained in [marketing discipline](@running-marketing-discipline).

## What has the Government said on supply?

On 13 March 2026 the petroleum ministry said no cases of fuel dry-outs had been reported at any of 1 lakh retail outlets by the oil marketing companies {{PIB release 13 Mar 2026}}. On 11 June 2026 it notified a temporary order that allowed limits on bulk buying at retail outlets, including 200 litres of diesel a day per customer or vehicle, with any direction issued under it valid at first for up to 90 days {{MS HSD Temporary Order 2026, G.S.R. 474(E)}}. A release of 29 June 2026 says the temporary measures (it dates the order 12 June 2026, the gazette 11 June) were withdrawn from 1 July 2026 {{PIB release 29 Jun 2026}}.

## If a pump near you is shut or dry

In practice, look for a notice on the board, then try another outlet and call the oil company number displayed there. Complaints are covered on [the customers page](@customers).
