Many pumps have a manager who runs the floor for the dealer. The Petroleum Rules ask that all operations in a service station run under an experienced, responsible supervisor who knows the licence terms and has safety training {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 118}}; the dealer stays answerable for quality, quantity and price {{MDG 2024, 1.6}}. This page uses the public sector oil companies' guidelines (MDG 2024); a private company's pump should ask for its own. As on 17 November 2026.

## What is the manager's day, in order?

| When | Task | Rule or practice |
|---|---|---|
| Before sales | Check the Legal Metrology seal and certificate of each dispenser | Rule {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}} |
| Before sales | Test every nozzle with the 5-litre measure; note the litres in the stock register | Rule {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}} |
| Before sales | Draw a nozzle sample and record the morning density at 15 degrees C | Rule {{MDG 2024, 1.4.1}} |
| Morning | Record meter readings and product and water dips; display opening stock and prices | Rule {{MDG 2024, 1.3}} |
| Through the day | Shift handover, cash, complaints | In practice |
| Tanker arrives | Follow the unloading steps and enter stock | Rule {{MDG 2024, 1.2}} |
| Close | Check the register adds up | In practice; keeping reports and invoices at the pump is a rule {{MDG 2024, 1.5}} |

If a seal is found tampered, sales through that dispenser stop until it is replaced and the oil company is told {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}}.

## Which duties are rules for the whole pump?

- **Daily records.** The PESO licence says the licensee must keep daily records of receipts and issues of petroleum and show stock and records to an inspector on demand {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 15}}.
- **Licence on display.** The licence number must be marked prominently on the premises {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 133}}, and an extract of the rules and licence conditions must be exhibited in a conspicuous place {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 134}}.
- **Legal Metrology.** Fuel dispensers are re-verified every twenty-four months {{Legal Metrology General Rules, G.S.R. 905(E)}}.
- **Customer notices.** Working hours and the complaint book notice must be displayed prominently {{MDG 2024, 1.6}}.

## What is practice, not a rule?

Staff numbers, shift splits and cash banking differ by pump and are set by the dealer. See [staff and daily running](@running) for the other roles and [measurement](@equipment-metrology) for dispenser checks.
