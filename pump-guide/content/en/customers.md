## What should I do first at the pump?

1. Ask the dealer for the suggestion/complaint book. It must be kept, handed over on demand, and a message that it exists must be displayed {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(iv)}}.
2. Write the date, time, pump, product and what went wrong, and photograph the page. This is practice, not a rule.
3. Note the dealer's and the oil company's contacts displayed at the outlet {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(viii)}}.
4. If the dealer refuses the book, that is itself an irregularity {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.17}}. Go to the next step.

Public sector oil companies list complaint registers at all outlets, toll-free numbers, websites, apps, email and CPGRAMS as their routes {{PIB 23 Jul 2026}}. The dealer rules on this page are from their MDG 2024; a private company's dealer should ask for that company's own rules.

## Where can I complain after that?

As on 9 November 2026:

| Route | Contact | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Company person on the outlet board | Name and number on the board {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(viii)}} | Any pump problem |
| Bharat Petroleum | SmartLine 1800 22 4344 {{BPCL SmartLine page}} | Any pump problem |
| Other oil companies | The company's website, and the outlet board | Any pump problem |
| National Consumer Helpline | 1915 or 1800 11 4000, daily except national holidays, 8 am to 8 pm; WhatsApp +91 88000 01915 {{NCH contact page}} | Any consumer complaint |
| [CPGRAMS](https://pgportal.gov.in/) | Online portal {{PIB 23 Jul 2026}} | Grievance to a government body |

## Which problem goes where?

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Short delivery | Write it in the book. A variation in delivery should be reported to the Weights & Measures department and the oil company {{MDG 2024, clause 1.4.2(d)}}. In practice, you can also tell your state's Legal Metrology department. |
| Bad quality | Ask for the filter paper test {{MDG 2024, clause 1.4.1(a)}}. The oil company samples on a specific complaint {{MDG 2024, clause 2.2}}. |
| Overcharging | The outlet must display the authorised selling prices {{MDG 2024, clause 1.3(i)}}. Tell the oil company. |
| Rude staff | Service must be prompt and courteous {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(iii)}}. Write it in the book. |
| No air or toilet | Air during operating hours {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(ii)}} and a clean toilet {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(vi)}} must be provided. |

## What if it is still not settled?

For a refund or compensation, a consumer can file a complaint with a District Commission, and may file it electronically {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.35}}. The Commission cannot admit it after two years from the cause of action unless there was sufficient cause {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.69}}. Ask an advocate about your case.

## How should a dealer handle a complaint?

In practice, not as a rule: answer the same day, write what you did in the book, and fix the cause. See [the complaint book in MDG 2024](/guides/customer-service/complaint-book/).
