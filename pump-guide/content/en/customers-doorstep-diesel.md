## The short answer

As on 25 November 2026, doorstep delivery is allowed for diesel only, to heavy vehicles, machinery and stationary equipment, from a PESO-licensed vehicle. The sources we opened say petrol delivery was not proposed, and they do not describe delivery into a private car.

## What the 2019 rule says

G.S.R. 384(E) of 29 May 2019 changed the licence for road vehicles carrying petroleum (Form XIX) so that such a vehicle can refuel aircraft, heavy vehicles, machinery and stationary equipment on site {{G.S.R. 384(E), 29 May 2019}}. The Ministry confirmed in November 2019 that this covers diesel, with no proposal for petrol or kerosene {{PIB 20 Nov 2019}}.

| Condition | Source |
|---|---|
| Not into equipment that is running | {{G.S.R. 384(E), 29 May 2019}} |
| Emergency plan for each site, copy to the District Authority | {{G.S.R. 384(E), 29 May 2019}} |
| At least 6 metres from protected works for Class B petroleum (4.5 metres for a refueller of up to 3,000 litres) | {{G.S.R. 384(E), 29 May 2019}} |
| Hose no longer than 10 metres | {{G.S.R. 384(E), 29 May 2019}} |

## Who may do it

PESO's procedure for mobile dispensers asks for a valid PESO licence, an oil company's ownership or authorisation with a contract, a calibration licence for the tank and meter, and a driver with a HAZCHEM certificate {{PESO mobile dispenser SOP}}. It adds that a mobile dispenser delivers only to registered customers, with a geo-fencing feature {{PESO mobile dispenser SOP}}.

Oil companies run their own schemes. Hindustan Petroleum's page names start-ups recognised by DPIIT as "DDD Resellers" with an agreement with the oil company {{HPCL mobile dispenser page}}. Terms differ by company.

## Where a pump fits

The rule lets the vehicle be loaded at a specially prepared area attached to a Form XIV service station, at least 10 m by 10 m, with 4.5 m clear all round {{G.S.R. 384(E), 29 May 2019}}. The licence must cover it; a pump cannot assume it.

## What we could not confirm

We did not check later amendments or state orders. Ask PESO or the oil company before relying on a detail. For legal questions, ask an advocate.
