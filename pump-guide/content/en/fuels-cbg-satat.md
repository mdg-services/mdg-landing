Compressed biogas, or CBG, is biogas made from waste and biomass sources {{LS answer 6 Aug 2026}}. This page gives what the Petroleum Ministry and Parliament say about it, as on 21 November 2026. For the other biofuels, see [biofuel outlets](@fuels-biofuel-outlets).

## What is SATAT?

SATAT stands for Sustainable Alternative Towards Affordable Transportation. The Government launched it on 1 October 2018 to build a system for making CBG from waste and biomass and to promote its use along with natural gas {{LS answer 6 Aug 2026}}. Oil and gas marketing companies invite entrepreneurs to offer CBG for purchase, and the plants are set up by entrepreneurs, public sector enterprises and others on their own business assessment {{LS answer 6 Aug 2026}}.

## How far has it got?

| Date | What the Government said |
|---|---|
| 8 December 2022 | The scheme envisaged 5,000 plants making 15 million tonnes a year by 2023-24. 3,694 letters of intent were issued and 38 plants commissioned (as on 31 October 2022) {{PIB 8 Dec 2022, SATAT}} |
| 6 August 2026 | 217 plants were functional as on 31 July 2026, about 1,773 tonnes a day, including 180 under SATAT and the CBG-CGD scheme {{LS answer 6 Aug 2026}} |

## Is blending compulsory?

The National Biofuels Coordination Committee announced on 24 November 2023 a phased CBG blending obligation for CNG sold for transport and piped gas sold to homes on city gas networks {{PIB 25 Nov 2023}}. The Minister gave the steps on 6 August 2026: 1% for FY 2025-26, 3% for 2026-27, 4% for 2027-28 and 5% from 2028-29 {{LS answer 6 Aug 2026}}. The sources describe the obligation for the CNG (Transport) and PNG (Domestic) segments of city gas networks. They do not mention petrol pump dealers.

## What does it mean for a petrol pump?

On 8 December 2022 the Ministry said 90 retail outlets were selling CBG {{PIB 8 Dec 2022, outlets}}. The guidelines revised on 8 November 2019 ask authorised entities to install facilities for at least one new generation alternate fuel, biofuel included, at their proposed outlets within three years of the outlet's operation {{PIB 8 Dec 2022, outlets}}. For the wider picture see [CNG and EV](@fuels-cng-ev) and [LNG for trucks](@fuels-lng-trucks).

In practice, CBG reaches a pump only through the oil company's own arrangement. Ask the company in writing what your pump may sell, and do not buy gas from anyone else.
