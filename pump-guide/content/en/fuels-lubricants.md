## The short answer

As on 27 November 2026, packed engine oil sold at a petrol pump falls under the Packaged Commodities Rules, 2011. No retail dealer may sell it above the retail sale price {{Packaged Commodities Rules 2011, rule 18(2)}}. This is general information, not legal or tax advice.

## What pumps usually sell

In practice a pump sells the lubricants of its own oil company. Hindustan Petroleum sells under HP Lubricants, whose automotive range includes passenger car, two-wheeler and heavy-duty diesel engine oils {{HP Lubricants site}}. Bharat Petroleum sells under MAK Lubricants for bikes, scooters, cars, trucks and tractors {{BPCL MAK Lubricants page}}. Other oil companies, Indian Oil among them, may sell their own ranges; check their own websites.

## The MRP rule

| Point | Source |
|---|---|
| The retail sale price is the maximum price, inclusive of all taxes | {{Packaged Commodities Rules 2011, rule 2(m)}} |
| No retailer, wholesaler or maker may sell above it | {{Packaged Commodities Rules 2011, rule 18(2)}} |

## What the packet must show

Every package must carry the maker's (and packer's) name and address, the common name of the product, the net quantity and the month and year of manufacture {{Packaged Commodities Rules 2011, rule 6(1)}}. The retail sale price must say it is the maximum retail price inclusive of all taxes {{Packaged Commodities Rules 2011, rule 6(1)}}.

## The standard

The Indian standard for engine oils is IS 13656:2019, "Internal combustion engine crankcase oils for automotive application (Diesel and Gasoline)" {{BIS standards list, IS 13656:2019}}. The older IS 496 was withdrawn and superseded by IS 13656 {{BIS Know Your Standard, IS 496:1982}}. We could not confirm from BIS pages whether BIS certification is compulsory for engine oil; check with BIS.

## GST

The 18 per cent schedule of the notification effective 22 September 2025 includes petroleum oils of heading 2710 and lubricating preparations of heading 3403 {{IGST Rate Notification 9/2025, Schedule II}}. Check the CBIC site for later amendments.
