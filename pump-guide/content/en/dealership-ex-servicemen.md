## What the defence category is

Every advertised location is placed in a category. In all states except Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Nagaland and Mizoram, 8% of locations belong to Combined Category 1 (CC1): 2% in the SC/ST group, 2% in OBC and 4% in Open {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.2}}. CC1 holds two kinds of people together: defence personnel, and paramilitary, central or state government and public sector employees {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.2}}. So the 8% is shared with them.

## Who qualifies as defence personnel

The brochure lists three groups {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vii)}}:

1. Widows or dependents of armed forces members who died in war or in harness due to attributable causes.
2. Ex-servicemen disabled in war, or disabled in peace due to attributable causes.
3. Able-bodied ex-servicemen.

An ex-serviceman who is also SC/ST or OBC applies in that group's CC1 line {{Selection brochure June 2023, advert details}}. The usual conditions still apply: age 21 to 60, a 10th pass (an armed forces equivalence certificate is accepted) and the land rule {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(iv), cl.4(v), cl.4(vi)}}. See [who can apply](@dealership-eligibility).

## Which certificate is needed

| Group | Document |
|---|---|
| 1 and 2 (dependents, disabled) | Original eligibility certificate from DGR for that location {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vii)}} |
| 3 (able-bodied) | Copy of discharge order or pension order, sent to the oil company {{DGR CC1 guidelines, para 3}} |

A DGR certificate for one location is not valid for another {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vii)}}. It may be dated after your application but must reach the oil company within 10 days of the oil company's intimation {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4 note}}. DGR asks for attested papers, among them the newspaper cutting of the advertisement {{DGR CC1 guidelines, para 5}}.

## How selection works, and what is not assured

You apply to the oil company and, where needed, to DGR at the same time {{DGR retail outlet page}}. For locations on land the dealer arranges, and for corpus fund locations, the oil company holds a draw of lots; for other company-owned sites it holds bidding {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14}}. DGR has no say in the final selection {{DGR retail outlet page}}. Qualifying and holding a certificate give no assurance of a pump.

Dealerships come only through the oil companies' own advertisements. Paying anyone for one is a sign of fraud.
