Signs at a pump do two jobs: they remind people of what the law forbids, and they show the licence. Only a few are written into the rules. All as on 15 November 2026.

## Which signs does the law require?

| Sign or notice | What the Petroleum Rules 2002 say |
|---|---|
| Licence number | Every service station must have the number of its licence prominently marked on it {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 133}} |
| Rules and conditions | An extract of rules 3 to 12, 102 to 115, 116 to 134, 146 to 148 and 152 to 160, and of the licence conditions, must be exhibited in a conspicuous place {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 134}} |

The rules do not set the size, colour or wording of these; check the oil company's checklist and your licence.

## What do the signs warn about?

These are the rules behind the usual warnings:

- **No smoking, no flame.** No smoking at a service station except where the licensing authority allows, and no fire or heat source that could ignite vapour {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 117}}.
- **Engine off.** Petrol is not put in a vehicle with its engine running, and nobody connected with the vehicle may smoke while it is refuelled {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 15}}.
- **No filling of loose containers.** Petrol is not filled into a container that is not clamped or fitted to a vehicle, with listed exceptions such as an approved container up to 25 litres for a vehicle that has run dry {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 16}}.
- **Fire fighting kit.** Each pump has at least two tins or drums of dry sand and two foam or dry chemical extinguishers kept ready {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 20}}. See [fire extinguishers](@safety-fire-extinguishers).

## What about the mobile phone sign?

We found no mention of mobile phones in the Petroleum Rules 2002. So that sign, like most poster-style notices, is oil company practice, not a rule we can quote.

In practice, oil companies also ask for emergency phone numbers, fire point and assembly point signs. We did not find these in the rules we opened, so check the oil company's checklist. For price and measure displays, see the [Legal Metrology page of the guide](@equipment-metrology) and your Legal Metrology office.

The 4 March 2024 amendment to the Petroleum Rules did not change rules 133 or 134, and changed rule 117 only in its sub-rule (4) {{G.S.R. 154(E), 4 Mar 2024}}. OISD standards for retail outlets are sold by OISD and we could not open them.
