Do you need a licence to open an EV charging station? The Ministry of Power's guidelines answer it, as on 25 November 2026. For a petrol pump's extra steps, see [CNG and EV charging at a petrol pump](@fuels-cng-ev).

## Who can set one up?

Any entity. The guidelines call setting up and running a charging station a de-licensed activity, open to any entity that follows them {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 5(1)}}. They were issued on 17 September 2024 and replace all earlier Ministry of Power versions {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, cover letter}}. We found no later replacement; PIB still cited them in December 2025 {{PIB, 9 Dec 2025}}.

They cover chargers in private parking, offices and public places such as petrol pumps and highways {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 2}}.

## What the guidelines require

| Point | Rule |
|---|---|
| Charger standards | BIS standards listed in Annexure I {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 6(1)}} |
| Safety | CEA safety regulations 2023 {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 6(2)}} |
| Connection | Low-tension connection up to 150 kW on a separate application {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 5(3)}} |
| Listing | Public stations register on BEE's national database {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 13}} |

The distribution company must give a new connection within a maximum of 3 days in a metropolitan area, up to 90 days where mains must be extended {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 5(2)}}.

## At a petrol pump

The Government may prioritise oil company fuel outlets for public charging stations, and outlets with a charger should show it on their signs {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 11(4)}}. The connection's document list, marked tentative, includes PESO approval for chargers at petrol pumps {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, annexure III(B)}}. In practice, a dealer also needs the oil company's permission.

## Battery swapping

Swapping stations have separate guidelines of 10 January 2025, which apply clause 5 of the 2024 guidelines to them {{MoP swapping guidelines 10 Jan 2025}}.
