## What are E20, E85 and E100?

The number after E is the share of ethanol in the fuel. E20 is petrol with 20 per cent ethanol blended in {{PIB 15 Mar 2024}}. E85 is a high-ethanol blend designed for flex-fuel vehicles {{PIB 5 Jun 2026}}. ETHANOL 100 is ethanol sold as a vehicle fuel {{PIB 15 Mar 2024}}.

## How far has blending come?

As on 18 November 2026, the government's blending figures are {{PIB 10 Jul 2026}}:

| Ethanol supply year | Blending |
|---|---|
| 2020-21 | about 8.1% |
| 2022-23 | 12.1% |
| 2023-24 | 14.6% |
| 2024-25 | 19.2% |
| 2025-26 (November to June 2026) | 20% |

## What is a flex-fuel vehicle?

Flex-fuel vehicles can operate on ethanol blends from E20 to E100 {{PIB 5 Jun 2026}}. Two Hero MotoCorp flex-fuel motorcycles launched on 3 June 2026 are compatible with blends from E20 to E85 {{PIB 3 Jun 2026}}. Check your own vehicle's manual before using any blend other than E20.

## Where can I get E85 or E100?

- ETHANOL 100 launched on 15 March 2024 at 183 IndianOil outlets in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, New Delhi and Tamil Nadu {{PIB 15 Mar 2024}}.
- E85 launched on 5 June 2026 at 48 public sector outlets, with a stated plan of 500 outlets by December 2026 and about 5,000 by December 2027 {{PIB 5 Jun 2026}}.

These are announced plans and launch counts, not a list of today's outlets. In practice, a dealer should ask the oil company before planning to sell a new fuel. See [the fuels overview](@fuels) and [CNG and EV](@fuels-cng-ev).
