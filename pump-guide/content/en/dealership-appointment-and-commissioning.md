## 1. The land, ready for building

Within 2 months (Group 1) or 4 months (Group 2) of the [letter of intent](@dealership-letter-of-intent), you make the land available with all its documents {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}}. Before the oil company invests anything, the land must be developed to road level, with the entry, exit and service lanes, a retaining wall and a compound wall at least 1.5 metres high, as the company approves {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4 Note 1}}. On a dealer-owned site the land must be in your name or leased to you for the advertised period; on a company-owned site it must be registered in the company's name by long lease or sale {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.

## 2. Approvals and the PESO licence

The company prepares the layouts and the applications for statutory approvals and licences. You coordinate with the authorities for every NOC, and you must obtain the approvals needed to run the outlet {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8(D)}}. Under the Petroleum Rules, PESO first sanctions the plans and specifications of the premises {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 131}}, and the District Authority decides on a no-objection certificate for the site plan, within three months of receiving the application {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 144}}. Details are on [the PESO licence](@licences-peso) and [applying online](@licences-apply-online).

## 3. Building: who provides what

| Item | Company-owned site | Dealer-owned site |
|---|---|---|
| Developed land, lanes and walls | Dealer | Dealer |
| Tanks, dispensers, signs, automation | Oil company | Oil company |
| Sales office, toilet, electricity and water connections | Oil company | Dealer |
| Driveway and canopy | Oil company | Dealer |
| Generator or inverter | Dealer | Dealer |

All rows from the brochure {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8}}. The dealer also provides the fire-fighting and safety equipment {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8(B)}}. The fixed fee (dealer-owned) or total bid (company-owned) is due within 15 days of receiving the NOC, and the company starts installing only after it is paid {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}}. The approximate investment is stated in the advertisement {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8(C)}}.

## 4. Appointment and commissioning

1. Pay the interest-free security deposit, for example Rs 5 lakh for a regular outlet in the Open category, less the 10% already paid {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.20}}.
2. Give a fresh affidavit {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.19}}.
3. Receive the letter of appointment with the executed dealership agreement, before commissioning {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.19}}.

If progress falls behind the LOI's timelines without a justifiable reason, the LOI is withdrawn {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.19}}. For the weights-and-measures side of the dispensers, see [metrology](@equipment-metrology).
