Biofuel at a pump means two different things: biofuel already blended into the petrol and diesel an oil company supplies, and separate biofuels such as biodiesel, E100 or compressed bio gas. This page gives what the Petroleum Ministry says on each, as on 19 November 2026.

## What does the national policy say?

The National Policy on Biofuels was notified on 4 June 2018 with indicative targets of 20% ethanol in petrol and 5% biodiesel in diesel across the country by 2030 {{PIB 14 Sep 2020}}. The policy was amended in June 2022, and the 5% target now covers blending or direct sale of biodiesel {{PIB 21 Dec 2023}}.

## What can be sold as biodiesel?

- The Ministry notified guidelines for sale of biodiesel for blending with high speed diesel on 30 April 2019 {{PIB 21 Dec 2023}}.
- The permission is only for pure biodiesel (B-100), not for any mixture of whatever percentage {{MoPNG biodiesel page}}.
- A 2019 amendment allowed direct sale of B-100 for blending with diesel to all consumers, within the blending limits and BIS standards {{PIB 21 Dec 2023}}.
- BIS lists IS 15607 for B100 and says IS 1460 diesel can carry up to 7% biodiesel {{BIS petroleum standards deck}}. See [diesel grades](@fuels-diesel-grades).
- State and UT authorities may inspect outlets selling biodiesel for quality and quantity, and the Ministry has asked states to act against unauthorised product sold in the name of biodiesel {{PIB 24 Jul 2023}}.

## Which outlets sell biofuels?

Public sector oil companies sell fuels blended with biofuels from all existing outlets across the country, except Lakshadweep and Andaman and Nicobar Islands, depending on availability {{PIB 14 Sep 2020}}. On 8 December 2022 the Ministry reported 90 outlets selling compressed bio gas and 3 selling E100 {{PIB 8 Dec 2022}}. Those are dated figures. See [E100](@fuels-ethanol-100) and [CNG and EV](@fuels-cng-ev).

## What must a new outlet do?

The guidelines revised on 8 November 2019 require authorised entities to install facilities for marketing at least one new generation alternate fuel, such as CNG, biofuels, LNG or EV charging, within three years of operation of the outlet {{PIB 14 Sep 2020}}. Details are in the [dealership process](@dealership-process) and the oil company's own advertisement.

## What should a dealer do?

In practice, a dealer sells what the oil company supplies and approves. Before selling any separate biofuel, ask the company in writing, and take no product from anyone else.
