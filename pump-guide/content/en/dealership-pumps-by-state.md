## How many retail outlets does India have?

As on 24 November 2026, the latest count on the PPAC site is dated 1 April 2026: 103,023 retail outlets, including those of private companies {{PPAC state-wise retail outlets}}. A year earlier it was 96,724, and on 1 April 2012 it was 45,104 {{PPAC state-wise retail outlets}}. PPAC says the source of the figures is the oil companies {{PPAC state-wise retail outlets}}. Note that this is a count of outlets as the oil companies report them. It says nothing about how many new dealerships are open for application; those are only advertised by the oil companies themselves, and paying anyone for one is a fraud sign.

## By region

PPAC groups states into five regions. On 1 April 2026: North 32,666, South 28,789, West 25,899, East 12,414, North East 3,255 {{PPAC state-wise retail outlets}}. Small places have few outlets: Delhi 401, Goa 155, Sikkim 78 {{PPAC state-wise retail outlets}}. Counts can fall as well as rise; Delhi had 404 a year earlier {{PPAC state-wise retail outlets}}.

## Where this comes from

- [PPAC, Retail Outlets page](https://ppac.gov.in/infrastructure/retail-outlets): the state-wise sheet, April 2012 to April 2026, with the oil companies named as its source.
