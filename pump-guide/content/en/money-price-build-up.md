## What is in the price of a litre?

A government reply in the Rajya Sabha on 12 December 2018 named these elements of the retail price of petrol and diesel: excise duty, VAT, BS IV premium, marketing cost and margins, and dealers' commission, followed by 'etc.' {{PIB 12 Dec 2018}}. It is a 2018 list, not a current breakdown. Petrol prices are market-determined, and the public sector oil companies decide them {{PIB 21 Aug 2025}}.

As on 18 November 2026, PPAC's table shows petrol in Delhi at Rs 102.12 a litre on 17 November 2026 {{PPAC RSP table 17 Nov 2026}}.

## What does the dealer get?

The commission is set by the petroleum ministry and oil company circulars {{PPAC dealer commission page}}. The latest petrol row on PPAC's page, effective 1 December 2024, is:

| Item | Petrol |
|---|---|
| Commission | Rs 3144.03 a kilolitre plus 0.870 per cent of the product billable price {{PPAC dealer commission page}} |

The product billable price is set by each oil company {{PPAC dealer commission page}}. This is what the source states, not what a pump earns. See [dealer commission](@money-commission).

## What do the taxes add?

Excise duty is a fixed amount per litre; VAT is a percentage set by each state {{PIB 19 Sep 2020}}. For petrol, PPAC lists these central excise items {{PPAC excise tariff table}}:

- basic excise duty Rs 1.40 a litre
- special additional excise duty Rs 3.00 a litre
- Agriculture Infrastructure and Development Cess Rs 2.50 a litre
- road and infrastructure cess Rs 5.00 a litre

These add up to Rs 11.90 a litre (our arithmetic). Note 3 on the same PPAC page says an additional basic excise duty of Rs 2 a litre has been levied on unblended petrol (not blended with ethanol or methanol) intended for retail sale from 1 November 2022 {{PPAC excise tariff table}}. The page does not say whether a later notification changed this; check the Department of Revenue notifications.

State rates differ. Three examples from PPAC's table, on the page updated 18 November 2026 {{PPAC VAT rates page}}:

| State | Petrol |
|---|---|
| Delhi | 19.40% VAT |
| Goa | 21.5% VAT plus 0.5% green cess |
| Chhattisgarh | 24% VAT plus Rs 2 a litre VAT |

Rates change, so check PPAC's page or the state's notification for the current rate. Ask a chartered accountant for tax advice.
