## What must the pump offer a customer?

As on 18 November 2026, these duties are from the guideline for public sector oil company dealers (MDG 2024). A private company's dealer should ask that company for its own rules.

1. The dealer should ensure quality product at correct quantity and correct price {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(i)}}.
2. The dealer keeps filter paper (Whatman 2) to check petrol, and gives it to customers on demand {{MDG 2024, clause 1.4.1(a)}}.
3. A suggestion and complaint book is kept and shown on demand {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(iv)}}.
4. The dealer's name and number, and the oil company contact for complaints, are displayed {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(viii)}}.

## How does the oil company test fuel?

Oil companies are to draw samples after a specific complaint against an outlet, or after a density or filter paper test failure {{MDG 2024, clause 2.4}}. Samples are drawn from the nozzles of the dispensing units {{MDG 2024, clause 2.2.2}}, and sealed and labelled {{MDG 2024, clause 2.4}}.

Tests follow Bureau of Indian Standards specifications, such as IS 2796 for petrol and IS 1460 for diesel {{MDG 2024, clause 2.10}}. Samples should preferably reach the lab within 10 days and be tested within the next 15 days, with results sent to the dealer preferably within 5 days after that {{MDG 2024, clause 2.10}}. These are targets, not promises to the complainant.

## What can I do if I think the fuel is bad?

1. Keep the receipt and note the date, time, pump, nozzle and product. This is practice, not a rule.
2. Write in the complaint book and note the oil company number on the board {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(viii)}}.
3. Use the oil company's helpline or the National Consumer Helpline (1915) {{NCH contact page}}. The routes are listed on [the short delivery page](@customers-short-delivery).

## Can I go to a consumer commission?

The Consumer Protection Act defines a defect as a fault in quality, quantity, potency, purity or standard {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.2(10)}}. A complaint about goods sold may be filed with a District Commission {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.35}}. Where the defect cannot be found without a test, the Commission sends a sealed sample to a laboratory, and the complainant first deposits the fee {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.38(2)}}. The two-year limit is in {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.69}}. For money and court steps see [refund and consumer court](@customers-refund). For a legal question ask an advocate.
