OISD standards are the oil industry's own safety rulebooks. This page says what the OISD website and the Petroleum Rules 2002 state, as on 23 November 2026. It does not reproduce the standards, which OISD sells. It is not legal advice.

## What is OISD?

OISD is a technical directorate under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. It formulates and coordinates self-regulatory safety measures for the oil and gas industry, and was established in 1986 {{OISD website, About Us}}. Its listed roles include developing and revising the standards, external and surprise safety audits, a safety audit before new facilities are commissioned, and incident investigation {{OISD website, About Us}}.

## Which standards name petrol pumps?

OISD publishes one list of standards with the current edition of each. Reading its titles, only one names petroleum retail outlets:

| Standard | Title on the OISD list | Edition shown |
|---|---|---|
| OISD-STD-225 | Storage, Handling and Dispensing at Petroleum Retail Outlets | October 2010 {{OISD standards list}} |
| OISD-RP-110 | Recommended Practices on Static Electricity | October 2018 {{OISD standards list}} |
| OISD-STD-113 | Classification of Area for Electrical Installations at Hydrocarbon Processing and Handling Facilities | October 2013 {{OISD standards list}} |
| OISD-STD-135 | Inspection of Loading and Unloading Hoses for Petroleum Products | September 2021 {{OISD standards list}} |

The last three are general standards; their titles do not say they are for retail outlets. Which of them an oil company asks of its outlets is in its own checklist, so ask the oil company. Two more cover refuelling of other fuels: OISD-STD-179 for CNG, April 2016, and OISD-STD-210 for LPG for automotive use, August 2023 {{OISD standards list}}.

## How do the Petroleum Rules use OISD?

The Petroleum Rules 2002 define an OISD standard as a technical standard formulated by OISD and approved by the Safety Council {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 2}}. They name OISD-STD-105, 116, 117, 118, 141 and 156 as approved specifications {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 2}}. Standard 225 is not in that list. Rule 117 uses OISD-STD-117 for the fire-fighting scale of installations; a 2024 amendment points new installations to OISD standard 244 {{G.S.R. 154(E), 4 Mar 2024}}. For the pump-level rules, see [fire extinguishers](@safety-fire-extinguishers), [earthing](@safety-earthing), [tanker unloading](@safety-tanker-unloading) and [electrical fittings](@equipment-flameproof-fittings).

## What does a standard cost?

OISD sells its standards to registered users: Rs 10,000 plus GST of Rs 500, so Rs 10,500, for one standard {{OISD purchase page}}.
