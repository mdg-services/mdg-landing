Shell sells fuel in India as a private company. This page repeats what shell.in says as on 23 November 2026. This is no promise of a dealership. The public sector guideline (MDG 2024) does not bind Shell; see [how a dealership is chosen](@dealership-process) for that route.

## What two routes does Shell publish?

| | Dealership | Retailership |
|---|---|---|
| Who owns the station | The dealer: land, infrastructure and fuel are the dealer's {{Shell India dealership page}} | Shell: land, infrastructure and fuel are owned by Shell {{Shell India retailership page}} |
| Land | The dealer secures it; Shell helps with key permits {{Shell India dealership page}} | Shell finds and secures it {{Shell India retailership page}} |
| Investment Shell states | About Rs 75 lakh to Rs 3 crore, excluding land {{Shell India dealership page}} | Two figures on one page: an average of Rs 10 lakh, including a Rs 7 lakh refundable deposit, and a minimum of Rs 18 lakh {{Shell India retailership page}} |

Shell calls these figures indicative and subject to change {{Shell India retailership page}}. The retailership page does not reconcile its two numbers, so ask Shell which applies before relying on either.

## What does Shell say about fees and income?

For dealerships, Shell says there is no security deposit for allotment, but there is a minimal non-refundable application fee {{Shell India dealership page}}. The dealer receives a commission on fuel sales and pays pre-agreed royalties on non-fuel sales {{Shell India dealership page}}. This page gives no income figure; Shell says earnings are not guaranteed {{Shell India retailership page}}.

## How does the selection go?

For a dealership, Shell asks for the proposed site location, plot size or layout and a short profile by email. If the details suit, you are invited to talk to the Shell team {{Shell India dealership page}}. For a retailership, the steps are: check eligibility, submit the form, and wait for a reply, which Shell aims to give within 15 working days {{Shell India retailership page}}. It says selection is likely to take three to four months {{Shell India retailership page}}.

Shell says an appointment follows its standard terms and a signed agreement, at its sole discretion {{Shell India retailership page}}.

## Who should I trust?

Only Shell's own pages. Shell warns of scams on SMS, WhatsApp and social media, and says it does not sell shares or business opportunities online or on unofficial platforms {{Shell India fraud alert}}. Paying anyone else for a dealership is a sign of fraud. MDG Services does not sell or arrange dealerships. Ask an advocate or chartered accountant before you invest.
