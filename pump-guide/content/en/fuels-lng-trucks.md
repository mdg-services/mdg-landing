LNG is liquefied natural gas. This page gives what the Petroleum Ministry says about LNG stations for trucks, as on 21 November 2026. It does not give LNG prices, because we found no official dated price page.

## How many LNG stations are there?

On 7 August 2025 the Ministry told Lok Sabha that 13 LNG retail stations had been commissioned by public sector oil and gas companies, and 16 retail stations owned by private entities were also operational {{PIB 7 Aug 2025}}. That is 29 together. It is the latest official count we could open on 21 November 2026, so check with the operator for today's position.

## Where are they meant to be?

The Government is pursuing stations across the Golden Quadrilateral, national highways, the East-West and North-South highways and major mining clusters {{PIB 7 Aug 2025}}. In November 2020 the Minister laid the foundation stone of the first 50 LNG stations on these routes, with Indian Oil to build 20 and Bharat Petroleum and HPCL 11 each {{PIB 19 Nov 2020}}. That was a plan; the 2025 count above shows what was running.

## What rules apply?

- The Government has recognised LNG as a transport fuel and notified emission standards for LNG vehicles {{PIB 7 Aug 2025}}.
- The Static and Mobile Pressure Vessels (Unfired) Rules were amended in 2025 to let LNG vehicles with spark ignition or compression ignition engines operate in hazardous areas, and to allow mobile LNG dispensing in railways, mining, waterways and testing laboratories {{PIB 7 Aug 2025}}.
- A 2020 gas regulator notification lets an entity set up an LNG retail outlet without a city gas authorisation, for liquid LNG to the transport sector only {{PIB 7 Aug 2025}}.

Storage and licensing details are on the PESO site; see [PESO licences](@licences-peso).

## What does it mean for a petrol pump dealer?

In practice, an LNG point at a pump is the oil company's decision, not the dealer's alone. Ask the company in writing before planning any gas facility. See [CNG and EV](@fuels-cng-ev) and [compressed biogas](@fuels-cbg-satat) for the other gas fuels.
