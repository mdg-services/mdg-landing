## What does BS VI diesel mean?

BS VI is the fuel quality India moved to on 1 April 2020, skipping BS V. The main change is sulphur: from 50 ppm in BS IV to a maximum of 10 ppm in BS VI fuel {{PIB 25 Jul 2022}}. The Ministry says this allowed particulate filters and SCR systems in BS VI vehicles {{PIB 25 Jul 2022}}.

## What does the diesel standard IS 1460 set?

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) publishes IS 1460 for automotive diesel. Its sixth revision is dated 2017, and a BIS note on petroleum products lists it with BS VI limits added {{BIS petroleum standards deck}}. A later revision may exist, so check the edition in force with BIS or your oil company.

The BS VI column of that BIS note, and a Bharat Petroleum data sheet for its BS VI diesel that says it conforms to IS 1460:2017, agree:

| Property | Limit |
|---|---|
| Density at 15 degrees C | 810 to 845 kg/m3 {{BIS petroleum standards deck}} |
| Sulphur | 10 mg/kg (ppm) at most {{BPCL HSD data sheet}} |
| Cetane number | 51 at least {{BPCL HSD data sheet}} |
| Viscosity at 40 degrees C | 2.0 to 4.5 cSt {{BPCL HSD data sheet}} |
| Biodiesel (FAME) | 7 per cent by volume at most {{BPCL HSD data sheet}} |

Other companies' data sheets are on their own sites.

## How much biodiesel can diesel carry?

Blending of biodiesel in diesel was allowed up to 5 per cent in 2002 and up to 7 per cent in 2017 {{BIS petroleum standards deck}}. Blends of 6 to 20 per cent biodiesel with the rest as IS 1460 diesel have a separate standard, IS 16531 {{BIS petroleum standards deck}}.

On policy, the National Policy on Biofuels sets an indicative target of 5 per cent biodiesel in diesel by 2030 {{PIB 2 Dec 2021}}. The Petroleum Ministry says it has issued guidelines for sale of biodiesel for blending with high speed diesel (2019) and cut GST on biodiesel for blending from 12 to 5 per cent {{PIB 4 Dec 2025}}.

## What should a dealer do?

In practice, a dealer sells what the oil company supplies and keeps its quality records. For the product's grade or blend, ask the oil company in writing. For density and quality checks, see [the tank page](@equipment-tanks).
