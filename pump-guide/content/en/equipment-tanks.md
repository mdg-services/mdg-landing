The tank lies out of sight under the forecourt, so safety depends on how it is fitted and tested. All as on 9 November 2026.

## How is the tank fitted?

| Part | What the licence conditions say |
|---|---|
| Tank and pit | Gas-tight, outside any building, in a masonry or concrete pit packed round with sand, earth or clay so no air space is left; a welded tank tested to 0.25 kg per square centimetre and buried on private or leased land, at least 1.5 metres from the boundary, needs no pit {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, conditions 1 and 2}} |
| Fill and dip pipes | Carried down nearly to the bottom; no other openings except to fill, ventilate or dip {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 4}} |
| Vent pipe | One for each tank, to open air, at least 4 metres high and 4 metres from adjoining property or the nearest opening of the sales room {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 5}}; joined to another tank's vent only with written sanction since 4 March 2024 {{G.S.R. 154(E), 4 Mar 2024}} |

## How is a tank tested?

1. After it is installed, re-installed or majorly repaired, and before use, a competent person tests it by water pressure; since 4 March 2024 a third party inspection agency may too {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 126}} {{G.S.R. 154(E), 4 Mar 2024}}.
2. A certificate of safety is needed before petrol is first stored, and when a tank is installed or its position shifted {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 130}}.

In the Petroleum Rules as amended up to 4 March 2024, the latest we opened, we did not find a repeat-test interval for these tanks.

Before repair, a tank must be cleared of petroleum and vapour and certified "gas-free" in writing by a competent person; the licensee keeps that certificate at least three months {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 8}}.

## What leak checks apply?

CPCB's guidelines for setting up new petrol pumps ask for a daily volume-balance report, manual gauging once a month checked against the automatic gauge, and a leak test of all underground tanks and pipelines every 7 years {{CPCB new pump guidelines 7 Jan 2020, B.1, B.2 and B.4}}. The Supreme Court directed State Pollution Control Boards to see them followed {{CPCB OM, 16 Jun 2023}}. They are written for new pumps: ask the State Pollution Control Board or your oil company if they reach an older one. The same memo names a CPCB addendum of 16 August 2021, which we did not open {{CPCB OM, 16 Jun 2023}}.

A leak or spill of more than 165 litres must be reported by the oil company within 24 hours, and the tank stays out of use until PESO and the Board are satisfied {{CPCB new pump guidelines 7 Jan 2020, A.3}}. In practice, stop sales from a tank you suspect and call your oil company. To read the level, see [what is dip](/guides/stock-variation/what-is-dip/) and the [dip chart calculator](/tools/dip-chart-calculator/).
