## How does the dealer commission work?

The oil company pays the dealer a commission on each kilolitre (KL) of petrol and diesel sold. It has two parts: a fixed amount per KL, and a percentage of the product billable price, which the oil company sets {{PPAC dealer commission table}}. PPAC, the Petroleum Planning and Analysis Cell, names circulars of the petroleum ministry and the oil companies as its source {{PPAC dealer commission table}}.

## What are the latest rates?

As on 9 November 2026, the last row of PPAC's table is 1 December 2024, and the table shows no revision between 19 February 2019 and 30 October 2024 {{PPAC dealer commission table}}.

| Effective | Petrol | Diesel |
|---|---|---|
| 19 Feb 2019 | Rs 2,637.80/KL + 0.859% | Rs 2,000.60/KL + 0.257% |
| 30 Oct 2024 | Rs 3,154.00/KL + 0.859% | Rs 2,340.16/KL + 0.257% |
| 1 Dec 2024 | Rs 3,144.03/KL + 0.870% | Rs 2,332.51/KL + 0.266% |

## A worked example

These numbers are made up: a pump sells 100 KL of petrol in a month, and the billable price is Rs 55,000 per KL (the oil company sets the real one). The petrol rate is the one effective 1 December 2024.

| Step | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed part | 3,144.03 x 100 KL | Rs 3,14,403 |
| Percentage part | 0.870% x 55,000 x 100 KL | Rs 47,850 |
| Commission | 3,14,403 + 47,850 | Rs 3,62,253 |

That is about Rs 3.62 a litre, paid before the pump's costs. It shows the method, not what any pump earns.

## What is the commission not?

It is not the selling price, and not profit. In practice, staff, electricity, losses and interest are paid out of it.

Where the company built the facilities, a licence fee per KL may be recovered: PPAC lists Rs 443.50 for petrol and Rs 369.58 for diesel at "a" sites, and Rs 199.97 and Rs 166.64 at "b" sites, with 18% GST {{PPAC dealer commission table}}. The June 2023 dealer guideline lists different figures ({{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8(E)}}; see [the cost page](@money)), so check your own agreement. Since 1 August 2017, eligible dealers also get slab-based incentives through credit notes {{PPAC dealer commission table}}.
