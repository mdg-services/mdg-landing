## The order of the checks

1. **Documents.** Within 10 days of being picked you upload the documents and pay the initial security deposit; scrutiny starts only after both arrive {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}.
2. **Corrections.** A deficiency that can be corrected gets 21 days {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}. Some cannot be corrected at all, such as a change in the plot's khasra or survey number {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.23}}.
3. **Land evaluation.** The committee's visit, with at least 10 days' notice {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(I)}}.
4. **FVC.** Only if the land is found suitable, again with 10 days' notice {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}.
5. **Letter of intent.** If the FVC is in order, you are told to collect it from the oil company's office within 10 days {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}. See the [letter of intent](@dealership-letter-of-intent).

## What the land committee looks at

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| Location | Inside the advertised area or stretch, as on the advertisement date |
| Size | Minimum frontage, area and depth as advertised, after leaving the road's right of way |
| National highway | Meets NHAI norms |
| Power lines | No high-tension line above 11 kV crossing the plot |

All four come from the brochure {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(I)}}. If you offered more than one plot, the committee asks you in writing which one to evaluate {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4 Note 1}}. If the land fails, your candidature is rejected and selection goes on with the other applicants. At a location reserved for SC or ST, you may instead be considered with Group 3 applicants {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(I)}}. Plot sizes are on [land size](@site-land-size) and highway rules on [highway access](@site-highway-access).

## What happens at the FVC

- The committee checks the application details against the documents uploaded after selection {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.15}}.
- You show every original you uploaded, and hand over the original affidavits {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.15}}. Not showing originals is a deficiency that cannot be corrected {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.23}}.
- A false or hidden fact found at any stage can end the application, or the dealership later {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.22}}.

Nobody needs to be paid for a land check or FVC. The oil companies warn of fake messages asking for money for "land evaluation" {{Oil companies fraud notice}}.

## If a check fails

The selection is cancelled and the next person is taken: a fresh draw among the remaining eligible applicants, or the next highest bidder {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.17}}.
