## What "company-owned" does and does not mean

The advertisement describes a CC or CFS site as one where the company expects you to have or arrange suitable land and give it to the company on sale or long lease {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.24}}. The company can also take the land straight from its owner {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.3}}. So "company-owned" is about who owns the built outlet, not a plot the company already holds. For what CODO, DODO and COCO mean, see [company-owned or dealer-owned](@dealership-company-owned-vs-dealer-owned).

## Two routes when you do not own a plot

1. **A firm offer (Group 2).** A landowner agrees to sell or lease you the plot and signs an Appendix III affidavit on or before your application date. It works at any location, but Group 1 applicants are drawn first {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi), cl.14(H)}}. See [land groups](@dealership-land-groups).
2. **SC/ST locations (Group 3).** You may apply without land and are asked for it within 90 days only if no Group 1 or Group 2 applicant is selected {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}. These sites are built under the [Corpus Fund Scheme](@dealership-corpus-fund-scheme) {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.3}}.

For a CC or CFS site, a leased plot's deed must allow sub-lease to the company {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.

## Who builds what on a CC site

You hand over the land developed: entry and exit, service road and boundary walls. The company provides tanks, dispensers, automation, sales room, driveway and canopy {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8}}. You pay the company a licence fee per kilolitre sold, which is higher on CC sites than on dealer-owned ones {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8}}:

| Licence fee, incl. GST | Petrol (Rs/KL) | Diesel (Rs/KL) |
|---|---|---|
| CC site, including Corpus Fund | 435.83 | 363.19 |
| Dealer-owned (DC) site | 196.51 | 163.76 |

## How a CC site is allotted

Outside the Corpus Fund, a CC site goes by bidding; you bid online with the application {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(B), cl.14(G)}}. After the letter of intent the full bid is paid within 15 days of the NOC {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}}. A person whose family unit already holds a CC or Corpus Fund dealership cannot apply for another CC site {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.10}}.

Figures are as on 26 November 2026, for the public sector companies only; each advertisement rules. Full costs: [what it costs](@money). A dealership comes only through the oil companies' advertisements; MDG Services does not sell or arrange one.
