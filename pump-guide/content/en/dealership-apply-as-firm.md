This page is as on 25 November 2026 and describes BPCL's published brochure; it is not legal advice, so ask an advocate or the oil company. Other oil companies may set their own conditions; read the brochure named in your advertisement.

## Which applicants are allowed

BPCL's brochure for regular and rural outlets, valid from June 2023, has two kinds of applicant: individual (a proprietorship or a partnership) {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 4}} and non-individual entity (government bodies, registered societies, charitable trusts, companies) {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 5}}.

## Partnership firms

Each partner must meet the individual conditions, such as Indian citizenship, a PAN and age 21 to 60 {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 4}}. Land owned by a partner counts as the firm's land {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 4(viii)}}. Each partner files a separate form and fee, clubbed online, with the proposed partnership deed {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 4(viii)}}.

A selected sole proprietor normally makes the spouse a 50 per cent partner after the letter of intent, unless the spouse is employed or does not wish to {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 7}}.

## Companies, societies and trusts

These apply only in the open category, except that a primary agricultural credit society can also apply for one reserved category {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 5}}. The conditions are:

- Registered in India, with registration or incorporation at least 3 years before the application {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 5}}.
- Land owned or on long lease in the entity's own name, not in a person's name {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 5}}.
- Societies and companies: a net profit in the previous 3 financial years, certified by a chartered accountant {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 5}}.
- A resolution authorising the signatory {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 5}}.

A Hindu Undivided Family cannot apply {{BPCL dealer selection brochure June 2023, para 10A(viii)}}.

## Where to apply

Locations and application forms come only through the oil company's own advertisement for that location. Nobody else can allot a dealership, and paying anyone for one is a sign of fraud. See [who can apply](@dealership-eligibility) and [the selection process](@dealership-process).
