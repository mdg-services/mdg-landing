## Before the draw

Draws are used for dealer-owned ("B" or "DC") sites and Corpus Fund sites {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(A)}}. Company-owned sites use [bidding](@dealership-bidding-process). The list of eligible applicants for each location is put on the portal {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}. Applicants who hold the land (Group 1) are drawn first; Group 2 (a firm offer of land) is drawn only if Group 1 has nobody, or nobody suitable {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}.

## The draw day

- The draw is computerised, among the eligible applicants of the location {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}.
- It is held at the District Collector's or District Magistrate's office as far as possible, or in a public hall, hotel or oil company office, in the presence of invited guests {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}.
- Only messages to your registered mobile number and email count, so check them {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}.

## Where the result appears

As on 26 November 2026, each advertisement on the dealer chayan portal lists its locations with the mode of selection and a status {{Dealer Chayan advertisement pages}}. After the draw the status reads "Results of draw declared." Its List Of Applicants shows each applicant's form number, group and status, with "Selected" against the provisionally selected applicant {{Dealer Chayan advertisement pages}}. The [portal page](@dealership-chayan-portal) explains the portal itself.

## What the winner does next

1. Within 10 days of the message, pay the initial security deposit online and upload the documents. If you miss it, a reminder gives 10 more days, and after that the candidature is cancelled {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}.
2. The initial deposit is 10% of the security deposit, for example Rs 50,000 on the Rs 5 lakh deposit of a regular outlet in the Open category {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.20}}.
3. A land check and [field verification](@dealership-field-verification) follow, each with 10 days' notice {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(I), cl.15}}.
4. A [letter of intent](@dealership-letter-of-intent) comes after that, not before 30 days from the result {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}}.

## Redraws

If the winner is cancelled at scrutiny, land check or field verification, or a letter of intent is withdrawn, the draw is held again among the remaining eligible applicants, who are told by email or SMS {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.17}}. If only one remains, that applicant is told of the provisional selection directly {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.17}}. No agency or person is allowed to ask you for money at any stage of selection {{Oil companies fraud notice}}.
