## Where hydrogen is dispensed

As on 22 November 2026, the official sources we opened describe hydrogen refuelling inside pilots, not as a normal pump product.

| Date | What the source says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 23 Sep 2023 | Indian Oil's first project to dispense green hydrogen at 350 bar for fuel cell buses; a dispensing facility at its R&D campus in Faridabad | {{PIB release 23 Sep 2023}} |
| 21 Oct 2024 | India's first hydrogen dispensing station is operational at that Faridabad centre | {{PIB release 21 Oct 2024}} |
| 3 Mar 2025 | Five pilot projects sanctioned: 37 vehicles and 9 hydrogen refuelling stations | {{PIB release 3 Mar 2025}} |
| 28 Oct 2026 | Twelve projects, 70 vehicles (27 buses, 43 trucks), 16 refuelling stations across 21 routes | {{PIB backgrounder 28 Oct 2026}} |

The counts grew between March 2025 and October 2026, so treat each as a snapshot. The releases do not say any station is on a petrol pump forecourt.

## The government scheme behind it

The Union Cabinet approved the National Green Hydrogen Mission to make India a global hub for green hydrogen {{PIB release 8 Dec 2025}}. The new and renewable energy ministry's transport guidelines of February 2024 name hydrogen refuelling stations as one of two thrust areas, next to hydrogen buses, trucks and four-wheelers {{PIB release 14 Feb 2024}}.

## Which rules apply

PESO's Gas Cylinders Rules 2016 are being amended for hydrogen dispensing stations {{G.S.R. 157(E), 5 Mar 2024 draft}}, and its Static and Mobile Pressure Vessels (Unfired) Rules 2016 for hydrogen vessels {{G.S.R. 422(E), 25 Jun 2025}}. The draft of 5 March 2024 would define a compressed hydrogen gas dispensing station and require it to be built and run to NFPA-2 and ISO 19880 {{G.S.R. 157(E), 5 Mar 2024 draft}}. It would add a Form H licence and extra application papers, among them a hazard study and an emergency response plan {{G.S.R. 157(E), 5 Mar 2024 draft}}.

Final rules have since added hydrogen items. The Third Amendment of 28 July 2025 sets extra tests for hydrogen cylinders and a standard for hydrogen storage roofs {{G.S.R. 501(E), 28 Jul 2025}}. The pressure vessel amendment of 25 June 2025 adds standards for hydrogen vessels {{G.S.R. 422(E), 25 Jun 2025}}. The final texts we opened do not repeat the dispensing station definition from the draft. The Third Amendment also adds a condition on hydrogen vehicle fuel systems to Form H {{G.S.R. 501(E), 28 Jul 2025}}, so a Form H may already be in force; we did not trace how. Check PESO for the current status. For petrol and CNG, see [CNG and EV at a pump](@fuels-cng-ev).
