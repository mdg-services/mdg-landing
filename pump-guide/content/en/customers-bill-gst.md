Many people ask why their petrol bill shows no GST: fuel is not under GST today. This is as on 19 November 2026, and not tax advice; ask a chartered accountant about your own case.

## Is there GST on petrol and diesel?

No. Five products, petroleum crude, high speed diesel, petrol, natural gas and aviation turbine fuel, have been kept out of GST for the time being; the GST Council decides the date they come in {{CBIC GST FAQ, 3rd edition, 15 Dec 2018}}. The Act says the same: central tax on them is levied from a date the Government notifies on the Council's recommendation {{CGST Act 2017, section 9(2)}}. The rate notification of 17 September 2025 still describes petrol, diesel and ATF as not in GST {{Notification 9/2025-IGST (Rate), Schedule II}}.

Until then the older taxes continue: VAT and central excise {{CBIC GST FAQ, 3rd edition, 15 Dec 2018}}. That FAQ is a 2018 edition, so we checked the Act and the 2025 rate notification, which say the same; we found no change as on 19 November 2026. For the pump's side, see [GST at a petrol pump](@running-gst).

## What does the bill show?

| Item on the bill | GST |
|---|---|
| Petrol or diesel | None; outside GST {{CGST Act 2017, section 9(2)}} |
| Lubricating oil and similar | 18 per cent schedule {{Notification 9/2025-IGST (Rate), Schedule II}} |

For taxable goods a registered seller must issue a tax invoice showing the description, quantity, value and tax charged {{CGST Act 2017, section 31(1)}}. A registered seller may skip the tax invoice if the goods supplied are worth less than Rs 200, on the conditions the rules set {{CGST Act 2017, section 31(3)}}.

We found no central rule that lists what a fuel bill must contain. In practice it shows the date, product, litres, price and total.

## Your right to a bill

The Consumer Protection Act lists not issuing a bill, cash memo or receipt for goods sold, in the manner prescribed, as an unfair trade practice {{Consumer Protection Act 2019, s.2(47)}}. A bill is your proof in a later complaint: see [short delivery](@customers-short-delivery) and [refunds](@customers-refund). For card or UPI, see [paying by UPI or card](@customers-digital-payments).
