A petrol pump needs two central approvals: a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the District Authority, and a licence from PESO, the Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation. Pollution, fire and Legal Metrology are usually handled by state offices, and practice differs by state. Facts are as on 9 November 2026.

## Which approvals does a petrol pump need, in order?

| Approval | Who gives it | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Letter of intent | The oil company | Your selection as dealer; PESO asks for a copy with the plan application {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}} |
| Prior approval of plans | PESO circle or sub-circle office | Layout of the premises {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 131}} |
| NOC | District Authority {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 144}} | No objection to a licence for your site |
| Licence in Form XIV | PESO circle or sub-circle office | Storing petrol and diesel in tanks {{PESO SOP, Petroleum Rules 2002}} |
| Pollution consent | State Pollution Control Board, if your state asks | See [pollution consent](@licences-pollution) |
| Fire approval | State fire service, if your state asks | May differ by state |
| Dispenser verification | Usually the state Legal Metrology department | See [Legal Metrology](@equipment-metrology) |
| GST registration | GST office, depending on what the pump sells | See [GST at a pump](@running-gst) |

## What is central, and what depends on the state?

The Petroleum Rules 2002 are made by the Central Government, so the PESO licence and rule 144 NOC apply in every state {{Petroleum Rules 2002, preamble}}. The NOC comes from the Commissioner or a Deputy Commissioner of Police in towns that have a Commissioner, and from the District Magistrate elsewhere {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 2}}. District offices issue it through an online system called LSDA {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}.

The NOC form records the Fire Department's comments on fire tender access {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, rule 144}}. Your state fire service may also issue its own certificate, so ask the district fire officer. Local registrations such as a trade licence depend on your city.

## How long does the PESO licence last?

Until 31 December of the year it is granted or renewed up to, for at most three years {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 142}}. Steps, documents and fee are on the [PESO licence page](@licences-peso).

## Who gives you a dealership?

Only the oil companies, through their own advertisements. A payment to anyone for a dealership, site or licence is a fraud sign. See [how a dealer is chosen](@dealership-process).
