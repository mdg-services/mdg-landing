Nayara Energy sells fuel through its own retail outlets, run by franchisees it calls dealers. This page repeats what Nayara's own website says as on 14 November 2026. Nothing here is a promise of a dealership.

## Is Nayara's selection the same as the public sector companies'?

No. Nayara is a private company, so it chooses franchisees by its own method. The MDG 2024 selection guideline holds only for the public sector oil companies; see [how a dealership is chosen](@dealership-process) for that route.

## How does Nayara say it selects a dealer?

Nayara's FAQ says it assesses the identified plot for business potential and screens the applicant for financial capability. The land papers and personal details go to Nayara for review. If both suit, a Letter of Appointment is issued {{Nayara Energy FAQs}}.

An Indian national with a suitable plot who will lease it to Nayara is eligible. An applicant without land can be considered by buying or leasing land and leasing it on to Nayara {{Nayara Energy FAQs}}.

## Where does the application go, and what does it ask for?

The application form is an Expression of Interest, sent with land papers and documents showing financial and business capability, and a demand draft for the application fee {{Nayara Energy FAQs}}. The FAQ states the fee as INR 5 Lakhs, non-refundable once the Letter of Appointment is issued {{Nayara Energy FAQs}}. Check the current amount with the company.

Nayara's pages show land sizes that differ from page to page: the apply page gives 30 m frontage and depth in a city or town and 45 m on highways {{Nayara Energy apply page}}, the eligibility page 25 m and 35 m {{Nayara Energy eligibility page}}. Ask Nayara which applies to your site.

## Who should I contact, and what does Nayara warn about?

For an enquiry about setting up a petrol pump, Nayara gives the toll-free number 1800 1200 330 {{Nayara Energy apply page}}.

It warns that unauthorised persons or companies have misrepresented themselves in its name, and that it has not authorised any third party to act for it {{Nayara Energy apply page}}. Paying anyone other than the company for a dealership is a sign of fraud. MDG Services does not sell or arrange dealerships.

Ask an advocate before signing a lease or agreement.
