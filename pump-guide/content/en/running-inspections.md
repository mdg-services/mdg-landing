This page reports what official texts say, as on 20 November 2026. It is not legal advice; for a particular inspection ask an advocate or your oil company. The oil company rules below are the public sector guideline (MDG 2024); a private company's dealer should ask that company.

## Who may inspect, under which text?

| Who | Under | What they may do |
|---|---|---|
| Gazetted officer of the Centre or State, authorised by order; police officer not below Deputy Superintendent | MS and HSD Order 2005, clause 7 | Enter and search the premises, take samples, seize stock, inspect and seize books and records {{MS HSD Order 2005, clause 7(1)}} |
| Oil company officer not below Sales Officer | Same clause {{MS HSD Order 2005, clause 7(1)}} | The same powers; also random sampling over and above routine samples {{MDG 2024, 2.4}} |
| Controller of Explosives, District Magistrates, Magistrates, police officer not below Inspector | Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 198 | Enter, inspect and search where petroleum is stored; inspect tanks, plant and appliances; take samples; seize petroleum or equipment {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 198}} |
| Sales Officer, inspecting teams, senior officers, mobile labs | MDG 2024, chapter 2 and 7 | Random sampling of at least 2 per cent of the network every month {{MDG 2024, 2.4}}; surprise quality checks by mobile labs {{MDG 2024, 7.1}} |

The PESO licence also says free access must be given to any inspector or sampling officer at reasonable times {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 23}}. Dispensers are a separate check: see [Legal Metrology verification](@equipment-metrology), which is re-verification every 24 months {{G.S.R. 905(E), 18 Dec 2025}}.

## What do they check?

- **Product quality.** Samples from tank, nozzle or vehicle are tested against the BIS specifications {{MS HSD Order 2005, clause 8(1)}}. See [density](@running-density-check).
- **Stock and records.** Officers may inspect a dealer's books, registers and documents {{MS HSD Order 2005, clause 7(1)}}; the dealer keeps inspection reports at the outlet {{MDG 2024, 1.5}}.
- **Equipment and safety.** Tanks, plant and appliances against the Act and rules {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 198}}.

## What should happen during one?

An officer who seizes stock or documents must record the reasons in writing and give a copy {{MS HSD Order 2005, clause 7(2)}}. A show cause notice follows within 30 days of the inspection, or of the test result if samples were drawn {{MDG 2024, 8.5.6}}. In practice, a manager asks to see the officer's identity and authorisation and keeps copies. The actions are in [marketing discipline](@running-marketing-discipline).
