## What the portal is, and what is on it

Its own heading reads "Retail Outlet (Petrol Pump) Dealer Selection Portal for Oil Marketing Companies" {{Dealer Chayan portal home}}. It has two tabs, Advertisements 2018 and Advertisements 2023, one for each selection brochure {{Dealer Chayan portal home}}. For the stages after you apply, see the [selection process](@dealership-process). For who may apply, see [eligibility](@dealership-eligibility).

## Are any advertisements open today?

As on 20 November 2026 each advertisement listed on both tabs carries the label Closed {{Dealer Chayan portal home}}. The 2023 tab says the last date for all locations was extended to 17 October 2023, except where a corrigendum gave another date {{Dealer Chayan portal home}}. We cannot give a date for the next round, because no one has published one. Check the portal and the newspapers; an advertisement states its last date and the facility closes right after it {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(D)}}.

## How to register

1. Keep a valid PAN, an email address and a mobile number ready {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}.
2. Open the portal, choose the Advertisements 2023 tab and click Register Now {{Selection brochure June 2023, Appendix IC}}.
3. Verify the email with an OTP, then the mobile number with a second OTP {{Selection brochure June 2023, Appendix IC}}.
4. Your username and password arrive by email {{Selection brochure June 2023, Appendix IC}}.

Use correct contact details. Every message goes only to the mobile number and email you registered with, and missed messages are not excused {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}. An account with no login within 30 minutes is deleted, and you can register again {{Dealer Chayan FAQ}}.

Registration is only an account. You apply later, online, against one advertised location, and pay the fee through the portal's payment gateway {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}.

## Fraud: what the oil companies say

A notice on the portal warns of people using the oil companies' names to offer dealerships and ask for "registration charges" {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. The companies say officials do not write from Gmail or Yahoo addresses or use WhatsApp, and all payments go only through the portal {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. A dealership comes only through the oil companies' own advertisements, and paying anyone for one is a fraud sign. MDG Services does not sell or arrange dealerships. Report suspicious approaches to the local police station {{Oil companies fraud notice}}.
