This page reports a published guideline, as on 20 November 2026. It is not legal advice. Rules differ by oil company, and the date and version matter, so ask your oil company, and an advocate for any family or property question. The text here is the reconstitution guideline the oil companies framed on an industry basis, in effect from 4 July 2018, as listed on Hindustan Petroleum's site {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, A}}.

## Can a dealer sell the dealership?

Not by a private sale. Any change of owner or partner is a reconstitution: the oil company checks the incoming person, gives an in principle approval and a fresh agreement follows {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, I.9}}.

| Situation | What the guideline says |
|---|---|
| First 3 years | New minority partners, outsiders too, up to 49 per cent in all {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, G.1}} |
| After 3 years | The sole proprietor or all partners may resign and transfer the share to family, existing or outside partners {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, G.3}} |
| Dealer dies | Reconstitution in favour of legal heirs or family, with their consent {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, G.4}} |
| No eligible or willing heir | Dealership terminated {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, G.4}} |

Dealerships given under the SC or ST category have their own share limits {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, G.12}}.

## Who can come in, and what do they show?

- Anyone coming in must meet the dealer selection criteria, except land, and must not be disqualified {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, H.1}}, and must be an Indian citizen {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, H.4}}.
- For heirs, age and education can be relaxed; the minimum age is 18, and a local guardian runs the pump for a minor {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, H.1}}.
- A legal heir or succession certificate establishes the heirs {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, H.5}}.

## What happens after a death?

1. The oil company writes within 10 days of hearing of it, to the nominee first {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, I.4}}.
2. The family has 30 days to apply, extendable by 30 {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, I.4}}.
3. Meanwhile the oil company may let heirs or surviving partners run the pump for 6 months {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, G.10}}.
4. A complete proposal is to be decided within 90 days {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, H.12}}.

The PESO licence is separate; see [licence validity and renewal](@licences-renewal). Eligibility for a new dealer is in [who can apply](@dealership-eligibility).
