This page explains who licenses a CNG station and what the licence covers, as on 23 November 2026. For the wider question of adding CNG or an EV charger to a petrol pump, see [CNG and EV charging at a petrol pump](@fuels-cng-ev). It is not legal advice; ask PESO or an advocate.

## Who licenses a CNG station?

PESO, the Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation. A CNG or compressed biogas dispensing station needs a licence in Form G of the Gas Cylinders Rules 2016. The circle offices of PESO grant the approval and the licence {{PESO GCR SOP}}.

## What does PNGRB do?

PNGRB, the gas regulator, authorises entities to lay, build, operate or expand city gas networks {{PNGRB CGD regulations 2008, reg 1}}. Gas that goes to an online CNG station before compression counts as piped gas under those regulations {{PNGRB CGD regulations 2008, reg 2(1)(i)}}. The Form G documents PESO lists do not include a PNGRB paper {{PESO GCR SOP}}.

## What are the steps?

1. **Prior approval.** Apply in Form C with a scaled layout plan, the fee, and proof of the land or the retail outlet licence details. PESO checks the layout against OISD 179 {{PESO GCR SOP}}.
2. **Licence.** Apply with the fee, the as-built drawing, and clearances from bodies such as the pollution board, fire service and panchayat {{PESO GCR SOP}}.
3. **Inspection and endorsement.** An officer inspects the premises and endorses the licence if all is in order {{PESO GCR SOP}}.
4. **Renewal.** Send the original licence, proof of land and the latest hydrotest details of the cascades. If the application reaches PESO on or before expiry, the licence stays in force until PESO decides {{PESO GCR FAQ}}.

One online PESO application can now cover the Petroleum, Gas Cylinders and SMPV(U) Rules for a fuel station {{PESO circular 8 Jul 2026}}. Renewals are on [licence renewal](@licences-renewal).

## What changed in June 2026?

Rule 48 of the Gas Cylinders Rules was omitted from 5 June 2026, and item (vi) of the documents list in Form C went with it {{GCR Second Amendment 2026, G.S.R. 447(E)}}. PESO's guide still cites rule 48 for the local authority NOC of a standalone station {{PESO GCR SOP}}. Ask your PESO circle office which papers it now wants.
