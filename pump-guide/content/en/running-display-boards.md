This page reports what the public sector oil companies' dealer guideline (MDG 2024) says, as on 22 November 2026. It applies to those companies' outlets; a private company's dealer should ask that company. It is not legal advice.

## What must be on display?

| What | Source |
|---|---|
| Retail selling prices of MS, HSD, CNG and Auto LPG sold at the outlet | {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.15}} |
| Working hours, and holiday notices well in time | {{MDG 2024, 1.6}} |
| Dealer's name and telephone number, and those of the oil company personnel to call with a complaint | {{MDG 2024, 1.6}} |
| A message that the suggestion or complaint book is available | {{MDG 2024, 1.6}} |
| Messages on the Customer Service Cell and customer education posters supplied by the oil company | {{MDG 2024, 1.6}} |
| Density and opening stock of the day | {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.17}} |
| A message that filter paper is available to customers to check petrol quality, on the Customer Education Board | {{MDG 2024, 1.4.1}} |

The guideline also lists contact details of the persons to call for complaint, grievance or emergency {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.17}}. Who to call is on [what to do at the pump and where to complain](@customers). The working hours are covered in [petrol pump timings](@running-opening-hours); the density is explained in [the density check](@running-density-check).

## What goes on the pumps themselves?

Each dispensing unit carries a message that the meter must return to zero before each delivery {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}}; a sticker ensuring zero before delivery is also listed {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.17}}. Each nozzle of a multi-product dispenser shows the name of its product {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.17}}. The toilet rules are displayed near the toilet, with the Darpan QR code for feedback and the maintenance sheet {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.14}}.

## What if a board is missing?

The guideline treats these as minor irregularities, and several count as one for taking action {{MDG 2024, clause 8.4}}. The action is a warning-cum-guidance letter first, Rs 10,000 per irregularity on the second instance and Rs 25,000 on the third {{MDG 2024, clause 8.4}}. The actions are minimum, and a case must be established before action {{MDG 2024, clauses 8.5.1 and 8.5.2}}.

## What does this page not cover?

Safety signs and the licence notices are on [petrol pump safety signs](@safety-signage). We found no size, colour or wording for boards in the guideline; the oil company may have circulars on this, which we have not read. Ask the oil company for the current checklist.
