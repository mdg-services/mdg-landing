## What are the stages, in order?

1. **Advertisement.** The company advertises in newspapers and on the portal, with the last date {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(C), cl.14(D)}}.
2. **Application.** You register, fill the form and pay the fee online. One application per location {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}.
3. **List and draw.** Eligible applicants are listed on the portal. A computerised draw or bid opening follows, as far as possible at the District Collector's or District Magistrate's office {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}.
4. **Provisional selection.** The chosen applicant uploads documents and pays 10% of the security deposit within 10 days {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}.
5. **Land check.** A company committee visits the plot, with 10 days' notice {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(I)}}.
6. **Field verification.** Original papers are checked, with 10 days' notice {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.15}}.
7. **Letter of intent.** Issued after field verification, not before 30 days from the result {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}}.

## Draw of lots or bidding: which applies?

The site type, which the company sets, decides {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.3, cl.14}}.

| Site type | How chosen | Fixed fee or minimum bid |
|---|---|---|
| Dealer-owned ("B" or "DC") | Draw of lots | Fixed fee Rs 15 lakh regular, Rs 5 lakh rural |
| Company-owned ("A" or "CC") | Bidding; a draw only if bids tie | Minimum bid Rs 30 lakh regular, Rs 10 lakh rural |
| Corpus Fund (SC/ST) | Draw of lots | None listed |

Amounts are as on 9 November 2026, include GST and are not refundable {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}}.

## What if only one applicant qualifies?

At a draw-of-lots location the lone eligible applicant is declared provisionally selected without a draw; at a bidding location the lone bid is opened {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}. Group 1 (land held) goes first; Group 2 (firm offer) only if Group 1 has nobody suitable. If a winner is found ineligible, a redraw is held or the next highest bidder is chosen {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H), cl.17}}.

## What fees and complaints are official?

The application fee for a regular outlet in the Open category is Rs 10,000, and for a rural one Rs 8,000, including GST {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.12}}. See [what it costs](@money) for every fee. A complaint against a selection needs a Rs 5,000 demand draft and must come within 30 days of the draw {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.18}}. No payment goes to an agent {{Oil companies fraud notice}}.
