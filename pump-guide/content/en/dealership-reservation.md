Which petrol pump locations are reserved, and for whom? This is the June 2023 brochure of the public sector oil companies, the newest brochure on Hindustan Petroleum's dealership page on 26 November 2026 {{HPCL dealership page, 26 Nov 2026}}.

## How are the shares split?

| Group | SC/ST | OBC | Open |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defence and government personnel (CC1) | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| Sportspersons, freedom fighters, agricultural credit societies (CC2) | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| Physically handicapped | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Rest of the category | 19.50 | 24 | 44.50 |
| Total | 22.50 | 27 | 50.50 |

Figures are per cent of locations, in all states except four {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.2}}. In Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Nagaland and Mizoram, 70, 80, 80 and 90 per cent go to ST, the rest to Open {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.2}}. Each advertisement names a location's category {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14}}, and invites candidates of that category {{HPCL dealership page, 26 Nov 2026}}. For the defence share, see [ex-servicemen](@dealership-ex-servicemen).

## What proof does each category need?

- SC/ST: a certificate from the competent authority, and a caste validity certificate where applicable {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vii)}}.
- OBC: certificate plus the creamy-layer undertaking {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vii)}}.
- Disabled: at least 40 per cent disability, certified by the notified medical authority {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vii)}}.

A provisionally selected candidate must send these within 10 days of being told, with 10 more if late {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4}}.

## Do fees differ by category?

The application fee for a regular outlet is Rs 3,000 for SC/ST locations, Rs 5,000 for OBC and Rs 10,000 for others; for a rural outlet, Rs 2,500, Rs 4,000 and Rs 8,000 {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.12}}. These are the brochure's figures, not a cost estimate.

## What is special at SC/ST locations?

An applicant may apply without offering land, and is asked to offer it later {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}. The Ministry of Cooperation says the Government has also allowed PACS into Combined Category 2 {{PIB, 4 Feb 2026}}. See [eligibility](@dealership-eligibility) and [rural outlets](@dealership-rural-outlet).

Dealerships come only through the oil companies' own advertisements, and payment to anyone for one is a fraud sign {{OMC fraud notice, Dealer Chayan}}. Ask the oil company about your own case.
