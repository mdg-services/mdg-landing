## The short answer

As on 27 November 2026, a tank vehicle that carries petrol or diesel in bulk by road needs its own PESO licence in Form XI {{PESO Rules FAQ}}. A "tank vehicle" is a vehicle with a tank of more than 1,000 litres {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 2}}. The licence covers one named vehicle.

## What the rules require

- Petrol (Class A) and diesel (Class B) in bulk by road: licence needed {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 75(1)}}.
- Class C: no licence for transport {{PESO Rules FAQ}}.
- The tank must be built to the Third Schedule and be of a type approved by the Chief Controller {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 63(1)}}, fabricated and mounted by an approved manufacturer {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 63(2)}}.

## Who issues it, and for how long

A Controller of Explosives grants Form XI {{Petroleum Rules 2002, First Schedule}}; in PESO's own list the licensing offices are the circle and sub-circle offices {{PESO licence guidelines}}. A licence stays in force until 31 December of the year it is granted for, up to three years {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 142(2)}}. It can be renewed for three calendar years if the rules were not broken {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 148(2)}}. A renewal application must reach the office at least 30 days before expiry {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 148(5)}}. We have not stated the fee: check the licence fee list on the PESO site.

## What the vehicle must carry

| Item | Source |
|---|---|
| The licence or its authenticated copy, shown to an inspector on demand | {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XI, condition 1}} |
| A portable 10 kg dry chemical powder extinguisher (or equivalent), plus a 1 kg dry chemical one in the cab | {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 72}} |
| The word FLAMMABLE and the product name on each side and the rear | {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Third Schedule, para 14}} |
| An electrical bonding cable at least 5 metres long | {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XI, condition 4}} |

With more than 5 kilolitres on board, the driver and one more person must attend the vehicle while it moves {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 73(2)}}.

## How it differs from the pump's licence

The pump's licence (Form XIV) is to store petroleum in tanks at the pump {{Petroleum Rules 2002, First Schedule}}. The tanker's Form XI is for the vehicle on the road. A tanker may be loaded or unloaded only at a place approved in writing by the Chief Controller {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 76(1)}}; see [tanker unloading](@safety-tanker-unloading). See also [petroleum classes](@licences-petroleum-classes).
