## How do you open a petrol pump?

This is the usual route to a Hindustan Petroleum, Indian Oil or Bharat Petroleum dealership. The company advertises a location, you apply online, and the company picks one eligible applicant, mainly by draw of lots or bidding {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14}}. Hindustan Petroleum lists the June 2023 brochure as the newest and the 2018 one as earlier {{HPCL dealership page}}. Facts are as on 9 November 2026.

## What are the steps, in order?

1. The company advertises the location in newspapers, with details on the portal {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(C)}}.
2. You register on the portal, fill the online form and pay the application fee there {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}.
3. A computerised draw of lots or bid opening picks one eligible applicant {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(H)}}.
4. The chosen applicant uploads documents and pays 10% of the security deposit within 10 days {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}.
5. A company committee checks the land, then your original papers {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(I), cl.15}}.
6. A letter of intent follows. You ready the land; the company prepares the approval applications {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16, cl.8(D)}}.
7. A letter of appointment and the dealership agreement come before the outlet starts {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.19}}.

More on the [selection process](@dealership-process).

## What should you decide before you apply?

**Land.** You need land you own, or hold on a lease of at least 19 years 11 months (or the period the advertisement states), or a firm offer for it {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}. See [who can apply](@dealership-eligibility) and [land and site](@site).

**Money.** Fees, deposits and what you build yourself depend on the site type the company chooses {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.3}}. See [what a petrol pump costs](@money).

**Time.** After the letter of intent you get 2 months (land held) or 4 months (firm offer only) to give the land papers {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}}.

## How do you spot a fake dealership offer?

The oil companies warn that unauthorised people use their names to offer dealerships {{Oil companies fraud notice}}.

| Sign | What the notice says |
|---|---|
| The approach | An unexpected email, letter, message or call |
| The demand | Payment as a "registration charge", or for land evaluation |
| The bank account | Not in the name of an oil company |
| The sender | Officials do not use Gmail or Yahoo, or WhatsApp |

Pay only through the official portal, and report anything suspicious to the local police station and the nearest oil company office {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. MDG Services does not sell, allot or arrange dealerships or licences.

## Which companies does this cover?

The portal and notice name Hindustan Petroleum, Indian Oil and Bharat Petroleum {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. Hindustan Petroleum's land advertisements are for land only, not dealerships {{HPCL land notices page}}, and company-run outlets use a separate service-provider selection {{HPCL dealership page}}. Nayara Energy, Jio-bp and Shell have their own routes, not covered here.
