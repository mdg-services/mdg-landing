## Is there any way in except the advertisement?

No. HPCL first identifies a location, then advertises it in leading newspapers, one English and one Hindi or vernacular {{HPCL dealership page}}. A dealership comes only through that advertisement. If someone offers you one for money, or asks you to pay for "land evaluation" or a "registration charge", that is a fraud sign {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. HPCL also says various fake websites invite online applications and that it has not authorised any website to deal on its behalf {{HPCL fake site alert}}. Nobody at MDG Services sells or arranges a dealership either.

## Step by step, as HPCL publishes it

| Step | Where it happens |
|---|---|
| See the advertisement for a location | Newspapers; HPCL's dealership page; the dealer selection portal {{HPCL dealership page}} |
| Read the eligibility, terms and procedure | In the advertisement itself {{HPCL dealership page}} |
| Register, fill the form, pay the fee | Online, on the dealer selection portal only {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}} |
| Draw of lots or bid opening | As the brochure sets for the site type; see [the selection process](@dealership-process) |

Eligibility differs by location and category, so [check the eligibility pages](@dealership-eligibility) and then the advertisement. Draw of lots is explained in [the selection process](@dealership-process).

## Fees, contact and cost

The application fee is in the advertisement and the brochure: Rs 10,000 regular and Rs 8,000 rural at locations not reserved for SC/ST or OBC, including GST, as on 14 November 2026 {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.12}}. This page gives no setup-cost figure. Pay application fees and deposits only on the portal {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. Report suspicious messages to the local police station {{Oil companies fraud notice}}.
