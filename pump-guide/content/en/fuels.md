## What is E20 petrol?

As on 9 November 2026, public sector oil companies say the normal petrol at their outlets across the country is E20, with 20% ethanol blended in, and that E0 or E10 petrol is not sold there {{Lok Sabha Q164, 30 Jul 2026}}. Its BIS standard is IS 17021 {{MDG 2024, annexure 7}}.

| | E10 | E20 |
|---|---|---|
| Ethanol in the petrol | 10% {{PIB Q&A 10 Jul 2026}} | 20% {{PIB 23 Jul 2026}} |
| BIS standard in MDG 2024 | IS 2796 {{MDG 2024, annexure 7}} | IS 17021 {{MDG 2024, annexure 7}} |
| Density at 15°C in MDG 2024 | 720-775 kg/m³ {{MDG 2024, clause 7.6}} | 720-775 kg/m³ {{MDG 2024, clause 7.6}} |

## Why does India blend ethanol in petrol?

The Government names energy security, less crude oil import, cleaner combustion and farmers' income {{PIB Q&A 10 Jul 2026}}. The National Policy on Biofuels 2018, amended in 2022, moved the 20% target to the ethanol supply year 2025-26 {{PIB Q&A 10 Jul 2026}}.

| Ethanol supply year {{PIB 23 Jul 2026}} | Average blending |
|---|---|
| 2022-23 | about 12% |
| 2023-24 | about 14.6% |
| 2024-25 | about 19.2% |
| 2025-26 (November to June) | 20% |

## Is E20 petrol safe for my vehicle?

These are the Government's statements.

- **Safety.** E20 is safe within the prescribed specification and the manufacturer's recommendations {{PIB 23 Jul 2026}}.
- **Mileage.** Some vehicles may lose 3-5% of fuel economy {{PIB Q&A 10 Jul 2026}}. Another reply says about 2-6% in certain E10-designed vehicles {{Lok Sabha Q164, 30 Jul 2026}}.

For your own vehicle, check the manual or ask its maker.

## What should a petrol pump dealer check?

MDG 2024, the public sector companies' rules for dealers, says:

- Test every tanker compartment for water with a water finding paste suitable for ethanol blended petrol {{MDG 2024, clause 1.2(vii)}}.
- After water is removed from a tank, check the ethanol content. If it is not in the desired range, stop sales and tell the company's controlling office {{MDG 2024, clause 4.3}}.
- Sell the right quality of ethanol blended petrol. Blending it correctly is the oil company's job {{MDG 2024, clause 3.1(f)}}.
- Density: the range is the same as for other petrol grades. See [E20 petrol density in MDG 2024](/guides/density/e20-petrol/).

No separate display of lower blends is considered necessary. Dispensers that sell E85 are prominently labelled {{Lok Sabha Q164, 30 Jul 2026}}.

## Will pumps sell more than E20?

The latest statement we found, dated 23 July 2026, says no decision has been taken to raise blending above 20%, and any change will follow studies and consultation {{PIB 23 Jul 2026}}. E85, with 85% ethanol, is only for flex fuel vehicles built for it {{PIB 23 Jul 2026}}. Check the Ministry's press releases for anything newer.
