## Where the advertisement is published

Hindustan Petroleum advertises in two leading newspapers, one English and one Hindi or local {{HPCL dealership page}}. Every location is also hosted on the dealer chayan portal {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(C)}}. If the papers differ, the English one prevails {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.24}}. Whether any is open: see the [dealer chayan portal](@dealership-chayan-portal).

## The columns, one by one

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Location | A stretch, such as "within 2 km of a chowk on SH 8"; the plot must fall inside it |
| Type of RO | Regular: on a highway or within town limits. Rural: outside both |
| Monthly sales | Expected petrol plus diesel, in KL |
| Category | SC, ST, OBC, Open and their sub-groups |
| Type of site | CC, DC or CFS |
| Minimum dimension | Frontage, depth and area of the plot |
| Finance | Working capital and building money |
| Mode of selection | Draw of lots or bidding |
| Fee or minimum bid, deposit | Rs lakh |

These are the brochure's columns {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.24}}; regular and rural are defined in {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.1}}. The sample location is a real row from Jamui, Bihar {{Dealer Chayan portal, 26 Nov 2026}}.

## Three real rows, read

From Bharat Petroleum's Bihar advertisement of June 2023, all in Araria district, all on a 35 m by 35 m plot (1,225 sq m) {{BPCL Bihar advertisement 2023}}:

| Row | Type | Sales (KL) | Category | Site | Finance (WC + build) | Selection | Fee or bid | Deposit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Rural | 115 | SC | CFS | 0 + 0 | Draw | 0 | 2 |
| 69 | Rural | 70 | Open | DC | 16 + 35 | Draw | 5 | 4 |
| 225 | Regular | 250 | Open | CC | 32 + 12 | Bidding | 30 | 5 |

Money is in Rs lakh. On a CFS site the company builds the outlet and lends working capital {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.9}}; on a DC site the dealer builds {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.3}}. See [company-owned or dealer-owned](@dealership-company-owned-vs-dealer-owned).

## The small print at the bottom

That advertisement gave a first term of 15 years, renewable every 5 years, and called itself a notice inviting applications, not an offer of a dealership {{BPCL Bihar advertisement 2023}}. Read any corrigendum too {{Dealer Chayan portal, 26 Nov 2026}}. Then fill the [application form](@dealership-application-form).

A dealership comes only through these advertisements, and the companies have appointed no agent {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. MDG Services does not sell or arrange dealerships.
