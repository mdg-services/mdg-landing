The land a petrol pump needs depends on where it is. National highways have one national table; for other roads, start with each oil company advertisement. Facts are as on 9 November 2026.

## How big must the plot be?

| National highway | Minimum frontage by depth |
|---|---|
| Rural stretch, plain or rolling terrain | 35 m by 35 m |
| Urban stretch (town of 20,000 or more), plain or rolling terrain | 30 m by 30 m |
| Hilly or mountainous terrain | 20 m by 20 m |

These are MoRTH norms of 26 June 2020 {{MoRTH norms 26 Jun 2020}}. MoRTH later allowed 20 m by 20 m for urban plots bought under the older norm {{MoRTH letter 28 Jan 2021}}. Check MoRTH's circulars page for anything newer.

On other roads, each advertisement lists the minimum frontage, depth and area for each location {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.24}}; state and local road offices may add their own rules, so ask the district office.

## What must a lease say?

- The land is with you on the application date, owned or on a lease of at least 19 years 11 months, or as advertised {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.
- With several owners, all must sign the lease deed {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.
- For company-owned sites, the lease must allow sub-lease {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.
- You develop the land to road level, with entry and exit lanes and a compound wall at least 1.5 m high {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi), note 1(d)}}.

## Which distance rules apply?

| Rule | Distance |
|---|---|
| New outlet from school, hospital of 10 beds or more, designated residential area | 50 m; with PESO's extra safety measures if 50 m is not possible, but never under 30 m; no high-tension line over the outlet {{CPCB guidelines 7 Jan 2020}} |
| Outlet from a water body | 50 m {{CPCB addendum 16 Aug 2021}} {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}} |
| National highway, from an intersection | 300 m rural, 100 m urban {{MoRTH norms 26 Jun 2020}} |
| National highway, from a toll plaza, barrier or level crossing | 1,000 m {{MoRTH norms 26 Jun 2020}} |
| National highway, between two fuel stations | 300 m; 1,000 m on a divided rural road {{MoRTH norms 26 Jun 2020}} |

"Designated" means under local law, so the local master plan matters. PESO says the CPCB guidelines are not mandatory where construction began on or before 7 January 2020 {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}.

## What gets a site rejected?

The selection brochure rejects land that is short of the advertised size, is notified for acquisition, has other land between it and the road, or has a line above 11 kV crossing it. On a national highway it must also meet NHAI norms {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi), cl.14(I)}}.

## Do not pay anyone to book land

The oil company makes no commitment to take land you offer {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi), note 1(e)}}. Nobody can book a site or dealership for you; they come only from the oil companies' own advertisements, and a payment to anyone for one is a fraud sign.
