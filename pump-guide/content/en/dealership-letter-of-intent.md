## When the LOI comes

After [field verification](@dealership-field-verification), you are told to collect the LOI from the oil company's office, with 10 days' time {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}. It is issued only if the information in your application is found correct and no selection complaint or court case is pending, and never before 30 days from the declaration of the result {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}}. You give a fresh affidavit at that time {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}}.

## What the LOI asks of you

| Deadline or duty | Rule |
|---|---|
| Land with all documents, developed as required | 2 months for Group 1, 4 months for Group 2 {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}} |
| Fixed fee (dealer-owned site) or total bid (company-owned site) | Within 15 days of receiving the NOC {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}} |
| Security deposit | Before the letter of appointment {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.20}} |
| Other conditions written in the LOI | Within the time the LOI states {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.19}} |

The land documents let the oil company prepare the layouts and the applications for statutory approvals {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}}. What happens next, up to opening day, is on [from LOI to commissioning](@dealership-appointment-and-commissioning).

## When an LOI is withdrawn

- The land is not provided, or the facilities are not developed, in time, or other LOI terms are not met. A show cause notice comes first {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}}.
- Progress is not in line with the timelines, unless there are justifiable reasons {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.19}}.
- The fixed fee or bid is not paid in time, or the holder surrenders or withdraws {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}}.

In each case the initial security deposit is forfeited {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.16}}. The location then goes to a redraw among the remaining applicants, or to the next highest bidder {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.17}}.

## "LOI for sale"

An LOI is issued to the selected candidate and is not a saleable right. At the LOI stage the guidelines allow one reconstitution (more only after a death or incapacity), the original holder or heirs must keep at least 51 per cent, and a resignation means the LOI is withdrawn {{OMC reconstitution guidelines 2018, B.1, B.2, B.6}}. The oil companies say no agency or person is authorised to ask for money at any stage {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. MDG Services does not sell or arrange dealerships. Changes after commissioning are on [dealership transfer](@dealership-transfer).
