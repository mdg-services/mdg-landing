## What is the dealer asked to provide?

As on 18 November 2026, this is from the guideline for public sector oil company dealers (MDG 2024). A private company's dealer should ask that company for its own rules.

| Facility | What MDG 2024 says |
|---|---|
| Air | Provided during the outlet's operating hours {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(ii)}}; calibrated air gauge {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.14(a)}} |
| Toilet | Clean toilet in the premises {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(vi)}} |
| Telephone | Available in the premises {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(vii)}} |
| First aid box | With valid medicines {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(ix)}} |
| Complaint book | Given on demand, with a notice that it exists {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6(iv)}} |

## Is any of it "free"?

The text does not say. The only places it says free of cost are the sealing wires, pliers and sample labels that oil companies supply {{MDG 2024, clauses 2.6-2.8}}. So "free air" and "free toilet" are not words we can quote from it. If a pump asks for money, ask the oil company.

## What about drinking water?

The customer services clause lists no drinking water duty {{MDG 2024, clause 1.6}}. The toilet protocol requires water to be available {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.14(b)}}. Oil companies can issue circulars on service standards, so ask yours whether one covers drinking water.

## The toilet rules

The dealer is to check the toilet daily for cleanliness, lighting, flush, water, a working latch and signage {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.14(b)}}. It must be accessible to all customers and transit travellers at all times, subject to safety protocols {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.14(b)}}. More in [the toilet rules](/guides/customer-service/toilet-rules/).

## If a facility is missing

A missing facility is a minor irregularity {{MDG 2024, clause 8.4}}. Use the complaint book, or the oil company contact shown at the outlet. See [short delivery complaints](@customers-short-delivery) for where else to complain.
