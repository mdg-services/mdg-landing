Two questions come up at the pump: will I pay more, and what if the payment fails? Below is what the government, NPCI and the RBI have published, as on 19 November 2026.

## Does the customer pay extra for UPI?

The government says no: consumers paying by UPI will not face transaction charges {{PIB, 8 Aug 2026}}, and UPI apps may not add a platform fee {{NPCI MDR FAQ, 15 Oct 2026}}. The change under discussion is a charge on the merchant, the merchant discount rate (MDR). NPCI's FAQ sets out the planned amounts for fuel:

| Fuel payment by UPI | Merchant pays (planned) |
|---|---|
| Rs 2,000 or less | Nothing {{NPCI MDR FAQ, 15 Oct 2026}} |
| Above Rs 2,000 | Flat Rs 5 per payment {{NPCI MDR FAQ, 15 Oct 2026}} |

The FAQ says it starts immediately after Diwali, on a date to be notified separately {{NPCI MDR FAQ, 15 Oct 2026}}; we found no notified date as on 19 November 2026. Merchants cannot pass it on to customers {{NPCI MDR FAQ, 15 Oct 2026}}.

## What about card payments?

In January 2017 the petroleum ministry said neither customers nor dealers would bear an additional levy on digital transactions at petrol stations {{PIB, 9 Jan 2017}}. We found no newer ministry rule; if a pump asks for a card fee, ask the oil company or your bank.

## What if the payment fails?

If your account is debited but the pump shows no confirmation:

1. Keep the SMS or app screen showing the reference.
2. Wait for the reversal: the limit is T + 5 days (T is the payment day) for UPI payments to a merchant and card payments at a terminal {{RBI circular, 20 Sep 2019}}.
3. If it is late, compensation is Rs 100 for each day beyond that, credited to you without a claim {{RBI circular, 20 Sep 2019}}.
4. If your bank does not put it right, complain to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman {{RBI circular, 20 Sep 2019}}.

If the payment went through but the fuel was short or bad, see [short delivery](@customers-short-delivery) and [refunds](@customers-refund). For the bill, see [petrol bill and GST](@customers-bill-gst).
