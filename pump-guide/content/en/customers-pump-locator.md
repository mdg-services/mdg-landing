## The short answer

There is no single national locator. Each oil company publishes its own, and each covers only its own pumps. As on 22 November 2026, these are what the companies' own pages describe.

## What each company offers

| Company | What its own page says |
|---|---|
| BPCL | A Fuel Station Locator page to locate the nearest fuel station or petrol pump on your route {{BPCL locator page}}; the BPCL SmartDrive app is listed on the same site {{BPCL locator page}} |
| Jio-bp | A fuel station locator to find an open fuel pump or a 24-hour petrol pump {{Jio-bp site}} |
| Nayara Energy | A locator with Search Nearby, an advanced search by state, city and locality, and Petrol Pump on Route {{Nayara locator}}; its helpline number is shown as 1800 1200 330 {{Nayara locator}} |
| HPCL | Its home page lists a Retail Outlet (Petrol Pump) Locator under Quick Access {{HPCL home page}} |

This page does not describe Indian Oil or Shell. For those pumps, use the companies' own websites.

## Highway and 24-hour pumps

HPCL says it has developed more than 120 Focus Outlets on major national highways, branded Club HP, and lists 24 X 7 operations, secured parking and driver amenities among their facilities, "as per the space available" {{HPCL highway page}}. These are one company's outlets; other companies may have their own. Opening hours are set by each pump and its company. See [opening hours of a pump](@running-opening-hours) and [how a highway pump gets its access](@site-highway-access).

## How many pumps are there?

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas said on 10 July 2026 that India operates over one lakh retail outlets {{PIB 10 Jul 2026}}. A split by company or by highway is not given there, so this page gives none.

## Before you set out

In practice, a locator listing can be out of date, so call the pump if you need it open or need a particular fuel. Paying: see [paying at a pump](@customers-digital-payments).
