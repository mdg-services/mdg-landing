## How many pumps does each company have?

PPAC collects these counts from the oil companies. The table is as on 1 November 2026, provisional, and the source says reported data may change {{PPAC Snapshot Nov 2026, table 14}}.

| Company | Retail outlets | Of which rural |
|---|---|---|
| Indian Oil | 43,242 | 14,054 |
| Bharat Petroleum | 25,589 | 6,836 |
| Hindustan Petroleum | 25,265 | 6,443 |
| Nayara Energy (shown as NEL) | 7,109 | 2,155 |
| Reliance BP Mobility group | 2,305 | 130 |
| Shell | 338 | 75 |
| MRPL and others | 292 | 81 |
| All India | 1,04,140 | 29,774 |

The three public sector companies together have 94,096 outlets by our addition of their three figures {{PPAC Snapshot Nov 2026, table 14}}. The Reliance BP Mobility row is PPAC's combined column for RIL, RBML and RSIL {{PPAC Snapshot Nov 2026, table 14}}. Its pumps carry the Jio-bp name; see [Jio-bp dealerships](@dealership-jio-bp). "Others" in PPAC's note includes MRPL, HMEL, AGCL, CPCL, IMC and IPPL outlets {{PPAC Snapshot Nov 2026, table 14}}.

## Who is allowed to sell petrol and diesel?

The central rules name three existing public sector companies: IOCL, BPCL and HPCL {{MoPNG amendment 10 Aug 2026, para 9.2}}. Any new entrant, private or foreign, needs the Central Government's authorisation to market petrol (motor spirit) and diesel {{MoPNG resolution 8 Nov 2019}}. As amended on 10 August 2026, an applicant pays a non-refundable fee of Rs 25 lakh and must set up at least 100 outlets, with at least 5% in notified remote areas within five years of authorisation {{MoPNG amendment 10 Aug 2026, para 6.2}}. The 2019 text also required a net worth of at least Rs 250 crore {{MoPNG resolution 8 Nov 2019, para 6.1.1}}; the amendment keeps a net worth condition, so read the current text.

## What this means if you want a pump

A pump with one of these brands is run by a dealer chosen by that company, or by the company itself. Read [company-owned or dealer-owned](@dealership-company-owned-vs-dealer-owned) first. The public sector companies pick dealers by [one common process](@dealership-process); private companies such as [Nayara Energy](@dealership-nayara) run their own. A dealership comes only through the company's own advertisement, and paying anyone for one is a fraud sign {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. MDG Services does not sell or arrange dealerships.
