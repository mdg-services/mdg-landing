## What makes premium petrol different?

In practice, premium petrol is petrol with something extra: an additive package and often a higher octane number. HPCL explains octane number as how far petrol can be compressed before it ignites by itself, which causes knocking {{HPCL poWer95 product sheet}}. As on 17 November 2026, the Ministry of Petroleum says premium fuels are niche products, sold in limited quantities at a significant price premium, because specialised performance-enhancing additives are blended in {{PIB Q&A 10 Jul 2026}}. They are not separate nationwide base fuel streams {{PIB Q&A 10 Jul 2026}}.

## What do the rules say?

BIS sets the standard for petrol, IS 2796:2017 Motor Gasoline {{BIS petroleum standards presentation}}. Its table of requirements shows RON 91 and 95 for the Bharat Stage VI petrol of 2020, and density of 720 to 775 kg per cubic metre at 15 degrees C {{BIS petroleum standards presentation}}.

## What do the oil companies say about their premium petrol?

These are the companies' own statements, not tested by us.

| Company and brand | What it says |
|---|---|
| HPCL, poWer 95 | RON 95 minimum, regular petrol 91; keeps to IS 2796:2017 for the BS VI grade {{HPCL poWer95 product sheet}} |
| BPCL, Speed | 95+ octane petrol with multi-functional additives {{BPCL Speed Fuels page}} |

The pages also claim better mileage or longer engine life. BPCL writes that Speed "provides a better mileage" and "enhances engine life" {{BPCL Speed Fuels page}}. HPCL lists "reduces fuel consumption" for poWer 95 {{HPCL poWer95 product sheet}}. Treat these as the companies' claims.
