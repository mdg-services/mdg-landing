A petrol pump runs on a small team with one set of daily checks. The dealer answers for the pump, and each person on the shift has a part. This page uses the public sector oil companies' Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024; a private company's dealer should ask for that company's own rules. All as on 9 November 2026.

## Who does what at a petrol pump?

| Role | Duties |
|---|---|
| Dealer or owner | Answers for correct quality, quantity and price {{MDG 2024, 1.6}}; pays at least the minimum wages {{MDG 2024, 1.5}}; keeps inspection reports and invoices at the pump {{MDG 2024, 1.5}} |
| Manager | In practice: runs the shift, checks the day's records, deals with complaints |
| Nozzle attendant | Wears a uniform and badge {{MDG 2024, 5.1.17}}; shows zero before each delivery {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}}; engine off, no smoking by anyone in or with the vehicle {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 15}} |
| Cashier | In practice: takes payment, issues bills, enters the day's sales |
| Tanker receiver | Follows the unloading steps: earthing, seals, density, dips {{MDG 2024, 1.2}} |

The oil companies should train dealers and staff in safe handling and courteous service {{MDG 2024, 3.3}}.

## What does a day look like?

1. Check the Legal Metrology seals and certificate of each dispenser {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}}.
2. Test every nozzle with the 5-litre measure and note the litres used in the stock register {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}}.
3. Draw a sample from the nozzle and record the morning density at 15 degrees C {{MDG 2024, 1.4.1}}.
4. Record the pump meter readings and the product and water dips {{MDG 2024, 1.3}}, and display the opening stock and selling prices {{MDG 2024, 1.3}}.
5. When a tanker arrives, stop sales from the receiving tank, earth the tanker, keep a dry-powder extinguisher near it, and check density against the invoice within 3.0 kg per cubic metre {{MDG 2024, 1.2}}. See [receiving a tanker](/guides/receiving-a-tanker/).
6. Keep the complaint book available and the working hours displayed {{MDG 2024, 1.6}}.

## Which records must be kept?

- The daily stock register, with the water dip record {{MDG 2024, 1.3 and 1.4}}: [daily stock register](/guides/stock-variation/daily-stock-register/).
- The density register {{MDG 2024, 1.2}}: [density register](/guides/density/density-register/).
- The inspection reports, test reports and invoices {{MDG 2024, 1.5}}.
- The "gas-free" certificate after a tank repair, for at least three months {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 8}}.

## What about pay?

Minimum wages are fixed by the appropriate Government, by skill, area and arduousness; the Centre sets a floor that states must not go below {{PIB note on Code on Wages, 23 Nov 2025}}. See the MDG guide on [staff wages and uniform](/guides/customer-service/staff-wages-and-uniform/), and your state labour department's notification. For tax on a pump's sales see [GST](@running-gst).
