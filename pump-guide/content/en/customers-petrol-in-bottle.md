## The short answer

As on 21 November 2026, the pump's licence (Form XIV under the Petroleum Rules 2002) says petrol shall not be filled from the tank or the pump into a container other than one securely clamped or fitted to a motor vehicle {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 16}}. So under its licence a pump should not fill your bottle just because you ask. The attendant's side of this is in [what a nozzle attendant must do](@running-attendant-duties).

## The exceptions

The same condition lists cases where the ban does not apply. These three concern petrol {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 16}}; a fourth covers petroleum Class B, not petrol {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 16(iv)}}.

| Case | Source |
|---|---|
| Clearing a tank | {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 16(i)}} |
| Testing the pump's delivery with a standard measure | {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 16(ii)}} |
| An approved container of up to 25 litres, when filling is absolutely necessary for a vehicle that has run dry and cannot be brought to the pump | {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV, condition 16(iii)}} |

The last one is the case that matters to a customer. It says the container must be approved, and that the filling must be absolutely necessary.

## What counts as an approved container?

Containers over one litre for this class of fuel must be of a type approved by the Chief Controller {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 4(1)}}. They are made of tinned, galvanized or rust-proofed sheet iron or steel {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 5(1)}}, have a screw cap {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 5(3)}} and carry a warning such as Petrol or Motor Spirit {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 5(7)}}. The rule allows a glass bottle of up to 2.5 litres of an approved type as a container for laboratory chemicals of this class {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 5(1)}}. It does not mention a plastic bottle.

## Local orders

This page covers the Petroleum Rules only. A local authority may have its own order about carrying fuel. We have not checked any, so we say nothing about any district. Ask your local police or district office, or an advocate for a legal question.

## What to do if your vehicle runs dry

Call a mechanic or a friend, or push or tow the vehicle to the pump. In practice, a pump may still refuse a can, because the pump and its oil company decide how to apply the condition.
