PESO is the Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation. Its Form XIV licence lets a pump store petrol and diesel in underground tanks and fuel vehicles {{Petroleum Rules 2002, Form XIV}}. Steps are as on 9 November 2026.

## What are the steps, in order?

1. **Letter of intent** from the oil company; PESO asks for a copy with the plan application {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}.
2. **Prior approval of the plan.** Apply online in Form IX with the drawing {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}; PESO returns a signed copy if satisfied {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 131}}.
3. **District Authority's NOC.** It should decide within three months {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 144(5)}}; PESO accepts it for three years {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, rule 144}}.
4. **Build and test.** A competent person or a TPIA (a recognised third-party inspection agency) water-tests the tanks before use {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, rule 126}} and certifies safety before fuel is first stored {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, rule 130}}.
5. **Apply for the grant** with the NOC, a drawing like the approved one, and a declaration with colour photographs that the pump is built as approved {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}, plus the tank test certificate {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 126}}. Mark the licence number on the premises {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 133}}.

## Which documents and distances matter?

The prior-approval list asks for land papers showing legal possession, a layout and site plan, and undertakings {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. The site plan must show everything protected within 100 metres {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 131}}. One undertaking says no school, hospital of 10 beds or more, residential area under local law or water body lies within 50 metres {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}.

Safety distances sit in the licence conditions, for example a vent pipe 4 metres from adjoining land {{Form XIV, condition 5}}.

## How long does it last, and what does it cost?

Renewal is for three calendar years if nothing was breached {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 148}}. The application must reach PESO 30 days before expiry, and a late one costs twice the fee {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 148}}. None is renewed if it comes over a year after expiry {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2018, rule 148(8)}}.

Fee: Rs 5,000 for the first 50 kilolitres of each class, plus Rs 50 for each extra kilolitre or part, up to Rs 50,000 a year {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2018, First Schedule}}. Plan approval costs Rs 2,000 {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2024, First Schedule}}. Example, made up: 60 kilolitres of diesel is Rs 5,000 plus 10 times Rs 50, or Rs 5,500 a year. A concession for women entrepreneurs and MSMEs was announced on 4 July 2024 {{PIB 4 Jul 2024}}; check the fee shown when you apply online.

## What if the layout changes?

PESO must approve the changed drawing in writing first; amend the licence after the work {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 146}}.
