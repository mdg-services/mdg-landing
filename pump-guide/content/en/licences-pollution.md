Whether a petrol pump needs consent from the State Pollution Control Board depends on how your state treats a pump that only dispenses fuel. Every new pump must meet CPCB's siting and spill guidelines. Facts are as on 9 November 2026.

## What do CPCB and the states say about petrol pumps?

| Paper | What it says |
|---|---|
| CPCB classification, 2016 | Fuel outlets that only dispense, earlier Green, are dropped: minor air pollution from filling; may be exempted from consent management {{CPCB classification 2016}} |
| CPCB report, January 2025 | No line for them. Automobile service stations and workshops are Orange {{CPCB classification 2025}} |
| Himachal Pradesh board, 24 February 2021 | Fuel dispensing without vehicle servicing is White, exempt from consent to establish and to operate {{HPSPCB report to NGT 2021}} |

Other states may say something different, or nothing in writing. If your pump also services vehicles, ask the board about that part.

## What must a new pump meet even if consent is exempt?

CPCB issued guidelines for new petrol pumps on 7 January 2020, after a National Green Tribunal order {{CPCB guidelines 7 Jan 2020}}.

| Subject | What they say {{CPCB guidelines 7 Jan 2020}} |
|---|---|
| Distance | 50 metres from schools, hospitals of 10 beds or more and designated residential areas; if 50 metres is not possible, PESO's extra safety measures apply, and never under 30 metres; no high-tension line over the outlet |
| Water bodies (addendum, 16 August 2021) | No outlet within 50 metres of a lake, pond, stream, river, wetland, canal or creek on revenue records; double-walled tanks or concrete protection walls from 50 to 100 metres {{CPCB addendum 16 Aug 2021}} |
| Spills | Auto cut-off nozzles, breakaway hose couplings, spill containment at the fill point |
| Tanks | Leak test of tanks and pipelines every 7 years; a leak that contaminates soil or groundwater is reported within 24 hours |
| Vapour | Vapour recovery for new outlets with sale potential of 300 kilolitres of petrol a month in cities above 1 lakh people; see [vapour recovery](@equipment-vrs) |

PESO says the guidelines are not mandatory for outlets whose construction began on or before 7 January 2020 {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}.

## How do you find out for your pump?

Ask your State Pollution Control Board in writing whether a pump that only dispenses needs consent, and keep the reply with your [licence papers](@licences-peso).
