This page reports what the public sector oil companies' dealer guideline (MDG 2024) says, as on 23 November 2026. It binds those companies' outlets only; a private company's dealer should read that company's own terms. It is not legal advice: for a dispute, ask an advocate.

## Is there a place to complain about the oil company?

In MDG 2024 we found no arbitration clause and no general forum for a dealer's complaints. What the guideline does lay down is a fair-hearing route when the company acts against a dealer. Any arbitration or dispute clause would sit in your own dealership agreement; see [the dealer agreement and termination](@dealership-agreement-termination).

## The steps when the company takes action

| Step | What the guideline says |
|---|---|
| Show cause notice | Issued within 30 days of the inspection (or of test results, if samples were drawn), with all reports it relies on {{MDG 2024, clause 8.5.6}} |
| Your reply | 10 days from issue of the notice {{MDG 2024, clause 8.5.7}} |
| Speaking order | The officer reviews the charges and your reply and passes an order preferably within 45 days, giving reasons {{MDG 2024, clause 8.5.8}} |
| Personal hearing | Before a termination for critical irregularities; if you miss the date one more chance is given, then the case may go ahead without you {{MDG 2024, clause 8.6}} |
| Appeal | Within 30 days of receiving a termination order, filed through the company's divisional, territory or regional office; decided preferably within 90 days {{MDG 2024, clause 8.9}} |

Irregularities must be established before any action is taken {{MDG 2024, clause 8.5.2}}. For a case the guideline does not cover, the company acts after an enquiry, following natural justice {{MDG 2024, clause 8.5.4}}. The penalties themselves are in [marketing discipline](@running-marketing-discipline).

## Who decides the appeal?

For most terminations, the Executive Director (Retail) at the head office or another officer of that level the company names; where the state office's approving authority was of Executive Director rank, a committee of two Executive Directors. For SC/ST dealerships, a committee of two Directors other than the Director (Marketing) {{MDG 2024, clause 8.9}}.

## What customers can do at your pump

The guideline asks the dealer to keep a suggestion or complaint book, ready for customers on demand {{MDG 2024, 1.6}}, and to display the contact details of the oil company people to call for complaints {{MDG 2024, 1.6}}. See [inspections](@running-inspections) for what the company checks.
