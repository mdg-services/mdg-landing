A pump's layout is a safety drawing first. Under the June 2023 guideline the oil company's office prepares the layouts for statutory approvals {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8(D)}}; PESO approves the plan for your site. Facts are as on 14 November 2026. For plot size and distances, see [land for a petrol pump](@site).

## What are the parts of the site?

In practice a pump has an approach and exit, a forecourt with canopy, dispenser islands, underground tanks, a sales room, an air and water point, toilets and a clear path for the tanker. The rules name some of these:

| Element | Rule or condition behind it |
|---|---|
| Underground tanks, in the positions on the approved plan | {{Form XIV, condition 1}} |
| Tank filling and dipping pipes, vent pipes, valves, pumps | Shown on the plan {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 131}} |
| Vent pipe: at least 4 m high and 4 m from adjoining land and from the sales room opening | {{Form XIV, condition 5}} |
| Sales room or kiosk | Listed in the licence {{Form XIV, description of premises}} |
| Licence number marked on premises | {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 133}} |

## Who approves the drawing, and when?

Before building, you apply with specifications and plans drawn to scale; PESO returns a signed copy if satisfied {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 131}}. The application is online in Form IX {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. See [the PESO licence](@licences-peso) for the whole sequence.

## What must the site plan show?

- The surroundings and all protected works within 100 metres of the edge of the facilities to be licensed {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 131}}.
- The position, capacity and elevation of tanks, filling and discharge points, vent pipes and fire-fighting facilities {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 131}}.
- Dimensions in metric only, signed by the applicant {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}.

The application also carries an undertaking that no school, hospital of 10 beds or more, residential area under local law or water body lies within 50 m {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}. PESO's FAQs name OISD-118 as the OISD practice for layout of oil and gas installations {{PESO FAQs on Petroleum Rules}}; whether a standard applies to a pump, ask the oil company. Tank design is in [tanks and equipment](@equipment-tanks).

## What if the built pump differs from the drawing?

No alteration may be carried out until the licensing authority approves the new drawing in writing; the licence is amended afterwards {{Petroleum Rules 2002, rule 146}}. A licence is liable to be cancelled if the premises do not conform to the approved plan {{Form XIV, conditions of licence}}.

## One drawing counts

Nothing on this page, and no sample drawing, replaces the drawing PESO approves for your site.
