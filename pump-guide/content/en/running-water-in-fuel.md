## When is water checked?

As on 24 November 2026, these duties are from the guideline for public sector oil company dealers (MDG 2024). A private company's dealer should ask that company for its own rules.

1. **Before unloading.** The dealer checks the product dip in every tank lorry compartment, and also checks each compartment for water with water finding paste. Sales from the receiving tank stop until unloading is done {{MDG 2024, clause 1.2(vii)}}.
2. **Every day.** A water dip of each tank is taken in the morning and recorded {{MDG 2024, clause 1.3}}; it forms part of the daily stock register {{MDG 2024, clause 1.4.1(b)}}.
3. **On each company visit.** The company's maintenance representative dips the underground tanks for water on every visit {{MDG 2024, clause 4.4}}.

## What if water is found?

If water is found in a tank, the dealer must immediately report it to the controlling company office {{MDG 2024, clause 1.4.1(b)}}, and steps are taken to remove it {{MDG 2024, clause 4.4}}.

If water is found in a tank lorry compartment, or the dip is short, the shortfall (including the quantity due to removing water) is made up by filling to the correct level through the dispensing pump. The quantity is written on the back of all invoice copies {{MDG 2024, clause 1.2(xi)}}.

For ethanol blended petrol, the ethanol content is checked after the water is removed and before the product is released. If it is not in the desired range, sales stop and the controlling office is told {{MDG 2024, clause 4.3(c)}}.

## What can a customer do?

At a public sector pump, filter paper for petrol is kept and given to customers on demand {{MDG 2024, clause 1.4.1(a)}}. In practice, if an engine runs badly right after filling, keep the receipt and note the date, pump and nozzle. Then follow [the quality complaint page](@customers-quality-complaint): complaint book, oil company number, helpline. For the daily density test see [the density check page](@running-density-check). We cannot say what any complaint will bring.
