## How is a CNG dealer paid?

As on 27 November 2026, the city gas company in each area sets the dealer's pay in its own notice and agreement. In the notices we opened it is a commission on each kg of CNG sold {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 5}}, {{BGCL DODO EOI Oct 2025, cl 2.13}}. BPCL calls it dealer service charges, under its own policy {{BPCL CNG DODO brochure, cl 3.3(iii)}}. Bengal Gas says the rate is told to the dealer before the agreement is signed {{BGCL DODO EOI Oct 2025, cl 2.16}}.

The company also fixes the retail price of CNG {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 4}}.

## What the notices state

| Company, notice | How the dealer is paid | Rate stated |
|---|---|---|
| Vadodara Gas, Feb 2026 | Model B: commission per kg. Model A: smaller commission per kg plus a fixed monthly charge {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 5}} | B: Rs 4.15 a kg. A: Rs 2.70 a kg plus Rs 1,74,000 a month. Excluding taxes, Vadodara-Chhota Udepur area {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 15}} |
| Bengal Gas, Oct 2025 | Commission per kg {{BGCL DODO EOI Oct 2025, cl 2.13}} | None in the notice |
| BPCL | Dealer service charges per kg {{BPCL CNG DODO brochure, cl 3.3(iii)}} | None in the brochure |
| Aavantika Gas, 2023 | Commission per kg, higher in the full dealer-built model {{Aavantika Gas EOI Sep 2023}} | None in the notice |

Vadodara's figures are for one area on one date, not the rate at another company. Check your area's current notice.

## What the commission is not

It is not profit. In Vadodara's notice the dealer arranges the land, electricals, canopy and fire equipment and runs the station {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 4}}. BPCL's brochure leaves the viability of the station to the applicant {{BPCL CNG DODO brochure, cl 3.3(vi)}}.

For petrol and diesel, see [dealer commission](@money-commission).

## What we could not confirm

We found no primary source on whether any government order sets CNG dealer commission; the notices we opened show the company setting it. Ask the company or the PNGRB.

For who awards a dealership and the land asked, see [CNG pump dealership](@dealership-cng-pump); for the licence, [CNG station licence](@licences-cng-station).
