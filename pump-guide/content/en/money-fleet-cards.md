## The short answer

A fleet card lets a transport business pay for fuel from a recharged account instead of cash. Each oil company runs its own. As on 22 November 2026, this page describes only what two companies publish on their own pages.

## HPCL DriveTrack Plus

HPCL describes DriveTrack Plus as a prepaid fleet card that gives control, convenience, security and rewards, usable for fuel and lubricants at all HPCL petrol pumps {{HPCL DriveTrack Plus page}}. The account is recharged by any digital mode of payment {{HPCL DriveTrack Plus page}}. The page also lists a transport management tool, roadside assistance and contactless fuelling at select pumps {{HPCL DriveTrack Plus page}}. Truck makers have co-branded cards on the same platform {{HPCL DriveTrack Plus page}}.

On joining, HPCL says enrolment was once a physical form, and that in January 2021 it introduced an online platform for enrolment, with real-time customer and vehicle validation {{HPCL Retail systemic page}}.

## BPCL SmartFleet

BPCL describes SmartFleet as a fuel management program for fleet owners {{BPCL SmartFleet page}}. Its app lets a fleet owner track vehicles and set ad hoc limits on the card balance {{BPCL SmartFleet page}}. A customer can self-enrol on the website or app, and start transactions in 15 minutes through an OTP based virtual card {{BPCL SmartFleet page}}. The account is recharged by credit or debit card, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI or net banking {{BPCL SmartFleet page}}.

## Indian Oil

This page does not cover Indian Oil's XTRAPOWER card; read about it on Indian Oil's own website.

## What this means for a pump

The companies' pages we read do not set out how a dealer accepts these cards, what the pump is paid, or when. HPCL lists the DriveTrack Plus program among the facilities of its highway Focus Outlets {{HPCL highway page}}. For anything more, ask your oil company's sales officer; the terms with the dealer come from the company. For other ways customers pay, see [digital payments at a pump](@customers-digital-payments).

We give no fee, limit or reward figures here; they can change, so read them on the company's page when you apply.
