A dispenser's meter is a legal measuring instrument. A Legal Metrology Officer verifies and stamps it, and it is re-checked every 24 months. The dealer tests it daily with a 5-litre measure. All as on 9 November 2026.

## Who verifies a dispenser, and how often?

Every weight or measure used in a sale must be verified and stamped by the Legal Metrology Officer of the State where it is used, and re-verified at set intervals {{LM (General) Rules 2011, rule 27}}. Since 18 December 2025 the interval for petrol and diesel dispensers is 24 months {{G.S.R. 905(E), 18 Dec 2025}}.

Government Approved Test Centres can verify dispensers too, at Rs 5,000 for each nozzle {{G.S.R. 346(E), 8 May 2026}}.

## What error is allowed, and what is the 5-litre test?

| Point | What the source says |
|---|---|
| Table error, two litres or more | 0.5 per cent, plus or minus {{LM (General) Rules 2011, Eighth Schedule Part IV}} |
| On a 5-litre measure | 25 millilitres (0.5 per cent of 5 litres, our arithmetic) |
| Minimum error figure | The limit is the greater of the table figure and a minimum figure, so it can be above 25 millilitres {{LM (General) Rules 2011, Eighth Schedule Part IV}} |
| Public sector oil companies' "within limit" band | 10 to 25 millilitres per 5 litres is recorded; recalibrate and re-stamp within 15 days {{MDG 2024, 5.1.2}} |
| The test | Each nozzle, daily, before sales, with a verified 5-litre measure kept only for pump testing {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}} |

Steps are in the MDG guide on [the 5-litre test](/guides/quantity/5-litre-test/).

## What if the delivery is short or a seal is broken?

1. Stop sales through that dispenser or nozzle if it delivers short beyond the limit with its seals intact {{MDG 2024, 5.1.2}}.
2. Tell the Legal Metrology office and the oil company; do not run it until it is re-stamped {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}}.
3. Check the seals daily and suspend a unit whose seal is tampered {{MDG 2024, 1.4.2}}. See [seals](/guides/quantity/wm-and-totaliser-seals/).

## What are the penalties under the Act?

The Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2026, in force for the Legal Metrology Act from 1 May 2026, changed penalty sections of the Legal Metrology Act: in several of them a first offence now gets an improvement notice and a second offence a penalty {{Jan Vishwas Act 2026, Schedule entry 66}} {{S.O. 2103(E), 27 Apr 2026}}. Ask the Legal Metrology office or an advocate which section applies.
