## Can a petrol pump add CNG or an EV charger?

In short: CNG needs a PESO licence; EV charging is de-licensed, but PESO approval is listed for the connection at a petrol pump. For CNG, a pump that already holds a Form XIV licence needs no fresh District Magistrate NOC, but added land does {{PESO GCR FAQ}}. Whether your oil company allows either is for the company to say. As on 9 November 2026.

| | CNG dispenser | EV charger |
|---|---|---|
| Rule | Gas Cylinders Rules 2016, Form G {{PESO GCR SOP}} | Ministry of Power guidelines, 17 September 2024 {{MoP EV guidelines 2024}} |
| Licence | PESO circle office grants it {{PESO GCR SOP}} | None; de-licensed {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 5(1)}} |
| Other papers | Layout plan, OISD 179 check, fire cover, local clearances {{PESO GCR SOP}} | Electricity connection; PESO approval listed for petrol pumps {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, annexure III(B)}} |

## How does a pump add CNG?

1. Ask your oil company first. Its terms for CNG at a dealer's pump are not in the official sources we opened.
2. Apply to PESO for prior approval. It needs a scaled plan showing the area within 100 metres, proof of the land or the Form XIV licence details, and the fee {{PESO GCR SOP}}.
3. Build as approved, then apply for the licence with the as-built drawing, a fire cover check and clearances from the pollution board, fire department and panchayat {{PESO GCR SOP}}.
4. On 8 July 2026 PESO announced an online module in which one application can cover the Petroleum Rules 2002, Gas Cylinders Rules 2016 and SMPV(U) Rules 2016 for a fuel station {{PESO circular 8 Jul 2026}}.

Safety distances are in Tables IA, IB and II of the Gas Cylinders Rules {{PESO GCR FAQ}}. Read them there; we give no figures.

## How does a pump add EV charging?

The Ministry of Power guidelines cover chargers at petrol pumps {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 2}}. The distribution company must give a new connection within 3 days in a metropolitan area, 7 days in other municipal areas and 15 days in rural areas (30 days where hilly), or 90 days if mains must be extended {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 5(2)}}. The Government may prioritise oil company outlets for public charging {{MoP EV guidelines 2024, para 11(4)}}.
