## The short answer

As on 27 November 2026, subsidised kerosene reaches homes through the public distribution system (PDS), not through ordinary petrol pump sales {{MoPNG PDS SKO Allocation Policy, 13 Jun 2025}}.

## How PDS kerosene is distributed

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas allocates PDS kerosene to states and UTs every quarter, for cooking and lighting only {{MoPNG PDS SKO Allocation Policy, 13 Jun 2025}}. The state governments carry out the distribution through their PDS network and decide the scale and criteria {{MoPNG PDS SKO Allocation Policy, 13 Jun 2025}}. Until 2024-25 there was a separate non-subsidised allocation for needs such as fisheries, melas and calamities; from 2025-26 there is one allocation, which states may sub-allocate for such needs {{MoPNG PDS SKO Allocation Policy, 13 Jun 2025}}.

## Allocation cuts and kerosene-free states

The Ministry says allocation fell substantially over 15 years, and 13 states and UTs had declared themselves PDS kerosene free {{MoPNG PDS SKO Allocation Policy, 13 Jun 2025}}. PIB gave the same count of 13 states for 2023-24 {{PIB release 17 Mar 2025}}. The 2026 notice lists 21 states and UTs as PDS kerosene free {{Gazette S.O. 1630(E), 29 Mar 2026}}.

## The 2026 exception for pumps

On 12 March 2026 the Minister told Parliament that kerosene was being made available through retail outlets and PDS channels {{PIB statement 12 Mar 2026}}. The Gazette notice of 29 March 2026 then permitted, in the 21 kerosene-free states and UTs, at most two oil company service stations per district, designated by the state and preferably company owned, to store up to 5,000 litres of PDS kerosene, for households' cooking and lighting only {{Gazette S.O. 1630(E), 29 Mar 2026}}. It ran for 60 days or until further orders {{Gazette S.O. 1630(E), 29 Mar 2026}}, and was extended to 30 June 2026 or further order, whichever is earlier {{Gazette S.O. 2854(E), 4 Jun 2026}}. That date has passed and we found no later extension, so check with your oil company or district office.

## Price and GST

Since 1 March 2020 PDS kerosene has been sold at a nil under-recovery price, and the state sets the consumer price {{MoPNG PDS SKO Allocation Policy, 13 Jun 2025}}. Kerosene oil PDS is in the 5 per cent schedule {{IGST Rate Notification 9/2025, Schedule I}}. For current prices, use the PPAC prices menu.
