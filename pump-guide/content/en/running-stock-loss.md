Petrol evaporates and some fuel is always lost in handling, so a pump's book stock never matches its dip stock exactly. The limit below is from the public sector oil companies' guideline, as on 26 November 2026.

## Who this applies to

The Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (MDG 2024) are for retail outlet dealerships of public sector marketing companies {{MDG 2024, cover}}. A private company's pump should ask its company for its own terms.

## The allowed variation

Stock is reconciled after taking the following into account {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.11}}:

| Allowance | Figure |
|---|---|
| Normal operational variation | 4% of tank stock |
| Petrol evaporation and handling loss | 0.75% of quantity sold up to an annual average of 600 KL; 0.60% beyond that |
| Diesel handling loss | 0.25% up to an annual average of 600 KL; 0.20% beyond that |
| Shrinkage and temperature variation | Counted only where and when the shrinkage allowance or temperature variation allowance applies |

The guideline's annexures 8 to 10 give a sample calculation and the method for the shrinkage and temperature variation allowances {{MDG 2024, annexure 8}}.

## If stock goes beyond the limit

1. **Gain (positive variation):** samples are drawn and sent to a laboratory, sales and supplies of all products are suspended at once, and the reason is studied. If the sample passes but another irregularity such as unauthorised purchase is found, action follows for that; if the sample fails, action is as for adulteration {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.11}}.
2. **Loss (negative variation):** with no other irregularity the limit is up to 4% as above, and samples are drawn for testing. Beyond it, supplies continue during the investigation, the dealer's explanation is called, and supply is suspended at once if the explanation is not satisfactory {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.11}}.

Keeping the daily sales and density registers matters here, since missing them is itself an irregularity {{MDG 2024, clause 5.1.12}}. See [dealer records](@running-dealer-records) and the [density check](@running-density-check). For what the whole guideline says about irregularities, see [marketing discipline](@running-marketing-discipline).
