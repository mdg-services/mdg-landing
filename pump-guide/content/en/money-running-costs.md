A pump's costs are many small heads, and most are not published anywhere. This page lists them, says who sets each one, and says where to check the current figure. It is not a budget and adds up to no total. Figures are as on 14 November 2026.

## Which cost heads does a pump have?

| Cost head | Who sets it | Where to check |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel bought from the oil company | The company sets the price; the dealer's share is the commission {{PPAC dealer commission table}} | [Commission page](@money-commission), PPAC table |
| Licence fee per KL, where the company built the facilities | The oil company's agreement | Your dealership agreement; see below |
| PESO licence fee | Petroleum Rules {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2018, First Schedule}} | PESO site, fee schedule; [PESO page](@licences-peso) |
| Dispenser verification | Legal Metrology rules {{G.S.R. 346(E), 8 May 2026}} | Consumer Affairs site; [metrology page](@equipment-metrology) |
| Staff wages | The dealer | Your state labour department |
| Electricity | The state's electricity board | Your tariff bill |
| Insurance | The insurer | Insurer's quote |
| Upkeep of dispensers, tanks, signs | The dealer and the service provider | Service contract |
| Bank and card charges | The bank | Bank's charge list |
| Rent or lease | The landowner, where land is leased | [Lease page](@site-lease) |
| Taxes the dealer collects and pays | Central and state law | [GST page](@running-gst), a chartered accountant |

## Which heads have a published figure?

- **PESO licence:** Rs 5,000 for the first 50 kilolitres of each class of petroleum, plus Rs 50 for every extra kilolitre or part, up to Rs 50,000 a calendar year {{Petroleum Amendment Rules 2018, First Schedule}}. Check the fee shown when you apply online.
- **Dispenser verification:** the verification fee at a Government Approved Test Centre is Rs 5,000 for each nozzle {{G.S.R. 346(E), 8 May 2026}}, and the interval is 24 months {{G.S.R. 905(E), 18 Dec 2025}}.
- **Licence fee per KL:** the June 2023 dealer guideline lists petrol Rs 435.83 and diesel Rs 363.19 for company-owned sites, Rs 196.51 and Rs 163.76 for dealer-owned ones, with GST {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8(E)}}. PPAC lists other figures {{PPAC dealer commission table}}, so read your own agreement.

## What do the other heads depend on?

No official figure was found for these, so none is given. In practice, wages follow the number of staff and shifts, electricity follows the tariff and the equipment, and insurance follows the cover chosen. Ask local dealers, the electricity board and an insurer for real quotes.

Ask a chartered accountant how to book these costs; this page is no tax advice.
