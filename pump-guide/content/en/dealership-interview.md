## Is there an interview or a written test?

No. As on 26 November 2026, the June 2023 brochure says selection is "basically made through draw of lots or bidding", depending on the site type {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14}}. Its stages are application, list of eligible applicants, draw or bid opening, document scrutiny, land evaluation, field verification and the letter of intent. None of them is an interview {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14, cl.15, cl.16}}. The November 2018 brochure, which Hindustan Petroleum still lists, has no interview either {{Selection brochure Nov 2018}}.

Older rules did use interviews. In a letter of 23 June 2014 the petroleum ministry told the oil companies to cancel every allotment where an interview was still pending, and to select afresh under the "draw of lots" system {{MoPNG letter 23 June 2014}}.

## What is checked instead

| Stage | What is checked | Notice you get |
|---|---|---|
| Scrutiny | The documents you upload after you are picked | Corrections within 21 days {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}} |
| Land evaluation | That your plot is in the advertised area and meets the size and road rules | 10 days {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(I)}} |
| Field verification | The originals of everything you uploaded | 10 days {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.15}} |

You are not compared with other applicants at these checks. You are checked against the rules, and if you fail, the next applicant is taken {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.17}}. So the useful preparation is to meet the [eligibility rules](@dealership-eligibility) on the date you apply and keep every original ready. What happens at the checks is on the [field verification](@dealership-field-verification) page.

## Calls about a "dealership interview"

Fake offers often look like an interview call or an approval message followed by a request for a "registration charge" {{Oil companies fraud notice}}. Messages from the oil companies come only to the mobile number and email you registered on the portal {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.14(E)}}. A dealership comes only through the oil companies' own advertisements; MDG Services does not sell or arrange dealerships. Report a suspicious call to your local police station {{Oil companies fraud notice}}.

This page is about choosing a dealer. A job at a petrol pump is a different thing, and is not covered here.
