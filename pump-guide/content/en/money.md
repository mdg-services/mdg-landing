## What does it cost to start a petrol pump?

There is no single figure. The advertisement for each location lists the estimated working capital, the estimated fund to develop the outlet, the fixed fee or minimum bid, and the security deposit {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.24}}. It warns that sales volumes may change and tells applicants to make their own assessment {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.24}}. Fees for approvals are set by each authority: see [licences](@licences).

## What do you pay the oil company?

| Payment | Regular outlet | Rural outlet |
|---|---|---|
| Application fee, not refundable (Open, OBC, SC/ST) | Rs 10,000, Rs 5,000, Rs 3,000 | Rs 8,000, Rs 4,000, Rs 2,500 |
| Security deposit, refundable (Open, OBC, SC/ST) | Rs 5 lakh, Rs 4 lakh, Rs 3 lakh | Rs 4 lakh, Rs 3 lakh, Rs 2 lakh |
| Fixed fee, dealer-owned sites | Rs 15 lakh | Rs 5 lakh |
| Minimum bid, company-owned sites (not Corpus Fund) | Rs 30 lakh | Rs 10 lakh |

Amounts are as on 9 November 2026. The application fee, fixed fee and bid include GST {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.12, cl.13}}. Of the security deposit, 10% is paid within 10 days of the draw and the rest before the letter of appointment {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.20}}. The fixed fee or bid is due within 15 days of receiving the NOC {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.13}}.

Where the company has invested in land and facilities, a licence fee per kilolitre is also payable. The June 2023 guideline lists, as on 9 November 2026, petrol Rs 435.83 and diesel Rs 363.19 for company-owned sites, Rs 196.51 and Rs 163.76 for dealer-owned ones, with GST, "as applicable from time to time" {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8(E)}}.

## Who pays for what on the site?

| Item | Company-owned site | Dealer-owned site |
|---|---|---|
| Land levelling, entry, exit, boundary wall | Dealer | Dealer |
| Tanks, dispensers, signs, automation | Company | Company |
| Office, toilet, electrical room, connections | Company | Dealer |
| Canopy and driveway | Company | Dealer |
| Generator or inverter | Dealer | Dealer |

Fire-fighting and safety equipment is the dealer's, at own cost, at every site {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8}}. The selected candidate also arranges the working capital {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.8(C)}}. On company-owned sites, the land you offer is registered in the company's name by long-term lease or outright sale {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.4(vi)}}.

## Is there help for SC/ST locations?

Yes, under the Corpus Fund Scheme. The company builds a ready outlet at its cost on the land offered, and lends working capital equal to 7 days' sales, up to 18 KL of petrol and 18 KL of diesel, at SBI MCLR plus 1% or 11% a year, whichever is lower {{Selection brochure June 2023, cl.9}}.
