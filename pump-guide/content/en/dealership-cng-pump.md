## Who gives a CNG pump dealership?

The city gas company authorised for that area. PNGRB, the gas regulator, names it area by area: for example Vadodara Gas Limited for Vadodara, Bengal Gas Company Limited for the Kolkata area, Aavantika Gas Limited for Indore {{PNGRB authorised CGD list}}. Each company advertises for its own area, naming the road stretches it wants {{Aavantika Gas CNG brochure 30.05.2024, cl 1.1.1}}. The licence to dispense CNG is a separate PESO licence {{PESO GCR SOP}}; see [CNG station licence](@licences-cng-station).

## Who pays for what?

Most notices use the dealer-owned, dealer-operated (DODO) model. At Vadodara Gas the dealer arranges land, permissions, civil works, electricals, canopy and fire equipment, and runs the station. The company installs and maintains the CNG equipment and fixes the selling price {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 4}}. The dealer is paid a commission per kg sold {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 5}}, and leases the land to the company at a nominal rent: Rs 11,000 a year for at least 15 years at Vadodara Gas {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 2(A)(vi)}}, Rs 24,000 a year for at least 10 years at Bengal Gas {{BGCL DODO EOI Oct 2025, cl 2.12}}.

## What three notices asked

| | Vadodara Gas, Feb 2026 | Bengal Gas, Oct 2025 | Aavantika Gas, May 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net worth | Rs 1 crore | Rs 80 lakh | Rs 1 crore |
| Land | 900 sq m | 800 or 1,200 sq m | per advertisement |
| Land held for | 15 years | 10 years | 15 years |
| Application fee | Rs 11,800 | Rs 6,000 | Rs 10,000 plus GST |
| Deposits | Rs 15 lakh, then Rs 25 lakh | Rs 5 lakh, then Rs 15 lakh | Rs 5 lakh, then Rs 5 or 20 lakh |

Sources: {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 2, 3, 6, 8}} {{BGCL DODO EOI Oct 2025, cl 3, 5, 9, 10}} {{Aavantika Gas CNG brochure 30.05.2024, Annex III}}. Aavantika also asks a non-refundable Rs 15 lakh where the land cannot be sub-leased to it {{Aavantika Gas CNG brochure 30.05.2024, cl 1.1.11}}. Bengal Gas's notice closed on 8 December 2025 {{BGCL DODO EOI Oct 2025, cover}}; read the current one for your area.

## How is the dealer chosen?

At Vadodara Gas, a committee visits and marks the offered plots and declares a merit panel of up to five; a tie goes to the higher net worth {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 11.2}}. The station must start within 24 months of the letter of intent {{VGL DODO EOI Feb 2026, cl 11.4}}.
