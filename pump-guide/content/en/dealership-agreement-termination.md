This page reports what MDG 2024, the guideline of the public sector oil companies, says, as on 23 November 2026. It binds those companies' dealerships only; a private company's dealer should ask that company. It is not legal advice.

## What does the guideline say about the agreement?

MDG 2024 covers marketing discipline, not the agreement's terms. It mentions the Agreement once: its penalties are the minimum, and the company may take higher action, including termination at the first or any instance, in line with the Agreement {{MDG 2024, 8.5.1}}. For the agreement's term and clauses, read the document the company gives you, with an advocate.

## When can a dealership be terminated?

| Case | Termination under the guideline |
|---|---|
| Critical irregularity: adulteration of MS or HSD, tampered metering or totalizer seals, unauthorised fittings, storage, purchase or sales, or a tank lorry carrying unauthorised product | At the first instance {{MDG 2024, clause 8.2}} |
| Major irregularity, such as refusing inspection, no reference density, stock variation beyond limits, missing records or overcharging | After suspensions of 15 and 30 days, at the third instance {{MDG 2024, clause 8.3}} |
| Selling normal MS or HSD as branded fuel | At the second instance {{MDG 2024, clause 8.3}} |
| Fine not paid within 30 days, then not paid in two 15-day suspensions | Termination {{MDG 2024, clause 8.7}} |

Second and third instances are counted over five years from the first irregularity {{MDG 2024, 8.5.5}}. Minor irregularities carry a warning, then fines {{MDG 2024, clause 8.4}}; see [marketing discipline](@running-marketing-discipline) for all the penalties and [inspections](@running-inspections).

## What steps come before it?

1. The irregularity must be established before any action {{MDG 2024, 8.5.2}}.
2. A show cause notice goes to the dealer within 30 days of the inspection, or of the test results where samples were drawn {{MDG 2024, 8.5.6}}.
3. The dealer has 10 days to reply {{MDG 2024, 8.5.7}}.
4. The company passes a speaking order giving its reasons, preferably within 45 days of the reply {{MDG 2024, 8.5.8}}.
5. For critical irregularities, the dealership's signatories get a personal hearing first, and one more chance if they miss the date {{MDG 2024, clause 8.6}}.

## Can the dealer appeal?

Yes. The dealer may appeal within 30 days of the order, through the concerned company office; the decision should preferably come within 90 days {{MDG 2024, clause 8.9}}. The authority is the Executive Director (Retail) at head office or a nominated ED-level officer, a committee of two EDs where an ED in the State Office approved the termination, and two Directors for SC/ST dealerships {{MDG 2024, clause 8.9}}. See also [dealer grievances](@running-dealer-grievance).
