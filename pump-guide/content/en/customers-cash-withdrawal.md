Can you ask the pump for cash instead of finding an ATM? The rules come from the RBI, as on 25 November 2026.

## Is cash at a pump allowed?

Yes, where the bank allows it. The RBI says the facility is available at merchants designated by the acquirer bank, meaning the bank that supplied the card machine, after checks {{RBI FAQ, 22 Nov 2022}}. A designated merchant has to show clearly that it gives cash and what it charges {{RBI FAQ, 22 Nov 2022}}. We found no rule that makes a pump offer it, so a pump has it only if its bank has designated it.

## The limits at a glance

| Point | What the RBI says |
|---|---|
| Cards | Debit cards and full KYC prepaid cards; not credit cards {{RBI FAQ, 22 Nov 2022}} |
| Amount | Rs 2,000 per transaction, Rs 10,000 a month {{RBI FAQ, 22 Nov 2022}} |
| Charge | Not more than 1% of the amount {{RBI FAQ, 22 Nov 2022}} |
| Buy fuel first? | No {{RBI FAQ, 22 Nov 2022}} |

The facility also covers UPI and the overdraft cards of Jan Dhan accounts {{RBI FAQ, 22 Nov 2022}}. The machine must print a receipt, and if you also buy fuel it must show the cash amount separately {{RBI FAQ, 22 Nov 2022}}.

## If something goes wrong

1. Keep the receipt or SMS.
2. If the account is debited but the machine printed no charge slip, the bank must reverse it within T + 5 days and pay Rs 100 a day after that, without your asking {{RBI circular, 20 Sep 2019}}.
3. Complain first to your card bank. If it does not answer or the answer is not satisfactory, go to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman {{RBI FAQ, 22 Nov 2022}}.

If a UPI or card payment for fuel failed, see [paying by UPI or card](@customers-digital-payments). For a wrong fuel amount, see [refunds](@customers-refund).
