Jio-bp sells fuel through Reliance BP Mobility Limited. This page repeats what its own brochure and notices say as on 15 November 2026. Nothing here is a promise of a dealership.

## Is Jio-bp's selection the same as the public sector companies'?

No. Jio-bp is a private joint venture {{Jio-bp fraud warning}}, and it selects dealers under its own brochure {{Jio-bp dealer brochure}}. The MDG 2024 selection guideline holds only for the public sector oil companies; see [how a dealership is chosen](@dealership-process) for that route.

## How does Jio-bp say it selects a dealer?

It invites applications for merit-based selection at identified locations, and lists one outlet type, DODO (dealer owned, dealer operated) {{Jio-bp dealer brochure}}. The steps it gives:

1. Send an Expression of Interest on partners.jiobp.in.
2. Upload the application form and documents within 15 days of the email with your login.
3. A Field Manager shows a Jio-bp ID card and meets you.
4. Documents are evaluated, then a committee interview.
5. A Letter of Intent goes to the selected candidate {{Jio-bp dealer brochure}}.

Dealership and lease run for 30 years {{Jio-bp dealer brochure}}.

## What does it ask for?

| Item | Brochure, as on 15 November 2026 |
|---|---|
| Application fee | Rs 5,000 including GST, non-refundable, paid with the Expression of Interest |
| Processing charge | Rs 3.54 lakh or Rs 1.77 lakh, by demand draft before the final interview; refunded if not selected |
| Security deposit | Refundable, no interest; Rs 10 lakh to Rs 34 lakh by number of dispensers |

{{Jio-bp dealer brochure}}

The brochure also states land sizes and construction cost ranges by road type, and says it is purely a business proposition that promises no returns or profit {{Jio-bp dealer brochure}}. Read those pages yourself; this page gives no estimate.

## Who should I contact, and what does Jio-bp warn about?

Use only the company's own contacts. For registration or form queries the brochure gives 022-68515959 (Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM) and jiobp.dealership@zmail.jiobp.com {{Jio-bp dealer brochure}}. Its customer care toll-free line is 1800-8919-023 {{Jio-bp fraud warning}}.

Jio-bp warns that fraudsters use its name and logo through fake websites, calls and WhatsApp to take money for dealership allotment {{Jio-bp fraud warning}}. Paying anyone other than the company is a sign of fraud. MDG Services does not sell or arrange dealerships. Ask an advocate before signing a lease or agreement.
