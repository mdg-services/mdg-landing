# PRODUCT.md — mdgservices.in (MDG Services / Dealer's कवच)

Drafted 9 Oct 2026 from the repo's own docs (README, DECISIONS.md D1–D11, the dealer brochure),
the standing site rules and the team's notes on its users. To be confirmed by the founder;
edit freely.

## Register

**brand** — a public marketing site and its film pages. The design is part of what is being
sold: trust. (The admin and client apps are separate repos with their own, product, register.)

## Users

Indian petrol-pump dealers (outlet owners), mostly men aged 35–65, Hindi belt first (UP, MP,
Rajasthan, Bihar, Haryana, Delhi), one or two outlets, often run with a son or a manager.

- **Device and network:** low-to-mid Android phones (5–6.5", often a few years old), Chrome or
  the browser WhatsApp opens; 4G that drops to 3G/2G at the pump; data-conscious.
- **How they arrive:** a link forwarded on WhatsApp, opened between customers at the pump in
  bright daylight, or at home at night. One hand, short attention, sceptical of sales pitches.
- **Reading:** Hindi first, comfortable with a few English words of their trade (DSR, stock,
  compliance, app). Many read Devanagari slowly; numbers and plain words land, jargon does not.

## Purpose

Make a dealer trust MDG Services with his pump's daily compliance and paperwork: show what
changes for him (reports ready, deadlines kept, inspections calm) and what it costs, then get
him to call, enrol or watch the full film.

## Brand personality

Calm competence. A reliable senior who has seen every inspection: plain-spoken, warm, never
loud. The कवच (shield) and the परछाई (a shadow that is always there) are the two images.
Navy (#2C2E80) and gold/amber (#F5A524) from the brochure; Devanagari is a first-class face.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS gloss: purple gradients, glassmorphism, hero-metric cards, neon on black.
- Pushy ad energy: countdowns, flashing "limited offer", walls of exclamation marks.
- A YouTube clone: the film pages should feel like ours, not a skin of someone else's player.
- Anything that shows how the work is done: no internal screens, no tools, no method, no AI.
- Real dealer data, real codes, or any oil company's name, logo or colours.

## Strategic principles

1. **Outcomes only.** What a dealer gets, what it costs, who answers. Never the method.
2. **Hindi first, words he would say.** Short sentences; numbers in digits on screen.
3. **Works on his phone, on his network.** Light pages, fast first paint, honest loading.
4. **Big, obvious, forgiving.** Large tap targets, nothing that needs precision or a second
   guess; every state (loading, muted, ended, offline) tells him what to do next.
5. **Calm motion.** Motion explains or confirms; it never decorates or nags.

## Accessibility needs

- Older eyes in sunlight: high contrast, large Devanagari (body ≥ 17px, controls ≥ 44–48px),
  no thin grey text.
- Low literacy: icons with words, never icons alone for important actions.
- One-handed use: primary actions in the lower half of the screen where a thumb reaches.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; never rely on colour alone.
