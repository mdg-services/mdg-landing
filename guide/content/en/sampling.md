## The three samples, side by side

MDG says "3-tier" but never lists the tiers {{2.1}}. This page takes them to be the three samples the lab compares {{2.5(G)}}.

| | Supply location sample | Tank lorry sample | Nozzle sample (RO sample) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drawn by | The supply location, at the filling point where lorries are loaded {{2.2.1(i)}} | The dealer or his representative {{2.2.2.1}} | An oil company official, mobile lab or other authorised sampler {{2.2.2.2}} |
| When | Every day, and again after a switch of storage tank {{2.2.1(i)}} {{2.2.1(ii)}} | On every tank lorry, before decanting {{2.3}} | At random, or after a trigger such as a failed density test or stock variation past the limit {{2.4}} |
| Size | At least 4 x 1 litre of MS and 2 x 1 litre of HSD {{2.2.1(i)}} | 2 x 1 litre of MS and/or 1 x 1 litre of HSD {{2.3}} | Per tank, 6 x 1 litre of MS or 3 x 1 litre of HSD, in three equal sets {{2.4.1}} {{2.4.2}} |
| Held by | The supply location {{2.2.1(iii)}} | The dealer. The transporter may take a second set {{2.3}} | The dealer, the oil company's division, territory or regional office, and the lab {{2.4.1}} {{2.4.2}} |
| How long kept | At least 30 days {{2.9.1}} | The two latest loads for each tank {{2.9.2}} | Until testing and what follows it are finished {{2.9.2}} {{2.9.3}} |

The lab tests the nozzle sample against the latest tank lorry sample and the supply location sample that goes with it {{2.5(G)}}.

## Where to go next

- On every tank lorry, check density and dips first. Then draw, seal, label and sign your sample before you decant, and keep the samples from the two latest loads for each tank {{1.2(x)}} {{2.3}} {{2.9.2}}. The steps are in [the tank lorry retention sample](@sampling-retention).
- An inspector at your pump: [inspector samples](@sampling-inspector-samples) and [the mobile lab](@sampling-mobile-lab).
- A result that fails: [what happens when a sample fails](@sampling-failed).

> **Note:** if you cannot show your tank lorry samples at an inspection, a sample you produce later is not considered for testing, and any action is against the dealer only {{2.5(E)}}.
