## What MDG asks you to keep

| Record | What it covers | When | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily stock register | One register for all products, from the oil company | Daily | {{1.3(ii)}} |
| Morning meter and dip record | Meter readings; each tank's product and water dip | Every morning | {{1.3}} |
| Water dip | Part of the register; water found goes to the controlling office at once | Every day | {{1.4.1(b)}} |
| Nozzle testing litres | Litres used to test each nozzle | Before sales | {{1.4.2(d)}} |
| Entries after a tanker | "Appropriate entries" in the register or on the stock display board. MDG does not list them; the receipt is the plain reading | After decanting | {{1.2(xiii)}} |
| Opening stock | On display with the selling prices | Each day | {{1.3(i)}} {{5.1.17}} |
| Density | Separate register, Annexure 1 format | Each morning and after each tanker | {{1.4.1(c)}} {{1.2(xiv)}} {{annexure 1}} |

See the [density register](@density-register) and [receiving a tanker](@receiving-a-tanker). The morning clause says the readings are taken "separately" without saying from what {{1.3}}. General practice, not an MDG rule: give each tank's product dip and water dip its own line, and check your oil company's register for these columns.

> **Note:** MDG prints no register form. If your oil company's columns differ from the layout below, follow the company's.

## Keeping it, and the stock check

Keep the oil company's daily sales register and the density register for this financial year and the one before {{5.1.12}}. That clause says "daily sales register" and clause 1.3 says "daily stock register": confirm with your oil company which book it means. Not displaying the opening stock is a minor irregularity {{5.1.17}} {{8.4}}.

The stock check starts from the opening dip, receipts, sales as per meter and closing dip {{annexure 9}}. See [stock variation](@stock-variation) and [shrinkage and TVA](@stock-shrinkage-tva). For missing records: [records to keep](@inspection-records-to-keep), [penalties](@penalties), [repeat offences](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid).
