## What to do, step by step

1. **File the report.** Keep it in the outlet file of inspection reports {{1.5(iv)}}, and the inspection record for the previous five years {{5.1.12}}. If you were not given a copy, ask your oil company: MDG does not say one is handed over at the visit.
2. **Match each remark** to the table below.
3. **Count them.** Each irregularity found at the same time counts in its own class {{8.5.3}}.
4. **Wait for the notice.** If an irregularity is established, it comes within 30 days of the inspection, or of the test results if samples were drawn, with the reports it rests on {{8.5.6}}. See [the notice timeline](@inspection-show-cause-notice).
5. **Fix what you can now.** General practice, not an MDG rule: put right display, toilet and housekeeping items at once, and keep dated photographs.

## Where a remark lands

| The remark matches | How MDG treats it | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| An irregularity listed in Chapter 5 | Critical, major or minor. What each costs: [penalty classes](@penalties-classes) | {{8.1}} |
| A lapse in the service standards | Minor | {{5.1.18}} |
| Several lettered minor items of clause 8.4 | Together, one irregularity for taking action | {{8.4}} |
| Two or more irregularities at the same time | Each counted in its class, with a compounding effect | {{8.5.3}} |
| Something MDG does not list | Suitable action after enquiry, in line with natural justice | {{8.5.4}} |

Action needs the irregularity to be established first {{8.5.2}}. Who approves the action is on [the penalty table page](@penalties-beyond-the-table).

For example (a made-up outlet): a report remarks on a missing price board, a dirty toilet and poor housekeeping. All three are lettered minor items, so they count as one irregularity for action {{8.4}}.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
