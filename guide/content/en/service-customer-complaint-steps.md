## What to do, by kind of complaint

MDG prints no complaint-handling procedure. The table matches each kind of complaint with a duty MDG already sets. The matching is general practice, not an MDG rule.

| The complaint | What the dealer or staff does | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Any complaint | Give prompt service and stay courteous | {{1.6(iii)}} |
| Quantity: "I got less" | Show that the unit starts from zero before each delivery and shows the closing reading after it. See [zero before delivery](@quantity-zero-before-delivery) | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{circular}} |
| Quality: "the petrol is poor" | Give filter paper on request. See [the filter paper test](@density-filter-paper) | {{1.4.1(a)}} |
| Price: "the rate is wrong" | Point to the displayed retail price of each product | {{5.1.15}} |
| The customer wants it recorded or taken further | Hand over the complaint book on request, and point to the displayed names and numbers of the dealer and oil company contacts | {{1.6(iv)}} {{1.6(viii)}} {{5.1.17}} {{circular}} |

Charging above the authorised price is a major irregularity {{5.1.13}} {{8.3}}: see [overcharging](@penalties-branded-fuel-overcharging).

## A 5 litre check

MDG gives a customer no right to a 5-litre check {{1.4.2(b)}}. General practice, not an MDG rule: a dealer can offer to fill the verified 5-litre measure from that nozzle in front of the customer. If it shows a variation, the daily-test clause tells the dealer to notify W&M and the oil company at once {{1.4.2(d)}}: see [the 5 litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test).

## If the customer goes to the oil company

A specific complaint against an outlet is one of the cases where the company draws samples {{2.2.2.2}}. See [what happens after a complaint](@inspection-after-customer-complaint).

## What MDG does not say

MDG does not say how fast a complaint must be answered, or what the dealer must do with an entry in the book {{1.6(iv)}}. The contact numbers are on the board at the outlet, so none are printed here.

A missing complaint book, contact board or zero sticker is minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} {{8.4(iii)(c)}}: see [the complaint book page](@service-complaint-book).
