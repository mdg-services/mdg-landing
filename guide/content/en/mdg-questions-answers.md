## Stock variation

### What is the allowed tank variation at a retail outlet?

4% of tank stock, plus the handling loss in the next answer, and shrinkage or TVA where they apply {{5.1.11}}. Annexure 9 works the 4% on the closing dip, after the book stock is worked out {{annexure 9}}. See the [stock variation limit](@stock-variation).

### What is the allowed operation loss for HSD when sales are under 600 KL a year?

MDG calls it handling loss: 0.25% of the quantity sold, up to an annual average of 600 KL. The quantity above 600 KL gets 0.20%. For MS the rates are 0.75% and 0.60% {{5.1.11}}. MDG does not say over what period the average is counted, so ask your oil company. See the [calculator](@tools-stock-variation).

### What happens if stock variation goes beyond the limit?

A positive variation stops sales of all products at once and sends samples to the lab. A negative one lets sales continue while the dealer explains, and supply stops if the explanation is not found satisfactory {{5.1.11}}. With the sample passing and nothing else found, it is a major irregularity {{8.3}}. See [positive and negative variation](@stock-positive-negative).

## Nozzle delivery

### What is the permissible limit of variation in nozzle delivery?

MDG 2024 prints a band, not one figure: ±10 ml to ±25 ml per 5 litres. Inside it, sales go on, but without recalibration and re-stamping within 15 days they stop. Beyond the Legal Metrology limit, that nozzle stops at once {{5.1.2(a)}}. The exact limit is in the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and Rules. See the [5-litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test).

## Density

### By what time must the morning density be recorded?

Before the day's sales start and by 10 AM at the latest, at 15°C {{1.4.1(c)}} {{5.1.9}}. Record it again after each tanker, once at least 50 litres have gone through the pump {{1.2(xiv)}}. See [reference density](@density-reference-10am).

### How far may a tanker's density differ from the invoice?

By no more than ±3.0 kg/m³, with your reading brought to 15°C by the ASTM table {{1.2(iv)}}. See the [invoice check](@density-invoice-check).

### What density range do petrol and diesel need at 15°C?

Petrol 720 to 775 kg/m³. Diesel 810 to 845 (BS VI up to B7) or 820 to 860 (B8 to B20) {{7.6}}. See the [density range](@density-range).

## Penalties and repeat instances

### What are critical, major and minor irregularities?

Irregularities in Chapter 5 fall into three classes {{8.1}}. Critical means termination at the first instance {{8.2}}. Major usually means a 15-day suspension of sales and supplies first {{8.3}}. Minor starts with a warning-cum-guidance letter {{8.4}}. See [penalties](@penalties) and [which class is my lapse](@penalties-classes).

{{penalty-note}}

### What is the cycle for counting second and third instances?

Five years. When an irregularity is established, the oil company checks the five years before that date, and earlier ones in that window count, including under the previous guideline {{8.5.5}}. The clause counts from the date of establishing and does not mention the inspection report date. See [repeat offences](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid).
