## The triggers, by what stops

| Finding | What stops | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| A metering or totaliser seal tampered, or a part added, removed or changed | That unit: sales suspended, unit sealed, samples of all products go to the lab | {{5.1.2(b)}} {{5.1.3}} {{5.1.4}} |
| Your daily check finds a tampered seal | That unit stops at once and is reported to the company, until the seal is replaced | {{1.4.2(a)}} |
| Short delivery beyond the legal limit, seals intact | That unit or nozzle, until recalibrated and re-stamped | {{5.1.2(a)}} |
| Short delivery inside the permitted band | Sales go on and the reading is recorded. Recalibrate and re-stamp within 15 days, or the unit stops until done | {{5.1.2(a)}} |
| Your nozzle check finds a delivery variation | You report it and do not run the unit until it is re-stamped and set right | {{1.4.2(d)}} |
| No reference density at inspection | All products, at once; samples go to the lab | {{5.1.9}} |
| Positive stock variation beyond the limit | All products, at once; samples go to the lab | {{5.1.11}} |
| Density or filter paper test fails | All products, once the company officer has informed the Territory, Divisional or Regional Manager; priority samples | {{2.5(K)}} |
| Mobile lab clinical test fails | MDG words it three ways: all products, sales and supplies, or the concerned product. See [the mobile lab visit](@sampling-mobile-lab) | {{2.5(K)}} {{7.4 Note (ii)}} {{7.6.1(b)}} |
| Major pipeline leak | The company's representative advises the dealer to suspend sales | {{4.3(b)}} |
| Ethanol in petrol outside the desired range, after water is removed | Sales stop; inform the controlling office | {{4.3(c)}} |
| A tank lorry unloading into a tank | Sales from that tank stop before unloading starts and stay stopped until it ends | {{1.2(vii)}} |
| Negative stock variation beyond the limit | Sales go on. Supply to the outlet stops if the dealer's explanation is not found satisfactory | {{5.1.11}} |

This table is for petrol and diesel outlets. Kerosene (SKO-LDO) dealerships have their own list of irregularities {{8.10}}.

## What MDG 2024 leaves open

- When the all-product stops end, and who lifts them.
- How the dealer's duty to stop a unit on any delivery variation {{1.4.2(d)}} fits the note that a unit inside the permitted band keeps selling {{5.1.2(a)}}.
- When sales must stop at an automated outlet. MDG says only that a malfunction is reported at once and the dealer acts as the company official directs {{1.5(viii)}}.

For these, ask your oil company.
