## What counts

| What | In MDG's terms | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Unauthorised purchase | Buying from any source other than those the principal oil company authorises | {{5.1.6}} |
| Unauthorised sale | Any sale of petrol or diesel other than through the outlet's own dispensing units | {{5.1.6}} |
| Unauthorised storage | A storage facility inside the licensed area that the company has not approved | {{5.1.5}} |
| Interconnection | A storage facility outside the licensed premises with an interconnection into the outlet. MDG defines the word as an unauthorised connection between tanks inside the licensed premises | {{5.1.5}} |

Separately, the dealer is not to buy, sell or exchange petroleum products with any other dealer or anyone but the principal oil company {{1.5(iii)}}. The heading of 5.1.6 also names any other product that could be used as a substitute for petrol or diesel {{5.1.6}}. None of these clauses states a minimum quantity or an exception.

## What it costs

Termination of the dealership at the first instance, for storage and for purchase or sale {{8.2(v)}} {{8.2(vi)}}.

{{penalty-note}}

Before termination is recommended or approved, the dealership's signatories or their nominees get a personal hearing {{8.6}}. A termination can be appealed within 30 days {{8.9}}: see [the appeal](@inspection-appeal).

## How a finding can start

A positive stock variation beyond the limit stops sales and supplies of all products at once. Samples go to the lab and a study looks for the reason. If the sample passes but an unauthorised purchase is established, action follows for that {{5.1.11}}. See [positive and negative variation](@stock-positive-negative).

For example (made-up outlet): the sample passes, and the study shows a load bought from a source the company did not authorise. That is unauthorised purchase, so termination at the first instance, after the hearing {{5.1.6}} {{8.2}} {{8.6}}.

A tank lorry carrying an unauthorised product, found unloading, is a separate critical irregularity {{5.1.7}} {{8.2}}: see [unauthorised product in a tanker](@tanker-unauthorised-product-found).

## What MDG 2024 leaves open

- Which sources the principal oil company authorises. Ask your oil company.
- What counts as a substitute product or an unapproved facility. MDG gives no examples.
