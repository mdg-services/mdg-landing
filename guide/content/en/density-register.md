## What goes in the register

As laid out, one form covers one tank of one product for one month {{annexure 1}}. The entries, by the moment they are made:

| Moment | What goes in | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Every morning | The opening density at 15°C from a pump nozzle sample, against that day's date. Before the first sale, by 10 AM | {{1.4.1(c)}} {{5.1.9}} |
| A tanker arrives | The reading of each compartment's sample, and the 15°C density printed on the invoice. Before decanting | {{1.2(viii)}} {{1.1(v)}} {{2.2.1(iv)}} |
| The load has gone into the tank | The 15°C density of the mixed product, from a nozzle sample drawn after at least 50 litres went through the pump | {{1.2(xiv)}} |

## What MDG leaves open

Ask your oil company about these two points:

- **Temperature on the compartment readings.** MDG says each compartment's density and temperature are recorded {{1.2(viii)}}. The form states the invoice and after-decanting entries at 15°C, not the compartment entry {{annexure 1}}. The ±3.0 check is made on the 15°C figure {{1.2(iv)}}.
- **Several compartments or several loads in a month.** The form does not say how its entries are meant to be used then {{annexure 1}}.

## An example

An illustration with a made-up outlet and figures: Tank No. 2, petrol (MS), kg/m³ at 15°C.

| Day | What was recorded | Figures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Morning reading | 743.5 |
| 2 | Morning reading | 743.5 |
| 3 | Morning reading, then a tanker | Morning 743.0. Compartments 744.5 and 745.0 against an invoice density of 743.0. After decanting and 50 litres through the pump: 744.0 |
| 4 | Morning reading | 744.0 |

On day 3 the compartments read 1.5 and 2.0 above the invoice figure, both within ±3.0 kg/m³ {{1.2(iv)}}. Which of these figures an inspector asks for depends on the time of the visit: see [the reference density rule](@density-reference-10am).

## Corrections and other records

MDG has no correction rule for the dealer's register. The only one covers the mobile lab's Record Book and test reports: strike through the old value and write the new one beside it, with name, designation and signature {{7.6.2}}. General practice, not an MDG rule: do the same, and never overwrite.

For the other records MDG lists, and the amounts for missing ones, see [the records to keep](@inspection-records-to-keep).
