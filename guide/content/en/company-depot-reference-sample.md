## What the depot keeps

| What | Detail | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Daily sample | At least 4 x 1 litre of MS and 2 x 1 litre of HSD, from the filling point where lorries are loaded | {{2.2.1(i)}} |
| After a tank switch-over | Taken again once the earlier product has flushed out of the pipeline | {{2.2.1(ii)}} |
| Number | Each sample has a traceable number, the location keeps a record, and the number goes in your delivery documents | {{2.2.1(iii)}} {{3.1(e)}} |
| How long | At least 30 days | {{2.2.1(iii)}} {{2.9.1}} |

## How the lab uses it

- The lab tests the depot sample, if within the retention period, with your pump's sample and your tank lorry sample before a decision is taken {{2.5(I)}}. How it compares them is on [what happens when a sample fails](@sampling-failed).
- The depot sample used is from the tank named in your delivery documents {{2.4.1(iii)}} {{2.4.2(iii)}}.
- If the depot sample was already sent for testing earlier, the lab uses that test report {{Chapter 2, General Notes (1)}}.
- When a mobile lab sample fails, the supply location is to be told at once by phone so that the depot sample can be retained {{2.4.4}}.

## Why a doubt should be raised early

The 30 days is a minimum, and MDG sets no step for a doubt raised after it, apart from the earlier lab report above. It does not say how to ask the depot to hold or test a sample. On receipt of a lorry, a doubt about quality goes to the supply location or the area field officer for instructions {{1.2(ix)}}.

For example (made-up dates): a lorry is filled at the depot on 2 March. A doubt raised in the second week of March is well inside the 30 days. One raised in mid-April may find the sample gone.

General practice, not an MDG rule: when you raise a doubt, write down the invoice number, date, lorry number, tank number and product, and send them to your sales officer the same day.

## What MDG does not say

MDG does not tell the dealer to raise a doubt within 30 days, and it does not say what the lab compares against when the depot sample has gone and was never tested.
