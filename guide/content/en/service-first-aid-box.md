## What MDG 2024 requires

The dealer makes a first aid box available, with the necessary valid medicinal aids {{1.6(ix)}}. Its absence, or medicines beyond expiry, is an irregularity {{5.1.14(a)}}. Point 14 of the circular of 20.03.2025 has the inspector mark the Q&Q kit and first aid box YES or NO {{circular}}.

MDG gives no contents list, size, place or checking interval. Ask your oil company.

## What to keep in the box

General practice, not an MDG rule, and not medical advice: workplace boxes usually hold bandages, dressings, plasters and tape, antiseptic, scissors and tweezers, an eye wash bottle and a first aid instruction card. Also general practice: check expiry dates at a fixed interval and replace before they lapse.

## What a missing or expired box costs

The box appears under 8.4(ii) and, through the circular, under 8.4(iii)(f). MDG does not say which an inspector uses; both are minor {{8.4(ii)}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}} {{5.1.18}}. The circular sets no penalty of its own {{circular}}.

The action is a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity from the third time {{8.4}}.
