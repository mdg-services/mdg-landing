## What to match, detail by detail

On arrival, each invoice detail is checked against the real lorry {{1.1}}. The rest of the receipt order is on [receiving a tanker](@receiving-a-tanker).

| On the invoice | Compare with | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Tank lorry number | The number on the lorry | {{1.1}} |
| Seal or lock number | The seal or lock on the delivery manifold and manhole covers (see [seal or lock mismatch](@tanker-seal-or-lock-mismatch)) | {{1.1(ii)}} {{1.2(ii)}} |
| Compartments, product and quantity in each | The compartments and the dip of each | {{1.1(iii)}} {{1.2(vii)}} |
| Time the lorry left the supply point | When it arrived, and what the tracking link showed (see [VTS and route deviation](@tanker-vts)) | {{1.1(iv)}} {{1.5(vii)}} |
| Density at 15°C | Each compartment's 15°C density, within ±3.0 kg/m³ (see [invoice density check](@density-invoice-check)) | {{1.1(v)}} {{1.2(iv)}} |
| Dip rod with calibration certificate | The rod and certificate the driver shows (see [dip rod and certificate](@tanker-dip-rod-and-certificate)) | {{1.1(vi)}} {{1.2(v)}} |

The supply location fills in the invoice: it writes the density, the filling point, the tank number and the release time {{2.2.1(iv)}}, and records the seal or lock numbers {{3.2}}. MDG does not say what the dealer does if one of these is missing.

## Who to call

| What you find | Who to contact | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Doubt about quality | The supply location or the Area Field Officer, for further instructions | {{1.2(ix)}} |
| Deviation in time or route | The supply location, before accepting the lorry; decant only on further instructions | {{1.5(vii)}} {{2.3 Note (ii)}} |
| Driver will not sign the sample label | Do not decant; contact the supply location or the sales officer | {{2.3 Note (i)}} |

## What MDG does not say

MDG gives no step for a wrong lorry number or a missing invoice detail, and does not say whether to reject the load. A dip that is short against the certificate is made up as set out on [short dip and water](@tanker-short-dip-water) {{1.2(xi)}}. Ask your oil company how it wants a hold or a refusal handled.

General practice, not an MDG rule: write the mismatch, the name of the person you informed and the time on your copy of the invoice.
