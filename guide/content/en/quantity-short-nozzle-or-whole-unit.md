## Who stops, and when

| What is found | What stops | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Short beyond the legal limit, W&M seals intact | Sales through that unit. In a dual DU or MPD, through that nozzle. Recalibrate and re-stamp before sales restart | {{5.1.2(a)}} |
| Inside the band MDG prints, ±10 ml to ±25 ml per 5 litres, seals intact | Nothing at first: it is recorded. Recalibrate and re-stamp within 15 days of the observation, or that unit or nozzle stops | {{5.1.2(a)}} |
| A variation in the dealer's own morning nozzle test | The dealer does not operate the dispensing unit until it is re-stamped and the delivery is right | {{1.4.2(d)}} |
| A seal missing, different or with a broken wire; a tampered totalizer; an unauthorised fitting or part change | The whole unit stops and is sealed, and samples of all products go to the lab | {{5.1.2(b)}} {{5.1.3}} {{5.1.4}} |

MDG points to the permissible limits in the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and its Rules {{5.1.2(a)}}. It prints only the band above.

## What the dealer does

1. Tell W&M and the controlling oil company office at once {{1.4.2(d)}}.
2. Arrange recalibration with the W&M inspector: the dealer or the company's maintenance representative does this {{4.3(a)}}. W&M does the re-stamping {{1.4.2(d)}}.
3. Do not operate the unit until it is re-stamped and the delivery is right {{1.4.2(d)}}.

The daily test is on [the 5 litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test) page.

## Two wordings MDG leaves open

The daily-test rule keeps the dispensing unit off {{1.4.2(d)}}. The short-delivery rule names the nozzle for a dual DU or MPD {{5.1.2(a)}}. MDG defines neither term and does not say which wording governs a variation the dealer finds himself. Ask your oil company which applies to your unit. General practice, not an MDG rule: ask in writing and keep the reply.

## What it costs

Short delivery with the seals intact is a minor irregularity {{8.4(iii)(h)}}. The amounts are on [the 5 litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test) page and in [penalty classes](@penalties-classes). Tampered seals, a tampered totalizer and unauthorised fittings are critical {{8.2}}: see [W&M and totalizer seals](@quantity-seals) and [first steps for a broken seal](@quantity-broken-seal-first-steps).
