## What MDG says

| Point | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| The irregularity | A tank lorry carrying unauthorised product is found decanting into the outlet's tanks | {{5.1.7}} |
| Class and action | Critical; termination at the first instance | {{8.2(vii)}} |
| Before any action | The irregularity must be established | {{8.5.2}} |
| Before termination | A personal hearing for the signatories to the dealership or their nominees | {{8.6}} |

{{penalty-note}}

A show cause notice is issued for every irregularity {{8.5.6}}: see [show cause notice](@inspection-show-cause-notice). Buying from an unauthorised source is a different irregularity: see [unauthorised purchase and sale](@penalties-unauthorised-purchase-sale).

## The receipt checks MDG asks for

These are the checks before decanting. General practice, not an MDG rule: written down, they are your own record of what arrived.

| Check | Clause |
|---|---|
| Density at 15°C within ±3.0 kg/m³ of the invoice density | {{1.2(iv)}} |
| Each compartment's sample read on its own and recorded in the register | {{1.2(viii)}} |
| In doubt about quality: ask the supply location or the Area Field Officer | {{1.2(ix)}} |
| Bottom samples before decanting, label signed with the driver, last two loads kept for each tank | {{1.2(x)}} {{2.3}} {{2.9.2}} |

The note to 5.1 asks the dealer to retain the samples of the last two loads and describes the practice as a safeguard for innocent dealers {{5.1 Note (i)}}. See [retention samples](@sampling-retention) and [receiving a tanker](@receiving-a-tanker).

## What MDG does not say

MDG does not define "unauthorised product". It does not say who finds the lorry decanting, or that doing every check ends the matter; it says only that the irregularity must be established first {{8.5.2}}. It does not say what the dealer does on the spot if the product is found to be wrong during decantation. Ask your oil company.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
