## What the 4% is of

Clause 5.1.11 says 4% of tank stock and does not define "tank stock" {{5.1.11}}. Annexure 9 puts a figure on it: the book closing stock is judged against the closing stock as per dip, within plus or minus 4% of the dip {{annexure 9}}. Read together, the 4% is taken on the closing dip.

## How the check is made

Annexure 9, in our words {{annexure 9}}:

1. Book closing stock: the opening dip stock plus receipts, minus the shrinkage volume (only where shrinkage applies), minus sales as per meter.
2. Closing stock as per dip.
3. Work out 4% of the closing dip. The book closing stock must fall within that distance of the dip, either side.

For TVA the method is the same {{annexure 10}}. The handling-loss percentages are separate allowances {{5.1.11}}, and Annexure 9 has no step for them. See [shrinkage and TVA](@stock-shrinkage-tva) and [the 600 KL annual average](@stock-600-kl-slab).

## An example

For example (a made-up tank, no shrinkage): opening dip 12.000 KL, receipts 18.000 KL, sales as per meter 20.200 KL. The book closing stock is 12.000 + 18.000 − 20.200 = 9.800 KL. The closing dip is 10.000 KL, and 4% of it is 0.400 KL, so the band is 9.600 to 10.400 KL. The book figure is inside {{annexure 9}}.

The band moves with the dip: on a 5.000 KL dip it is only 0.200 KL each side.

This example shows the 4% band only. MDG gives no worked example that combines it with the handling-loss percentages, so none is shown. Use the [stock variation calculator](@tools-stock-variation) and ask your oil company.

## What MDG does not say

- **Whether the check is for one tank or one product.**
- **How the handling-loss percentages combine with the 4%** in the Annexure 9 comparison.
