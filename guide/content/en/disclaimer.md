These guides explain the Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (MDG 2024) in plain words for petrol pump dealers and their staff. They are written and published by MDG Services. They are not the official text, and they are not connected with any oil company or government body.

## How we write them

- Every rule, figure and deadline is taken from the Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (Ver. 1, effective 24.10.2024) or a circular the oil companies have published, and the clause number is shown beside it so you can check it in the official document. A figure from the replaced Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2012 (Ver. 7) is shown only for comparison, and is labelled as older.
- We explain in our own words. We do not copy the guidelines, their forms or the test standards they refer to.
- Where the guidelines leave something to the oil company, or are unclear, we say so instead of guessing.
- Each page shows the date it was last checked. When we learn of a new circular, we check the pages it affects.
- Examples use made-up outlets and are marked as examples.
