## Selling normal fuel as branded fuel

- MDG states it in one line: the intentional sale of normal MS or HSD as branded fuel {{5.1.10}}.
- MDG defines neither "branded fuel" nor "intentional", and does not say how intent is shown. The irregularity must be established first {{8.5.2}}.
- The differential price runs from the last inspection {{8.3}}. MDG gives no formula, so ask your oil company how it is worked out.

## Overcharging

- It is charging above the authorised selling price of petrol, diesel, CNG or Auto LPG {{5.1.13}}.
- MDG gives no tolerance, rounding rule or method of proof. If unsure which price is authorised for your outlet, ask your oil company.
- Kerosene wholesale dealers have their own rule on overcharging {{8.10}}. See [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers).

## Displaying the prices

The dealer displays the authorised retail selling prices of the products sold {{1.3(i)}} {{5.1.15}}. The circular of 20.03.2025 lists the same duty and sets no penalty of its own {{circular}}. See [display boards](@service-display-boards).

General practice, not an MDG rule:

- Check the price board against the pump display whenever a price changes.
- Make sure every attendant knows which nozzle sells which grade.
- Note the date and time of each price change.
