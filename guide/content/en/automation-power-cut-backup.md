## What MDG asks

| What | Clause |
|---|---|
| Ensure a power backup, a generator or UPS, for power failure | {{1.5(ix)}} |
| Keep the automation working ("No automation, No Operation") | {{1.5(viii)}} |
| Report any malfunction at once to the authorised official, then act on the direction | {{1.5(viii)}} |

Clause 1.5(viii) speaks of malfunctioning and does not mention power cuts {{1.5(viii)}}. MDG prints no steps for a cut. These are the clauses that bear on it, in the order a dealer meets them:

1. **The backup takes over** {{1.5(ix)}}.
2. **If the automation stops anyway, report it at once** and follow the direction {{1.5(viii)}}.
3. **If the direction is manual mode, or leaving the ATG off, it needs written permission** {{5.1.16 Note}}. [Manual mode](@automation-manual-mode) shows what counts.

General practice, not an MDG rule: note the time the power failed and the time you reported.

## If there is no permission

Manual mode, or an ATG left off or not working, without permission is a major irregularity {{5.1.16(a)}} {{5.1.16(b)}} {{8.3(vii)}}. The first instance is a penalty of Rs 1,00,000.

{{penalty-note}}

The full ladder is on [the automated outlets page](@automation). MDG lists no separate irregularity for having no backup. For matters it does not list, the oil company acts after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}. See [classes of irregularity](@penalties-classes).

## What MDG does not say

- **How long the backup must run**, or how big it must be.
- **Whether sales must stop** while mains and backup are both down. Ask your oil company.

## An example

For example (a made-up outlet): the mains fails at 6:40 PM and the generator does not start, so the automation stops. The manager calls the authorised official at once {{1.5(viii)}} and asks for any manual mode instruction by email before switching a unit to manual {{5.1.16 Note}}. He notes both times and files the email, which is general practice, not an MDG rule.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
