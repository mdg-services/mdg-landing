## What MDG 2024 says follows a complaint

| Step | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Sampling | Besides random sampling, the company is to draw samples when there is a specific complaint against the outlet. The samples come from the nozzles of all petrol and diesel tanks and are sealed and labelled. | {{2.2.2.2}} {{2.4}} |
| A stop on sales | If a density or filter paper test fails, or a mobile lab's clinical test fails, the company officer suspends sales and supply of all products after informing the Territory, Divisional or Regional Manager. Samples go for priority testing. | {{2.5(K)}} |
| A toilet letter | If a customer or transit traveller complains that the toilet is not accessible, a photograph is taken or the complaint is shared, and a letter listing the penalty is issued instantly. | {{5.1.14(b)}} |

For the bottles, labels and what you keep, see [inspector samples](@sampling-inspector-samples). For the filter paper test, see [the filter paper page](@density-filter-paper).

## Who comes

MDG 2024 does not name the team that visits after a complaint. The steps above follow its own words for any visitor: officials, mobile labs, agencies the company authorises and authorities the law empowers draw samples {{2.2.2.2}}, and random sampling is done by the Sales Officer, Inspecting Teams, Senior Level Officers and Mobile Labs {{2.4}}. See [who can inspect](@inspection-who).

## What must be ready

- **The complaint book**, handed over when a customer asks, with a message that it is available {{1.6(iv)}}.
- **Contact names** of the dealer and the oil company people to call about complaints {{1.6(viii)}} {{5.1.17}}.

Both are minor if missing {{8.4}}. The counter side is on [the complaint book page](@service-complaint-book) and in [handling a customer complaint](@service-customer-complaint-steps).

## What MDG 2024 leaves open

- Whether a complaint visit is announced.
- How the company checks a complaint before sending a team.
- How long after a complaint a visit happens.

For these, ask your oil company. General practice, not an MDG rule: note the visitor's name, designation, arrival time and the reason given for the visit.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
