## The two steps

Both allowances are a percentage of the quantity sold {{5.1.11(i)}} {{5.1.11(ii)}}.

| Fuel | Allowance | Up to 600 KL | Additional quantity beyond 600 KL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Petrol (MS) {{5.1.11(i)}} | Evaporation and handling loss | 0.75% | 0.60% |
| Diesel (HSD) {{5.1.11(ii)}} | Handling loss | 0.25% | 0.20% |

The higher rate covers the first 600 KL. The lower rate covers only what is sold beyond it, not the whole quantity.

In the same clause they sit beside the 4% of tank stock and the shrinkage or TVA allowance {{5.1.11}}. The whole limit is on the [stock variation page](@stock-variation), and the 4% on [four percent of what](@stock-four-percent-of-what).

## What MDG does not say

The clause says "annual average of 600 KLs" and stops there {{5.1.11(i)}}. It does not say:

- how the annual average is worked out;
- over what period the 600 KL is counted;
- how the two rates apply when an inspection covers a shorter period;
- how its officer adds the loss to the Annexure 9 comparison, which has no step for it {{annexure 9}}.

Ask your oil company about each. MDG gives no monthly equivalent, so none is shown here.

## An example

For example (a made-up outlet), an illustration of the rate step only, not a ruling on how the year is counted. Suppose 800 KL is sold.

| Fuel | First 600 KL | Remaining 200 KL | Allowance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Petrol | 600 × 0.75% = 4.500 KL | 200 × 0.60% = 1.200 KL | 5.700 KL |
| Diesel | 600 × 0.25% = 1.500 KL | 200 × 0.20% = 0.400 KL | 1.900 KL |

For the whole stock sum, see the [stock variation calculator](@tools-stock-variation).
