## The safety checklist

| Item | What MDG and the circular say | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Safety equipment | Provided as the statutory requirements say; kept in working condition | {{1.6(xi)}} |
| Trained people | The dealer is trained to operate it; trained staff are available to handle it | {{1.6(xi)}} |
| PESO rules | Observed by the dealer | {{1.5(ii)}} |
| During unloading | A portable DCP extinguisher kept near the tank lorry at a suitable distance | {{1.2(i)}} |
| Safety signs | Proper signs such as No Smoking and switch off engine (point 16); the oil company's letter format adds "No Mobile" as an example | {{circular}} |

The other unloading steps are in [receiving a tanker](@receiving-a-tanker).

## What MDG does not say

Beyond the DCP extinguisher at unloading {{1.2(i)}} and the [first aid box](@service-first-aid-box), MDG lists no equipment, numbers, training interval or certificate, and prescribes no wording, size, language or place for signs. The equipment list is whatever the statutory rules require {{1.6(xi)}}. General practice, not an MDG rule: ask your oil company which statutory list it holds your outlet to.

## What a miss costs

Safety signs are point 16, so a NO is acted on under 5.1.18 or the respective clause, and 5.1.18 is a minor irregularity {{circular}} {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}}. The action is a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity from the third time {{8.4}}.

{{penalty-note}}

Equipment and training are not among the 20 points by name, and 8.4 has no line for them. Point 20 covers any other statutory compliance {{circular}}. MDG does not say whether an inspector uses it for missing equipment. For an irregularity it does not name, the oil company acts after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}.

## An example

For example (a made-up outlet), before a tanker arrives: the dealer checks that the DCP extinguisher works and a trained staff member is on duty {{1.6(xi)}}; once parked, the lorry has the extinguisher near it {{1.2(i)}}.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
