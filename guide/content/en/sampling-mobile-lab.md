## What happens at your outlet

1. The mobile lab draws nozzle samples of MS and HSD from every tank {{2.4.4}} {{7.3}}.
2. It runs clinical tests: appearance, colour, density at 15°C and distillation, plus kinematic viscosity at 40°C for HSD. They run whatever the density result {{7.4}}.
3. If the sample passes, no further action is taken and the balance samples go back to the outlet {{2.4.4}} {{7.6}}.

The limits follow the Indian Standards: IS 2796, IS 17586 and IS 17021 for petrol, IS 1460 and IS 16531 for diesel. They are revised from time to time, so check the current version of each {{7.6}}. The test report sets the density the officer measures beside the density you recorded for your tank after the last receipt, and prints a maximum variation of 3.0 kg/m³ between the two {{annexure 12a}} {{annexure 13a}}. If that record is missing at the visit, the officer still recommends action even when the sample passes {{5.1.9}}. See [reference density](@density-reference-10am).

## If a sample fails

1. The officer notes the meter reading of every dispensing unit and the dip of each tank of that product in the test report (Annexure 12 for MS, Annexure 13 for HSD), and advises you to suspend sales of it. You sign every copy with the outlet seal {{7.6.1}}.
2. Three sets of 1-litre bottles are drawn: 6 per MS tank, 3 per HSD tank, on the Annexure 11 label {{2.4.4}} {{7.5}}. You keep one set. The oil company keeps one and tests one, rather than sending it straight to the lab {{2.4.4}}.
3. The mobile lab hands the samples to the oil company's representative, preferably within 5 days. The supply location is phoned at once, so its depot sample is kept {{2.4.4}}.
4. The officer tells the State Level Coordinator straight away {{7.6.1(d)}}. The coordinator and the oil company arrange to collect and test the related reference samples under chapter 2: see [what happens when a sample fails](@sampling-failed) {{7.6}}.

Two points are open, so confirm them with your oil company. MDG describes the suspension in three ways: advice to suspend the product {{7.6.1(b)}}, suspension of sales and supplies by the mobile lab {{7.4 Note (ii)}}, and suspension of all products by an oil company officer {{2.5(K)}}. Chapter 7 also does not say how its rule on "established adulteration" {{7.4 Note (i)}} fits with the chapter 2 testing.

## If you refuse

Refusing or obstructing a mobile lab's sampling or inspection is a major irregularity {{5.1.8}}. The minimum action is a 15-day suspension of sales and supplies the first time, 30 days the second time, and termination of the dealership the third time {{8.3}}.
