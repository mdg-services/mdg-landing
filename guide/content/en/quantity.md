## What the dealer checks every day

Clause 1.4.2 puts six quantity duties on the dealer. They sit under the general duty to give customers quality product in the correct quantity at the correct price {{1.6(i)}}.

| Duty | What MDG asks | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Seals and certificate | Check every day that the W&M seal on the metering unit and the totalizer of each dispensing unit is correct, and that the unit's W&M certification is valid | {{1.4.2(a)}} |
| 5-litre measure | Keep a standard 5-litre measure, verified by W&M, for exclusive use in pump testing | {{1.4.2(b)}} |
| Lube measures | Every measure that dispenses bulk lube oil on the island or in service bays must be stamped by W&M | {{1.4.2(c)}} |
| Nozzle check | Check every nozzle for correct delivery each day before sales begin, and record the testing quantity in the stock register | {{1.4.2(d)}} |
| Leaks | Look for leaks in the pump, hose and nozzle every day, and report any leak to the oil company at once | {{1.4.2(e)}} |
| Zero | Bring the meter back to zero after each delivery, show zero to the customer before the next, and display a message about it on each pump | {{1.4.2(f)}} |

The dealer also records the pump meter reading and the tank dips each morning {{1.3}}.

Three of these duties have their own page: [the 5-litre test and nozzle check](@quantity-5-litre-test), [the W&M and totalizer seals](@quantity-seals) and [zero before every delivery](@quantity-zero-before-delivery).

## Short delivery: seals intact or seals tampered

One question splits every quantity case in two: are the W&M seals intact? The answer decides whether the case is minor or critical.

| | Seals intact | Seals tampered |
|---|---|---|
| What MDG calls it | Short delivery with W&M seals intact {{5.1.2(a)}} | Seals of the metering unit found tampered {{5.1.2(b)}} |
| Does the quantity matter? | Yes. The unit is found delivering short | No. Penal action follows even if the delivery is correct or excess {{5.1.2(b)}} |
| Class | Minor {{8.4(iii)(h)}} | Critical {{8.2}} |
| Penalty | Warning-cum-guidance letter, then Rs 10,000, then Rs 25,000 per irregularity {{8.4}} | Termination at the first instance {{8.2}} |
| Sales | Beyond the legal limit, sales through that unit or nozzle stop until it is recalibrated and re-stamped. Within the permissible band, the finding is recorded and sales continue, but the fix is due in 15 days {{5.1.2(a)}} | Sales from that unit are suspended, the unit is sealed and samples of all products go to the lab {{5.1.2(b)}} |

Minor penalties count in a particular way. One or more of the miscellaneous minor irregularities found together count as a single irregularity for taking action {{8.4}}. The second and third instances are counted over five years {{8.5.5}}. See [how repeat offences are counted](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid). The oil company may still take higher action than the MDG minimum {{8.5.1}}.

The full seals-intact rule, with the 15-day deadline, is on the [5-litre test page](@quantity-5-litre-test). MDG does not say in so many words whether a finding inside the permissible band also counts as the minor irregularity above. Ask your oil company how it records these.

## Tampering: the three critical findings

MDG 2024 lists three quantity findings as critical, each ending in termination of the dealership at the first instance {{8.2}}.

- **Seals of the metering unit tampered.** A seal is deemed tampered if it is missing, if a seal other than the one embossed by the W&M inspector is in place, or if the sealing wire is broken {{5.1.2(b)}}.
- **Totalizer tampered.** This covers a seal that allows the reading to be manipulated, a totalizer deliberately made non-functional, and a non-working totalizer not reported to the oil company {{5.1.3}}.
- **Unauthorised fittings, parts or software.** Adding, removing, replacing or manipulating any part of the dispensing unit, including a chip, electronic parts or the manufacturer's software, is deemed tampering {{5.1.4}}.

In each case sales from the unit are suspended, the unit is sealed, and samples of all products are drawn and sent to the lab {{5.1.2(b)}} {{5.1.3}} {{5.1.4}}. Before an oil company recommends termination for a critical irregularity, the signatories to the dealership get a personal hearing {{8.6}}. The details of each finding are on the [seals page](@quantity-seals).

## Who fixes a pump that delivers wrong

- **Recalibration.** If the pump is delivering short or excess, the oil company's maintenance representative or the dealer must arrange recalibration at once, with the help of the W&M Inspector {{4.3(a)}}.
- **Re-stamping.** The Weights & Measures Department does it. Until it is done and the delivery is set right, the dealer must not operate the unit {{1.4.2(d)}}.
- **A broken W&M seal found by the oil company's maintenance representative.** The representative informs the concerned company official and records it in the pump maintenance report, which the dealer also signs {{4.3(a)}}.
- **Breakdowns.** The oil company attends breakdown maintenance on its dispensing pumps, and the dealer must promptly inform it of any breakdown {{4.2(a)}}. See [what the oil company must fix](@company-breakdowns-leaks).

## Who checks a petrol pump

| Who | What they check | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| The dealer | Seals, certificate, every nozzle and leaks, every day | {{1.4.2}} |
| The oil company's maintenance representative | The maintenance report asks whether the metering unit (W&M seal) calibration was checked and found correct, and whether there is any unauthorised fitting | {{annexure 6}} |
| W&M | Re-stamps after recalibration. Its opinion on whether a seal was tampered is final | {{1.4.2(d)}} {{5.1.2(b)}} |
| Oil company officers and other authorities | Central and State Government authorities, as well as oil company officers, can check the outlet. What they establish is treated as an irregularity under MDG | {{8.7}} |

## One morning, worked through

At 6:40 AM, before the first sale, the manager looks at unit 3 and sees the sealing wire on its totalizer cut in two.

- **What MDG tells the manager to do:** stop sales through unit 3 at once, report to the controlling oil company office immediately, and keep unit 3 off until the seals are replaced {{1.4.2(a)}}. Other units are not named in that clause.
- **How MDG classes the wire:** a broken wire counts as tampering {{5.1.2(b)}}.

> **Note:** MDG says nothing that softens this for a dealer who finds a broken seal and reports it first. The daily check and the immediate report are the dealer's duty under 1.4.2(a). If a seal is damaged, report it to the oil company straight away, before any inspection, and ask the oil company what follows.

## What to have ready

- The W&M certificate for each dispensing unit, valid {{1.4.2(a)}}.
- The W&M-verified 5-litre measure, kept only for pump testing {{1.4.2(b)}}.
- Stamped lube measures {{1.4.2(c)}}.
- A message about zero on every pump {{1.4.2(f)}}.
- Power backup, a generator or UPS, so that electronic totalizers keep working in a power cut {{1.5(ix)}}.

MDG does not say how long a W&M certificate lasts or how it is renewed. Ask your oil company or the local W&M office. For a specific case under any of these rules, ask the oil company, and a lawyer if termination is in question.
