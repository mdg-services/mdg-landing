## What the dealer does each day

Clause 1.4.2 sets six duties, on top of the general duty to give the right quality, quantity and price {{1.6(i)}}.

| When | What the dealer does | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Every day | Looks at the W&M seal on the metering unit and totalizer of each dispensing unit, and confirms its W&M certificate is valid | {{1.4.2(a)}} |
| Every day, before the first sale | Tests every nozzle and writes the testing quantity in the stock register | {{1.4.2(d)}} |
| Every day | Looks over pump, hose and nozzle for leaks and reports any at once | {{1.4.2(e)}} |
| Kept at the outlet | A W&M-verified 5-litre measure, used for pump testing only | {{1.4.2(b)}} |
| Kept at the outlet | A W&M stamp on every measure used for bulk lube oil | {{1.4.2(c)}} |
| Every delivery | Meter back to zero, zero shown to the customer, a message about it on each pump unit | {{1.4.2(f)}} |

Each morning the dealer also writes down the pump meter reading and the dip of each tank {{1.3}}. Three of these duties have their own page: [the 5-litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test), [W&M and totalizer seals](@quantity-seals) and [zero before every delivery](@quantity-zero-before-delivery).

## Short delivery: seals intact or tampered

Every quantity case starts with one question: are the W&M seals intact? That decides minor or critical.

| | Seals intact | Seals tampered |
|---|---|---|
| MDG heading | Short delivery with seals intact {{5.1.2(a)}} | Metering-unit seals found tampered {{5.1.2(b)}} |
| Does the quantity matter? | Yes: the unit is delivering short | No: action follows even when the delivery is correct or in excess {{5.1.2(b)}} |
| Class and action | Minor {{8.4(iii)(h)}}: warning-cum-guidance letter at the first instance, Rs 10,000 per irregularity at the second, Rs 25,000 from the third on {{8.4}} | Critical: termination at the first instance {{8.2}} |
| Sales | Short beyond the legal limit: stop until recalibrated and re-stamped. Inside the permissible band: recorded and sales go on, but recalibration and re-stamping are due within 15 days or sales stop {{5.1.2(a)}} | The unit stops selling, is sealed, and samples of all products go to the lab {{5.1.2(b)}} |

{{penalty-note}}

The band and the 15 days are on the [5-litre test page](@quantity-5-litre-test). For the count of instances, see [how repeat offences are counted](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid).

Tampered seals are one of three critical quantity findings. The other two are a tampered totalizer and unauthorised fittings, chips or software. Each ends in termination at the first instance {{8.2}}. See [W&M and totalizer seals](@quantity-seals).

## If a pump delivers wrong

- **Short or excess:** the oil company's maintenance representative or the dealer should arrange recalibration at once, with the W&M Inspector's help {{4.3(a)}}.
- **Breakdown:** the oil company attends breakdowns on its own pumps, and the dealer must tell it promptly {{4.2(a)}}. See [what the oil company must fix](@company-breakdowns-leaks).

A tanker compartment that dips short is a different problem, dealt with at receipt. See [short dip and water](@tanker-short-dip-water).
