## What the 20 points are

A circular dated 20.03.2025 lists 20 protocols that a dealer must ensure at the outlet, under clause 5.1.18 of MDG 2024 {{circular}} {{5.1.18}}. The oil company sends it to every dealer.

At an inspection, the inspecting authority checks that all 20 are available and practised, and records each as **YES** or **NO** {{circular}}. If a deviation is found, the company acts under 5.1.18 or under the respective clause that fits {{circular}}.

MDG asks the dealer to keep the minimum standard of customer service set by oil company circulars from time to time {{1.6(xii)}} {{5.1.18}}. "From time to time" means later circulars can add to or change what is expected. Check what your oil company has sent.

## The 20 points

The numbers are the circular's own. A clause column that shows only the circular means MDG's text has no matching rule, so the circular is the whole basis. Where MDG gives more detail, it is added here.

### At the pumps and on the forecourt

| No. | The circular asks | What compliant looks like | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every product available at the outlet, always | All products are available. The circular says "always" and lists no exception. | {{circular}} |
| 2 | Dispensing units manned during working hours by CA, DSM or CSA | A staff member of the kind the circular names stands at every dispensing unit for the whole of working hours. | {{circular}} |
| 3 | Show "zero" before delivery and the closing reading after | The meter goes back to zero after each delivery. A message to this effect is on each pump unit. The attendant shows the customer zero before delivery starts and the final reading after it ends. | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{5.1.17}} {{circular}} |
| 4 | Marshal and regulate the queue in the forecourt | Staff guide vehicles and keep the queue in order. The circular says no more than this. | {{circular}} |
| 8 | Courteous behaviour of employees | Prompt service and courteous behaviour at all times. | {{1.6(iii)}} |
| 9 | Digital payment available and enabled | Credit or debit card, loyalty machine, QR code and similar modes are available and switched on. | {{circular}} |

### Facilities for customers

| No. | The circular asks | What compliant looks like | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Purified drinking water available | Drinking water is available to customers. The circular says "purified" and sets no standard, so ask your oil company what it accepts. | {{circular}} |
| 6 | Manned air facility | Air with a calibrated air gauge is available to customers throughout the outlet's operating hours, and someone mans it. | {{5.1.14(a)}} {{1.6(ii)}} {{circular}} |
| 7 | Toilet protocol followed | The daily checks are done: clean at all times, lighting, flush working, water, working latch, signage, open to all customers and transit travellers (subject to safety protocols), and the "Darpan" QR code for feedback near the toilet. The protocol is displayed near the toilet, and a maintenance sheet is kept and displayed. See [the toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules). | {{5.1.14(b)}} |
| 14 | Q&Q kit and first aid box available | A first aid box with medicines not past their expiry date. The circular does not say what the "Q&Q kit" holds, so ask your oil company. MDG separately requires calibrated hydrometers and thermometers, the ASTM table and other testing kits as applicable. | {{5.1.14(a)}} {{1.6(ix)}} {{1.5(vi)}} |

| No. | The circular asks | What compliant looks like | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Dispensing units, pedestals, island and lube display unit clean | Kept clean to your oil company's housekeeping standard. | {{5.1.17}} {{1.6(x)}} |
| 11 | Driveway entry and exit roads free of obstacles, clean and lit | Entry and exit roads are clear, clean and properly illuminated. | {{5.1.17}} {{1.6(x)}} |
| 12 | Emblem and light poles, monolith and signages, RVI fascia etc. clean | Those items are kept clean. The circular does not spell out "RVI". | {{5.1.17}} |
| 13 | Sales room neat and clean | The sales room is kept neat and clean. | {{5.1.17}} |

MDG's own words here are short. Poor housekeeping is an irregularity {{5.1.17}}. The outlet must be adequately lit and clean as per the housekeeping standards the oil companies lay down {{1.6(x)}}. The standard itself belongs to the oil company, not to MDG, so ask for it.

| No. | The circular asks | What compliant looks like | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | Any other statutory compliance, or direction from a competent authority | You follow it. The example in the circular's format letter (its Annexure 1) is a district administration's "No Helmet No Fuel" order in some cities. | {{circular}} |

## What a NO costs

The circular sets no penalty of its own. A deficiency attracts the penalty for minor irregularities {{5.1.18}}, and "customer service standard" is a minor irregularity in 8.4 {{8.4}}.

| Instance | Action, per irregularity |
|---|---|
| First | Warning-cum-guidance letter |
| Second | Rs 10,000 |
| Third and later | Rs 25,000 |

Source for the table: {{8.4}}. Three things to know:

- **Counting.** If several of the irregularities in 8.4(iii)(a) to (h) are found together, they count as one irregularity when action is decided. Customer service standards are item (f) and the toilet is item (g) {{8.4}}.
- **The 5-year window.** The second and third instance are counted over five years from the date an irregularity is established {{8.5.5}}. See [how repeat offences are counted](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid).
- **The respective clause.** The circular lets the company act under 5.1.18 or under the respective clause {{circular}}. For the items it ties to other clauses, that means 5.1.14(a) for air and first aid, 5.1.14(b) for the toilet and 5.1.17 for housekeeping and displays. Price display has its own clause, 5.1.15. All of these are minor irregularities in 8.4 {{8.4}}. Item 20 is open-ended, and MDG names no specific penalty for it. Ask your oil company which clause it would use.

## Acknowledge the circular

The protocol is to be communicated to every dealership, with the acknowledgement kept in the records {{circular}}. This is what the circular and MDG ask for:

- The oil company sends you a letter on its letterhead listing the 20 points, and asks you to acknowledge it to your controlling office in the format that comes with it {{circular}}.
- The acknowledgement is a short letter that names the outlet, gives its customer code and the date of the circular, and carries the date, place, name and signature of the dealer {{circular}}.
- MDG asks for the oil company's letters to be kept in a file at the outlet, available for ready reference at all times {{1.5(iv)}}. The circular is one of those letters.

General practice, not an MDG rule: keep your signed acknowledgement with the circular, and if you cannot find the circular, ask your controlling office for a copy.

## A related duty in the same clause

The note under 5.1.18 adds that the dealer must meet all applicable statutory obligations to staff, including minimum wages and other benefits, among them PMSBY and PMJJBY {{5.1.18}} {{1.5(x)}}. See [staff wages and uniform](@service-staff-wages-uniform).
