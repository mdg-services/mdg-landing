## The 20 points in plain words

Point numbers are the circular's; the wording is ours. At inspection each point is marked **YES** or **NO** {{circular}}. Later circulars can add to the list {{5.1.18}}, so check what your oil company has sent.

| Point | What you need in place | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No product runs out, ever. | |
| 2 | Every dispensing unit is manned (CA, DSM or CSA) through working hours. | |
| 3 | The meter is at zero before each delivery, and the customer is shown the zero and then the closing reading. | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{5.1.17}} |
| 4 | Staff guide vehicles and keep the forecourt queue in order. | |
| 5 | Purified drinking water. MDG sets no standard; ask your oil company. | |
| 6 | A manned air facility with a calibrated gauge. | {{5.1.14(a)}} {{1.6(ii)}} |
| 7 | The toilet protocol is followed. See [the toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules). | {{5.1.14(b)}} |
| 8 | Staff are quick and polite. | {{1.6(iii)}} |
| 9 | Digital payment is on offer and switched on. | |
| 10 | Dispensing units, pedestals, island and lube display are clean. | {{5.1.17}} {{1.6(x)}} |
| 11 | Entry and exit roads are clear, clean and lit. | {{5.1.17}} {{1.6(x)}} |
| 12 | Emblem, light poles, monolith, signage and fascia are clean. | {{5.1.17}} |
| 13 | The sales room is neat and clean. | {{5.1.17}} |
| 14 | A first aid box and a "Q&Q kit" are available. See [the first aid box](@service-first-aid-box). | {{5.1.14(a)}} {{1.6(ix)}} |
| 15 | Utility bins and dustbins are clean and easy to reach. | {{5.1.17}} |
| 16 | Safety signs such as "No Smoking" and "switch off engine". | |
| 17 | Working hours are shown; holiday notices go up well ahead. | {{1.6(v)}} |
| 18 | Names and phone numbers of the oil company people to call with a complaint are shown. | {{5.1.17}} {{1.6(viii)}} |
| 19 | The retail selling price of every product is shown. See [the display boards](@service-display-boards). | {{5.1.15}} {{1.3(i)}} |
| 20 | Any other legal duty or direction of a competent authority is followed. | |

The rows with no clause rest on the circular alone {{circular}}.

## What a NO can cost

A shortfall under 5.1.18 draws the minor-irregularity ladder: a warning-cum-guidance letter, then Rs 10,000, then Rs 25,000 per irregularity {{5.1.18}} {{8.4}}.

{{penalty-note}}

Several NOs found together: see [repeat offences](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid).

## Acknowledge the circular

The circular asks the dealer to confirm receipt to the controlling office, in the format sent with it, and to keep the acknowledgement in the records {{circular}}. MDG also asks for oil company letters to be kept in a file at the outlet {{1.5(iv)}}.
