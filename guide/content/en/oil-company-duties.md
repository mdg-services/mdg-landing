## What the oil company does

| Topic | What the oil company does | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Quality tests | Tests quality at each step of handling, as the oil industry's quality control manual (IQCM) lays down, and records the results in its standard formats | {{3.1(a)}} {{3.1(c)}} |
| Water in depot tanks | Takes water dips in its storage tanks and takes remedial steps where needed | {{3.1(b)}} |
| Density at the filling point | Checks appearance, colour and density every two hours and at each tank change. Writes the density at 15°C and the tank number on every lorry's invoice | {{3.1(h)}} |
| Retained samples | Draws a sample each morning and at each tank change, keeps it at least 30 days, numbers it and puts the number in the delivery documents | {{3.1(e)}} |
| Ethanol in petrol | Blends within the prescribed limits and checks that ethanol is present before release. The dealer must see that the right quality of blended product is sold | {{3.1(f)}} |
| Kerosene | Releases it only after visual, appearance, colour, density and flash point checks, and dyes PDS kerosene blue. See [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers) | {{3.1(d)}} {{3.1(g)}} |
| Lorry papers | Fills only lorries with a valid calibration certificate and explosives licence | {{3.1(i)}} |
| Seal numbers | Seals or locks the lorry and records the seal or lock numbers on the invoice | {{3.2}} |
| GPS and VTS | Keeps GPS running. Does not fill a lorry whose VTS is missing or not working as prescribed. Time and route deviation alerts are visible to the dealer too | {{3.2}} |
| Sample kit | Supplies sample containers, numbered seals, sealing wire, pliers and labels free of cost. The dealer asks for them | {{2.6(B)}} {{2.8}} |
| Repairs | Attends breakdowns of its own equipment at your outlet | {{4.2(a)}} |
| Training | Keeps making efforts to train dealers and their staff. No number of sessions is set | {{3.3}} |

It also checks lorries for leaks after filling, allows no mixed loads in lorries with a common manifold, and sends PDS kerosene only in separate blue lorries {{3.1(j)}} {{3.1(l)}}. Malpractice found in a lorry is dealt with under the Industry Transport Discipline Guidelines {{3.1(k)}}. What you do when the lorry arrives is on [receiving a tanker](@receiving-a-tanker) and [tank lorry VTS](@tanker-vts). For repairs, see [breakdowns and leaks](@company-breakdowns-leaks).

## Watching the filling and the sample kit

For ex-MI supplies, you or your representative may watch the lorry being filled at the supply location and travel with it to your outlet. The outlet should also follow the lorry's movement {{3.2}}. MDG does not spell out "ex-MI". General knowledge, not MDG text: MI usually means marketing installation, the company's depot. Ask which of your supplies this covers.

What to do with the samples from the kit is on [retention samples](@sampling-retention).

## What MDG does not cover

- **A penalty for the company.** The action chapter sets actions against dealers and kerosene dealers, and none for company lapses {{chapter 8}}.
- **A deadline for the sample kit.** You ask "in time", with no number of days {{2.6(B)}}.
