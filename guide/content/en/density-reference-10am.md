## What the rule says

Two records make up your **reference density**, the figure an inspector measures your fuel against. Both go in the [density register](@density-register).

- **Every morning:** before the first sale, take a sample at the pump nozzle and write down its density at 15°C. The latest time is 10 AM {{1.4.1(c)}} {{5.1.9}}.
- **After every tanker:** run at least 50 litres through the pump to clear the line, then take a nozzle sample and write down the density of the mixed product {{1.2(xiv)}}.

Until a tanker is decanted, the reference is the invoice density. After decanting, it is the mixed-tank reading you wrote down {{2.10}} {{3.1(h)}}.

## Which figure the inspector asks for

The inspector asks for the latest figure that should exist at the time of the visit {{5.1.9}}.

| When the inspector visits | Figure asked for |
|---|---|
| Before 10 AM | The previous morning's reading, or the reading taken after the tanker if one came the previous day |
| After 10 AM, no tanker unloaded yet today | This morning's reading |
| After 10 AM, tanker already unloaded today | The reading taken after that unloading |

If the figure is not there, MDG calls it "non-availability of reference density" {{5.1.9}}. At a mobile lab inspection a missing figure counts the same way, but the lab tests the fuel at the outlet first and the officer recommends the action {{5.1.9}}. See [mobile lab inspections](@sampling-mobile-lab).

## What happens if it is missing

Sales and supplies of every product stop at once. Samples of every product are drawn and sent to a lab {{5.1.9}}. The lab result decides what comes next.

- **The sample passes.** It is a major irregularity. Minimum action: a 15-day suspension of sales and supplies the first time, 30 days the second time, termination of the dealership the third time {{8.3}}.
- **The sample fails.** The action is the one for adulteration, a critical irregularity with termination at the first instance {{5.1.9}} {{8.2}}.

{{penalty-note}}

See [how repeat offences are counted](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid).

> **Note:** the penalty for a missing record applies even when the fuel passes the lab {{5.1.9}}.

## An example

For example (a made-up outlet): a petrol tanker is unloaded at 2 PM on Tuesday. An inspector arrives at 8:15 AM on Wednesday and asks for the after-tanker figure. If that entry was skipped, sales and supplies of every product stop at once {{5.1.9}}.
