## What NANO asks of the dealer

At an automated outlet the key idea is NANO, "No automation, No Operation" {{1.5(viii)}}. The dealer has four jobs:

1. **Keep the automation working** {{1.5(viii)}}.
2. **Report any malfunction straight away** to the oil company's authorised official {{1.5(viii)}}.
3. **Follow the direction that official gives** {{1.5(viii)}}.
4. **Have a generator or UPS**, so a power cut does not stop the automation or the electronic totaliser {{1.5(ix)}}.

> **Note:** MDG does not say in plain words that sales must stop when the automation fails. It says to report at once and follow the official's direction. Ask your oil company, and if the answer is "run it in manual mode", get that in writing first.

## When manual mode or a switched-off gauge becomes an irregularity

Where automation has been completed, three things are irregularities if there is no permission from the competent authority {{5.1.16}}.

| What happens at the outlet | Clause |
|---|---|
| A dispensing unit (MPD) works in manual mode | {{5.1.16(a)}} |
| The tank gauge (ATG) is switched off or not working | {{5.1.16(b)}} |
| Anyone deliberately makes another part of the automation system stop working, fully or partly | {{5.1.16(c)}} |

Only an email or a signed letter from a company official counts as permission, and the dealer keeps a record of it. A phone call or SMS does not {{5.1.16}}. The steps are on [the manual mode page](@automation-manual-mode).

Tampering with the dispensing unit itself, including its chip, electronic parts or software, is a different and critical irregularity {{5.1.4}} {{8.2}}. See [seals and fittings](@quantity-seals).

## What the penalty is

All three automation irregularities are major, with their own ladder {{8.3(vii)}}.

| Instance | Action |
|---|---|
| First | Penalty of Rs 1,00,000 |
| Second | Penalty of Rs 2,00,000 and 7 days' suspension of sales and supplies |
| Third | Termination of the dealership |

{{penalty-note}}

## What MDG leaves open

- **Who the competent authority is.** Clause 5.1.16 says "competent authority" and its note says "Company official". No rank is named. Ask your oil company {{5.1.16}}.
- **How findings made together are counted.** Irregularities found together each count as an instance in their own class {{8.5.3}}, yet the penalty entry lists (a), (b) and (c) as one item {{8.3(vii)}}. Ask your oil company. See [repeat offences](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid).
