## What it is for

It is your own copy of what was in the lorry. If an inspector's nozzle sample later fails, the lab tests it against this one {{2.5(G)}}.

## On every tank lorry, in order

Start after the density and water checks (see [receiving a tanker](@receiving-a-tanker)), once density is within limits {{2.3}}.

1. **Rinse.** Rinse the empty aluminium container with the lorry's own product {{2.3}}.
2. **Draw.** From each compartment run off at least 20 litres, then draw a bottom composite in proportion to what each holds. Fill 2 x 1 litre for MS and/or 1 x 1 litre for HSD {{2.3}}.
3. **Seal and label.** Seal the container and the wooden box, and fill in the Annexure 3 label {{2.3}} {{2.7}}.
4. **Sign.** You or your representative and the driver both sign the label {{2.3}}.
5. **Mark the tank number** on the container if the product has more than one tank {{2.3}} {{2.9.2}}.
6. **Then decant** {{1.2(x)}}.

The label ties the sample to one delivery, recording lorry, invoice, product, outlet, tank, both densities and the seal numbers {{annexure 3}}.

## Which samples to keep

For each MS and HSD tank, keep the samples from its two latest loads:

| Product | Per load {{2.3}} | For the last two loads of one tank {{2.9.2}} |
|---|---|---|
| MS | 2 x 1 litre | 4 x 1 litre |
| HSD | 1 x 1 litre | 2 x 1 litre |

## When not to decant yet

If the driver will not sign, do not decant. Contact the supply location or the sales officer {{2.3 Note (i)}}.

If you notice an unusual time or route deviation by the lorry, tell the supply location or the sales officer and decant only after further instructions {{2.3 Note (ii)}}. See [vehicle tracking](@tanker-vts).

## The transporter's set and the inspector

A transporter may ask for a second signed set against payment and holds it until testing and what follows are finished {{2.3}} {{2.9.4}}.

An inspector takes your sealed box into custody without opening it: see [inspector samples](@sampling-inspector-samples). If the inner container is later found leaking or tampered with, responsibility rests with the dealer or transporter whose sample it is, and the Note below applies {{2.5(C)}}.

> **Note:** if you cannot show your tank lorry samples at an inspection, a sample you produce later is not considered for testing. The nozzle sample is compared with the supply point sample, and any action is against the dealer only {{2.5(E)}}. If your samples were already sent for testing, it is compared with that earlier result {{Chapter 2, General Notes (3)}}.
