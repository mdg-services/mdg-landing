## What MDG says about a government check

Under existing laws, Control Orders, Acts and Rules, authorities of the Central and State Government may check a dealership, besides oil company officers {{8.7}}. If such an authority establishes a malpractice or irregularity, MDG takes it as one under MDG as well, and the oil company acts on the authority's advice {{8.7}}. Any authority the law empowers may draw samples {{2.2.2.2}}.

## Where MDG touches what an officer may check

| Subject | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Seals and delivery | A tampered seal: the Weights & Measures department's opinion is final, and penal action is taken even if delivery is correct or excess. See [seals](@quantity-seals) | {{5.1.2(b)}} |
| Short delivery, seals intact | Delivery short beyond the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 limits stops sales through that dispensing unit or nozzle until it is recalibrated and re-stamped | {{5.1.2(a)}} |
| Recalibration | The dealer or the company's maintenance representative arranges it with the Weights & Measures Inspector | {{4.3(a)}} |
| PESO | The dealer observes its rules | {{1.5(ii)}} |
| Control Order records | Kept, and produced to inspecting officials on demand | {{1.5(v)}} |

A tampered seal is a critical irregularity {{8.2}}: see [penalty classes](@penalties-classes).

## What MDG leaves open

Clause 8.7 does not name these authorities, their powers or how they must conduct a check. It points only to "existing laws, Control Orders, Acts / Rules" {{8.7}}. Clause 5.1.8, on refusing or obstructing an inspection, does not say who carries the inspection out {{5.1.8}}. MDG also does not say whether the 30 days for a show cause notice run from the date of a government check {{8.5.6}}. Ask your oil company, and read the Act or Order the officer cites.

General practice, not an MDG rule: note the officer's name, designation and the law cited, and keep any paper handed over with your other inspection papers.

For example (a made-up outlet): a Weights & Measures officer finds one dispensing unit delivering short beyond the Legal Metrology limits, with the seals intact. Sales through that unit stop until it is recalibrated and re-stamped {{5.1.2(a)}}.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
