## What a customer can check and ask for

MDG lists what the dealer must provide, not customer rights. The table doubles as a dealer checklist.

| What you can check or ask for | What the dealer must do | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Zero before fuel flows | The attendant shows the zero setting before each delivery, and the meter goes back to zero after each one | {{1.4.2(f)}} |
| A zero message on the pump | Every pump unit carries one | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{5.1.17}} |
| The closing reading | The final meter reading is shown once the delivery ends | {{circular}} |
| Filter paper to test petrol | Whatman 2 paper is kept and given to a customer who asks. See [the filter paper test](@density-filter-paper) | {{1.4.1(a)}} |
| The complaint book | Kept, and handed over whenever a customer asks | {{1.6(iv)}} {{5.1.17}} |
| Price, density, opening stock, contacts | Displayed. See [the display boards page](@service-display-boards) | {{1.6(viii)}} {{5.1.15}} {{5.1.17}} |

The contact numbers are on the board at the outlet, so none are printed here. The 20.03.2025 circular is a joint format for all three oil companies. It lists 20 minimum service obligations and sets no penalty of its own {{circular}}. See [how they are checked](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations).

## Zero before delivery, step by step

1. After the last delivery, the meter goes back to zero {{1.4.2(f)}}.
2. Before the next delivery starts, the attendant shows the customer the zero setting {{1.4.2(f)}}.
3. The fuel is delivered.
4. Once it ends, the closing meter reading is shown {{circular}}.

## What a miss costs the dealer

| If this is missing | How MDG treats it | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| The zero message on a pump | Minor irregularity | {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Zero or the closing reading not shown | Customer service standard: minor-irregularity action | {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}} |
| The complaint book, or not handing it over | Minor irregularity | {{8.4(iii)(c)}} |

The action for a minor irregularity is a warning-cum-guidance letter at the first instance, Rs 10,000 per irregularity at the second, and Rs 25,000 from the third on {{8.4}}.

{{penalty-note}}

MDG's lists do not name a missing filter paper; unlisted matters get action after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}. See [how repeat offences are counted](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid).

Cheating on quantity is a different class. Tampered seals, a tampered totalizer and unauthorised fittings are critical, with termination at the first instance once established {{8.2}} {{8.5.2}}. See [W&M and totalizer seals](@quantity-seals).
