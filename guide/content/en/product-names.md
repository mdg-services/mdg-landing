## The names, one line each

| Name | What it is | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| MS | Motor Spirit, petrol. MDG's test table says Motor Spirit and Motor Gasoline are the same product | {{introduction}} {{7.6}} |
| Petrol grades | The table lists MG, E5, E10, E12, E15 and E20, and MG 91, 95 and 98 with minimum octane numbers of 91, 95 and 98. General knowledge, not MDG text: the number after E is the ethanol share in percent | {{7.6}} {{annexure 5}} |
| HSD | High Speed Diesel. The table also calls it automotive diesel fuel | {{introduction}} {{7.6}} |
| Diesel grades | BS VI up to B7, and biodiesel blends B8–B20. General knowledge, not MDG text: the number after B is the biodiesel share in percent | {{7.6}} |
| Branded fuel | Fuel sold under an oil company's brand. Selling normal MS or HSD as branded, on purpose, is major. A branded sample must still meet at least the IS minimum | {{5.1.10}} {{8.3}} {{annexure 5}} |
| XP95, Speed, Power | General knowledge, not MDG text: oil company brand names for premium fuel. MDG names none of them, so ask your oil company which grade a brand is | None |
| SKO | Superior Kerosene Oil, supplied under the Public Distribution System. See [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers) | {{introduction}} {{chapter 6}} |
| LDO | Appears only in "SKO-LDO dealerships". Light diesel oil (trade reading) | {{5}} {{8.10}} |

## Density at 15°C

| Fuel | kg/m³ | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Petrol (MS), every BS VI grade MDG names | 720–775 | {{7.6}} |
| Diesel (HSD), BS VI up to B7 | 810–845 | {{7.6}} |
| Diesel blended with biodiesel, B8–B20 | 820–860 | {{7.6}} |

These are the lab ranges, not your daily test: see [the density range](@density-range) and [what density is](@density-board).

## Staff names and short forms

| Name | What it is | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| DSM | The circular lists CA/DSM/CSA among the staff who man the dispensing units, without spelling them out. MDG 5.1.17 speaks of "Driveway Salesmen". DSM is read as driveway salesman (trade reading) | {{circular}} {{5.1.17}} |
| CA, CSA | Not spelled out in the circular. Ask your oil company | {{circular}} |

OMC, RO, DU, NANO and the rest are in the [glossary](@glossary).
