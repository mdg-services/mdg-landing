## When MDG brings termination

The notice, the reply and, for critical irregularities, a hearing come first: see [the show cause timeline](@inspection-show-cause-notice). For a petrol pump dealer, MDG itself names termination in three places:

- A critical irregularity, at the first instance {{8.2}}.
- A major irregularity, at the third instance, or the second for selling normal fuel as branded fuel {{8.3}}.
- A fine left unpaid through the extended suspension {{8.7}}.

See [the penalty pages](@penalties).

{{penalty-note}}

## Who approves the action

| Action | Who approves | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Termination, and every critical irregularity | The Regional, State or Zonal head of the oil company, General Manager level or above | {{8.8(i)}} |
| Termination of an SC/ST dealership | The Director (Marketing) at Head Office | {{8.8(i)}} |
| A major irregularity | IndianOil: the State Retail Head. BPCL and HPCL: an authorised officer of Deputy General Manager rank or above, at State, Region or Zonal level | {{8.8(ii)}} |
| A minor irregularity | The head of the Territory, Regional or Divisional office | {{8.8(iii)}} |

## The appeal

The dealer appeals within 30 days of receiving the termination order. The appeal is addressed to the Appellate Authority and sent through the divisional, territory or regional office {{8.9}}.

| Case | Appellate Authority | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Not SC/ST; approved below ED rank in the State Office | The ED (Retail) at Head Office, or another ED-level officer there whom the company nominates | {{8.9}} |
| Not SC/ST; approved by an ED-rank officer in the State Office | A committee of 2 EDs whom the company nominates | {{8.9}} |
| An SC/ST dealership | A committee of two Directors other than the Director (Marketing) | {{8.9}} |

For example, an order received on 10 November 2026 leaves until 10 December 2026 to file. MDG does not say how days are counted.

## What MDG 2024 leaves open

- It gives no appeal form and does not list the papers to attach.
- Clause 8.9 speaks of termination only. It sets out no separate appeal for a suspension or a fine {{8.9}}.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
