## Who this covers

Oil companies appoint SKO dealers, also called wholesale dealers. State Governments appoint the ration shop retailers. The dealer lifts the kerosene at the oil company's depot; in a few places, such as hilly areas, it is delivered {{chapter 6}}. The company's side is on [oil company duties](@oil-company-duties).

## The seven duties

| Duty | Clause |
|---|---|
| Sell blue-dyed kerosene only to the people it is meant for | {{chapter 6, item 1}} |
| Charge no more than the price fixed by the Government or the oil company | {{chapter 6, item 4}} |
| Do not refuse a consumer on a working day in working hours while you have stock, unless the Government or the oil company directs otherwise | {{chapter 6, item 2}} |
| Do not close the premises or storage in working hours without the oil company's prior written permission | {{chapter 6, item 3}} |
| Take all reasonable steps to keep adequate stock at all times | {{chapter 6, item 5}} |
| Display a stock-cum-price board at the business and the storage | {{chapter 6, item 6}} |
| Keep a daily account of purchase, sale and stock | {{chapter 6, item 7}} |

## Irregularities and what follows

| Class | Lapses | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Short delivery with Weights & Measures seals tampered; tampered totaliser seals; unauthorised buying, selling or exchange of kerosene or a substitute; a gap between physical and book stock beyond permissible limits (see below) | Termination at the first instance, if proven |
| Major | Overcharging; not following Government regulations or company guidelines on selling kerosene, if any | Allocation cut by 50% for 3 calendar months, after State Government approval |
| Minor | Short delivery with Weights & Measures seals intact | A warning letter |

{{penalty-note}}

Before acting, the oil company consults the local Civil Supplies Authorities. If they do not agree to a suspension or a cut, a strong warning letter goes instead {{8.10}}. Clause 8.10 carries other provisions over "as applicable"; MDG does not say how 8.5 and 8.9 apply to kerosene dealers. The 8.9 appeal is for termination only {{8.9}}. Ask your oil company.

### If stock does not match the book

When an officer authorised only for search and seizure under the Kerosene Control Order, 1993 establishes a variation beyond permissible limits {{8.10}}:

1. Sales and supplies stop at once, and the dealer is asked to explain.
2. If the explanation is satisfactory, they resume.
3. If not, the action for unauthorised purchase or sale applies, which is critical.

## What MDG leaves open

- **A class for other lapses.** 8.10 names none for a closed godown or a missing stock-cum-price board {{8.10}}.
- **A kerosene stock limit.** No figure is given. The limits in 5.1.11 are for MS and HSD {{5.1.11}}. See [stock variation](@stock-variation).
- **Stopping sales versus consulting Civil Supplies.** A stock variation stops sales at once, yet action needs that consultation. MDG does not say how the two fit {{8.10}}.
