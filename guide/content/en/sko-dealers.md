## Who this covers

MDG 2024 is written for retail outlet (RO) and kerosene dealerships {{cover page}}. Its introduction counts about 6,400 SKO dealerships on 01.04.2024 {{introduction}}. Chapter 6 describes the system {{chapter 6}}:

- SKO dealers, also called wholesale dealers, are appointed by the oil companies after the Dealer Selection Process.
- The retailers, the ration shops, are appointed by the State Governments.
- The oil company provides PDS kerosene ex its depots or installations, and the dealers uplift it.
- From the depot onward, the dealer is responsible for transport, under the guidance and supervision of the State Government.
- In a few places, such as hilly areas, the product is delivered to the dealer.

Clause 8.10 speaks of "SKO-LDO dealerships". MDG does not spell out LDO. General knowledge, not MDG text: LDO usually means light diesel oil. Chapter 6 lists duties for SKO dealers only.

## The seven duties

| No. | The dealer must | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sell, distribute or supply blue-dyed kerosene to the persons it is meant for | {{chapter 6, item 1}} |
| 2 | Not refuse to sell, distribute or supply to any consumer on any working day during working hours, if he has stock, unless the Government or the oil company directs otherwise | {{chapter 6, item 2}} |
| 3 | Not keep the business premises, including the storage, closed during working hours on a working day without the oil company's prior written permission | {{chapter 6, item 3}} |
| 4 | Not sell above the price fixed by the Government or the oil company | {{chapter 6, item 4}} |
| 5 | Take all reasonable steps to keep adequate stock at the premises and the storage at all times | {{chapter 6, item 5}} |
| 6 | Display a stock-cum-price board prominently, at a conspicuous place at the business and the storage | {{chapter 6, item 6}} |
| 7 | Keep a proper account of daily purchase, sale and stock, at the premises or the tank lorry, every day | {{chapter 6, item 7}} |

Oil company officials inspect these dealerships. Irregularities lead to action under 8.10 {{chapter 6}}.

## What the oil company must do for kerosene

Chapter 3 sets the company's side. See [oil company duties](@oil-company-duties) for the full list. For kerosene:

- Release it only after visual, appearance, colour, density and flash point checks as per the quality manual {{3.1(d)}}.
- Dye PDS kerosene blue at the prescribed dosage {{3.1(g)}}.
- Carry PDS kerosene in a separate tank lorry. Never use a lorry that carries MS or HSD. The lorry is painted blue {{3.1(l)}}.

## Irregularities and penalties

| Class | What counts | Penalty | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Short delivery with Weights & Measures seals tampered. Totalizer seals found tampered. Unauthorised purchase, sale or exchange of kerosene or a product that can substitute for it. Stock variation between physical and book stock beyond permissible limits | Termination of the dealership at the first instance, for proven critical irregularities | {{8.10}} |
| Major | Overcharging. Not following Government regulations and the company's operating guidelines or instructions, if any, on the sale and distribution of kerosene | Allocation reduced by 50% for 3 calendar months, after due approval from the State Government | {{8.10}} |
| Minor | Short delivery with Weights & Measures seals intact | Warning letter | {{8.10}} |

### Stock variation

MDG sets out a procedure for a stock variation beyond permissible limits that is established by an officer authorised for only search and seizure under the Kerosene Control Order, 1993. Then {{8.10}}:

1. Sales and supplies are suspended immediately.
2. The dealer's explanation is called for.
3. If the explanation is satisfactory, sales and supplies resume.
4. If it is not, the action is the one for unauthorised purchase or sale, which is listed as critical.

Clause 8.10 gives no percentage. The limits in 5.1.11 are for MS and HSD {{5.1.11}}. See [stock variation](@stock-variation) for outlets.

### A worked example

This is an illustration, not a real case.

A dealer's monthly allocation is 20 kilolitres. Overcharging is established against the dealer. The major-irregularity penalty cuts the allocation by 50% for 3 calendar months, so the dealer gets 10 kilolitres a month for those 3 months. That holds once the State Government has approved and the local Civil Supplies Authorities, who must be consulted, have agreed. If they do not agree, a strong warning letter goes to the dealer instead {{8.10}}.

## The civil supplies check

Any action against a kerosene dealership is taken only in consultation with the local Civil Supplies Authorities. The reason MDG gives is that the company's action should not disrupt the supply of PDS kerosene to the common people. If the Civil Supplies Authorities do not agree to an action that may involve suspending supplies or reducing the allocation, a strong warning letter goes to the dealer instead {{8.10}}.

## What MDG leaves open

- **Which duty falls in which class.** The seven duties and the irregularity lists are not matched one to one. Overcharging is named as major, and duty 4 is about price. For the others, such as a closed godown or a missing stock-cum-price board, 8.10 names no class {{8.10}}. The second major item covers Government regulations and the company's operating guidelines or instructions, if any, on sale and distribution of kerosene. MDG does not say whether the Chapter 6 duties count as those. For irregularities not specifically covered, 8.5.4 says the oil company's competent authority takes suitable action after enquiry, in line with natural justice {{8.5.4}}. Whether it applies to kerosene dealers is covered by the point below.
- **The permissible limit for kerosene stock.** No figure is given.
- **Which petrol pump provisions apply.** 8.10 says "as applicable" and lists none {{8.10}}. Rules for the show cause notice, counting instances over five years and the appeal against termination are written in 8.5 and 8.9. MDG does not say how they apply to a kerosene dealership. Ask your oil company.
- **The immediate suspension and the civil supplies check.** Stock variation says sales and supplies are suspended immediately. The paragraph on civil supplies says action is taken only in consultation with those authorities. MDG does not say how the two fit together.

This page is general information. For a specific notice or order, speak to your oil company or a lawyer.
