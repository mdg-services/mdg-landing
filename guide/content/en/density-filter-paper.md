## What MDG requires of the dealer

On filter paper, MDG asks for three things {{1.4.1(a)}}:

- **Hold the paper.** Keep Whatman 2 filter paper for checking the quality of MS, which is petrol.
- **Hand it over when asked.** A customer who wants to check the petrol can ask for it.
- **Show a message.** Put it on the Customer Education board or poster, in a prominent place. The oil company provides the posters {{1.6(viii)}}. See [the boards MDG requires](@service-display-boards).

The clause names MS only. See [the density rules](@density). To check quantity rather than quality, see the [5-litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test).

## What a failed test sets off

- **Samples are drawn.** Oil companies are told to draw samples after a filter paper failure {{2.2.2.2}}.
- **Sales and supplies stop.** The oil company officer stops sales and supplies of every product, and the samples go to the lab as a priority {{2.5(K)}}.
- **The lab result decides.** A lab finding of adulteration is a critical irregularity, with termination at the first instance {{5.1.1}} {{8.2}}. See [failed samples](@sampling-failed).

## What MDG does not say

- **The method.** MDG gives no steps, waiting time or description of a pass. Ask your oil company for its method.
- **The weight of the test.** MDG groups it with the indicative tests, and a joint industry team can take samples whatever such a test shows, even a clean paper {{2.4.3}}. Action follows the lab results {{2.5(K)}}.
- **A missing paper.** It is not listed as an irregularity. For unlisted matters the oil company acts after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}. Ask your oil company how it treats it.
