## What MDG requires of the dealer

MDG 2024 asks for three things about filter paper {{1.4.1(a)}}.

- **Keep the paper.** Keep filter paper (Whatman 2) to check the quality of MS, which is petrol.
- **Give it on demand.** Any customer who wants to check the petrol for themselves can ask for it.
- **Put up a message.** A suitable message about it goes on the Customer Education board or poster, displayed prominently at the outlet. The oil company supplies the posters {{1.6(viii)}}.

The clause names MS only. It does not set out a filter paper test for diesel. The other quality clauses next to it cover all products: the daily water dip and the morning density record {{1.4.1(b)}} {{1.4.1(c)}}. See the [density rules](@density).

## What a failed filter paper test sets off

- **Samples are drawn.** Besides random sampling, oil companies are told to draw samples after a density failure or a filter paper test failure {{2.2.2.2}} {{2.4}}. The samples come from the nozzles of all tanks of both MS and HSD {{2.2.2.2}}.
- **Sales and supplies stop.** The oil company officer suspends sales and supplies of all products, after informing the territory, divisional or regional manager. Samples are drawn and sent to the lab on priority {{2.5(K)}}.
- **The lab result decides.** MDG says the laid-down procedure is followed and action is taken on the test results {{2.5(K)}}. MDG's definition of adulteration covers a product that no longer meets the BIS specification because a foreign substance was introduced. It also covers a sample whose results do not fall within the permissible limits of the test method when compared with the reference sample {{5.1.1}}. If the lab result shows adulteration, that is a critical irregularity, with termination at the first instance {{8.2}}.

A clean filter paper does not rule out sampling. A joint industry team can take samples whatever an indicative test such as the filter paper, density or stock check showed {{2.4.3}}.

This is general information. For a notice in your own case, speak to your oil company and to a lawyer.

## What MDG does not say

MDG does not say how to carry out the filter paper test, how long to wait, or what a pass looks like.

Chapters 5 and 8 do not list a missing filter paper as a separate irregularity. For matters that are not listed, the oil company takes suitable action after enquiry, in line with natural justice {{8.5.4}}. Ask your oil company how it treats a missing paper.

The 20.03.2025 Minimum Service Obligations circular lists the availability of a "Q&Q kit" (item 14) {{circular}}. It does not say what is in it. Ask your oil company whether the filter paper is part of it. See the [20-point minimum service obligations](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations).

## How the test is commonly done

General practice, not MDG text. An IndianOil display board describes the test as follows.

- Put two drops of petrol from the dispensing pump on the filter paper.
- Petrol evaporates quickly. A paper that is clean after the drop has dried is the normal result.
- A stain left on the paper points to a possible adulteration.
- If the spot stays pinkish, that is the colour of the petrol, not a stain.
- If in doubt, repeat the test and tell the oil company.

The boards do not agree on timing. The English IndianOil board says the drop should dry in about two minutes. The Hindi board says a stain shows within five minutes if the petrol is adulterated. MDG gives no time, so use the one on your oil company's board.

The paper is an indicator only. MDG calls the filter paper, density and stock checks indicative tests {{2.4.3}}, and action follows the lab results {{2.5(K)}}.

## Customers and the paper

Because MDG says "on demand", staff should be able to find the paper at once. Practical point, not an MDG rule: keep unused paper where the pump staff can reach it without calling the manager.

The same IndianOil display-board list has a "Check and Fill" board that invites customers to test the fuel with the filter paper, a density check and a 5-litre measure. It also has a check record board where the person who tested the fuel can note the type of check: density, 5-litre measure or filter paper. These are IndianOil boards, not MDG clauses. See [every board MDG requires](@service-display-boards).
