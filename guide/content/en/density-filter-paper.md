## What MDG requires of the dealer

The Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (MDG 2024) are the oil companies' rules for their dealers under the dealership agreement. On filter paper they ask the dealer for three things {{1.4.1(a)}}.

- **Hold the paper.** Keep Whatman 2 filter paper for checking the quality of MS, which is petrol.
- **Hand it over when asked.** A customer who wants to check the petrol for themselves can ask for it.
- **Show a message.** A suitable message on this goes on the Customer Education board or poster, in a prominent place at the outlet. The oil company provides the posters {{1.6(viii)}}.

The clause names MS only. It does not set out a filter paper test for diesel. The quality clauses next to it name no particular product: the daily water dip and the morning density record {{1.4.1(b)}} {{1.4.1(c)}}. See [the density rules](@density).

## What a failed filter paper test sets off

- **Samples are drawn.** On top of random sampling, oil companies are told to draw samples after a density failure or a filter paper test failure {{2.2.2.2}} {{2.4}}. They draw from the nozzles of every MS and HSD tank {{2.2.2.2}}.
- **Sales and supplies stop.** The oil company officer stops sales and supplies of every product after informing the territory, divisional or regional manager, and the samples go to the lab as a priority {{2.5(K)}}.
- **The lab result decides.** MDG says the laid-down procedure is followed and action rests on the test results {{2.5(K)}}. Its definition of adulteration covers a product that no longer meets the BIS specification because a foreign substance was introduced. It also covers a sample whose results, set against the reference sample, fall outside the permissible limits of the test method {{5.1.1}}. If the lab finds adulteration, that is a critical irregularity, with termination at the first instance {{8.2}}.

Qualifiers on the action: MDG's amounts are the minimum and the oil company may take higher action, up to termination {{8.5.1}}. No action can follow until the irregularity has been established {{8.5.2}}.

A clean filter paper does not rule out sampling. A joint industry team can take samples whatever an indicative test, such as the filter paper, density or stock check, showed {{2.4.3}}.

## What MDG does not say

MDG does not say how to carry out the filter paper test, how long to wait, or what a pass looks like. Ask your oil company for its method and follow that. The paper is only an indicator: MDG calls the filter paper, density and stock checks indicative tests {{2.4.3}}, and action follows the lab results {{2.5(K)}}.

Chapters 5 and 8 do not list a missing filter paper as an irregularity of its own. For matters that are not listed, the oil company takes suitable action after an enquiry, in line with natural justice {{8.5.4}}. Ask your oil company how it treats a missing paper.

The 20.03.2025 minimum service obligations circular, a common format for the dealers of all three oil companies, lists having a "Q&Q kit" available (item 14) {{circular}}. It does not list what the kit holds. Ask your oil company whether the filter paper is part of it. See the [20-point minimum service obligations](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations).

## Customers and the paper

Because MDG says the paper goes to a customer who asks, staff should be able to find it at once. Practical point, not an MDG rule: keep unused paper where the pump staff can reach it without calling the manager.

MDG also asks for other boards and displays at the outlet. The oil company may give you a list of them. Confirm the current list with it. See [the boards MDG requires](@service-display-boards).
