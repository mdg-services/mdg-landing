## The same range in every state

MDG 2024 prints no range for any state {{7.6}}. A pump in Assam, Punjab, Rajasthan, Haryana or Bangalore works to the same limits. What changes from place to place is the reading, not the limit.

| Where or when | What changes | Range that applies |
|---|---|---|
| Any state, any season | Nothing in the printed range | Petrol 720–775; diesel 810–845; B8–B20 blends 820–860 {{7.6}} |
| A hot afternoon, for example on the plains of Rajasthan, Punjab or Haryana | The hydrometer reads lower. Convert to 15°C first {{1.2(iv)}} | The same |
| A cold morning | The hydrometer reads higher | The same |
| Diesel processed from Assam crude | A Government of India notice may guide any relaxation of the diesel range, and its time frame {{7.6}} | Ask your oil company |

General knowledge, not MDG text: warm fuel reads a lower density and cold fuel a higher one. For example (a made-up diesel, worked out with the [15°C calculator](@tools-density-at-15)): a diesel of 829.0 kg/m³ at 15°C reads 836.0 on a 5°C morning and 811.4 on a 40°C afternoon. Both convert back to 829.0. The fuel did not change. See [why density changes with temperature](@density-temperature).

## What the Assam crude footnote says

The footnote sits in the diesel table, against the 810–845 figure {{7.6}}. It appears again in the Annexure 5 test table and in the diesel mobile lab report {{annexure 5}} {{annexure 13a}}. It says any density range relaxation and time frame for fuel processed from Assam crude "may be guided by the notifications issued by Government of India" {{7.6}}.

What it does and does not say:

- **Diesel only.** It is on the 810–845 figure. It is not on petrol and not on the 820–860 blend figure.
- **"If any".** It does not promise a relaxation.
- **Fuel, not pumps.** The words are about fuel processed from Assam crude, not about every pump in a state.
- **No figure.** MDG prints no relaxed number, no end date and no list of places.

For the range in force, ask your oil company. General practice, not an MDG rule: keep its answer with the date. The tanker check against the invoice, within ±3.0 kg/m³, has no state exception {{1.2(iv)}}. The other ranges are on the [density range page](@density-range).
