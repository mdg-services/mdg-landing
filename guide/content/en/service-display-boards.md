## Boards and notices MDG names

Rows without a remark are minor irregularities if missing {{8.4(iii)}}. For a notice MDG does not name, the oil company acts after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}; “not named” does not mean “not required”.

| Notice | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Opening stock of the day | Displayed; after each decanting the stock register or stock display board is brought up to date | {{1.3(i)}} {{1.2(xiii)}} {{5.1.17}} |
| Selling price of every product | The retail selling price of each product sold | {{1.3(i)}} {{5.1.15}} {{circular}} |
| Density | Must be displayed; no format given | {{5.1.17}} |
| Zero sticker | A message on each pump unit that the meter goes back to zero before each delivery | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{5.1.17}} |
| Product name on each nozzle | On each nozzle of an MPD (read in the trade as a multi-product dispenser; MDG does not spell it out) | {{5.1.17}} |
| Contacts | The dealer's and the oil company's names and phone numbers to call about a complaint, grievance or emergency, shown prominently | {{5.1.17}} {{1.6(viii)}} {{circular}} |
| Complaint-book message | Shown prominently; not named in 8.4. See [complaint book](@service-complaint-book) | {{1.6(iv)}} |
| Working hours, holiday notices, safety signs | Hours shown prominently, holiday notices well in time, signs such as No Smoking and switch off engine. Hours and signs are minor through the circular; holiday notices are named in neither the circular nor 8.4 | {{1.6(v)}} {{circular}} {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}} |
| Customer education poster and Customer Service Cell messages | The poster carries a message that filter paper (to check petrol) is available on request; the oil company provides the rest. Not named in 8.4 | {{1.4.1(a)}} {{1.6(viii)}} |
| Toilet protocol and maintenance sheet | Protocol near the toilet, sheet kept and displayed, “Darpan” QR code. Not named by item in 8.4; a failing toilet is minor {{8.4(iii)(g)}}. See [toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules) | {{5.1.14(b)}} |

Your oil company may give a longer list and its own formats: ask it for the stock, rate and toilet-sheet formats. Kerosene dealers have their own rule: a stock-cum-price board at the place of business and storage {{chapter 6, item 6}}. See [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers).

## What a missing notice costs

For a notice that counts as minor: a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity from the third time {{8.4}}.
