## What MDG 2024 asks you to display

Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (MDG 2024) is the set of rules the oil companies apply to their petrol pump dealers under the dealership agreement. It binds the dealer through the dealership agreement.

MDG 2024 asks the dealer to follow the statutory rules on petroleum sales, including display of the daily stock and the price board {{1.5(i)}}. The table below lists each notice MDG names, where it comes from, and how a missing one is classed.

| Notice | What MDG says | Clause | If missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening stock of the day | Displayed by the dealer. After each decanting, the stock register or stock display board is brought up to date | {{1.3(i)}} {{1.2(xiii)}} {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Selling price of every product | The retail selling price of each product sold is displayed | {{1.3(i)}} {{5.1.15}} {{circular}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(a)}} |
| Density | Not displaying it is listed as an irregularity. The clause gives no format | {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Zero sticker | A message on each pump unit that the meter goes back to zero before each delivery | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Product name on each nozzle | On each nozzle of an MPD (read in the trade as a multi-product dispenser; MDG does not spell it out) | {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Contacts for complaint, grievance or emergency | The people to call. Also the dealer's own name and phone number, and the names and phone numbers of the oil company people, shown prominently | {{5.1.17}} {{1.6(viii)}} {{circular}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Complaint-book message | A message that the book is available, shown prominently | {{1.6(iv)}} | Not named in 8.4 |
| Working hours and holiday notices | Working hours shown prominently. Holiday notices put up well in time | {{1.6(v)}} {{circular}} | Working hours: minor, through the circular {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}} |
| Customer education board or poster | Shown prominently, with a message that filter paper is available to customers on request | {{1.4.1(a)}} | Not named in 8.4 |
| Customer Service Cell messages and Customer Education posters | The ones the oil company provides from time to time | {{1.6(viii)}} | Not named in 8.4 |
| Toilet protocol and maintenance sheet | Protocol displayed near the toilet. Maintenance sheet kept and displayed. “Darpan” QR code near the toilet | {{5.1.14(b)}} | Not named by item in 8.4. A toilet that fails the checks is minor {{8.4(iii)(g)}}. See [toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules) |
| Safety signs | Proper safety signs, such as No Smoking and switch off engine | {{circular}} | Minor, through the circular {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}} |

For an irregularity MDG does not specifically name, the oil company acts after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}. Do not read “not named” as “not required”.

### Where MDG says each one goes

- **On each pump unit:** the zero message {{1.4.2(f)}}.
- **On each nozzle:** the product name {{5.1.17}}.
- **Near the toilet:** the toilet protocol and the QR code {{5.1.14(b)}}.
- **Prominently:** the complaint-book message, working hours, the dealer's and oil company's contact details, and the customer education board {{1.6(iv)}} {{1.6(v)}} {{1.6(viii)}} {{1.4.1(a)}}.
- **No place stated:** density, opening stock and prices. Ask your oil company which board format it wants.

Kerosene dealers have their own rule: a stock-cum-price board, displayed prominently at the place of business and storage {{chapter 6, item 6}}. See [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers).

## Other boards your oil company may ask for

MDG names the notices above. An oil company may also give its dealers a list of further boards, posters and formats, and clause 1.6(viii) already says the dealer displays the Customer Service Cell messages and Customer Education posters the company provides {{1.6(viii)}}.

This guide does not publish any oil company's list, because lists change and differ between companies. Ask your oil company for its current list, and for the format it wants on the stock board, the rate board and the toilet sheet.

## What a missing board costs

A missing price, density, stock, pump-sticker, nozzle-name or complaint-contact notice is a minor irregularity. The action is a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity from the third time {{8.4}}.

Read the amounts with these points:

- They are the minimum. The oil company may take higher action, up to termination {{8.5.1}}.
- The irregularity must be established before any action is taken {{8.5.2}}.
- Instances are counted over five years, and irregularities under the previous guideline within those five years count too {{8.5.5}}.
- A notice that 8.4 does not name, such as the complaint-book message, is handled after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}.
- Clause 8.4 says irregularities under lines (a) to (h) of 8.4(iii) count as one irregularity for taking action {{8.4}}. Clause 8.5.3 says that when two or more irregularities are found at the same visit, each is counted as an instance in its own class, and action is taken on each with a compounding effect {{8.5.3}}. The text does not say how the two fit together. Ask your oil company how it counts a visit with more than one finding.

Illustration, for a made-up outlet: an inspector finds the density board blank and the price board missing at the same visit. Under 8.4 both are lines of 8.4(iii), which count as one irregularity for taking action. Under 8.5.3, two findings at one visit are each counted as an instance, with a compounding effect. MDG does not say which reading the oil company follows, so ask it.
