## What MDG 2024 requires you to display

MDG 2024 asks the dealer to follow the statutory rules on petroleum sales, including display of the daily stock and the price board {{1.5(i)}}. The table below lists each notice MDG names, where it comes from, and what a missing one is classed as.

| Notice | What MDG says | Clause | If missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening stock of the day | Displayed by the dealer. After each decanting the stock register or stock display board is updated | {{1.3(i)}} {{1.2(xiii)}} {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Selling price of every product | The authorised retail selling price is displayed for each product sold | {{1.3(i)}} {{5.1.15}} {{circular}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(a)}} |
| Density | Non-display is listed as an irregularity. The clause does not give a format | {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Zero sticker | A message on each pump unit that the meter returns to zero before each delivery | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Product name on each nozzle | On each nozzle of an MPD (read in the trade as a multi-product dispenser; MDG does not spell it out) | {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Contacts for complaint, grievance or emergency | The authorised persons to call. Also the dealer's own name and phone number, and the oil company people's names and phone numbers, displayed prominently | {{5.1.17}} {{1.6(viii)}} {{circular}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Complaint-book message | A message that the book is available, displayed prominently | {{1.6(iv)}} | Not listed by name in 8.4 |
| Working hours and holiday notices | Working hours displayed prominently. Holiday notices displayed well in time | {{1.6(v)}} {{circular}} | Working hours: minor, through the circular {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}} |
| Customer education board or poster | Displayed prominently, with a message that filter paper is available to customers on demand | {{1.4.1(a)}} | Not listed by name in 8.4 |
| Customer Service Cell messages and Customer Education posters | Those provided by the oil company from time to time | {{1.6(viii)}} | Not listed by name in 8.4 |
| Toilet protocol and maintenance sheet | Protocol displayed near the toilet. Maintenance sheet maintained and displayed. “Darpan” QR code near the toilet | {{5.1.14(b)}} | Not named by item in 8.4. A toilet that fails the checks is minor {{8.4(iii)(g)}}. See [toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules) |
| Safety signs | Proper safety signs, such as No Smoking and switch off engine | {{circular}} | Minor, through the circular {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}} |

For an irregularity MDG does not specifically name, the oil company acts after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}. Do not read “not listed” as “not required”.

### Where MDG says each one goes

- **On each pump unit:** the zero message {{1.4.2(f)}}.
- **On each nozzle:** the product name {{5.1.17}}.
- **Near the toilet:** the toilet protocol and the QR code {{5.1.14(b)}}.
- **Prominently:** the complaint-book message, working hours, the dealer's and oil company's contact details, and the customer education board {{1.6(iv)}} {{1.6(v)}} {{1.6(viii)}} {{1.4.1(a)}}.
- **No place stated:** density, opening stock and prices. Ask your oil company which board format it wants.

Kerosene dealers have their own rule: a stock-cum-price board, displayed prominently at the place of business and storage {{chapter 6, item 6}}. See [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers).

## Boards in an oil company's display list

IndianOil keeps a list of boards that should be available at an outlet. The list below comes from that document. It is **not MDG text**. Other oil companies may use different boards and wording, so ask yours for its current list.

| Board in the list | What it carries |
|---|---|
| Stock board | Stock, density and rate for petrol, diesel and oil, with the explosives licence number, tax ID and working hours. Dated and signed by the proprietor or manager. The sample is headed for MDG 2012 |
| Rate display board | Today's petrol and diesel price per litre, with the date and dealer code, and ways to check the price by SMS, the company's app or its website |
| Customer rights board | Reminds customers to check zero before filling, ask for a cash memo, check quality and quantity, and use the air, water, first aid, toilet and complaint book. Shows who to contact |
| “Check & Fill” board (English and Hindi) | How a customer can do the filter paper test, the density check and the 5-litre quantity check, and where to report a doubt. The Hindi version also lists signs that kerosene may have been mixed into petrol |
| Toilet maintenance sheet | A monthly sheet with a daily tick for each toilet check |
| Toilet protocol | The daily toilet checks as a notice |
| Check record display | A board for the person who tested the fuel to write their name, address, phone, vehicle number and the test done |
| Tank truck decantation do's and don'ts | Precautions while unloading a tanker |
| Ethanol blending message | A notice that petrol contains ethanol and what that can mean for a vehicle's tank if water gets in |
| Safety do's and don'ts | Separate lists for the underground tank area, the pump island and driveway, and the meter and electrical rooms |
| Explosive rules | The licence conditions for storing and dispensing petroleum |
| Toilet hygiene (Swachhta) poster | Asks customers to give feedback on the toilet by SMS, mobile app or the company website |
| Signs | Gents and ladies washroom, air, drinking water, no smoking, keep phone silent, suggestion box, entry and exit, CCTV and similar |

On the toilet poster: MDG 2024 itself names a “Darpan” QR code for toilet feedback {{5.1.14(b)}}. Ask your oil company which feedback arrangement it wants shown.

## What a missing board costs

A missing notice that 8.4(iii) names (prices, density, opening stock, zero sticker, nozzle names, complaint contacts) is a minor irregularity. The first instance gets a warning-cum-guidance letter, the second Rs 10,000 per irregularity and the third onwards Rs 25,000 per irregularity {{8.4}}. Irregularities under lines (a) to (h) of 8.4(iii) count as one irregularity for taking action {{8.4}}. A notice that is not named there, such as the complaint-book message, is handled after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}.

For example, an inspector finds the density board blank and the price board missing on the same visit. Both fall under 8.4(iii), so they are one irregularity for action, and a first-time outlet gets one warning-cum-guidance letter {{8.4}}.
