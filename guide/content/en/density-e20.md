## E20 beside the other petrol grades

MDG 2024 names six BS VI petrol grades: MG, E5, E10, E12, E15 and E20 {{7.6}}. General knowledge, not MDG text: the number is the ethanol share by volume, and E20 goes up to 20 per cent.

| Point | MG, E5, E10, E12, E15 | E20 |
|---|---|---|
| Density at 15°C | 720–775 kg/m³ | 720–775 kg/m³ |
| Standard it is tested to | IS 2796 or IS 17586 | IS 17021 |
| Ethanol content method | IS 2796 (E5, E10), IS 17586 (E12, E15) | IS 17021 |

Clauses: {{7.6}} for the first two rows, {{annexure 7}} for the third. Standards are revised, so check the current version of each, or ask your oil company.

MDG has no separate E20 density limit. Your daily work stays the same: the tanker's 15°C reading within ±3.0 kg/m³ of the invoice {{1.2(iv)}}, and the morning reading in the register {{1.4.1(c)}}. See the [density range page](@density-range) and the [invoice check](@density-invoice-check).

## Water: where E20 needs more care

General knowledge, not MDG text: ethanol mixes with water, so ethanol-blended petrol takes up water more readily than plain petrol, and the water can later separate out. This is what MDG asks of the dealer:

- **Every tanker compartment:** test for water with a paste suitable for ethanol blends {{1.2(vii)}}. See [water finding paste](@tanker-water-finding-paste).
- **Every day:** record the water dip, and report any water at once {{1.4.1(b)}}.
- **After water is removed from an ethanol-blended tank:** the ethanol content is checked before release. If it is outside the desired range, sales stop and the controlling office is told {{4.3(c)}}. MDG does not give the range or say who checks. See [water in the tank](@company-water-in-tank).
- **The vent pipe:** the company fitter's maintenance report has a line on the quality of the silica gel in the vent pipe of an ethanol-blended petrol tank {{annexure 6}}.

The oil company must blend ethanol within the prescribed limits and check its presence before release. The dealer must see that the right quality of blended product is sold {{3.1(f)}}.
