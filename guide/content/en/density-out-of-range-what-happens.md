## What happens, moment by moment

| Moment | What is compared | What MDG says |
|---|---|---|
| Tanker arrives, before decanting | Each compartment's 15°C reading against the invoice density | Within ±3.0 kg/m³ {{1.2(iv)}}. If you doubt the quality, contact the supply location or the Area Field Officer {{1.2(ix)}} |
| An inspector's density test fails | The officer's reading against the reference density in your register {{2.10}} {{annexure 4}} | An oil company officer suspends sales and supplies of all products; samples go for testing on priority {{2.5(K)}} |
| Mobile lab visit | The officer's reading against your tank density after the last receipt | A printed maximum variation of 3.0 kg/m³ {{annexure 12a}} {{annexure 13a}}. Failing density or any clinical test is acted on as established adulteration, with sales and supplies suspended {{7.4 Note (i)}} {{7.4 Note (ii)}} |

A missing reference density has its own rule: [reference density](@density-reference-10am) {{5.1.9}}.

Established adulteration is critical, with termination at the first instance {{8.2}}.

{{penalty-note}}

See [when a sample fails](@sampling-failed) and the [mobile lab page](@sampling-mobile-lab).

## The lab's ranges

At 15°C: petrol 720–775 kg/m³, diesel up to B7 810–845, and B8 to B20 blends 820–860 {{7.6}}. The [density range page](@density-range) has the footnotes.

## What MDG does not say

- **A tolerance for your own morning reading.** It asks only that you record it {{1.4.1(c)}}.
- **What counts as a "density failure" at an inspection.** Apart from the mobile lab form, MDG prints no figure for it.
- **Whether a variation above 3.0 is a failure by itself, or what to do with a tanker outside ±3.0 beyond calling.** Ask your oil company.

## An example

For example (a made-up outlet): the invoice shows 743.0 kg/m³ and the 15°C reading is 747.0, which is 4.0 away. The manager doubts the quality and calls the supply location {{1.2(ix)}}. A mobile lab later reads 746.0 against the tank figure of 744.5 recorded after the last receipt: a variation of 1.5, inside the 3.0 the form prints {{annexure 12a}}.
