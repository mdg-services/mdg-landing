## Who can sample when the quick tests pass

A failed density test, a failed filter paper test, stock variation past the limit, a special drive or a specific complaint each call for samples. These come in addition to random sampling, so a pass does not rule sampling out {{2.2.2.2}} {{2.4}}.

| Who | Can sample even when the quick tests pass? | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Company team, random check | Yes. It is over and above the MDG samples, and covers at least 2% of the outlet network every month | {{2.4}} |
| Joint industry team | Yes. It may sample irrespective of density, stock variation or filter paper results | {{2.4.3}} |
| Mobile lab | Yes. Its checks are surprise checks, and clinical tests run irrespective of the density result | {{7.1}} {{7.4 Note (i)}} |
| Any other authority the law empowers | Named among those who may draw samples | {{2.2.2.2}} |

MDG does not say how an outlet is picked for a random check, or that the team must give a reason.

## What the dealer gets

- Payment for the samples, with a cash memo {{2.2.2.2}}.
- Samples sealed and labelled as per Annexure 4 {{2.4}} {{annexure 4}}. A joint industry team's samples are also signed by the dealer or his representative and the team {{2.4.3}}.
- One of the sets drawn stays with the dealer {{2.4.1}} {{2.4.2}}.
- The lab result, pass or fail, preferably within 30 days of drawal; if all the samples pass, the dealer is told in writing {{2.5(F)}}.
- After a mobile lab check: if the product meets the specification, the balance samples come back to the outlet {{7.6}}.

For example (a made-up outlet): the density reading and the filter paper test both pass. The team still draws nozzle samples from each tank, pays for them, obtains a cash memo and leaves the dealer his set {{2.2.2.2}} {{2.4.1}}.
