## What MDG 2024 expects about staff

| Duty | What MDG says | Clause | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wages and benefits | Meet every statutory duty owed to employees at the outlet, including minimum wages and benefits such as PMSBY and PMJJBY | {{1.5(x)}} {{5.1.18}} | No separate line in 8.2 to 8.4 |
| Uniform and badges | Driveway salesmen in uniform and wearing badges | {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(e)}} |

Courtesy {{1.6(iii)}}, manned pumps and a marshalled queue are customer-service points {{circular}}: see [what a petrol pump must provide](@customer-service).

## Wages and benefits

MDG states no amount and no formula. It names the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) and the Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) among the benefits, with no premium, enrolment steps or rule on who pays: use the schemes' official sources. For the minimum wage, ask your State labour authority or a labour-law adviser.

MDG 2024 has no wage penalty line, and does not say what the oil company does about unpaid wages. The wage note sits under 5.1.18, and the text does not say whether that clause's minor-penalty line covers it {{5.1.18}}; for matters MDG does not name, the oil company acts after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}. Ask your oil company, and take legal advice on your own case.

## Uniform and badges

Driveway salesmen at the outlet must be in uniform and wear badges {{5.1.17}}. A lapse is a minor irregularity: a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity from the third time {{8.4(iii)(e)}} {{8.4}}.

{{penalty-note}}

MDG does not describe the uniform or the badge. Ask your oil company if it has a standard. See [penalties](@penalties).

## How MDG 2012 differed

MDG 2012 (Ver. 7, 30.11.2022) is an older guideline, replaced on 24.10.2024; it is not the rule today.

| | MDG 2012 (replaced) | MDG 2024 (current) |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum wage | Higher of the oil company's notified wage and the State's minimum wage {{2012:1.5(x)}} | No formula given |
| PMSBY and PMJJBY | All employees to be covered {{2012:5.1.18}} | Named among the benefits |
| Wage penalty | Major: 20% of monthly dealer margin (average of the last 3 months) first time, 30% second time, 40% plus 15 days' suspension third time onwards {{2012:8.3(ix)}} | No wage line in 8.2, 8.3 or 8.4 |

MDG 2024 does not repeat the 2012 lines on e-payment of salaries and wage records {{2012:5.1.18}}.

> **Note:** the wage penalty ladder is gone from the MDG 2024 lists. The duty is not {{1.5(x)}} {{5.1.18}}.
