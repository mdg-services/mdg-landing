## What MDG 2024 expects of the dealer about staff

| Duty | What MDG says | Clause | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wages and benefits | Comply with all applicable statutory obligations, including minimum wages and other benefits (including PMSBY and PMJJBY) for employees at the outlet | {{1.5(x)}} {{5.1.18}} | No separate line in 8.2 to 8.4 |
| Uniform and badges | Driveway salesmen in uniform and wearing badges | {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4(iii)(e)}} |
| Courtesy | Prompt service and courteous behaviour at all times | {{1.6(iii)}} {{circular}} | Minor, as a customer service shortfall {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}} |
| Pumps manned | Every dispensing unit manned during working hours | {{circular}} | Minor, as above |
| Queue | Queue marshalled and regulated on the forecourt | {{circular}} | Minor, as above |
| Zero shown | The pump attendant shows the zero setting before each delivery | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{circular}} | Minor, as a customer service shortfall {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}} |
| Trained staff | Staff trained to handle and operate the safety equipment | {{1.6(xi)}} | Not named in 8.4 |

The circular calls the attendants CA, DSM and CSA. MDG does not spell these out. See the [glossary](@glossary).

MDG 2024 also says oil companies should keep working to train dealers and their staff, including on customer service and courteous behaviour {{3.3}}.

## Minimum wages

MDG 2024 requires the dealer to pay minimum wages and other benefits as part of all applicable statutory obligations {{1.5(x)}}. A note under 5.1.18 repeats it {{5.1.18}}.

It does not state an amount or a formula. The figure comes from outside MDG. The older MDG 2012 tied it to the higher of the wage notified by the oil companies and the State Government's statutory minimum wage {{2012:1.5(x)}}. MDG 2024 does not repeat that sentence.

Confirm the figure for your State and for each type of staff with your State labour authority or a labour-law adviser.

## PMSBY and PMJJBY

MDG 2012 printed the full names: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) and Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) {{2012:5.1.18}}.

MDG 2024 names them among the benefits the dealer must provide to employees {{1.5(x)}} {{5.1.18}}. It gives no premium, no enrolment steps and no rule on who pays. Use the schemes' official sources for those.

## How MDG 2012 differed

MDG 2012 (Ver. 7, 30.11.2022) is replaced. It is shown only for comparison.

| | MDG 2012, Ver. 7 (replaced) | MDG 2024 (current) |
|---|---|---|
| Wage clause | A separate clause, 5.1.18, “Payment of wages” {{2012:5.1.18}} | Clause 1.5(x) and a note under 5.1.18 {{1.5(x)}} {{5.1.18}} |
| Minimum wage | Higher of the oil company's notified wage and the State Government's minimum wage {{2012:1.5(x)}} | “Payment of minimum wages”, no formula given |
| Other benefits | PF, ESI, bonus, annual leave and gratuity as notified by oil companies or the law {{2012:5.1.18}} | “Other benefits” within all applicable statutory obligations |
| E-payment of salaries | Required {{2012:5.1.18}} | Not repeated |
| PMSBY and PMJJBY | All employees to be covered {{2012:5.1.18}} | Named among the benefits |
| Wage records | Kept at the outlet for inspection at all times {{2012:5.1.18}} | Not named in the list of records in 5.1.12, which ends with any other record the oil company specifies in writing {{5.1.12}} |
| Penalty | Major irregularity: 20% of monthly dealer margin (average of last 3 months) first time, 30% second, and 40% plus 15 days' suspension third time onwards {{2012:8.3}} | No wage line in 8.2, 8.3 or 8.4 |

> **Note:** the penalty ladder is gone from the MDG 2024 lists. The duty is not. Clause 1.5(x) and the note under 5.1.18 still require minimum wages and benefits {{1.5(x)}} {{5.1.18}}.

MDG 2024 does not say what the oil company does about unpaid wages. The wage note sits under clause 5.1.18, whose deficiency line points to minor penalties {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}}, but the note is written as a separate statement and the text does not say whether that line covers it. Two general rules in chapter 8 may matter: actions in MDG are the minimum and the oil company may take higher action {{8.5.1}}, and for irregularities not specifically covered the oil company acts after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}. This guide cannot say whether or how a company applies them to wages. Ask your oil company, and take legal advice on your own case.

## Uniform and badges

Driveway salesmen at the outlet must be in uniform and wear badges {{5.1.17}}. Failing this is a minor irregularity: a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity the third time onwards {{8.4(iii)(e)}} {{8.4}}.

MDG does not describe the uniform or the badge. Ask your oil company if it has a standard.

Irregularities under lines (a) to (h) of 8.4(iii) count as one irregularity for taking action, so a missing badge and a missing board found together are one {{8.4}}. See [penalties](@penalties).

## Manners, manned pumps and training

The circular of 20.03.2025 checks courteous behaviour, manned pumps and a regulated queue as three of its 20 points. The inspector marks each YES or NO {{circular}}. See [all 20 points](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations) and [what a petrol pump must provide](@customer-service).

Staff also need to know how to use the outlet's safety equipment. The dealer is to be trained in operating it, and trained staff should be available {{1.6(xi)}}.
