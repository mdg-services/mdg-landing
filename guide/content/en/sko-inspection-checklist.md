## The checklist

| # | What Chapter 6 asks | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Only blue-dyed kerosene, sold to the persons it is meant for | {{chapter 6, item 1}} |
| 2 | No refusal of a consumer on a working day in working hours while you hold stock, unless the Government or the oil company directs otherwise | {{chapter 6, item 2}} |
| 3 | No closing of the premises or storage in working hours without the oil company's prior written permission | {{chapter 6, item 3}} |
| 4 | No price above the one fixed by the Government or the oil company | {{chapter 6, item 4}} |
| 5 | All reasonable steps to keep adequate stock at the premises and storage at all times | {{chapter 6, item 5}} |
| 6 | A stock-cum-price board, prominent and conspicuous, at the business and the storage | {{chapter 6, item 6}} |
| 7 | A proper account of daily purchase, sale and stock, at the premises or on the tank lorry | {{chapter 6, item 7}} |

MDG does not list what an officer checks. General practice, not an MDG rule: keep the account written up, any written permission for a closure, and the board where the officer can see them.

## What an inspection can lead to

Clause 8.10 sorts a lapse as critical, major or minor. Critical: termination at the first instance, if proven. Major, such as overcharging: allocation cut by 50% for 3 calendar months, after State Government approval. Minor, such as short delivery with seals intact: a warning letter {{8.10}}.

{{penalty-note}}

Any action follows consultation with the local Civil Supplies Authorities. If they do not agree to it, a strong warning letter goes instead {{8.10}}. Clause 8.10 carries other provisions over to kerosene dealers only "as applicable" and does not say how 8.5 applies {{8.10}}. Which lapse sits in which class, and the stock-gap steps, are on [kerosene dealer rules](@sko-dealers).

## What MDG does not say

- **A limit for stock gaps.** 8.10 says "beyond permissible limits" and gives no figure. The limits in 5.1.11 are for MS and HSD {{8.10}} {{5.1.11}}.
- **A class for the other duties.** 8.10 names none for a refusal, a closure, a missing board or a missing account, and does not say whether its "non-observance" line covers them. Ask your oil company.
