## What MDG asks for

| Facility | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Air | Provided to customers during the outlet's operating hours | {{1.6(ii)}} |
| Air gauge | The air facility has a calibrated air gauge | {{5.1.14(a)}} |
| Manned air | Item 6 of the circular, a joint format of all three oil companies: the air facility is manned. The inspector marks it YES or NO | {{circular}} |
| Telephone | Telephone facilities are available in the premises | {{1.6(vii)}} {{5.1.14(a)}} |

The first aid box sits in the same clause: see [the first aid box](@service-first-aid-box). All the customer facilities are on [what a pump must provide](@customer-service).

## What a lapse costs

No air facility or telephone is a minor irregularity {{8.4(ii)}}. A shortfall in a circular point, such as manned air, draws the action for customer service standards or the respective clause {{5.1.18}} {{circular}}. Both are minor {{8.4}}.

The action is a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity from the third time {{8.4}}.

{{penalty-note}}

The circular sets no penalty of its own {{circular}}. See [penalty classes](@penalties-classes).

## What MDG does not say

- **The gauge.** No calibration interval, and no word on who calibrates it. Ask your oil company.
- **The telephone.** No standard: not the kind of telephone, who may use it, or whether a charge is allowed. Ask your oil company.
- **Charging for air.** Not addressed.
- **Manning.** The circular does not say who mans the air point.

General practice, not an MDG rule: write the date the gauge was last calibrated where staff can show it to an inspector.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
