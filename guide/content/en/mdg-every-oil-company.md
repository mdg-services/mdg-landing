## One document for all three

The cover of the Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (MDG 2024) says it is for retail outlet and kerosene dealerships of public sector marketing companies {{cover page}}. There is no separate text per company. The text itself shows it:

- The form a dealer signs on receiving MDG 2024 offers the three company names as alternatives, so one form fits all {{handing-over form}}.
- The sample labels for supply-location, tanker and outlet samples leave a place for whichever company's logo applies {{annexure 2}} {{annexure 3}} {{annexure 4}}.
- A mobile lab works for the whole industry and carries the logo of all three companies. A company can also run its own {{7.2}}.
- The three classes of irregularity and their minimum actions are written once, for every dealer {{8.1}} {{8.2}} {{8.3}} {{8.4}}.

## What your own company decides

MDG leaves some matters to the oil company. For these, confirm with your company, not with this guide.

| Matter | Who decides | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Approval of action on a major irregularity | IndianOil: the State Retail Head. BPCL and HPCL: an authorised officer not below Deputy General Manager | {{8.8}} |
| Customer-service standards | Circulars the company issues from time to time | {{1.6(xii)}} {{5.1.18}} |
| Housekeeping standards, posters and messages to display | The company | {{1.6(viii)}} {{1.6(x)}} |
| Records beyond the ones MDG lists | Any record the company specifies in writing | {{5.1.12}} |
| Shrinkage allowance or TVA in stock variation | Counted only where and when it applies, so ask whether your location is one | {{5.1.11}} |

## The 20.03.2025 circular

It is one common format for all three companies. Each issues the 20 service points to its dealers on its own letterhead, and the dealer signs a handing-over letter for it. It sets no penalty of its own {{circular}}. See [the 20 service obligations](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations).

## Other companies, and later changes

The cover is addressed to public sector marketing companies {{cover page}}. If your outlet belongs to any other company, ask that company which document applies to you. Circulars can follow MDG 2024, so read your company's current letters. Where to read the text is on the [PDF and updates page](@official-pdf-and-updates).

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
