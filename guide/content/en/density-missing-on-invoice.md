## What the invoice should carry

The supply location gives the density at 15°C, the filling point, the storage tank number and the release time on the invoice {{2.2.1(iv)}}. The density it observed goes on every lorry's invoice with the tank number {{3.1(h)}}. On arrival you match the invoice details, the recorded density among them, with the lorry {{1.1(v)}}. For the full receipt check, see [receiving a tanker](@receiving-a-tanker).

## Before you unload

MDG prints no steps for a missing figure. These are the clauses that bear on it, in the order a dealer meets them:

1. **Spot the gap at the receipt check** {{1.1(v)}}.
2. **Know the benchmark.** Your 15°C reading is checked against the invoice density, within ±3.0 kg/m³ {{1.2(iv)}}: see [the invoice density check](@density-invoice-check). Until decanting, the reference density is the figure in the dispatch document {{2.10}}.
3. **If you doubt the quality, contact the supply location or the Area Field Officer** {{1.2(ix)}}.
4. **General practice, not an MDG rule:** ask the supply location to confirm the density and tank number in writing before you unload, and note who confirmed and when.
5. **Take your own readings as usual.** Each compartment's density and temperature are recorded {{1.2(viii)}}.

After decanting, your own reading becomes the reference. Take it once at least 50 litres have gone through the pump {{1.2(xiv)}} {{2.10}}. See [the reference density rule](@density-reference-10am).

## What MDG does not say

- **Which figure to use** when the invoice has no density, since it names no substitute.
- **Whether you may unload** while you wait for confirmation, or should hold the lorry back.
- **What happens when the tank number is missing.** Ask your oil company about all three.

## An example

For example (a made-up outlet): a tanker arrives with an invoice that shows the tank number and release time but no density. The manager asks the supply location for the 15°C density in writing, a practice step MDG does not set, notes who gave it and when, and takes each compartment's reading {{1.2(viii)}}.
