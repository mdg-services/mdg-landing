## The table to lay out

| Lay out | What MDG expects | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| The file | Inspection reports, letters from the oil company, test reports and invoice copies, ready for reference at all times | {{1.5(iv)}} |
| Records | The daily sales register, density register, pump maintenance records, inspection record, purchase invoices, sales bills, licences, and any record the company asked for in writing. How long each is kept: [records to keep](@inspection-records-to-keep) | {{5.1.12}} |
| Test kit | Calibrated hydrometer and thermometer, the ASTM table and other kits, in working order: [the density kit](@density-kit) | {{1.5(vi)}} |
| 5-litre measure | Verified by the Weights & Measures Department, used only for pump testing | {{1.4.2(b)}} |
| Tank lorry samples | The last two loads of each tank, sealed and labelled; where a product has more than one tank, the tank number is on the container | {{2.3}} {{2.9.2}} |
| Displays | Opening stock of the day, selling prices, density, and contact details for a complaint or emergency; a zero sticker and the product name on each nozzle | {{1.3(i)}} {{5.1.15}} {{5.1.17}} |
| Complaint book | At hand, to give a customer who asks | {{5.1.17}} |
| Toilet | The protocol displayed near the toilet, and the maintenance sheet kept and displayed | {{5.1.14(b)}} |
| Facilities | Air with a calibrated gauge, a telephone, and a first aid box with medicines not past expiry | {{5.1.14(a)}} |

## Why the evening

General practice, not an MDG rule: do this check each evening, not only before a visit you expect, because MDG calls mobile lab checks surprise checks {{7.1}}.

For example (a made-up outlet): on the evening round, the manager finds a strip of tablets in the first aid box past its expiry date and replaces it before closing {{5.1.14(a)}}.
