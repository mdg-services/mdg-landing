## Which fines the 30-day rule covers

The rule applies to some major and minor irregularities where the action is a fine alone, with no suspension of sales and supplies. MDG does not list them {{8.7}}. On a plain reading of the ladders, the fine-only steps are the minor penalties of Rs 10,000 and Rs 25,000 per irregularity {{8.4}} and the first automation penalty of Rs 1,00,000 {{8.3}}.

{{penalty-note}}

The second automation step carries 7 days' suspension, so it does not fit the description {{8.3}}. MDG does not say whether recovery of the differential price for normal fuel sold as branded fuel is covered. Ask your oil company.

## A timeline with dates

Illustration: the company's notice of a fine to a made-up outlet is dated 1 June 2026, and the fine is not paid. MDG does not say whether the 30 days count the notice day. This table takes it as day 0, so check the exact last day against your own notice.

| Day | Date | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 June 2026 | Notice of the fine |
| 1 to 30 | 2 June to 1 July | The fine falls due in this period. No suspension {{8.7}}. |
| 31 to 45 | 2 to 16 July | If unpaid, sales and supplies are suspended for 15 days. The fine is still to be paid in this period {{8.7}} |
| 46 to 60 | 17 to 31 July | If still unpaid, the suspension is extended by another 15 days {{8.7}} |
| After day 60 | After 31 July | If still unpaid, the dealership is terminated {{8.7}} |
