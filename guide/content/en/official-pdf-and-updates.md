## Read the MDG text

Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (MDG 2024) is the set of rules the oil companies lay down for their dealers under the dealership agreement, and the oil companies publish it themselves. We link the Bharat Petroleum copy. We do not host or copy the document.

- [MDG 2024 on Bharat Petroleum's website](https://www.bharatpetroleum.in/images/files/MDG-2024-Effective-24-10-2024.pdf)

IndianOil also publishes it, on iocl.com. We do not link to that site, so search it for "Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024".

Hindustan Petroleum dealers work from the same document: the acknowledgement form at its end names all three companies. We have not linked a Hindustan Petroleum copy. Ask your company or look on its website.

Check that you have the right edition. The cover reads "MDG 2024 (Effective 24.10.2024)" and each page footer reads "Ver. 1 / 24.10.2024" {{cover page}}. If a link stops working, search the company's site for the document's title. That is general practice, not an MDG rule.

## Version and effective date

| | MDG 2024 | MDG 2012 |
|---|---|---|
| Effective | 24.10.2024 | 8 January 2013, last amended on 30.11.2022 |
| Version stamp | Ver. 1 / 24.10.2024 | Ver. 7 / 30.11.2022 |
| Status | In force | Replaced by MDG 2024 |

The last page of MDG 2024 is a handing-over form. The dealer signs it to acknowledge receiving the copy, and it speaks of an "MDG - 2024 amendment". For what changed from the old edition, see [MDG 2012 vs MDG 2024](@mdg-2012-vs-2024).

## Circulars and updates we know of

MDG 2024 leaves some detail to the oil companies. The customer service standard follows their circulars {{1.6(xii)}}, and the record-keeping duty extends to any record the company asks for in writing {{5.1.12}}. So the PDF alone may not be the whole picture.

| Date | Source | What it is | Guide page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24.10.2024 | MDG 2024 itself | MDG 2024 (Ver. 1) takes effect {{cover page}} | [MDG 2024 guide](@hub) |
| 20.03.2025 | A circular in a common format for the dealers of all three oil companies; its model letter goes out on each company's letterhead | Customer Service Standards: 20 Minimum Service Obligations under clause 5.1.18 {{circular}} | [The 20 points](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations) |

### The circular of 20.03.2025

- **What it does:** it sets out 20 points that the dealer must ensure at the outlet under clause 5.1.18, ranging from having every product available to digital payment and displaying prices {{circular}} {{5.1.18}}.
- **What it does not do:** it sets no penalty of its own. A deviation is dealt with under 5.1.18, which points to the action for minor irregularities, or under whichever other clause fits {{circular}} {{5.1.18}}.
- **How it is checked:** the inspecting authority checks that each point is in place and practised, and notes YES or NO against it {{circular}}.
- **What reaches the dealer:** the circular says the protocol is to be passed to every dealership, with the acknowledgement kept on record. It includes a model letter for the oil company's letterhead and a format for the dealer to acknowledge receipt {{circular}}.

These two entries are all we have logged. Oil companies can also write to their dealers directly, and such letters may not be public. Check with your own oil company which ones apply to you.

## How to keep up with circulars we do not have

General practice, not an MDG rule:

1. Ask your sales officer or field officer, at each visit, for any circular issued under MDG 2024 since the last one you hold.
2. Put every circular, and the acknowledgement you signed, in the outlet's file. MDG 2024 already asks the dealer to keep company correspondence, inspection reports, test reports and invoices at the outlet for ready reference {{1.5(iv)}}.
3. Check your oil company's website for MDG notices.
4. If a circular is dated after the "checked" date on a page of this guide, read the circular first and confirm with your oil company how it applies to your outlet.

## MDG 2012 was replaced

MDG 2012 took effect on 8 January 2013 and was last amended on 30.11.2022 (Ver. 7) {{2012:cover page}}. MDG 2024 took over from it on 24.10.2024 {{cover page}}. Older articles and forwards may still quote 2012 figures, such as the old toilet penalty. See the [side-by-side comparison](@mdg-2012-vs-2024) before you rely on a figure you read elsewhere.
