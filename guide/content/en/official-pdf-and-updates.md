## Read the official text

MDG 2024 is published by the oil companies themselves. We link the Bharat Petroleum copy. We do not host or copy the document.

- [MDG 2024 on Bharat Petroleum's website](https://www.bharatpetroleum.in/images/files/MDG-2024-Effective-24-10-2024.pdf)

IndianOil also publishes it, on iocl.com. We do not link to that site, so search it for "Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024".

Hindustan Petroleum dealers follow the same document: the handing-over form at its end names all three companies. We have not linked a Hindustan Petroleum copy. Ask your company or look on its website.

Check you have the right edition. The cover reads "MDG 2024 (Effective 24.10.2024)" and the footer of each page reads "Ver. 1 / 24.10.2024" {{cover page}}. If a link stops working, search the company's site for "Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024". That is general practice, not an MDG rule.

## Version and effective date

| | MDG 2024 | MDG 2012 |
|---|---|---|
| Effective | 24.10.2024 | 8 January 2013, last amended on 30.11.2022 |
| Version stamp | Ver. 1 / 24.10.2024 | Ver. 7 / 30.11.2022 |
| Status | In force | Replaced by MDG 2024 |

The form at the back of MDG 2024 is headed "Format for Handing over MDG 2024". The dealer signs it to acknowledge receipt of the copy. It calls the document the "MDG-2024 amendment". For what changed from the old edition, see [MDG 2012 vs MDG 2024](@mdg-2012-vs-2024).

## Circulars and updates we know of

MDG 2024 leaves some detail to the oil companies. The customer service standard follows OMC circulars issued from time to time {{1.6(xii)}}, and records the company specifies in writing must be kept {{5.1.12}}. So the PDF is not the whole picture.

| Date | Source | What it is | Guide page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24.10.2024 | MDG 2024 itself | MDG 2024 (Ver. 1) takes effect {{cover page}} | [MDG 2024 guide](@hub) |
| 20.03.2025 | A circular for dealers of all three companies; its model letter is to go out on each company's letterhead | Customer Service Standards: 20 Minimum Service Obligations under clause 5.1.18 {{circular}} | [The 20 points](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations) |
| 16.12.2025 | IndianOil, Bihar State Office (a state-level letter, not a circular for all dealers) | Toilet cleanliness and upkeep under clause 5.1.14(b) | [Toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules) |

### The circular of 20.03.2025

- **What it does:** it turns clause 5.1.18 into a protocol of 20 points the dealer must ensure, from having every product available to digital payment and displaying prices {{circular}} {{5.1.18}}.
- **How it is checked:** the inspecting authority verifies that every point is available and practised. Each is recorded as YES or NO. A deviation leads to action under 5.1.18 or the clause that applies {{circular}}. A deficiency attracts the action prescribed for minor irregularities {{5.1.18}}.
- **What the dealer gets:** the circular says the protocol is to be communicated to every dealership, with the acknowledgement kept on record. It gives a model letter for the oil company's letterhead and a format for the dealer to acknowledge receipt to the controlling office {{circular}}.

### The Bihar State Office letter of 16.12.2025

IndianOil's Bihar State Office wrote to its dealers about toilet cleanliness and upkeep. It repeated the daily checks in clause 5.1.14(b) {{5.1.14(b)}}, asked for corrective steps within 15 days of the letter, and announced a focused toilet inspection drive from January 2026, with MDG action under Chapter 8 for non-compliance. Dealers in other states should ask their own company whether a similar letter applies to them.

These three entries are all we have logged. A state office can write to its own dealers, as the Bihar letter shows, so your oil company may have issued others.

## How to keep up with circulars we do not have

General practice, not an MDG rule:

1. Ask your sales officer or field officer, at each visit, for any circular issued under MDG 2024 since the last one you hold.
2. Put every circular, and the acknowledgement you signed, in the outlet's file. MDG 2024 already asks the dealer to keep company correspondence, inspection reports, test reports and invoices available at the outlet for ready reference {{1.5(iv)}}.
3. Check your oil company's website for MDG notices.
4. If a circular is dated after the "checked" date on a page of this guide, read the circular first and confirm with your oil company how it applies to your outlet.

## MDG 2012 was replaced

MDG 2012 took effect on 8 January 2013 and was last amended on 30.11.2022 (Ver. 7) {{2012:cover page}}. MDG 2024 took over from it on 24.10.2024 {{cover page}}. Older articles and forwards may still quote the 2012 figures, for example the old toilet penalty. See the [side-by-side comparison](@mdg-2012-vs-2024) before you rely on a figure you read elsewhere.
