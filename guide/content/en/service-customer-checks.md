Four checks a customer meets at a pump. MDG lists what the dealer must provide, not customer rights, so each row says what MDG gives and what it does not.

## What a customer can ask for

| Check | What MDG gives a customer | What is normal |
|---|---|---|
| **Density** | No test on demand. The pump displays density {{5.1.17}}; the dealer's records and instruments are for inspecting officials {{1.5(v)}} {{1.5(vi)}} | Lab ranges at 15°C: petrol 720–775, diesel 810–845 (820–860 for B8–B20 blends) kg/m³ {{7.6}}. See [what density is](@density-board) |
| **Quantity** | Zero before each delivery {{1.4.2(f)}}, the closing reading after it {{circular}}. The 5-litre measure is the dealer's testing tool {{1.4.2(b)}}; MDG does not say a customer may ask for the test | MDG prints a band of ±10 to ±25 ml per 5 litres; the exact limit is in Legal Metrology law. Beyond the limit, sales through that nozzle stop {{5.1.2(a)}}. See the [5-litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test) and the [checker](@tools-5-litre-test) |
| **Filter paper** (petrol) | Kept, and handed to a customer who asks {{1.4.1(a)}} | MDG gives no method. An officer's failed test stops sales and supplies of every product while samples are tested {{2.5(K)}}. See the [filter paper test](@density-filter-paper) |
| **Complaint book** | Handed over on request {{1.6(iv)}} | MDG does not say what the dealer does with an entry. See the [complaint book](@service-complaint-book) |

Some oil company customer posters invite a comparison with the dealer's density record, or a delivery check with the 5-litre measure. That is the poster's offer, not an MDG clause.

## If something looks wrong

1. Ask to see zero before fuel flows, and the closing reading after.
2. Read the density board. Ask which reading it shows and when it was taken.
3. For petrol, ask for the filter paper.
4. Write in the complaint book, or call the oil company person whose name and number must be displayed {{1.6(viii)}} {{5.1.17}}. This guide prints no numbers.
5. A specific complaint is one trigger for the oil company to draw samples {{2.2.2.2}} {{2.4}}. See [who can inspect a pump](@inspection-who).

The dealer's side is on [what a pump must provide](@customer-service).
