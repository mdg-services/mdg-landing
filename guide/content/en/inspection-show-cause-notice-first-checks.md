## Four checks on the paper

| Check | What MDG 2024 says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Date of the notice | Within 30 days of the inspection. If petrol or diesel samples were drawn, within 30 days of the test results. | {{8.5.6}} |
| Findings and papers | All the irregularities are listed. Every report and document it rests on should come with it. | {{8.5.6}} |
| Date of issue | The 10 days to reply count from the date of issue, not from receipt | {{8.5.7}} |
| Instance | The five years before the date an irregularity is established decide whether it is the first, second or third, and the previous guideline counts. See [how repeats are counted](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid). | {{8.5.5}} |

MDG asks you to hold the inspection and test reports on file at the outlet {{1.5(iv)}}. General practice, not an MDG rule: keep them beside the notice to compare dates, and write on the notice the day it reached you.

## What comes after your reply

The [timeline page](@inspection-show-cause-notice) has the sequence. MDG sets four later points:

- **A speaking order**, preferably within 45 days of the company receiving your reply. It sets out the irregularities, your reply and why the reply is or is not accepted {{8.5.8}}.
- **A personal hearing** before termination is recommended or approved, for a critical irregularity. A missed date is followed by one more {{8.6}}.
- **A re-test request**, if a sample failed: within 10 days of receiving the lab report, not from the notice {{2.5(H)}}. See [failed samples](@sampling-failed).
- **An appeal**, for termination only, within 30 days of receiving the order {{8.9}}. See [the appeal](@inspection-appeal).

## What MDG 2024 does not say

- It gives no format for a notice or a reply, and does not say who signs the notice.
- It does not say how days are counted, or whether the 10 days can be extended.
- It does not say what follows if a notice is late.

## An example

For example, an inspection on 12 October 2026 draws petrol samples, and the lab result is dated 27 October. The 30 days run from the result, to 26 November, so a notice issued on 20 November is inside them {{8.5.6}}. If the day of issue is counted as day 0, the reply is due by 30 November {{8.5.7}}.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
