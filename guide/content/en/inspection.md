## Who can inspect your pump

- **Company officers and joint industry teams.** Sales officers, inspecting teams, senior officers and joint industry teams {{2.4}} {{2.4.3}}.
- **Mobile labs.** They draw nozzle samples and test them at the outlet {{2.4.4}}. See [the mobile lab visit](@sampling-mobile-lab).
- **Authorised agencies, and any authority the law empowers** {{2.2.2.2}}.

Central and State Government authorities can also check a dealership under other laws. What they establish counts as an irregularity under MDG {{8.7}}.

MDG fixes no number of visits per outlet. Random sampling by company officers and mobile labs must reach at least 2% of the whole outlet network every month {{2.4}}. Extra samples follow a density or filter paper failure, stock variation beyond the limit, a special drive or a complaint {{2.2.2.2}}.

## What a visit can cover

MDG gives inspectors no fixed script. The table groups the areas its rules reach, by topic, with the class MDG gives a failure.

| Topic | What is looked at | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Density | The density figure that should exist at that hour. [The 10 AM rule](@density-reference-10am) {{5.1.9}} | Major if the figure is missing {{8.3}} |
| Product quality | Nozzle samples from every petrol and diesel tank. [Failed samples](@sampling-failed) {{2.2.2.2}} | Adulteration is critical {{8.2}} |
| Quantity | Whether each nozzle delivers within the permitted limit per 5 litres. [The 5-litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test) {{5.1.2}} | Minor if it delivers short and the W&M seals are intact {{8.4}} |
| Seals | Meter and totaliser seals, added fittings. [Seals](@quantity-seals) {{5.1.2}} {{5.1.3}} {{5.1.4}} | Critical {{8.2}} |
| Stock | Stock against the permitted variation. [Stock variation](@stock-variation) {{5.1.11}} | Major if beyond the limit and the sample passes {{8.3}} |
| Records | Registers, bills, licences; test kit ready on request. [Records](@inspection-records-to-keep) {{5.1.12}} {{1.5(vi)}} | Major if missing since the last inspection, minor if only older ones {{8.3}} {{8.4}} |
| Sources and storage | Buying only from your own oil company, no unauthorised storage. [Which class](@penalties-classes) {{5.1.5}} {{5.1.6}} | Critical {{8.2}} |
| Facilities and displays | Air, toilet, first aid, price boards, housekeeping. [Customer service](@customer-service) {{5.1.14}} {{5.1.15}} {{5.1.17}} | Minor {{8.4}} |
| The 20 service points | Marked YES or NO. [The list](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations) {{circular}} {{5.1.18}} | Minor {{8.4}} |
| Automation | Manual mode or a switched-off ATG without written permission. [Automated outlets](@automation) {{5.1.16}} | Major {{8.3}} |

{{penalty-note}}

> **Note:** refusing, obstructing or creating circumstances that stop an inspection or sampling is a major irregularity in itself {{5.1.8}} {{8.3}}.

## During and after the visit

- The hour of arrival decides which density figure is asked for: see [the 10 AM rule](@density-reference-10am) {{5.1.9}}.
- Some findings stop sales on the spot while samples are tested: see [failed samples](@sampling-failed) {{5.1.9}} {{5.1.11}} {{2.5(K)}}.
- If an irregularity is found, a show cause notice follows, and no action is taken against the dealer until it is established {{8.5.6}} {{8.5.2}}. See [the show cause timeline](@inspection-show-cause-notice) and [the penalty pages](@penalties).
- To prepare, read [the records to keep](@inspection-records-to-keep) and [check your own pump](@tools-self-inspection).
