## What the label records

MDG makes the people drawing the sample responsible for checking the container before and after filling, and for correct labelling and sealing with the right seal number {{2.5(A)}}. In practice, read every line before you sign.

| Line on the Annexure 4 label {{annexure 4}} | What to check |
|---|---|
| Outlet, oil company, product, date and time | Your outlet; MS or HSD as drawn; right date and time |
| Dispensing unit and tank number | The unit and tank really sampled |
| Density at 15°C | Two figures: yours from the density register, and the officer's reading |
| Date and lorry number of the last receipt in that tank | Match your records |
| Plastic seal numbers | One set for the aluminium container, one for the wooden box; match the seals you see |
| Your signature, name, outlet seal, place and date | Filled by you or your representative |

Both labels carry a statement, signed by you, that the containers were rinsed in your presence with the same product and that you received one of the three sets {{annexure 4}} {{annexure 11}}. In practice, watch the rinse and have your set in hand before you sign.

A mobile lab uses the Annexure 11 label. It leaves out the density, last-receipt and lorry-number lines and has no outlet-seal line. It adds the mobile lab's reference number {{annexure 11}} {{7.5}}.

## Keeping your set

1. Take your set: 2 containers of MS or 1 of HSD for each tank sampled {{2.4.1}} {{2.4.2}}. The full split is in [what the inspector takes](@sampling-inspector-samples).
2. Keep it in safe custody {{annexure 4}}, until testing and the steps after it are finished {{2.9.2}}. In practice, leave seals and label untouched.
3. If every sample passes, the oil company tells you in writing and the retained samples can be disposed of {{2.5(F)}}. If one fails, see [what happens when a sample fails](@sampling-failed) for the 10-day re-test request {{2.5(H)}}.

For example (a made-up outlet): an officer draws petrol from a unit joined to MS tank 2. The label must show that unit and tank 2, and your containers and box must carry the seal numbers written on it {{annexure 4}}.

## What MDG does not say

MDG says only that the set stays in safe custody {{annexure 4}}. It gives no storage method and no steps for a wrong label line. Ask your oil company.
