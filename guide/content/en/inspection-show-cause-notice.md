## The clock, step by step

No action can be taken against a dealer until the irregularity is established {{8.5.2}}.

| Step | Time allowed | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Show cause notice issued | Within 30 days of the inspection, or of the test results if petrol or diesel samples were drawn. It lists all the irregularities. | {{8.5.6}} |
| What comes with it | All the reports and documents the notice rests on | {{8.5.6}} |
| The dealer's reply | 10 days from the date the notice is issued, not from receipt | {{8.5.7}} |
| Speaking order | The reasoned decision: the irregularities, the reply, and why it is or is not accepted. Preferably within 45 days of receiving the reply. | {{8.5.8}} |
| Personal hearing | Before termination, for critical irregularities | {{8.6}} |
| Fine, where it is the only action | Within 30 days of the company's notice. See [unpaid fines](@penalties-unpaid-fines). | {{8.7}} |

## An illustration with made-up dates

For example, an inspection on 5 October 2026 draws no samples.

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 5 Oct 2026 | Inspection |
| 20 Oct 2026 | The notice is issued with its reports. The latest date allowed was 4 Nov 2026 {{8.5.6}}. |
| 28 Oct 2026 | The reply is sent and received. It was due by 30 Oct 2026 {{8.5.7}}. |
| 12 Dec 2026 | The speaking order is passed, preferably by this date: 45 days after the reply is received {{8.5.8}}. |

MDG does not say how the days are counted.

## Before the notice, and the hearing

Some findings stop sales on the spot while samples are tested {{5.1.9}} {{5.1.11}}: see [failed samples](@sampling-failed). For a toilet finding, clause 5.1.14(b) adds an instant letter, and MDG does not say whether it replaces the notice {{5.1.14(b)}}.

Where a critical irregularity may lead to termination, the head of the State office, State Office (Retail) or Zonal office, or a nominee, hears the dealership's signatories or their nominees first. If they miss the date, one more is given, then the case can go ahead without them (ex parte) {{8.6}}. Clause 8.6 speaks of critical irregularities only. The appeal in clause 8.9 is for termination only: see [the appeal page](@inspection-appeal) {{8.9}}.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
