## Where the gap falls

| Gap per 5 litres | Against the MDG range | If an inspector finds it, seals intact |
|---|---|---|
| Under 10 ml | Below the range | MDG gives no separate step. Ask your oil company |
| 10 to 25 ml | Inside the range | Recorded and sales go on; recalibrate and re-stamp within 15 days {{5.1.2(a)}} |
| More than 25 ml short | Beyond the range | Sales through that unit or nozzle stop at once {{5.1.2(a)}} |

MDG prints only the range. For the limit itself, read the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and Rules, or ask W&M.

For example, a measure that held 4,985 ml in total is 15 ml short in 5 litres. That is 0.3 per cent: 300 ml in every 100 litres, 3 litres in every 1,000. It sits inside the range.

> **Note:** your own morning check follows 1.4.2(d): tell W&M and your oil company, and keep the unit off until it is re-stamped {{1.4.2(d)}}. The 15 days are for an inspector's finding {{5.1.2(a)}}. MDG does not say how the two fit. Ask your oil company.
