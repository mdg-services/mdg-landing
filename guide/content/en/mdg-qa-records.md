## Records

### What records must a petrol pump keep, and for how long?

The daily sales register, density register, maintenance records, purchase invoices and sales bills for this and the previous financial year, and the inspection record for the previous five years. Also the licences the outlet needs {{5.1.12}}. See [records to keep](@inspection-records-to-keep).

### What must be kept in a file at the outlet?

Inspection reports, the oil company's letters, test reports and invoice copies, ready for reference at all times {{1.5(iv)}}.

### Records since the last inspection are all there, but older ones are missing. Which class is that?

Minor, with a warning-cum-guidance letter at the first instance {{8.4}}. If the records since the last inspection are missing, it is major, with 15 days' suspension at the first instance {{8.3}}.

{{penalty-note}}

## Inspections

### Who can draw samples at an outlet?

Oil company officials, mobile labs, agencies the oil company authorises, any authority the law empowers, and joint industry teams {{2.2.2.2}} {{2.4.3}}. See [who can inspect](@inspection-who).

### Can a dealer refuse an inspection or sampling?

No. Refusing or obstructing either is a major irregularity {{5.1.8}}: 15 days' suspension at the first instance, 30 days at the second, termination at the third {{8.3}}.

{{penalty-note}}

### How many service points does an inspector mark YES or NO?

Twenty, in the circular of 20.03.2025 {{circular}}. A lapse draws the action for minor irregularities {{5.1.18}}. See [the 20 service points](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations).

## Notices, fines and hearings

### Show cause notice reply time: what must the notice and the final order contain?

The notice lists every irregularity and comes with its reports, within 30 days of the inspection, or of the test results where samples were drawn {{8.5.6}}, and the reply is due 10 days from issue {{8.5.7}}. The final order gives the irregularities, the reply and the reasons, preferably within 45 days of the reply {{8.5.8}}. See [the show cause timeline](@inspection-show-cause-notice).

### Two irregularities are found together. How are they counted?

Each counts as an instance in its own class and draws its own action {{8.5.3}}, while the miscellaneous minor items in 8.4 count as one irregularity {{8.4}}. MDG does not say how the two fit; ask your oil company.

### A fine is not paid. What happens?

For a fine with no suspension attached, the dealer has 30 days from the company's notice; from the 31st day sales and supplies are suspended for 15 days. If still unpaid, the suspension runs 15 days more; if the fine is still unpaid then, the dealership is terminated {{8.7}}. See [unpaid fines](@penalties-unpaid-fines).

{{penalty-note}}

### Is there a hearing before termination?

For critical irregularities, yes: the dealership's signatories or their nominees are heard before termination is recommended or approved. After a missed date they get one more chance, then the case can go ahead without them {{8.6}}.
