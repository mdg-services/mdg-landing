## The table is the floor

MDG calls its actions general guidelines and says they are minimum. The competent authority of the oil company can take appropriate higher action against a dealer if it deems it necessary, including termination in the first or any instance, in line with the provisions of the agreement {{8.5.1}}. MDG does not list what higher action can be, or when the company would deem it necessary. It does not set out the dealership agreement.

## Three cases

| Case | What MDG 2024 says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| A lapse on the list | The listed action is the minimum, and higher action stays open to the company | {{8.5.1}} |
| A lapse not on the list | The company acts after an enquiry, following the principles of natural justice | {{8.5.4}} |
| Any lapse | It must be established before any action is taken | {{8.5.2}} |

The steps before action, from notice to speaking order, are on the [show cause notice timeline](@inspection-show-cause-notice). A show cause notice is issued in all cases of irregularities {{8.5.6}}.

## Who approves

Clause 8.5.1 speaks of the "competent Authority" and names no officer. Clause 8.8 names who approves action by class:

| Action | Approved by | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Termination, and every critical irregularity | The Regional, State or Zonal head, General Manager and above. For an SC/ST dealership's termination, the Director (Marketing) at Head Office | {{8.8(i)}} |
| A major irregularity | IndianOil: the State Retail Head. BPCL and HPCL: an authorised officer of Deputy General Manager rank or above | {{8.8(ii)}} |
| A minor irregularity | The head of the Territory, Regional or Divisional office | {{8.8(iii)}} |

For who signs off a higher action in your case, ask your oil company.

## Appeal

The appeal in clause 8.9 is for termination arising out of MDG, within 30 days of receiving the order. MDG sets out no appeal for a suspension, a fine or any other action {{8.9}}. See [the appeal](@inspection-appeal) and [critical, major or minor](@penalties-classes).

## What MDG 2024 leaves open

- What "established" means in clause 8.5.2.
- What higher action looks like, short of termination.

General information, not legal advice. For your own notice, speak to an advocate or your oil company.
