## Who the team is, under MDG

MDG 2024 does not use the name QRC. It says random sampling is done by the Sales Officer, Inspecting Teams, Senior Level Officers and Mobile Labs, covering at least 2% of outlets every month {{2.4}}. Company officials, mobile labs, agencies the company authorises and any authority the law empowers may draw samples {{2.2.2.2}}. The clauses below apply to whichever of them walks in.

## Before, during and after the visit

| When | What to do or expect | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Ready beforehand | Keep the file of inspection reports, company letters, test reports and invoice copies at the outlet | {{1.5(iv)}} |
| Ready beforehand | Have the records MDG lists, and the Motor Spirit and High Speed Diesel Order records, ready to show on demand | {{5.1.12}} {{1.5(v)}} |
| Ready beforehand | Keep the hydrometer, thermometer, ASTM table and other test kits in working order | {{1.5(vi)}} |
| Ready beforehand | Have the reference density for the hour of the visit written down: [the 10 AM rule](@density-reference-10am) | {{5.1.9}} |
| During | Let the team inspect and draw samples | {{5.1.8}} {{8.3}} |
| During | The team pays for the samples and obtains a cash memo | {{2.2.2.2}} |
| During | The sample label puts the density in your register beside the officer's reading, and the signer confirms that one of the three sets was handed over | {{annexure 4}} |
| During | The team does not open your tank lorry sample box. It checks the outer seal and labels, takes custody and notes this in a joint memo that you acknowledge | {{2.5(C)}} |
| After | If an irregularity is established, a show cause notice follows within 30 days of the inspection, or of the test results if samples were drawn: [the notice timeline](@inspection-show-cause-notice) | {{8.5.2}} {{8.5.6}} |

## General practice, not MDG rules

- Meet the team at the office and let one named person go round with it.
- Note each name, designation, and the arrival and departure times.
- Ask for a copy of whatever the team writes.
- If the dealer is away, see [who can sign for the dealer](@inspection-dealer-absent-who-signs).

For example (a made-up outlet): a team arrives at 11:30 AM and a tanker was unloaded at 9 AM. The density it needs is the one taken after that unloading {{5.1.9}}.
