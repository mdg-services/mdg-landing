## What the rule covers

| Measure | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Any measure used to dispense bulk lube oil, on the pump island or in a service bay | It must be duly stamped by W&M | {{1.4.2(c)}} |
| The standard 5-litre measure | Verified by W&M and kept for pump testing only. It is a different duty from the lube measure stamp | {{1.4.2(b)}} |

For the 5-litre measure and the daily nozzle test, see [the 5 litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test).

## What MDG does not say

- **No penalty.** MDG names no irregularity and no amount for a lube measure without a stamp. Irregularities it does not name get suitable action after an enquiry, in line with the principles of natural justice {{8.5.4}}.
- **No validity period.** MDG does not say how long a stamp lasts or how it is renewed. Ask your W&M office.
- **No definition of "bulk".** If you are unsure whether a vessel counts, ask your oil company.

## Lube and the mobile labs

Mobile labs are meant to assess the quality of lube samples in surprise checks {{7.1}}, though MDG's mobile lab test list covers petrol and diesel only {{7.4}}. That is about the oil, not the measure. See [mobile lab inspections](@sampling-mobile-lab).

## What to do

General practice, not an MDG rule: look for the W&M stamp on every jug, can or other measure at the island and in the bay, and keep any unstamped one out of use until W&M has stamped it.

For example (a made-up outlet): a service bay pours lube from a steel jug that carries no W&M stamp. That jug does not meet {{1.4.2(c)}}. MDG gives no amount for it, and matters it does not name are dealt with after an enquiry {{8.5.4}}.

For the classes MDG does name, see [penalty classes](@penalties-classes).
