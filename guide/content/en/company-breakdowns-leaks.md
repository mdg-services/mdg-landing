## Who fixes what

| Situation | The oil company | The dealer | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company-owned pump or equipment breaks down | Arranges the repair | Handles it properly, does the day-to-day upkeep, tells the company promptly | {{4.2(a)}} |
| The dealer's own equipment | | Keeps it in good working order at all times | {{4.2(b)}} |
| Pump delivers short or excess | Representative or dealer arranges recalibration | Tells Weights & Measures and the controlling office | {{4.3(a)}} {{1.4.2(d)}} |
| Minor pipeline leak | Representative arranges the repair | | {{4.3(b)}} |

## Wrong delivery or a damaged seal

1. **Short or excess delivery:** the representative or you arrange recalibration at once with the Weights & Measures Inspector. Do not run the unit until it is re-stamped and the delivery is right {{4.3(a)}} {{1.4.2(d)}}.
2. **Weights & Measures seal found broken:** the representative informs the concerned company official and enters it in the pump maintenance report, which you sign {{4.3(a)}}.
3. **Seal tampered, found in your daily check:** sales through that unit stop at once, the controlling office is told immediately, and sales stay suspended until the seals are replaced {{1.4.2(a)}}.

See [meter and totaliser seals](@quantity-seals).

## The Maintenance Job Report

The company's fitter or chargeman fills it in after working on the company's equipment at your outlet {{4.1}}. It records why the fitter came, the equipment, the checklist results, the repairs and both signatures, in three copies, one for the outlet {{annexure 6}}. General practice, not an MDG rule: keep the outlet's copy. See [records to keep](@inspection-records-to-keep).

## What MDG does not say

- **Response time and remedy.** Chapter 4 sets no time for the company to attend and no remedy if it does not come {{4.2(a)}}. General practice, not an MDG rule: put the breakdown in writing to your controlling office, with the date and time of your first report.
- **Who pays** for parts or repairs. Ask your oil company.
- **What makes a leak minor or major.** Not defined {{4.3(b)}}.
