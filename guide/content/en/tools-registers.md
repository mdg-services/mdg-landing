## What each register records

| Register | What goes in | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Density | The morning density at 15°C, written before the first sale and by 10 AM | {{1.4.1(c)}} {{5.1.9}} |
| Density | Each tanker compartment's density and temperature, and the invoice's 15°C density; the gap should be within ±3.0 | {{1.2(viii)}} {{1.2(iv)}} |
| Density | The 15°C density of the mixed product, after at least 50 litres have gone through the pump | {{1.2(xiv)}} |
| Stock | Each tank's product dip and water dip, and the pump meter readings, every morning | {{1.3}} {{1.4.1(b)}} |
| Stock | The litres used to test each nozzle, and the entries after each tanker | {{1.4.2(d)}} {{1.2(xiii)}} |
| Stock | The stock check: opening dip stock plus receipts, less sales by meter, against the closing dip | {{annexure 9}} {{5.1.11}} |

## What the Excel files do

- **Density sheet:** one line for every reading. It works out the gap from the invoice and says OK or OUT for the 3.0 limit, and OK or LATE for the 10 AM morning reading. You type the density at 15°C from the printed ASTM table; the sheet never converts it. The [15°C calculator](@tools-density-at-15) is a helper only.
- **Stock sheets:** one sheet for petrol and one for diesel, a line for every day, up to 3 tanks and 8 nozzles. A Tankers sheet adds the day's receipts by itself. Grey columns work out sales by meter, book stock, closing stock, the variation and 4% of closing stock. A water dip above 0 turns red.
- Yellow cells are for typing, grey cells are formulas, and the files start empty. If Excel shows a yellow Protected View bar, press Enable Editing.

## Before you use them

- **Company register first.** MDG says the oil company supplies the daily stock register {{1.3(ii)}} and prints no form for it. For density, MDG attaches its own format {{annexure 1}}. Ask your oil company whether it accepts our layout, or copy the readings into its form.
- **Over 4% is not a finding.** MDG also counts handling loss on sales, and shrinkage or TVA where they apply {{5.1.11}}. Use the [stock variation calculator](@tools-stock-variation). The oil company must establish an irregularity before it acts {{8.5.2}}.
- **Missing records cost.** Not keeping the specified records is an irregularity {{5.1.12}}. See [the records to keep](@inspection-records-to-keep), the [density register rules](@density-register) and the [daily stock register rules](@stock-daily-register).
- **Check your own figures.** Nothing you type into these files is sent anywhere.
