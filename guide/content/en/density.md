## The three checks and the equipment

| When | What the dealer does | Held to | Read more |
|---|---|---|---|
| A tanker arrives | Samples each compartment, reads density and temperature, converts to 15°C {{1.2(viii)}} | Within ±3.0 kg/m³ of the invoice density {{1.2(iv)}} | [Invoice density check](@density-invoice-check) |
| After decanting | Runs at least 50 litres through the pump, then takes a nozzle sample {{1.2(xiv)}} | Becomes the reference density from then on {{2.10}} | [Reference density rule](@density-reference-10am) |
| Every morning | Takes a nozzle sample before the first sale {{1.4.1(c)}} | Recorded by 10 AM {{5.1.9}} | [Reference density rule](@density-reference-10am) |
| Always | Keeps calibrated hydrometers, thermometers and the ASTM table in working order {{1.5(vi)}}. Petrol also needs filter paper {{1.4.1(a)}} | Equipment shown when an inspector asks; filter paper handed to a customer who asks | [Filter paper test](@density-filter-paper) |

Do not convert to 15°C by eye. Use ASTM Table 53B or the [15°C calculator](@tools-density-at-15) {{1.2(iv)}} {{1.5(vi)}}. The limits MDG prints for each fuel are on the [density range page](@density-range).

## Records, display and what an inspector compares

- **Register.** Density records follow the Annexure 1 format and are kept for the present and previous financial year {{1.2(xiv)}} {{5.1.12}}. See [the density register](@density-register).
- **Board.** Density display is covered with the other displays on [the boards MDG requires](@service-display-boards) {{5.1.17}}.
- **Yardstick.** Until the lorry is decanted, an inspector compares samples with the invoice density. After decanting, it is your own after-decanting reading {{2.10}}.

## When density goes wrong

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| The reference figure an inspector needs is missing | Sales and supplies of every product stop at once and samples go to the lab {{5.1.9}}. If the sample passes, it is a major irregularity {{8.3}} |
| A density or filter paper test fails | The oil company officer suspends sales and supplies of every product and sends samples to the lab on priority {{2.5(K)}} |
| Density is not displayed | Minor irregularity {{8.4}} |

## Old rules and new

The receipt check, the 50-litre sample, morning density, reference density, the equipment and the register read the same in MDG 2012 as last amended (Ver. 7, 30.11.2022), which MDG 2024 replaced. See [what changed between the two](@mdg-2012-vs-2024).
