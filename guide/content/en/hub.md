## What MDG is

Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (MDG 2024) is usually the document dealers mean when they say *petrol pump ke niyam* (पेट्रोल पंप के नियम). It covers the daily running of a pump: fuel quality, quantity, price, records and customer service {{1.6}} {{5.1.12}}.

The first MDG was framed in 1981-82 {{introduction}}. The edition in force is **MDG 2024, Version 1, effective 24.10.2024** {{cover page}}. It replaced MDG 2012: see [what changed](@mdg-2012-vs-2024). The text has eight chapters and thirteen annexures, plus 12a and 13a. The annexures hold forms, labels and test tables; Annexure 1 is the format for the density record {{1.2(xiv)}} {{annexure 1}}.

A pump answers to more than MDG. The dealer must also follow the statutory rules, the explosives authority's rules and the Government's order on MS and HSD malpractices {{1.5(i)}} {{1.5(ii)}} {{1.5(v)}}. A malpractice that a government authority establishes also counts as an MDG irregularity {{8.7}}. The oil companies have duties too: see [oil company duties](@oil-company-duties) {{3.1}}.

## The three grades of irregularity

Chapter 8 sorts the irregularities MDG lists into three grades, and the grade sets the first action {{8.1}}.

| Grade | First action |
|---|---|
| Critical {{8.2}} | Termination at the first instance |
| Major {{8.3}} | Usually a 15-day suspension of sales and supplies, then 30 days, then termination. Branded fuel and automation have their own ladders |
| Minor {{8.4}} | A warning-cum-guidance letter, then Rs 10,000, then Rs 25,000 per irregularity |
