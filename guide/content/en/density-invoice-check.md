## The check, step by step

The supply location writes the 15°C density on the invoice {{2.2.1(iv)}}, and on arrival the dealer matches it against the lorry {{1.1(v)}}. The rest of the receipt checklist is on [receiving a tanker](@receiving-a-tanker). The density part runs in this order {{1.2(vii)}} {{1.2(viii)}}:

1. **Note the invoice density.** Your reading is compared with this figure {{1.1(v)}} {{1.2(iv)}}.
2. **Finish the dip and water checks first.** See [water finding paste](@tanker-water-finding-paste) {{1.2(vii)}}.
3. **Run off about 20 litres** at the manifold of each compartment, then fill a clear glass container from that compartment {{1.2(viii)}}.
4. **Read density and temperature** on each compartment's own sample {{1.2(viii)}}.
5. **Convert to 15°C** with ASTM Table 53B or the [15°C calculator](@tools-density-at-15), and compare. The result must be within ±3.0 kg/m³ of the invoice {{1.2(iv)}}.
6. **Record each compartment's reading** in the register {{1.2(viii)}}.

The retention sample follows once the density is within limits; see [retention samples](@sampling-retention) {{1.2(x)}} {{2.3}}. The invoice figure stays the reference density until the lorry is decanted; see [the reference density rule](@density-reference-10am) {{2.10}}.

## If a compartment is outside ±3.0 kg/m³

MDG gives one instruction. If you doubt the quality of the product, contact the supply location or the area field officer concerned {{1.2(ix)}}.

General practice, not an MDG rule: treat a compartment outside ±3.0 kg/m³ as a doubt, keep it out of the tank and call before decanting. Confirm with your oil company how it wants this handled.

## An example

An illustration with made-up figures. The invoice shows 743.0 kg/m³ at 15°C. After conversion with the ASTM table, a three-compartment tanker reads:

| Compartment | 15°C figure | Difference from 743.0 | Within ±3.0? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 744.5 | +1.5 | Yes |
| 2 | 745.0 | +2.0 | Yes |
| 3 | 746.5 | +3.5 | No |

Compartment 3 is 0.5 beyond the limit. You record all three readings {{1.2(viii)}} and, if you doubt compartment 3, contact the supply location or area field officer {{1.2(ix)}}.
