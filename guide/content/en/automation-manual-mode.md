## The steps when the automation fails

MDG 2024 does not print a step list. Read together, clauses 1.5(viii) and 5.1.16 point to this order of action {{1.5(viii)}} {{5.1.16}}:

1. **Report it immediately.** Tell the authorised official of the oil company that the automation has malfunctioned {{1.5(viii)}}.
2. **Take further action as that official directs** {{1.5(viii)}}.
3. **If the direction means manual mode, get it in writing first.** Only an email or a signed letter from a company official is admissible {{5.1.16}}.
4. **Keep the written authorisation** on record for future reference {{5.1.16}}.
5. **Do not disable anything on purpose.** Deliberately making any other component of the automation system stop working, wholly or partly, without authorisation, is an irregularity. This covers a deliberate act of the dealership, its staff or any other agency {{5.1.16(c)}}.

> **Note:** manual mode is not the problem. Manual mode without written authorisation is. A dispensing unit found in manual mode, or the tank gauge found off, with no email or signed letter behind it is the irregularity MDG describes {{5.1.16(a)}} {{5.1.16(b)}}.

## What counts as written authorisation

| How the permission reached you | Counts under MDG? |
|---|---|
| Email from a company official | Yes {{5.1.16}} |
| Signed letter from a company official | Yes {{5.1.16}} |
| Phone call | No. It is neither an email nor a signed letter. Ask the official to confirm by email |
| SMS or chat message | No. It is neither an email nor a signed letter. Ask for an email |

MDG says that only an email or a signed letter is admissible {{5.1.16}}.

## What to keep

MDG asks only for a record of the written authorisation {{5.1.16}}. It also asks the dealer to keep a file of correspondence from the oil company at the outlet, ready to show at all times {{1.5(iv)}}.

General practice, not an MDG rule. When a company official allows manual mode, it helps to note down:

- the email or letter itself, printed or saved, with its date;
- which dispensing unit or device it covers;
- the time manual mode started and the time the automation came back;
- the name of the official who allowed it.

## If there is no authorisation

The three automation irregularities are major irregularities {{8.3(vii)}}:

| Instance | Action | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| First | Penalty of Rs 1,00,000 | {{8.3(vii)}} |
| Second | Penalty of Rs 2,00,000 and suspension of sales and supplies for 7 days | {{8.3(vii)}} |
| Third | Termination of the dealership | {{8.3(vii)}} |

Instances are counted over five years {{8.5.5}}. The full picture is on [the automated outlets page](@automation), and the whole ladder is on [the penalties page](@penalties).

## A worked example

This is an illustration, not a real case.

At 9:00 AM the automation stops on two of an outlet's six dispensing units. The manager calls the company's authorised official at once {{1.5(viii)}}. The official says to run those two units in manual mode until a technician comes. The manager asks for that by email and does not switch the two units until it arrives.

The email comes at 9:40 AM. The manager saves it, prints a copy for the outlet's company correspondence file and notes the time manual mode began. At noon an inspector visits, sees the two units in manual mode and asks for the authorisation. The manager shows the email.

Had there been no email, the same sight would be an irregularity under 5.1.16(a) {{5.1.16(a)}}.

## What MDG does not say

- **Who can authorise.** The clause says "competent authority", the note says "Company official", and no rank is named {{5.1.16}}.
- **How fast the company must reply or send a technician.** No time is set.
- **How long an authorisation lasts.** Not stated.
- **Whether sales must stop while you wait.** MDG says to report immediately and follow the official's direction {{1.5(viii)}}. Ask your oil company.

For these, speak to your oil company. If a notice has already arrived, read [the show cause notice page](@inspection-show-cause-notice) and take advice. This page is general information, not advice on a particular case.
