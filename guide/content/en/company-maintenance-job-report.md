## What the report records

The checks below are general practice, not MDG rules, except where a clause is cited.

| Part of the Annexure 6 report | What to check before you sign | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Visit and equipment: purpose of visit, unit name, model, serial number, product, last visit | Matches your units and the reason he came | {{annexure 6}} |
| Yes/no checks: delivery rate, filters, hoses and nozzles for leaks, tank openings, wiring | Every line answered | {{annexure 6}} |
| Weights & Measures seal calibration | A broken seal, if found, is written in the report and you sign too | {{4.3(a)}} |
| Water dip in cm | A figure is entered. MDG has the representative dip for water on every visit | {{4.4}} |
| Tank interconnection and unauthorised fittings | Both answered. MDG classes an unauthorised fitting in a dispenser and an unauthorised connection between tanks as critical irregularities | {{annexure 6}} {{5.1.4}} {{5.1.5}} {{8.2}} |
| Silica gel on the vent of ethanol-blended MS | Answered | {{annexure 6}} |
| Repairs, parts replaced, parts required | Listed. After a major pipeline leak, the totaliser reading and tank dip are noted too, and you sign | {{4.3(b)}} |
| Date, arrival and departure times, signatures | Times are right; both signatures | {{annexure 6}} |

The form has three copies and the outlet gets the second {{annexure 6}}. For who fixes what, see [breakdowns and leaks](@company-breakdowns-leaks).

## Before you sign

General practice, not an MDG rule: do not sign with a blank line; ask for the missing entry to be written in front of you. For example, if the fitter replaced a hose but left "parts replaced" and the departure time empty, have both filled first.

Clause 4.3(a) calls the report the pump maintenance report {{4.3(a)}}, and MDG asks for pump maintenance records of the current and previous financial years {{5.1.12(ii)}}. General practice, not an MDG rule: keep your copy with them.

## What MDG does not say

MDG does not say what happens if the dealer does not sign, or whether he may add a remark of his own. Ask your oil company.
