## Density records

### How many litres must run through the pump before the after-tanker density sample?

At least 50 litres, to clear the line. Then draw a nozzle sample and write its 15°C density in the density register {{1.2(xiv)}}. See [the density register](@density-register).

### An inspector arrives at 9 AM. Which density figure can he ask for?

The previous morning's reading, or the after-tanker reading if a load came the day before. After 10 AM it is that morning's reading, or the after-tanker reading if a load has come that day {{5.1.9}}. See [reference density](@density-reference-10am).

### What happens if the reference density is not available at an inspection?

Sales and supplies of all products stop and samples go to the lab. A passing sample is a major irregularity (15 days' suspension at the first instance); a failing one is treated as adulteration, which is critical {{5.1.9}} {{8.3}} {{8.2}}.

## Samples

### Which tank lorry samples does a dealer draw and keep?

Per load, 2 x 1 litre of petrol and/or 1 x 1 litre of diesel, sealed and signed with the driver before decanting. For each tank, keep the samples of the last two loads {{2.3}} {{2.9.2}}. See [the retention sample](@sampling-retention).

### How many bottles does an oil company officer take from each tank?

Six 1-litre bottles from each petrol tank and three from each diesel tank, in three equal sets for the dealer, the oil company's office and the lab {{2.4.1}} {{2.4.2}}. A mobile lab takes them only if a sample fails {{2.4.4}}. See [inspector samples](@sampling-inspector-samples).

## Quality checks

### Where is the water dip recorded, and what if water is found?

Daily, in the stock register; report water to the controlling company office at once {{1.4.1(b)}}. On a tanker, test each compartment with water finding paste before decanting {{1.2(vii)}}. See [water finding paste](@tanker-water-finding-paste).

### Filter paper test for petrol: which paper must a dealer keep?

Whatman 2, to check petrol. A customer who asks must be able to use it {{1.4.1(a)}}. See [the filter paper test](@density-filter-paper).

### What follows a failed density or filter paper test?

The oil company officer suspends sales and supplies of all products and samples go for priority testing; a failed mobile lab test leads to the same {{2.5(K)}}. If a lab report then fails, the dealer can ask within 10 days of the report for his own sample to be re-tested, at his cost {{2.5(H)}}. See [failed samples](@sampling-failed).

### What does a mobile lab test?

Appearance, colour, density at 15°C and distillation, plus viscosity at 40°C for diesel. It runs them whatever the density result {{7.4}}. See [the mobile lab](@sampling-mobile-lab).
