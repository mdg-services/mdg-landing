## What to keep, and for how long

| How long | Record | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| This financial year and the one before | The company's daily sales register; the density register (Annexure 1 format); pump maintenance records; purchase invoices and sales bills | {{5.1.12}} {{1.2(xiv)}} |
| The previous five years | The inspection record | {{5.1.12}} |
| No period stated | The selling licence, Explosives Licence and other licences the outlet needs | {{5.1.12}} |
| As the company's letter says | Any other record the oil company asks for in writing | {{5.1.12}} |

Keep a file at the outlet of inspection reports, company letters, test reports and invoice copies {{1.5(iv)}}. Records the anti-malpractice Order and company or State instructions require must be shown on demand {{1.5(v)}}.

MDG leaves two points open:

- **Sales register or stock register?** Clause 5.1.12 names a daily sales register and clause 1.3 a daily stock register. MDG does not say if they are one book or two. Ask your oil company which books it expects {{5.1.12}} {{1.3}}.
- **"Previous five years".** Five financial years, or five years back from the visit? MDG does not say. General practice, not an MDG rule: keep to whichever reading reaches further back.

## The daily entries

- Morning meter readings and the product dip of each tank {{1.3}}.
- The morning water dip {{1.4.1(b)}}.
- Density at 15°C, before sales and by 10 AM {{1.4.1(c)}} {{5.1.9}}. See [the 10 AM rule](@density-reference-10am).
- The quantity used to test each nozzle {{1.4.2(d)}}.
- Each tanker's entries {{1.2(viii)}}: [tanker receipt](@receiving-a-tanker), [density register](@density-register), [stock register](@stock-daily-register).

Other papers have their own pages: [manual mode permission](@automation-manual-mode), [the toilet sheet](@service-toilet-rules), [the maintenance job report](@company-breakdowns-leaks), [the mobile lab report](@sampling-mobile-lab) and [retention samples](@sampling-retention).

## What a missing record can cost

| What the inspector finds | Class | Action MDG lists |
|---|---|---|
| Records since the last inspection are not kept | Major {{8.3}} | 15 days' suspension, then 30 days, then termination |
| Those are kept, but older required records are missing | Minor {{8.4}} | Warning-cum-guidance letter, then Rs 10,000, then Rs 25,000 per irregularity |

{{penalty-note}}

For example, an outlet last inspected on 12 March 2026 is visited on 10 October 2026. The financial year runs April to March, so registers and bills must go back to 1 April 2025. A missing June 2026 sales register is major {{8.3}}. If everything after 12 March 2026 is complete but the June 2025 pages are lost, it is minor {{8.4}}.
