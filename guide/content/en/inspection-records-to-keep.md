## What to keep, and for how long

The Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (MDG 2024) are the oil companies' rules for their dealers, applied through the dealership agreement. They bind the dealer through the dealership agreement. Clause 5.1.12 lists the records a dealer must keep, and not keeping any of them is treated as an irregularity {{5.1.12}}. The table groups them by how long they are kept.

| How long | Record | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| This financial year and the one before | The oil company's daily sales register; the density register, in the Annexure 1 format; pump maintenance records; purchase invoices and sales bills | {{5.1.12}} {{1.2(xiv)}} |
| The previous five years | The inspection record | {{5.1.12}} |
| No period stated | The selling licence, the Explosives Licence and any other licence the outlet needs to keep running. Hold whatever you need for continued operation. | {{5.1.12}} |
| As the company's letter says | Any other record the oil company asks for in writing | {{5.1.12}} |

On top of this, MDG asks for a file at the outlet holding inspection reports, letters from the oil company, test reports and invoice copies, ready for reference at all times {{1.5(iv)}}. The records that the Government's anti-malpractice Order and company or State instructions require must be shown to inspecting officials on demand {{1.5(v)}}.

**Two points MDG leaves open**

- **Sales register or stock register?** Clause 5.1.12 names the oil company's "daily sales register". Clause 1.3 says the dealer keeps the daily stock register the company provides {{1.3}}. MDG does not say whether these are two books or one. Keep the book or books your oil company gave you, and ask if you are unsure.
- **"Previous five years".** MDG does not say whether this means five financial years or five years counted back from the day of inspection. General practice, not an MDG rule: keeping inspection reports from 1 April 2021 covers both readings on 10 October 2026, so a dealer who wants no doubt can keep that far back.

General practice, not an MDG rule: an Indian financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March. In October 2026 the current year is 2026-27 and the previous year is 2025-26, so the registers, bills and invoices have to go back to 1 April 2025 {{5.1.12}}.

## The daily entries

| When | What goes in the book | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Every morning | Pump meter readings, plus the product dip and water dip of each tank, as a daily record beside the stock register | {{1.3}} |
| Every morning | The water dip, which is part of the daily stock register. If water shows up, tell the controlling company office at once. | {{1.4.1(b)}} |
| Every morning, before sales begin and by 10 AM | Density at 15°C of a nozzle sample | {{1.4.1(c)}} {{5.1.9}} |
| Before sales, each nozzle | The quantity used to test the nozzle, in the stock register | {{1.4.2(d)}} |
| Each tanker, before decanting | Density and temperature of the sample from each compartment, one by one | {{1.2(viii)}} |
| Each tanker, if the dip is short or water is found | The dip seen in each compartment and the quantity filled through the pump to correct the level, on the back of every invoice copy, signed by the dealer or representative and the driver | {{1.2(xi)}} |
| Each tanker, after decanting | Stock register entries and the stock display board | {{1.2(xiii)}} |
| Each tanker, once at least 50 litres have been dispensed | Density at 15°C of the mixed product, in the density register | {{1.2(xiv)}} |
| Every day | Opening stock and selling prices, shown to customers | {{1.3(i)}} {{5.1.17}} |

## Other papers MDG expects at the outlet

- **Written authorisation at an automated outlet.** Only an email or signed letter from a company official counts as permission for manual mode or a switched-off ATG. The dealer keeps a record of it {{5.1.16}}.
- **Toilet maintenance sheet.** The sheet is kept and displayed, and the toilet protocol is shown near the toilet {{5.1.14(b)}}.
- **Maintenance job reports.** When a W&M seal is found broken, the company's maintenance representative notes it in the pump maintenance report, and the dealer signs that report as well. For a major pipeline leak he writes the totaliser reading and tank dip on the job report and takes the dealer's signature {{4.3}}. The job report has its own format, Annexure 6 {{4.1}} {{annexure 6}}.
- **Test reports.** The outlet gets one of the five copies of a mobile lab test report {{7.6.1}}. Cash receipts for samples a mobile lab pays for should be preserved {{7.3.1}}.
- **Retention samples.** These are not paper, but inspectors look for them. Keep the tank-lorry samples of the last two loads for each tank {{2.9.2}}. If they are missing at inspection, that is noted, and a sample produced later is not considered {{2.5(E)}}. See [retention samples](@sampling-retention).

## What a missing record can cost

| What the inspector finds | Class | Action MDG lists | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| Records since the last inspection are not kept | Major | First instance: 15 days' suspension of sales and supplies. Second: 30 days. Third: termination of the dealership. | {{8.3}} |
| Records since the last inspection are kept, but older required records are not available | Minor | First instance: warning-cum-guidance letter. Second: Rs 10,000 per irregularity. Third and later: Rs 25,000 per irregularity. | {{8.4}} |

These are the minimum actions. The oil company may take higher action, up to termination, in line with the dealership agreement {{8.5.1}}, but the irregularity must be established first {{8.5.2}}. Second and third instances are counted over five years from the date an irregularity is established, and the count includes irregularities under the previous guideline {{8.5.5}}.

### An illustration

For example, an outlet was last inspected on 12 March 2026. An inspector visits on 10 October 2026. The registers, bills and invoices required go back to 1 April 2025.

- **Case A.** The sales register is missing for the whole of June 2026. That falls after the last inspection, so it counts as missing "since the last inspection": a major irregularity {{8.3}}.
- **Case B.** Everything after 12 March 2026 is complete, but the register pages for June 2025 cannot be found. The missing records are older than the last inspection: a minor irregularity {{8.4}}.

> **Note:** the density register has a second test. If the figure for the moment of the visit is not in it, sales and supplies of all products stop at once and samples go to the lab {{5.1.9}}. See [the 10 AM density rule](@density-reference-10am).
