## What MDG makes you keep, and for how long

Clause 5.1.12 lists the records. Not maintaining any of them is treated as an irregularity {{5.1.12}}.

| Record | How long to keep it | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Daily sales register of the oil company | Current and previous financial years | {{5.1.12}} |
| Density register, in the Annexure 1 format | Current and previous financial years | {{5.1.12}} {{1.2(xiv)}} |
| Pump maintenance records | Current and previous financial years | {{5.1.12}} |
| Inspection record | The previous five years | {{5.1.12}} |
| Purchase invoices and sales bills | Current and previous financial years | {{5.1.12}} |
| Selling licence, Explosives Licence and any other licence needed to keep the outlet running | No period stated. Hold the licences you need for continued operation. | {{5.1.12}} |
| Any other record the oil company specifies in writing | As its letter says | {{5.1.12}} |

On top of this, MDG asks for a file at the outlet holding all inspection reports, letters received from the oil company, test reports and copies of invoices, available for ready reference at all times {{1.5(iv)}}. The records that the Government's malpractice-prevention Order and company or State instructions require must be produced to inspecting officials on demand {{1.5(v)}}.

**Two points that MDG leaves open:**

- **Sales register or stock register?** Clause 5.1.12 says "daily sales register of OMC". Clause 1.3 says the dealer keeps the daily stock register the company provides {{1.3}}. MDG does not say whether these are two books or one. Keep the book or books your oil company gave you, and ask if you are unsure.
- **"Previous five years".** MDG does not say whether this means five financial years or five years counted back from the inspection day. General practice, not an MDG rule: keeping inspection reports from 1 April 2021 covers both readings on 10 October 2026, so a dealer who wants no doubt can keep that far back.

General practice, not an MDG rule: an Indian financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March. In October 2026 the current year is 2026-27 and the previous year is 2025-26, so the registers, bills and invoices have to go back to 1 April 2025 {{5.1.12}}.

## The daily entries

| When | What is written | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Every morning | The pump meter readings, and the product dip and water dip of each tank, as a daily record in addition to the stock register | {{1.3}} |
| Every morning | The water dip, which forms part of the daily stock register. If water is found, report it at once to the controlling company office. | {{1.4.1(b)}} |
| Every morning, before sales and by 10 AM | The density at 15°C of a sample drawn from the pump nozzle | {{1.4.1(c)}} {{5.1.9}} |
| Before sales, every nozzle | The quantity used to test the nozzles, in the stock register | {{1.4.2(d)}} |
| Each tanker, before decanting | The density and temperature of each compartment's sample, individually, in the register | {{1.2(viii)}} |
| Each tanker, if the dip is short or water is found | The observed dip, compartment by compartment, and the quantity filled through the dispensing unit, on the back of all invoice copies, signed by the dealer or representative and the driver | {{1.2(xi)}} |
| Each tanker, after decanting | Entries in the stock register and the stock display board | {{1.2(xiii)}} |
| Each tanker, after at least 50 litres are dispensed | The density at 15°C of the mixed product, in the density register | {{1.2(xiv)}} |
| Every day | Opening stock and the authorised selling prices, on display | {{1.3(i)}} {{5.1.17}} |

See [the density register](@density-register), [the daily stock register](@stock-daily-register) and [the tanker receipt checklist](@receiving-a-tanker).

## Other papers MDG expects at the outlet

- **Written authorisation at an automated outlet.** Only an email or signed letter from a company official counts as authorisation for manual mode or a switched-off ATG. The dealer must keep a record of it {{5.1.16}}.
- **Toilet maintenance sheet.** The sheet is to be maintained and displayed, and the toilet protocol is displayed near the toilet {{5.1.14(b)}}.
- **Maintenance job reports.** The company's maintenance representative notes a broken W&M seal in the pump maintenance report, which the dealer signs. For a major pipeline leak he records the totaliser reading and tank dip on the job report and takes the dealer's signature {{4.3}}. The report format is Annexure 6 {{4.1}} {{annexure 6}}.
- **Test reports.** The retail outlet gets one of the five copies of a mobile lab test report {{7.6.1}}. Cash receipts for samples paid for by a mobile lab should be preserved {{7.3.1}}.
- **Retention samples.** These are not paper, but they are checked. Keep the tank-lorry samples of the last two loads for each tank {{2.9.2}}. If they are missing at inspection, the fact is recorded and a sample produced later is not considered {{2.5(E)}}. See [retention samples](@sampling-retention).

## What a missing record costs

| What the inspector finds | Class | Action | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| Records since the last inspection are not maintained | Major | First instance: 15 days' suspension of sales and supplies. Second: 30 days. Third: termination of the dealership. | {{8.3}} |
| Records since the last inspection are kept, but older required records are not available | Minor | First instance: warning-cum-guidance letter. Second: Rs 10,000 per irregularity. Third and later: Rs 25,000 per irregularity. | {{8.4}} |

Instances are counted over five years from the date an irregularity is established {{8.5.5}}.

### A worked example

For example, an outlet was last inspected on 12 March 2026. An inspector visits on 10 October 2026. The registers, bills and invoices required go back to 1 April 2025.

- **Case A.** The sales register is missing for the whole of June 2026. That is after the last inspection, so it is "since last inspection": a major irregularity {{8.3}}.
- **Case B.** Everything after 12 March 2026 is complete, but the register pages for June 2025 cannot be found. The missing records are older than the last inspection: a minor irregularity {{8.4}}.

> **Note:** the density register has a second test. If the figure for the moment of inspection is not in it, sales and supplies of all products stop at once and samples go to the lab {{5.1.9}}. See [the 10 AM density rule](@density-reference-10am).
