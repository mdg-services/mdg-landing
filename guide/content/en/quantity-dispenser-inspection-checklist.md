## The dispenser, point by point

MDG lists what must be right at a dispenser, not an inspector's order of work. It does not describe how an inspector tests delivery. The circular of 20.03.2025, a joint format of the three oil companies, has the inspector mark its points YES or NO {{circular}}. General practice, not an MDG rule: use the table as your own morning round.

| What is seen | What should be right | If it is not | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| W&M seals | A seal on the metering unit and on the totalizer, embossed by the W&M inspector | Critical | {{1.4.2(a)}} {{5.1.2(b)}} {{8.2(ii)}} |
| W&M certificate | The unit's W&M certification is valid; the dealer checks it daily | MDG's lists name no class | {{1.4.2(a)}} |
| Totalizer | Working; a stopped one is reported to the oil company | Critical | {{5.1.3}} {{8.2(iii)}} |
| Delivery | Within the permissible limit; MDG prints a band of ±10 ml to ±25 ml per 5 litres | Minor, seals intact | {{5.1.2(a)}} {{8.4(iii)(h)}} |
| Inside the unit | No part, chip, electronic part or software added, removed or changed; no fitting that could alter delivery | Critical | {{5.1.4}} {{8.2(iv)}} |
| Zero sticker | A zero message or sticker on each unit | Minor | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{5.1.17}} {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Zero and closing reading | Zero shown before a delivery, the closing reading after it | Service standard, minor | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{circular}} {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}} |
| Nozzle labels | The product name on each nozzle of an MPD | Minor | {{5.1.17}} {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Leaks | The dealer looks daily at pump, hose and nozzle, and reports a leak to the oil company | MDG's lists name no class | {{1.4.2(e)}} |

Critical means termination at the first instance once established {{8.2}} {{8.5.2}}.

{{penalty-note}}

The amounts for minor lapses are in [penalty classes](@penalties-classes).

## Before the inspector comes

MDG already asks the dealer to check the seals and certificate daily {{1.4.2(a)}}, test every nozzle before sales {{1.4.2(d)}} and look for leaks daily {{1.4.2(e)}}.
