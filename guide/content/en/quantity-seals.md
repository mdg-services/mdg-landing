## The seals and the daily check

W&M seals sit on the metering unit and on the totalizer (in plain words, the counter that keeps the unit's running total). Each is a sealing wire closed with a lead seal embossed by the W&M inspector {{5.1.2(b)}}.

Every day, on each unit, the dealer confirms both seals are correct and the W&M certificate is valid {{1.4.2(a)}}. If a seal is found tampered, sales through that unit stop at once, the controlling oil company office is told immediately, and the unit stays closed until new seals are fitted {{1.4.2(a)}}.

## What counts as tampered

- **No seal** on the unit {{5.1.2(b)}}.
- **A seal the W&M inspector did not emboss** {{5.1.2(b)}}.
- **A sealing wire broken apart** {{5.1.2(b)}}.
- **Any other situation** that could let the delivery, the quantity or the totalizer be manipulated may also be treated as tampering {{5.1.2(b)}}.
- **A totalizer seal that allows the reading to be manipulated**, or a totalizer deliberately put out of action {{5.1.3}}.
- **A totalizer that stopped working and was not reported** to the oil company {{5.1.3}}. MDG calls for a generator or UPS, which matters for an electronic totalizer in a power cut {{1.5(ix)}}.

W&M's opinion is final {{5.1.2(b)}} {{5.1.3}}, and action follows even if the delivery was correct or in excess {{5.1.2(b)}}. For example, a totalizer's lead seal is not the one W&M embossed, though the unit delivers exactly 5 litres: it still counts as tampered.

> **Note:** MDG does not say that reporting a broken seal first changes how the finding is classed. Ask your oil company.

## Fittings, chips and software

Parts added, removed, swapped or interfered with count as tampering, as does any fitting or gear that could alter the delivery. That includes a chip, electronic parts and software; the manufacturer's independent view is sought {{5.1.4}}. The clause names no exception for repairs, so ask your oil company before anyone opens a unit.

## After a finding

- The unit stops selling, is sealed, and samples of all products go to the lab {{5.1.2(b)}} {{5.1.3}} {{5.1.4}}.
- The action is termination at the first instance {{8.2}}.
- The dealership's signatories or their nominees get a personal hearing before termination is recommended or approved {{8.6}}.
