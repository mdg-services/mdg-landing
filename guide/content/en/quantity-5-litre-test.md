## What the dealer does every day

"The 5-litre test" is our name for two MDG duties: the 5-litre measure {{1.4.2(b)}} and the daily nozzle check {{1.4.2(d)}}.

- **Keep the measure:** W&M-verified, for pump testing only {{1.4.2(b)}}.
- **Test every nozzle** before the day's sales {{1.4.2(d)}}.
- **Record the testing litres** in the [stock register](@stock-daily-register) {{1.4.2(d)}}.
- **Report a variation at once** to W&M and the oil company {{1.4.2(d)}}.
- **Keep the unit off** until it is re-stamped and the delivery is corrected {{1.4.2(d)}}.

The dealer or the oil company's maintenance representative should arrange recalibration at once, with the W&M Inspector's help {{4.3(a)}}. MDG gives no test method. General practice, not an MDG rule: note the nozzle number and the result beside the testing litres.

## If an inspector finds a nozzle off

With the W&M seals intact, there are two outcomes:

| What the inspector finds | Sales | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| Inside the range MDG prints (±10 ml to ±25 ml per 5 litres) | Go on | Recorded. Recalibrate and re-stamp within 15 days of the observation, or sales from the unit or nozzle stop {{5.1.2(a)}} |
| Short beyond the Legal Metrology limit | Stop at once through that unit or nozzle | Recalibrate and re-stamp before sales restart {{5.1.2(a)}} |

MDG prints only the range. For the limit itself, read the current Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and Rules, or ask W&M.

For example, nozzle 2 is found 15 ml short on 10 November. Taking the range as written, that is inside the limit: it must be recalibrated and re-stamped by 25 November, or sales from it stop on 26 November.

> **Note:** {{1.4.2(d)}} keeps a unit off after a variation in the morning test, while the 15 days in {{5.1.2(a)}} cover an inspector's finding. MDG does not say how the two fit. Ask your oil company.

## What a finding costs

With the seals intact, short delivery is a minor irregularity {{8.4(iii)(h)}}: a warning-cum-guidance letter at the first instance, Rs 10,000 per irregularity at the second, Rs 25,000 from the third on {{8.4}}. MDG does not say whether a nozzle found inside the permissible range counts as an irregularity.
