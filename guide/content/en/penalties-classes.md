## Beyond the table

- **Not on the list.** For an irregularity MDG does not name, the oil company acts after an enquiry, following the principles of natural justice {{8.5.4}}.
- **Older guideline, replaced on 24.10.2024.** MDG 2012 (Ver. 7, 30.11.2022) ranked a missing clean toilet and short delivery with seals intact as major {{2012:8.3}}. MDG 2024 ranks both minor {{8.4}}. See [MDG 2012 against MDG 2024](@mdg-2012-vs-2024).
- **Kerosene.** Wholesale dealers have their own irregularity lists {{8.10}}. See [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers).
