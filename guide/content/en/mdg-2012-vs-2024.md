A fine you read about that is not in Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (MDG 2024) may come from MDG 2012: Version 7, effective 8 January 2013, last amended on 30.11.2022 {{2012:cover page}}. MDG 2024 is Version 1, effective 24.10.2024 {{cover page}}.

## What changed, at a glance

| Topic | MDG 2024 (24.10.2024), in force | Older guideline, replaced on 24.10.2024 (MDG 2012, Ver. 7) |
|---|---|---|
| Unclean toilet ([toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules)) | Minor. Warning-cum-guidance letter, then Rs 10,000, then Rs 25,000 per irregularity {{8.4}} | Major. Rs 15,000, then Rs 25,000, then the higher of Rs 35,000 and 45% of average monthly margin (last 6 months), plus 7 days' suspension or until fixed, whichever is later {{2012:8.3(vii)}} |
| Short delivery, W&M seals intact ([5-litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test)) | Minor. Same ladder as above {{8.4}} | Major. Rs 25,000 per nozzle found short beyond the legal limit, then Rs 50,000 per nozzle plus 15 days' suspension, then termination; the second and third within one year of the first {{2012:8.3(x)}} |
| Short delivery at the pump | Sales stop only beyond the legal limit. Within it, recalibration is due in 15 days {{5.1.2(a)}} | Sales stop; recalibrate and re-stamp first, even within the limit {{2012:5.1.2(a)}} |
| Wages ([staff wages](@service-staff-wages-uniform)) | No wage penalty line in Chapter 8. The duty stays and names PMSBY and PMJJBY {{1.5(x)}}; unlisted matters are acted on after an enquiry {{8.5.4}} | Own penalty: 20%, then 30%, then 40% of average monthly margin (last 3 months), plus 15 days' suspension at the third instance {{2012:8.3(ix)}} |
| Customer service standards ([the 20 points](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations)) | New {{1.6(xii)}} {{5.1.18}}, and a minor item in {{8.4}} | No such clause |
| Toilet protocol | Eight points, including access for all customers and transit travellers at all times (subject to safety protocols), and a Darpan QR code {{5.1.14(b)}} | Seven points, the last being that the door is not kept locked {{2012:5.1.14(b)}} |
| Appeals ([appeal](@inspection-appeal)) | Adds a committee of 2 EDs where an ED-rank authority terminated; SC/ST: a committee of two Directors {{8.9}} | ED (Retail) or a nominated ED; SC/ST: one Director {{2012:8.9}} |
| Smaller changes | Minor items a to h count as one irregularity {{8.4}}. The dealer keeps a record of written automation authorisations {{5.1.16}} | Items a to e {{2012:8.4}}. Email or signed letter only {{2012:5.1.16}} |

{{penalty-note}}

Old clause numbers: toilet {{2012:8.3(vii)}} is now {{8.4(iii)(g)}}; short delivery {{2012:8.3(x)}} is now {{8.4(iii)(h)}}; automation {{2012:8.3(viii)}} is now {{8.3(vii)}}.

## What did not change

Apart from spelling and layout, these read the same in both: the critical list and the ladders that remain {{8.2}} {{8.3}} {{8.4}}, the 10 AM density rule {{5.1.9}}, stock limits {{5.1.11}}, records {{5.1.12}}, sampling, the show cause timeline {{8.5.6}} {{8.5.7}} {{8.5.8}}, the kerosene chapter {{8.10}} and Annexures 1 to 13a.
