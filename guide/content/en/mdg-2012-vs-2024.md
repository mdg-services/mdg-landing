If an article or forward quotes a fine you cannot find in MDG 2024, it may be quoting MDG 2012. We read the two texts side by side. "MDG 2012" here means Version 7, effective 8 January 2013 and last amended on 30.11.2022 {{2012:cover page}}. "MDG 2024" means Version 1, effective 24.10.2024 {{cover page}}.

## What changed, at a glance

| Topic | MDG 2024 (24.10.2024), in force | MDG 2012 (Ver. 7, 30.11.2022), replaced |
|---|---|---|
| Unclean toilet | Minor. Warning-cum-guidance letter, then Rs 10,000, then Rs 25,000 per irregularity {{8.4}} | Major. Rs 15,000, then Rs 25,000, then Rs 35,000 or 45% of monthly margin, plus 7 days' suspension or until the defect is fixed, whichever is later {{2012:8.3(vii)}} |
| Short delivery, W&M seals intact | Minor. Warning-cum-guidance letter, then Rs 10,000, then Rs 25,000 per irregularity {{8.4}} | Major. Rs 25,000 per nozzle, then Rs 50,000 per nozzle plus 15 days, then termination, the second and third within one year of the first {{2012:8.3(x)}} |
| Short delivery at the pump | Sales stop only beyond the legal limit. Within it, sales continue and recalibration is due in 15 days {{5.1.2(a)}} | Sales stop; recalibrate and re-stamp first, even within the permissible limit {{2012:5.1.2(a)}} |
| Wages | No wage penalty line in Chapter 8. Wages stay a duty {{1.5(x)}} | Own penalty: 20%, then 30%, then 40% of monthly margin plus 15 days' suspension at the third instance {{2012:8.3(ix)}} |
| Customer service standards | New {{1.6(xii)}} {{5.1.18}}, and minor item (f) in {{8.4}} | No such clause |
| Toilet protocol | Eight points: open to all customers and transit travellers at all times, and a Darpan QR code {{5.1.14(b)}} | Seven points, including "Toilet door is not kept locked" {{2012:5.1.14(b)}} |
| Minor items counted as one | Items a to h {{8.4}} | Items a to e {{2012:8.4}} |
| Automated outlets: authorisation | Email or signed letter only, and the dealer must keep the record {{5.1.16}} | Email or signed letter only {{2012:5.1.16}} |
| Appeals | Adds a committee of 2 EDs where an ED-rank authority terminated. SC/ST: a committee of two Directors {{8.9}} | ED (Retail) or a nominated ED. SC/ST: one Director {{2012:8.9}} |

If you hold an old clause number: the toilet penalty moved from {{2012:8.3(vii)}} to {{8.4(iii)(g)}}. Short delivery with seals intact moved from {{2012:8.3(x)}} to {{8.4(iii)(h)}}. The automated outlet penalty moved from {{2012:8.3(viii)}} to {{8.3(vii)}}. The wage penalty at {{2012:8.3(ix)}} has no 2024 equivalent.

## The toilet penalty: two versions

The first column is in force. Older articles and forwards may quote the second. Confirm with your oil company which applies to your case. For the daily toilet checks themselves, see [toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules).

| | MDG 2024 (24.10.2024), in force | MDG 2012 Ver. 7 (30.11.2022), replaced |
|---|---|---|
| Class | Minor {{8.4}} | Major {{2012:8.3(vii)}} |
| First instance | Warning-cum-guidance letter | Rs 15,000 |
| Second instance | Rs 10,000 per irregularity | Rs 25,000 |
| Third instance onwards | Rs 25,000 per irregularity | Rs 35,000 or 45% of the monthly dealer margin (average of the last 6 months), whichever is higher, plus suspension of sales and supplies for 7 days or until the toilet defect is rectified, whichever is later |

**For example:** a pump's monthly dealer margin (average of the last 6 months) is Rs 2,00,000, and it is found with no water in the toilet for the third time within five years. Under MDG 2024 the action for a third instance is Rs 25,000 per irregularity. If the inspector also notes a missing density display on the same visit, MDG 2024 counts both as one minor irregularity, so it stays one fine {{8.4}}. Under the replaced MDG 2012, 45% of Rs 2,00,000 is Rs 90,000. That is higher than Rs 35,000, so the fine would have been Rs 90,000, with a suspension of at least 7 days.

## Short delivery with seals intact

| | MDG 2024 (24.10.2024), in force | MDG 2012 Ver. 7 (30.11.2022), replaced |
|---|---|---|
| Class | Minor {{8.4}} | Major {{2012:8.3(x)}} |
| First instance | Warning-cum-guidance letter | Rs 25,000 per nozzle found delivering short beyond the permissible limit in the Legal Metrology Act and Rules |
| Second instance | Rs 10,000 per irregularity | Rs 50,000 per nozzle plus 15 days' suspension, if within one year of the first |
| Third instance | Rs 25,000 per irregularity | Termination, if within one year of the first |

**At the pump, the handling changed too.** Under MDG 2012, sales through a unit stopped as soon as it delivered short. The unit had to be recalibrated and re-stamped before sales resumed, even when the short or excess delivery was within the permissible limit {{2012:5.1.2(a)}}. Under MDG 2024, sales through the unit or nozzle stop only if it delivers short beyond the limits in the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and its Rules. Inside the permissible limit, which the text gives as between ±10 ml and ±25 ml per 5 litres, the finding is recorded and sales continue. Recalibration and re-stamping must then be done within 15 days of the finding, or sales from that unit or nozzle are suspended {{5.1.2(a)}}.

Short delivery with W&M seals tampered did not change. It is critical in both editions, with termination at the first instance {{2012:8.2}} {{8.2}}. See the [5-litre test](@quantity-5-litre-test) and [W&M and totaliser seals](@quantity-seals).

## Wages: the penalty is gone, the duty is not

MDG 2012 had a wage penalty of its own. It was a major irregularity: 20% of the monthly dealer margin (average of the last 3 months) the first time, 30% the second time, and 40% plus 15 days' suspension from the third time {{2012:8.3(ix)}}. Its clause 5.1.18, "Payment of wages", asked for the higher of the company-notified wage and the state minimum wage, salaries paid by e-payment, PF, ESIC, bonus, leave and gratuity as notified, cover under PMSBY and PMJJBY, and records kept at the outlet {{2012:5.1.18}}.

MDG 2024 states the duty in general terms. The dealer must comply with all applicable statutory obligations, including minimum wages and other benefits (including PMSBY and PMJJBY) {{1.5(x)}}. The same line is a note under 5.1.18 {{5.1.18}}. The e-payment rule, the list of benefits, the "whichever is higher" formula and the record line are not printed in the 2024 text, and Chapter 8 has no wage penalty line.

Do not read the missing penalty as the duty going away. The note sits under the customer service heading, and the text does not say which class a wage lapse falls in. MDG also says the company can act, after an enquiry, on matters it does not list {{8.5.4}}. Ask your oil company how it treats a wage lapse. See [staff wages and uniform](@service-staff-wages-uniform).

## Customer service standards: new in 2024

In MDG 2012, clause 5.1.18 was "Payment of wages". In MDG 2024 it is "Customer Service Standards". The dealer must keep the minimum standard of service set by the oil company's circulars, and a deficiency attracts the action for minor irregularities {{5.1.18}}. The same duty is now in {{1.6(xii)}} and is listed as item (f) of the minor irregularities {{8.4}}. The circular of 20.03.2025 turned it into 20 points: see the [20-point checklist](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations).

## Toilet rules: what was added

- **Access.** MDG 2012 point vii said the toilet door must not be kept locked {{2012:5.1.14(b)}}. MDG 2024 point vii says the toilet must be accessible to all customers and transit travellers at all times, subject to safety protocols {{5.1.14(b)}}.
- **Feedback.** MDG 2024 adds point viii: a "Darpan" QR code near the toilet for feedback {{5.1.14(b)}}.
- **What triggers a letter.** MDG 2024 adds a flush that is not working. It replaces the trigger "Toilet door found to be locked" with a toilet found not accessible to any customer or transit traveller, or a complaint to that effect from one. The photograph is taken or the complaint is shared, and a letter listing the penalty is issued instantly {{5.1.14(b)}}.
- **Unchanged.** Clean at all times, lighting, water, a working latch, signage, the protocol displayed near the toilet and a maintenance sheet kept and displayed are in both editions.

## Other changes

- **Minor items counted as one.** In MDG 2024, items a to h are one irregularity for the purpose of action {{8.4}}. A toilet lapse, a service gap and short delivery with seals intact found on one visit are one minor irregularity. In MDG 2012 the toilet and short delivery were major and sat outside the group {{2012:8.4}}.
- **Automated outlets.** MDG 2024 adds that the dealer must keep a record of the written authorisation (email or signed letter from a company official) {{5.1.16}}. See [manual mode authorisation](@automation-manual-mode).
- **Appeals.** A termination approved by an authority of ED rank in the State Office now goes to a committee of 2 EDs. SC/ST terminations go to a committee of two Directors other than Director (Marketing) {{8.9}}. In MDG 2012 the appeal went to the ED (Retail) or a nominated ED, and for SC/ST to one Director other than Director (Marketing) {{2012:8.9}}. See [appeal against termination](@inspection-appeal).
- **Personal hearing.** The hearing before a critical termination is the same. Only the list of offices named changed: "Regional office" became "State Office (Retail)" {{2012:8.6}} {{8.6}}.
- **Clause numbers.** Safety equipment is {{1.6(xi)}} in MDG 2024. MDG 2012 printed two clauses numbered 1.6(x).

## What did not change

We compared the two texts word for word. Apart from the items above, the network figures in the introduction, the date of a government letter quoted in 5.1.1, and changes of spelling and layout, they read the same.

- The critical list and its action: termination at the first instance {{8.2}}.
- The major ladder: 15 days, 30 days, termination. Branded fuel: recovery of the differential price, then termination. Automated outlets: Rs 1,00,000, then Rs 2,00,000 plus 7 days, then termination {{8.3}}.
- The minor ladder: warning-cum-guidance letter, Rs 10,000 per irregularity, Rs 25,000 per irregularity {{8.4}}.
- Reference density by 10 AM {{5.1.9}}, stock variation limits {{5.1.11}}, the records list {{5.1.12}}, and the 3-tier sampling chapter with its timelines (Chapter 2).
- The show cause timeline: a notice within 30 days, 10 days to reply, and a speaking order preferably within 45 days {{8.5.6}} {{8.5.7}} {{8.5.8}}. Also payment of fines within 30 days {{8.7}} and the five-year count of instances {{8.5.5}}.
- The kerosene (SKO) dealer chapter and its penalties {{8.10}}.
- Annexures 1 to 13a. MDG 2024 only adds a handing-over form at the end.

## Old offences and the new edition

The five-year count in {{8.5.5}} is worded the same in both editions: irregularities under the previous MDG within five years count as instances. The 2024 text does not explain how a finding that was major in 2012 is counted now that the toilet and short delivery are minor. It also does not set out how cases open on 24.10.2024 are handled. If your inspection was close to that date, or you have an earlier finding, ask your oil company which edition and which class it applied. For a specific notice, speak to your oil company's officer or a lawyer.
