## The limit and what it covers

MDG 2024 lets a normal gap between book stock and dip pass. It judges the rest only after the allowances below are counted {{5.1.11}}. The clause covers MS and HSD. Kerosene dealerships have their own list {{8.10}}: see [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers).

## The allowances you can count

| Allowance | Figure | Counted |
|---|---|---|
| Normal operating variation | 4% of tank stock (Annexure 9 works it on the closing dip) | Yes {{5.1.11}} {{annexure 9}} |
| Petrol (MS): evaporation and handling loss | 0.75% of the litres sold, up to a 600 KL annual average; 0.60% on the part beyond | Yes, see the Note {{5.1.11}} |
| Diesel (HSD): handling loss | 0.25% of the litres sold, up to a 600 KL annual average; 0.20% on the part beyond | Yes, see the Note {{5.1.11}} |
| Shrinkage allowance and temperature variation allowance (TVA), MS and HSD | A rupee amount per KL, which Annexures 8 and 10 turn into a percentage of the net price | Only where and when it applies {{5.1.11}} {{annexure 8}} {{annexure 10}} |

Read plainly, the higher handling-loss rate covers the first 600 KL and the lower rate only the part above it. For example, with 800 KL of MS sold: 600 x 0.75% + 200 x 0.60% = 4.5 + 1.2 = 5.7 KL. The [stock variation calculator](@tools-stock-variation) runs the whole sum.

> **Note:** MDG leaves two points to your oil company, so confirm both before relying on a figure.
> - For a negative variation, the clause words the limit as "up to 4%". It does not say whether handling loss, shrinkage and TVA also count, or how they apply to a positive variation {{5.1.11}}. The [calculator](@tools-stock-variation) sets a gain against the 4% alone.
> - It does not say over what period the 600 KL annual average is counted {{5.1.11}}.

## When the gap is beyond the limit

MDG does not define "positive" and "negative". In the trade, positive means the tank holds more than the books, and negative means less. A negative variation lets sales continue while the dealer explains, and supply stops if the explanation is not found satisfactory {{5.1.11}}. The [positive vs negative page](@stock-positive-negative) sets the two cases side by side.

> **Note:** a positive variation beyond the limit stops sales of all products at once, before any lab result comes back {{5.1.11}}.

## What it costs

| What is found | Class | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Variation beyond the limit, sample passes, nothing else established | Major {{8.3(iv)}} | 1st instance: 15 days' suspension of sales and supplies. 2nd: 30 days. 3rd: termination of the dealership |
| Positive variation, sample fails | Action as for adulteration, critical {{5.1.11}} {{8.2}} | Termination at the first instance |
| Positive variation, sample passes but an unauthorised purchase is established | Unauthorised purchase, critical {{5.1.6}} {{8.2}} | Termination at the first instance |
