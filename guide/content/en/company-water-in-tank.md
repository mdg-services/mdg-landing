## Who checks the tank for water

| When | Who | What | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| Every day | Dealer | Records the water dip. It forms part of the daily stock register, and each tank's morning dips are also kept | {{1.4.1(b)}} {{1.3}} |
| Every maintenance visit | Company's representative | Dips the underground tanks for water | {{4.4}} |

## If water is found

1. **Report it at once** to the controlling company office {{1.4.1(b)}}.
2. **Steps are taken to remove the water** {{4.4}}.
3. **Petrol blended with ethanol:** once the water is out, the ethanol content is checked before the product is released. If it is outside the desired range, stop sales and tell the controlling office {{4.3(c)}}.

The test follows IS 2796 (E5, E10), IS 17586 (E12, E15) or IS 17021 (E20), each in its latest revision {{annexure 7}}. Check the current version.

## If water seeps into the tank

The company's representative checks all the tank openings and arranges the fix: new packing and gaskets, and any missing caps put back {{4.3(c)}}.

> **Note:** MDG does not say who removes the water, how fast the company must come, or what the desired ethanol range is. Ask your oil company.

Water in a tank lorry compartment before decanting is a different case: see [short dip and water](@tanker-short-dip-water) and [water finding paste](@tanker-water-finding-paste). For other repairs, see [breakdowns and leaks](@company-breakdowns-leaks).
