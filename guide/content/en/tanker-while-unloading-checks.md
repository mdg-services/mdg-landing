## The card

The full 14 steps, in order, are on [receiving a tanker](@receiving-a-tanker).

| Stage | Check | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Before | Match the invoice to the lorry: lorry number, seal or lock, compartments with product and quantity, departure time, 15°C density, dip rod and certificate | {{1.1}} |
| Before | Level ground, 10 minutes to settle, earthing and bonding, DCP extinguisher near the lorry, no plastic or PVC hose | {{1.2(i)}} |
| Before | Seal or lock on the manifold and manhole covers against the invoice; release the master valve levers; then the certificate, the dip rod, and every compartment's dip and water paste. See [seal or lock mismatch](@tanker-seal-or-lock-mismatch) and [dip rod and certificate](@tanker-dip-rod-and-certificate) | {{1.2(ii)}} {{1.2(iii)}} {{1.2(v)}} {{1.2(vi)}} {{1.2(vii)}} |
| Before | Stop sales from the underground tank that will receive the product | {{1.2(vii)}} |
| Before | Each compartment's 15°C density within ±3.0 kg/m³ of the invoice, written in the register; then bottom retention samples, label signed by you and the driver | {{1.2(iv)}} {{1.2(viii)}} {{1.2(x)}} {{2.3}} |
| Before | Short dip or water: make it up, record it on the invoice copies, both sign (see [short dip and water](@tanker-short-dip-water)) | {{1.2(xi)}} |
| During | Sales from that tank stay stopped until decantation is complete; each product goes into its correct underground tank; the extinguisher stays near | {{1.2(vii)}} {{1.2(xi)}} {{1.2(i)}} |
| After | Lorry fully empty before it is released; stock register or stock display board entry | {{1.2(xii)}} {{1.2(xiii)}} |
| After | At least 50 litres through the pump, then a nozzle sample; its 15°C density goes in the density register | {{1.2(xiv)}} {{annexure 1}} |

## If something stops the job

- **Driver will not sign the sample label.** Do not decant. Contact the supply location or the sales officer {{2.3 Note (i)}}.
- **Unusual deviation in time or route.** Inform the supply location (before accepting the lorry) or the sales officer, and decant only on further instructions {{1.5(vii)}} {{2.3 Note (ii)}}. See [VTS and route deviation](@tanker-vts).
- **Doubt about quality.** Contact the supply location or the Area Field Officer {{1.2(ix)}}.

## What MDG leaves out

MDG says nothing about where staff stand or about mobile phones during unloading. It does require the dealer to observe PESO rules {{1.5(ii)}}; ask your oil company what applies at your outlet. Whether other tanks may keep selling is on [selling during unloading](@tanker-selling-during-decant).
