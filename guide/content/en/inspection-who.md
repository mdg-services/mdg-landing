## Who can inspect or sample

| Who | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Oil company officials | Sales officers, inspecting teams and senior officers sample at random, covering at least 2% of outlets every month | {{2.4}} |
| Mobile labs | Surprise checks: nozzle samples tested at the outlet. Run for the whole industry or by one company | {{7.1}} {{7.2}} {{2.4.4}} |
| Joint industry teams | Can sample whatever the quick tests showed; samples are jointly signed | {{2.4.3}} |
| Authorised agencies, and any authority the law empowers | Named among those who may draw samples. Central and State authorities can also check a dealership under other laws | {{2.2.2.2}} {{8.7}} |

## What the dealer is given

| When | What | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| At the visit | The sample label, with your signature and the name, designation and oil company of whoever drew the sample | {{annexure 4}} {{annexure 11}} |
| At the visit | One of the three sets of sample bottles, and payment for the samples with a cash memo | {{2.4.1}} {{2.4.2}} {{2.2.2.2}} |
| At the visit | A joint memo, which you acknowledge, if the sealed box of tank lorry samples is taken into custody | {{2.5(C)}} |
| Afterwards | Lab results in writing, preferably within 30 days of drawal | {{2.5(F)}} |
| Afterwards | A mobile lab test report: one of five copies. After a failed sample you sign every copy with the outlet seal | {{7.6.1}} |
| Afterwards | A show cause notice, within 30 days of the inspection (of the test results, where samples were drawn), with the reports it relies on | {{8.5.6}} |

## What the inspector records

- Each of the 20 service points as YES or NO {{circular}}.
- That your tank lorry samples were not made available {{2.5(E)}}.
- On the sample label, the density the inspector observed beside the density in your register {{annexure 4}}.
- After a failed mobile lab sample, the meter readings and tank dips {{7.6.1(b)}}.

## What MDG leaves open

- **Identity.** MDG does not say the officer must show an identity card or a letter. General practice, not an MDG rule: ask for the name and designation, note the arrival time, and file both with the visit's papers. Ask while the visit goes on, because stopping it is a major irregularity {{5.1.8}}.
- **A report on the day.** MDG does not say a general inspection report is handed over at the visit. You keep a file of inspection reports {{1.5(iv)}} and the inspection record for five years {{5.1.12}}. Ask your oil company for the report of a visit.
