## The short version

Marketing Discipline Guidelines 2024 (MDG 2024) is the set of rules the oil companies apply to their petrol pump dealers under the dealership agreement. It binds the dealer through the dealership agreement.

MDG 2024 puts the customer's basics on the dealer: the right fuel, in the right quantity, at the right price {{1.6(i)}}. On top of that come the amenities a customer expects at a pump: air, a clean toilet, a telephone, a first aid box, a complaint book, and notices that say who to call {{1.6}}.

In March 2025 a circular issued under MDG 2024 added a 20-point checklist called the Minimum Service Obligations. The inspector ticks each point YES or NO {{5.1.18}} {{circular}}. [All 20 points are explained here](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations).

> **Note:** a missing facility is a *minor* irregularity, but it still carries a penalty: a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity from the third time {{8.4}}. These are minimum amounts. The oil company may act higher, up to termination {{8.5.1}}, once the irregularity is established {{8.5.2}}.

This page is about petrol pump (RO) dealers. Kerosene dealers have their own duties: see [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers).

## What the dealer must provide

### Facilities

| Facility | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Right fuel, quantity and price | The dealer makes sure the product is of good quality, in the right quantity, at the right price | {{1.6(i)}} |
| Air | Offered to customers whenever the outlet is operating, with a calibrated air gauge. The circular asks for it to be manned | {{1.6(ii)}} {{5.1.14(a)}} {{circular}} |
| Clean toilet | Available on the premises, checked daily, open to all customers and transit travellers. See [toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules) | {{1.6(vi)}} {{5.1.14(b)}} |
| Telephone | A telephone available on the premises | {{1.6(vii)}} {{5.1.14(a)}} |
| First aid box | A box stocked with valid medicines that are within their expiry dates. See [first aid box](@service-first-aid-box) | {{1.6(ix)}} {{5.1.14(a)}} |
| Drinking water | The circular asks the dealer to make sure purified drinking water is available | {{circular}} |
| Digital payment | Card, loyalty machine, QR code and similar should be available and switched on | {{circular}} |
| Complaint book | Kept at the outlet, handed over on request, with a displayed message saying it exists. See [complaint book](@service-complaint-book) | {{1.6(iv)}} |

### People and forecourt

| What | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Service and behaviour | Quick service and courteous behaviour at all times | {{1.6(iii)}} {{circular}} |
| Pumps manned | An attendant at every dispensing unit during working hours | {{circular}} |
| Queue | The queue on the forecourt is marshalled and kept orderly | {{circular}} |
| Products | Every product always available at the outlet | {{circular}} |
| Zero before delivery | The pump attendant shows the zero setting before each delivery. See [zero before delivery](@quantity-zero-before-delivery) | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{circular}} |
| Light and cleanliness | The outlet is well lit and clean, to the housekeeping standards the oil companies set | {{1.6(x)}} {{5.1.17}} |
| Driveway | Entry and exit roads are clear of obstacles, clean and well lit | {{circular}} |
| Safety | Safety equipment as the law requires, kept in working order, with trained staff to handle it | {{1.6(xi)}} |

### Notices

The dealer also displays working hours, holiday notices, the complaint-book message, the contact names and numbers of the dealer and the oil company, prices, density, opening stock and more. The detail is in [boards and notices at a petrol pump](@service-display-boards).

## Is air filling at a petrol pump free?

MDG asks for the air facility. It does not say whether the dealer may charge for it.

Clause 1.6(ii) says only that the dealer provides air to customers while the outlet is operating {{1.6(ii)}}. Clause 5.1.14(a) mentions a calibrated air gauge {{5.1.14(a)}}. The circular asks for the air facility to be manned {{circular}}.

If you are charged and are not sure that is allowed, ask your oil company.

## What a customer can ask for

Each of these is a duty on the dealer:

- **Zero before filling.** The attendant shows the zero setting before each delivery {{1.4.2(f)}}.
- **Filter paper.** The dealer keeps Whatman 2 filter paper and gives it to customers on request, so they can check petrol quality {{1.4.1(a)}}. See [the filter paper test](@density-filter-paper).
- **The price.** The retail selling price of each product sold is displayed {{5.1.15}}.
- **The complaint book,** handed over on request {{1.6(iv)}}.
- **Names and numbers** of the dealer and of the oil company people a customer can call with a complaint {{1.6(viii)}}.
- **The toilet,** open to all customers and transit travellers at all times, within the safety protocols {{5.1.14(b)}}.

MDG does not say whether a customer may use the dealer's 5-litre measure. It says only that the dealer keeps one, verified by Weights & Measures, for exclusive use in testing the pump {{1.4.2(b)}}.

## The 20-point circular of March 2025

Clause 5.1.18 tells the dealer to keep to the minimum standard of customer service that the oil companies' circulars set {{5.1.18}} {{1.6(xii)}}. The circular dated 20.03.2025 sets 20 points. The inspecting officer checks each one and records YES or NO. If something is missing, the oil company acts under 5.1.18 or the clause that fits. The circular sets no penalty of its own {{circular}}.

| Group | The points in the circular |
|---|---|
| Products and staff | Every product available; every pump manned during working hours; queue marshalled and kept orderly; courteous staff |
| Meter | Zero shown before delivery, closing reading shown after it |
| Facilities | Purified drinking water; manned air; the toilet protocol; Q&Q kit and first aid box; digital payment |
| Cleanliness | Pump units, pedestals, islands and lube display; driveway entry and exit; emblem, light poles, monolith, signage and fascia; sales room; utility bins and dustbins |
| Notices | Safety signs (such as No Smoking and switch off engine); working hours; the name and phone number of the oil company person to call with a complaint; prices of all products |
| Anything else | Other legal compliance and directions from a competent authority |

The main MDG 2024 text does not mention drinking water, digital payment, queue marshalling or manned pumps. Those four come only from the circular {{circular}}.

The circular also asks the oil companies to pass it to every dealer and keep the dealer's written acknowledgement on record {{circular}}.

## What a missing facility costs

MDG 2024 puts shortfalls in the customer-facing areas below in the minor class {{8.4}}. The topic pages have the detail.

| Area | What falls under it | More |
|---|---|---|
| Amenities | Air, telephone, first aid box {{5.1.14(a)}}; the toilet {{5.1.14(b)}} | [first aid box](@service-first-aid-box), [toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules) |
| Notices and displays | Prices {{5.1.15}}; density, stock, pump sticker, nozzle names and complaint or emergency contacts {{5.1.17}} | [boards and notices](@service-display-boards) |
| Complaint book | Not kept, or not handed over when a customer asks {{5.1.17}} | [complaint book](@service-complaint-book) |
| Housekeeping and people | Poor housekeeping; driveway salesmen without uniform and badge {{5.1.17}} | [staff and uniform](@service-staff-wages-uniform) |
| Service standards | The circular points that have no clause of their own, such as drinking water and digital payment {{5.1.18}} {{circular}} | [the 20 points](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations) |

The circular says action follows 5.1.18 or the clause that fits {{circular}}. Several of its points name another clause: air and the first aid box point to 5.1.14(a), the toilet to 5.1.14(b), and cleanliness and the complaint contact to 5.1.17 {{circular}}. The text does not say how an inspector picks between them.

The action is the same in every area: a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity from the third time {{8.4}}.

Read the amounts with these points:

- They are the minimum. The oil company may take higher action, up to termination {{8.5.1}}.
- The irregularity must be established before any action is taken {{8.5.2}}.
- Instances are counted over five years, and irregularities under the previous guideline within those five years count too {{8.5.5}}.
- Clause 8.4 says irregularities under lines (a) to (h) of 8.4(iii) count as **one** irregularity for taking action. That note does not name the air, telephone and first aid line (8.4(ii)) {{8.4}}. Clause 8.5.3 says that when two or more irregularities are found at the same visit, each is counted as an instance in its own class, and action is taken on each with a compounding effect {{8.5.3}}. The text does not say how the two fit together. Ask your oil company how it counts a visit with more than one finding.

More in [penalties](@penalties) and, for fines that are not paid, [repeat offences and unpaid fines](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid).

## Where a customer can complain

- Call the dealer or oil company person whose name and number must be displayed at the outlet {{1.6(viii)}}.
- Ask for the complaint book. The dealer must give it on request {{1.6(iv)}}.
- For the oil company's helpline, use its official website. This guide does not print helpline numbers.
- For the toilet, MDG 2024 lists a customer's or traveller's complaint that it cannot be accessed as one of the situations in which a letter listing the penalty is issued {{5.1.14(b)}}.

The oil company is also meant to draw fuel samples after a specific complaint against an outlet {{2.4}}. Read more in [the complaint book guide](@service-complaint-book).
