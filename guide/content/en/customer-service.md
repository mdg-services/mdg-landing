## The short version

MDG 2024 puts the customer's basics on the dealer: the right fuel, in the right quantity, at the right price {{1.6(i)}}. On top of that come the amenities a customer expects at a pump. They are air, a clean toilet, a telephone, a first aid box, a complaint book, and notices that say who to call {{1.6}}.

In March 2025 the oil companies added a 20-point checklist called the Minimum Service Obligations. The inspector ticks each point YES or NO {{5.1.18}} {{circular}}. [All 20 points are explained here](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations).

> **Note:** a missing facility is a *minor* irregularity, but it still costs money. The first time is a warning-cum-guidance letter. The second time is Rs 10,000 per irregularity. The third time onwards is Rs 25,000 per irregularity {{8.4}}.

This page is about petrol pump (RO) dealers. Kerosene dealers have their own duties: see [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers).

## What the dealer must provide

### Facilities

| Facility | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Right fuel, quantity and price | The dealer ensures quality product at the correct quantity and the correct price | {{1.6(i)}} |
| Air | Provided to customers during the outlet's operating hours, with a calibrated air gauge. The circular asks for it to be manned | {{1.6(ii)}} {{5.1.14(a)}} {{circular}} |
| Clean toilet | Available on the premises. Daily checks, open to all customers and transit travellers. See [toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules) | {{1.6(vi)}} {{5.1.14(b)}} |
| Telephone | Telephone facilities available on the premises | {{1.6(vii)}} {{5.1.14(a)}} |
| First aid box | A box with valid medicines, not beyond their expiry dates. See [first aid box](@service-first-aid-box) | {{1.6(ix)}} {{5.1.14(a)}} |
| Drinking water | The circular asks the dealer to ensure purified drinking water is available | {{circular}} |
| Digital payment | Card, loyalty machine, QR code and similar should be available and enabled | {{circular}} |
| Complaint book | Kept, handed over on demand, with a displayed message that it exists. See [complaint book](@service-complaint-book) | {{1.6(iv)}} |

### People and forecourt

| What | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Service and behaviour | Prompt service and courteous behaviour at all times | {{1.6(iii)}} {{circular}} |
| Pumps manned | Every dispensing unit manned by the attendant staff during working hours | {{circular}} |
| Queue | Queue marshalled and regulated on the forecourt | {{circular}} |
| Products | All products always available at the outlet | {{circular}} |
| Zero before delivery | The pump attendant shows the zero setting before each delivery. See [zero before delivery](@quantity-zero-before-delivery) | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{circular}} |
| Light and cleanliness | Outlet adequately lit and clean, to the oil companies' housekeeping standards | {{1.6(x)}} {{5.1.17}} |
| Driveway | Entry and exit roads free of obstacles, clean and properly lit | {{circular}} |
| Safety | Safety equipment as the law requires, kept in working order, with trained staff to use it | {{1.6(xi)}} |

### Notices

The dealer must also display working hours, holiday notices, the complaint-book message, the dealer's and oil company's contact names and numbers, prices, density, opening stock and more. The full list is in [boards and notices at a petrol pump](@service-display-boards).

## Is air filling at a petrol pump free?

The MDG text asks for the air facility. It does not say whether the dealer may charge for it.

Clause 1.6(ii) says only that the dealer provides air to customers during the outlet's operating hours {{1.6(ii)}}. Clause 5.1.14(a) adds a calibrated air gauge {{5.1.14(a)}}. The circular asks for the air facility to be manned {{circular}}.

IndianOil's list of boards for its outlets includes a customer rights board that lists free air among what is available. That is the oil company's board, not MDG text. If you are charged and are not sure it is allowed, ask your oil company.

## What a customer can ask for

Each of these is a duty on the dealer:

- **Zero before filling.** The attendant shows the zero setting before each delivery {{1.4.2(f)}}.
- **Filter paper.** The dealer keeps Whatman 2 filter paper and gives it to customers on demand, so they can check petrol quality {{1.4.1(a)}}. See [the filter paper test](@density-filter-paper).
- **The price.** The authorised selling price of every product is displayed {{5.1.15}}.
- **The complaint book,** handed over on demand {{1.6(iv)}}.
- **Names and numbers** of the dealer and of the oil company people to call with a complaint {{1.6(viii)}}.
- **The toilet,** open to all customers and transit travellers at all times, subject to safety protocols {{5.1.14(b)}}.

IndianOil's customer rights and “Check & Fill” boards also tell customers to ask for a cash memo and say a customer can check the pump with the dealer's 5-litre measure. Those come from the oil company's board list, not from MDG clauses. MDG itself only says the dealer keeps a verified 5-litre measure for pump testing, and does not mention customers using it {{1.4.2(b)}}.

## The 20-point circular of March 2025

Clause 5.1.18 tells the dealer to keep the minimum standard of customer service set in oil company circulars {{5.1.18}} {{1.6(xii)}}. The circular dated 20.03.2025 sets 20 points. The inspecting officer checks each one and records YES or NO. If something is missing, the oil company acts under 5.1.18 or the clause that fits {{circular}}.

| Group | The points in the circular |
|---|---|
| Products and staff | All products available; every pump manned during working hours; queue marshalled and regulated; courteous behaviour |
| Meter | Zero shown before delivery and the closing reading shown after delivery |
| Facilities | Purified drinking water; manned air; toilet protocol; first aid box and Q&Q kit; digital payment |
| Cleanliness | Dispensing units, pedestals, island and lube display unit; driveway entry and exit; emblem, light poles, monolith, signage and fascia; sales room; utility bins and dustbins |
| Notices | Safety signs (such as No Smoking and switch off engine); working hours; name and phone number of the oil company person to call with a complaint; prices of all products |
| Anything else | Other legal compliance and directions from a competent authority |

The main MDG 2024 text does not mention drinking water, digital payment, queue marshalling or manned pumps. Those four come only from the circular {{circular}}.

The circular also says oil companies must pass it to every dealer and keep the dealer's written acknowledgement on record {{circular}}.

## What a missing facility costs

| If this is missing | Defined in | Listed as minor in |
|---|---|---|
| Air, telephone, or first aid box with in-date medicines | {{5.1.14(a)}} | {{8.4(ii)}} |
| Clean toilet | {{5.1.14(b)}} | {{8.4(iii)(g)}} |
| Display of prices | {{5.1.15}} | {{8.4(iii)(a)}} |
| Display of density, opening stock, zero sticker, product name on each nozzle, complaint and emergency contacts | {{5.1.17}} | {{8.4(iii)(b)}} |
| Complaint book kept or given on demand | {{5.1.17}} | {{8.4(iii)(c)}} |
| Housekeeping | {{5.1.17}} | {{8.4(iii)(d)}} |
| Driveway salesmen in uniform with badges | {{5.1.17}} | {{8.4(iii)(e)}} |
| A minimum service obligation with no clause of its own (drinking water, digital payment and so on) | {{5.1.18}} {{circular}} | {{8.4(iii)(f)}} |

The circular says action follows 5.1.18 or the clause that fits {{circular}}. Several of its points name another clause: air and the first aid box point to 5.1.14(a), the toilet to 5.1.14(b), and cleanliness and the complaint contact to 5.1.17 {{circular}}. The text does not say how an inspector picks between them.

The action for every line is the same: a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity the third time onwards {{8.4}}.

Five rules that decide how it adds up:

- Irregularities under lines (a) to (h) of 8.4(iii) count as **one** irregularity for taking action. Air, telephone and first aid are a separate line, 8.4(ii) {{8.4}}.
- If two or more irregularities are found at the same visit, 8.5.3 says each is counted as an instance against its class and action is taken for each, with a compounding effect {{8.5.3}}. The text does not say how that sits with the “count as one” note for lines (a) to (h). Ask your oil company how it counts a mixed finding.
- Second and third instances are counted over five years {{8.5.5}}.
- The actions in MDG are the minimum. The oil company can act higher {{8.5.1}}.
- A fine with no suspension must be paid within 30 days of the company's notice. If not, sales and supplies are suspended from the 31st day, for 15 days {{8.7}}.

More in [penalties](@penalties) and [repeat offences and unpaid fines](@penalties-repeat-and-unpaid).

## Where a customer can complain

- Call the dealer or oil company person whose name and number must be displayed at the outlet {{1.6(viii)}}.
- Ask for the complaint book. The dealer must give it on demand {{1.6(iv)}}.
- For the oil company's helpline, use its official website. This guide does not print helpline numbers.
- For the toilet, MDG 2024 lists a customer's or traveller's complaint that it is not accessible as one of the situations that lead to a letter listing the penalty {{5.1.14(b)}}.

The oil company is also meant to draw fuel samples after a specific complaint against an outlet {{2.4}}. Read more in [the complaint book guide](@service-complaint-book).
