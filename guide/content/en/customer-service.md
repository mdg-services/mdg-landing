## What the dealer must provide

| Facility | What MDG says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Quality, quantity, price | The dealer ensures quality product, correct quantity and correct price | {{1.6(i)}} |
| Air | Offered during operating hours, with a calibrated gauge. The circular asks for it to be manned. MDG does not say whether a charge may be made: ask your oil company | {{1.6(ii)}} {{5.1.14(a)}} {{circular}} |
| Clean toilet | On the premises, checked daily, open to all customers and transit travellers. See [toilet rules](@service-toilet-rules) | {{1.6(vi)}} {{5.1.14(b)}} |
| Telephone | Available on the premises | {{1.6(vii)}} {{5.1.14(a)}} |
| First aid box | Valid, in-date medicines. See [first aid box](@service-first-aid-box) | {{1.6(ix)}} {{5.1.14(a)}} |
| Complaint book | Kept, handed over on request, with a displayed message. See [complaint book](@service-complaint-book) | {{1.6(iv)}} |
| Zero before delivery | The attendant shows zero before each delivery; the circular adds the closing reading after it. See [zero before delivery](@quantity-zero-before-delivery) | {{1.4.2(f)}} {{circular}} |
| Safety equipment | As the law requires, in working order, with trained staff | {{1.6(xi)}} |
| Lighting and housekeeping | The outlet is kept lit and clean to the oil companies' housekeeping standards | {{1.6(x)}} {{5.1.17}} |
| Notices | Working hours, holiday notices, contact names and numbers, prices and more. See [boards and notices](@service-display-boards) | {{1.6(v)}} {{1.6(viii)}} {{5.1.15}} |
| Drinking water | Purified drinking water available | {{circular}} |
| Digital payment | Card, loyalty machine, QR code and similar, switched on | {{circular}} |

Drinking water, digital payment, queue marshalling and manned pumps appear only in the circular of 20.03.2025, not in the main MDG text {{circular}}. The circular lists 20 points in all, and the inspector marks each YES or NO {{5.1.18}} {{circular}}. [All 20 points are explained here](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations). Courtesy {{1.6(iii)}} and staff duties are in [staff wages and uniform](@service-staff-wages-uniform).

## What a customer can ask for

A customer can ask for the zero setting before filling, filter paper to check petrol {{1.4.1(a)}}, the complaint book, the posted prices and contact numbers, and an open toilet. See [what a customer can check](@quantity-zero-before-delivery), [the filter paper test](@density-filter-paper) and [the complaint book guide](@service-complaint-book).

## What a missing facility costs

Air, telephone, first aid box, toilet, complaint book, notices, housekeeping and the circular's service points are minor irregularities: a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, Rs 25,000 per irregularity from the third time {{8.4}}. The circular sets no penalty of its own {{circular}}.
