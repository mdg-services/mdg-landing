## What to show, and who must be at the pump

| What | What MDG and the circular say | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Working hours | Shown prominently at the outlet. The inspector marks it YES or NO (point 17) | {{1.6(v)}} {{circular}} |
| Holiday notice | Put up "well in time". No number of days is given | {{1.6(v)}} |
| Manned units | Every dispensing unit has a CA, DSM or CSA during working hours (point 2) | {{circular}} |
| Air | Offered during operating hours; the circular asks for it to be manned (point 6) | {{1.6(ii)}} {{circular}} |

MDG sets no minimum opening hours, no board format, and no definition of working hours or operating hours. Ask your oil company. Other boards are in [boards a pump must display](@service-display-boards).

## What a miss costs

Working hours and manned units are points of the circular. A NO is acted on under 5.1.18 or the respective clause, and 5.1.18 is a minor irregularity {{circular}} {{5.1.18}} {{8.4(iii)(f)}}. The action is a warning-cum-guidance letter the first time, Rs 10,000 per irregularity the second time, and Rs 25,000 per irregularity from the third time {{8.4}}.

{{penalty-note}}

Holiday notices are not among the 20 points, and 8.4 does not name them. MDG does not say what follows if one is missing. Check your oil company's circulars: the dealer keeps to them {{1.6(xii)}}. The [20 points](@inspection-minimum-service-obligations) are explained separately.

## An example

For example (a made-up outlet): the board says 6 AM to 10 PM. At 8 PM an inspector finds one dispensing unit with nobody at it. Point 2 is marked NO, and the action is under 5.1.18 {{circular}} {{5.1.18}}. If it is the first instance, the minimum is a warning-cum-guidance letter {{8.4}}.

Kerosene (SKO) dealers have a separate rule: the premises are not kept closed during working hours on a working day without the oil company's prior written permission {{chapter 6, item 3}}. See [kerosene dealers](@sko-dealers).
