Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what the Khata Kavach apps collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. There are two apps: Khata Kavach Business, used by the owner, managers and counter staff of a business that gives goods on credit (a fuel station, a building material shop, a gas agency, a distributor, a dairy, or any other trade), and Khata Kavach Customer, used by the businesses and people who buy from it on credit. Both are operated by VRUOOM PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us", "our"), the company behind MDG Services. Khata Kavach is a shared credit account book: it replaces paper slips and hand-written khata. It records; it does not lend, hold or move money.

1. Who this policy is for

Everyone who signs in to either app: owners, managers and counter staff of a business, and its customers. A business is set up by us at the owner's request; the business adds its staff and its customers. Nobody can sign up on their own.

2. Information we collect

Only what the shared account book and its bills need. We do not collect data for advertising, we do not sell it, and the apps contain no advertising or analytics trackers.

The apps do not access your location, contacts, microphone or files, and they do not read your photo library on their own. The Android apps ask for two permissions only, both about the network: internet access and knowing whether the phone is online.

Photos. A photo is added only when the counter staff tap the photo button on a sale. The phone's own camera app or gallery then opens, and only the photo that is taken or picked comes into Khata Kavach. On a phone with no signal the photo is kept on the phone and sent after its sale. Version 1.0 of the Android apps has no photo button; it comes with version 1.1. A business logo is added the same way, by the owner.

3. How we use it

  • To run the shared account book between a business and its customers.
  • To show each side the same entries, photos, bills, balances and requests.
  • To make a business's bills: the numbered bill PDF, with the business's details and the customer's billing details.
  • To keep the service secure: sign-ins, limits on wrong PIN attempts, and switching off lost phones.
  • To fix problems and answer support requests.

When a customer pays a bill by UPI, the customer's UPI app opens with the amount filled in and the money goes straight to the business. We do not take part in the payment. The business records it in the app.

4. Who can see it

A business sees its own customers, their vehicles, requests, sales, photos, bills and payments. A customer sees only their own accounts, vehicles, requests, sales, photos, bills and payments, at each business they buy from. Counter staff see what they need to serve a vehicle, not customers' phone numbers. No business sees another business's data and no customer sees another customer's.

Bill links. A bill PDF is opened through a private link that the business shares, for example on WhatsApp. Anyone who has the link can open that bill, and the link does not expire, so the business should share it only with the customer it is meant for. Please do not pass a bill link on to others.

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with the companies that host the service for us (cloud servers and file storage: Amazon Web Services; database: MongoDB Atlas; website hosting: Vercel), under their data protection terms, or when the law requires us to.

5. Where it is kept and how it is protected

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and kept on secure cloud servers. Photos, logos and bill PDFs are kept on secure cloud storage (Amazon Web Services). Sign-in links, codes and PINs are stored only in scrambled form. The business app keeps a copy of today's work on the business's own phones, so sales can be saved with no internet; it is sent to our servers when the phone is back online, and a photo follows its sale.

6. How long we keep it

Account-book entries, including the photo kept with a sale and the bills made from sales, are kept as long as the business's account with us is open, because they are financial records both sides rely on. Entries are never edited or deleted, only corrected by a new entry, so the history stays true. A bill that is cancelled stays on record as cancelled, and its number is never used again. Sign-in sessions end on their own; phones that are switched off lose access at once.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

You can sign out of any phone from the app's settings, and a business owner can switch off any phone or staff member at once.

To ask us to delete your account and data, email hello@mdgservices.in from or with the mobile number on your account, or ask the business to raise it with us. We will confirm who you are, delete what we are not required to keep by law (for example tax and accounting records), and tell you when it is done, normally within 30 days.

8. Children

Khata Kavach is a business tool for adults who run or work at a business, and for the businesses and people who buy from it on credit. It is not directed to children and is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the date above and, where it matters, tell you in the app.
