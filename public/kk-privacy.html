Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what the Khata Kavach apps collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. There are two apps: Khata Kavach Pump, used at a petrol pump by its owner, managers and nozzle staff, and Khata Kavach Transporter, used by a transport company that buys fuel on credit from that pump. Both are operated by VRUOOM PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us", "our"), the company behind MDG Services. Khata Kavach is a shared account book for fuel bought on credit: it replaces paper fuel coupons. It records; it does not lend, hold or move money.

1. Who this policy is for

Everyone who signs in to either app: pump owners, managers and nozzle staff, and transporters. A pump is set up by us at the owner's request; staff and transporters are added by the pump. Nobody can sign up on their own.

2. Information we collect

Only what the shared account book needs. We do not collect data for advertising, we do not sell it, and the apps contain no advertising or analytics trackers.

DataWhy we collect it
Pump name and code; names and roles of the pump's peopleTo show who is signed in and who made each entry.
A 4-digit PIN for each pump personTo sign in on the pump phone. We keep only a scrambled (salted hash) form of it, on our servers and on the pump's own phones so they can sign in without internet. Nobody, including us, can read the PIN back.
Mobile numbers of the pump owner and of each transporterTo identify the transporter, to send the sign-in link, and so the pump can call him. A transporter's number is not shown on the pump's nozzle screens.
Transporter company name, credit limit and termsThe basis of the credit account between the pump and the transporter.
Truck numbers (number plates) and tank sizesSo a truck can be found and filled against the transporter's account.
Fuel requests: truck, quantity, how long validSo the pump knows how much fuel each truck may take on credit.
Fills: truck, litres, rupees, product, who filled, date and time, an optional paper coupon numberThe entries in the shared account book, seen by both the pump and the transporter.
Payments: amount, how it was paid, cheque or UTR number, TDSTo keep the balance right.
Device details: a name for each phone (for example "Android · Chrome"), sign-in timesSo the owner can see which phones are signed in and switch one off.
A record of changes (who changed what, and when)So every correction to the account book can be traced.

The apps do not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. The Android apps ask for one permission only: internet access.

3. How we use it

  • To run the shared account book between a pump and its transporters.
  • To show each side the same entries, balances and requests.
  • To keep the service secure: sign-ins, limits on wrong PIN attempts, and switching off lost phones.
  • To fix problems and answer support requests.

4. Who can see it

A pump sees its own transporters, their trucks, requests, fills and payments. A transporter sees only their own accounts, trucks, requests, fills and payments at the pumps they buy from. Nozzle staff see what they need to fill a truck, not transporters' phone numbers. No pump sees another pump's data and no transporter sees another transporter's.

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with the companies that host the service for us (cloud servers and database: Amazon Web Services, MongoDB Atlas, Vercel), under their data protection terms, or when the law requires us to.

5. Where it is kept and how it is protected

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and kept on secure cloud servers. Sign-in links, codes and PINs are stored only in scrambled form. The pump app keeps a copy of today's work on the pump's own phones, so fills can be saved with no internet; it is sent to our servers when the phone is back online.

6. How long we keep it

Account-book entries are kept as long as the pump's account with us is open, because they are financial records both sides rely on. Entries are never edited or deleted, only corrected by a new entry, so the history stays true. Sign-in sessions end on their own; phones that are switched off lose access at once.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

You can sign out of any phone from the app's settings, and a pump owner can switch off any phone or staff member at once.

To ask us to delete your account and data, email aryan@mdgservices.in from or with the mobile number on your account, or ask your pump to raise it with us. We will confirm who you are, delete what we are not required to keep by law (for example tax and accounting records), and tell you when it is done, normally within 30 days.

8. Children

Khata Kavach is a business tool for adults who run or work at petrol pumps and transport companies. It is not directed to children and is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the date above and, where it matters, tell you in the app.
