Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what the Khata Kavach apps collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. There are two apps: Khata Kavach Pump, used by the owner, managers and nozzle men of a petrol pump that gives fuel on credit, and Khata Kavach Transporter, used by the transporters and fleet owners who buy fuel from it on credit. Both are operated by VRUOOM PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us", "our"), the company behind MDG Services. Khata Kavach is a shared account book: it replaces paper coupons and hand-written khata. It records; it does not lend, hold or move money.

1. Who this policy is for

Everyone who signs in to either app: owners, managers and nozzle men of a pump, and the transporters who buy from it. A pump owner sets up his own pump in the Pump app, and the pump adds its nozzle men and its transporters. A transporter cannot sign up on his own: the pump sends him a link.

2. Information we collect

Only what the shared account book and its bills need. We do not collect data for advertising, we do not sell it, and the apps contain no advertising or analytics trackers.

The apps do not access your location, contacts, microphone or files, and they do not read your photo library on their own. The Android apps ask for two permissions only, both about the network: internet access and knowing whether the phone is online.

Photos. A photo is added only when the nozzle man taps the photo button on a fill. The phone's own camera app or gallery then opens, and only the photo that is taken or picked comes into Khata Kavach. On a phone with no signal the photo is kept on the phone and sent after its fill. Version 1.0 of the Android apps has no photo button; it comes with version 1.1. A pump logo is added the same way, by the owner.

3. How we use it

  • To run the shared account book between a pump and its transporters.
  • To show each side the same entries, photos, bills, balances and requests.
  • To make a pump's bills: the numbered bill PDF, with the pump's details and the transporter's billing details.
  • To keep the service secure: sign-ins, limits on wrong PIN attempts, and switching off lost phones.
  • To fix problems and answer support requests.

When a transporter pays a bill by UPI, the transporter's UPI app opens with the amount filled in and the money goes straight to the pump. We do not take part in the payment. The pump records it in the app.

4. Who can see it

A pump sees its own transporters, their trucks, requests, fills, photos, bills and payments. A transporter sees only his own accounts, trucks, requests, fills, photos, bills and payments, at each pump he buys from. Nozzle men see what they need to fill a truck, not transporters' phone numbers. No pump sees another pump's data and no transporter sees another transporter's.

Bill links. A bill PDF is opened through a private link that the pump shares, for example on WhatsApp. Anyone who has the link can open that bill, and the link does not expire, so the pump should share it only with the transporter it is meant for. Please do not pass a bill link on to others.

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with the companies that host the service for us (cloud servers and file storage: Amazon Web Services; database: MongoDB Atlas; website hosting: Vercel), under their data protection terms, or when the law requires us to.

5. Where it is kept and how it is protected

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and kept on secure cloud servers. Photos, logos and bill PDFs are kept on secure cloud storage (Amazon Web Services). Sign-in links, codes and PINs are stored only in scrambled form. The Pump app keeps a copy of today's work on the pump's own phones, so fills can be saved with no internet; it is sent to our servers when the phone is back online, and a photo follows its fill.

6. How long we keep it

Account-book entries, including the photo kept with a fill and the bills made from fills, are kept as long as the pump's account with us is open, because they are financial records both sides rely on. Entries are never edited or deleted, only corrected by a new entry, so the history stays true. A bill that is cancelled stays on record as cancelled, and its number is never used again. Sign-in sessions end on their own; phones that are switched off lose access at once.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

You can sign out of any phone from the app's settings, and a pump owner can switch off any phone or staff member at once.

To ask us to delete your account and data, email hello@mdgservices.in from or with the mobile number on your account, or ask the pump to raise it with us. We will confirm who you are, delete what we are not required to keep by law (for example tax and accounting records), and tell you when it is done, normally within 30 days.

8. Children

Khata Kavach is a tool for adults who run or work at a petrol pump, and for the transporters and fleet owners who buy fuel from it on credit. It is not directed to children and is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the date above and, where it matters, tell you in the app.
