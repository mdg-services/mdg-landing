Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

This policy explains what information the Dealer Kavach mobile app ("the app", "Dealer Kavach") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Dealer Kavach is operated by Vroom Drive India Private Limited ("we", "us", "our"). The app is a support and operations tool for fuel and petrol-pump dealers and their staff.

1. Who this policy is for

Dealer Kavach is used by fuel-dealer owners, managers, and staff. You use the app through an account created for your dealership. This policy covers everyone who signs in and uses the app.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not collect data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell your data.

DataWhy we collect it
NameTo identify you within your dealership team and on chats and staff lists.
Email address and / or phone numberTo sign you in and secure your account.
User IDA unique account identifier used to run the app's features.
Messages you send — chat text, photos, and voice notesTo deliver team chat and support conversations, and task-proof photos you attach. Sent only when you choose to send them.
App interactionsBasic in-app activity (which screens and actions are used) to keep the app reliable and fix problems.
Device identifier — push notification tokenTo send you notifications, only if you grant notification permission.

Dealer Kavach does not collect your precise or approximate physical location, and does not access your contacts.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the core features: Kavach support tickets, team chat, services, records, and staff points.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To send notifications you have allowed (for example, a reply to your message).
  • To keep the app working reliably and to diagnose and fix issues.

4. How your information is stored and protected

Encryption in transit. All data exchanged between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

We keep your data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations.

5. Service providers we use

We use trusted third-party processors to run the app. They process data only on our behalf and under our instructions. We do not sell your data to them and they do not use it for their own advertising.

  • MongoDB Atlas — secure database hosting for your account and app data.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — secure storage for files you send (chat photos, voice notes, proof photos).
  • Expo — mobile app delivery and push-notification infrastructure.
  • Vercel — hosting for the app's web layer.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with third parties for advertising. Messages, photos, and voice notes you send are shared only with the intended recipients — your own dealership team and our support staff. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Notifications — you can turn push notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • Language — you can use the app fully in English or Hindi and switch any time.
  • Access & deletion — you can request a copy of your data, or request that your account and associated data be deleted.

8. How to request data deletion

To request deletion of your account and personal data, email aryan@mdgservices.in from the address linked to your account, or ask your dealership admin to raise the request. We will process verified requests within a reasonable time and delete data we are not required to keep by law.

9. Children

Dealer Kavach is a business tool intended for dealers and their staff. It is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
