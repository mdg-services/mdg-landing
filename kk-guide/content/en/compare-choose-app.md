## Nine questions to ask before you install

This page is for a business that sells to regular customers on credit, not for a loan to a friend. Ask each question of every app. The answer is on the app's own website or store listing; if you cannot find it, ask the company.

| # | Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does the customer see each entry? Can the customer ask before taking goods? | Both sides then work from the same line. |
| 2 | Who can enter a sale? | Counter staff need their own sign-in, not the owner's login. |
| 3 | Is there a limit per customer? Who can go past it? | A limit helps if it is checked before goods go out and an override is visible. |
| 4 | Does it work with no signal? | Counters and yards lose signal. Ask which screens still work. |
| 5 | Can you add a photo to a sale? | It gives you something to look at later. |
| 6 | Is the bill plain, or a GST tax invoice? | If you must issue GST invoices, you need an app that makes them, or a second one. |
| 7 | Does the company lend or handle money? | Does it only keep the record, or also offer loans or settle payments? |
| 8 | Which languages? | Your staff and customers use it all day. |
| 9 | What does it cost, and what is free? | Look for ads, message charges, extra phones and plan limits. |

## What each kind is good for

- **Paper, a register or Excel:** no phone, no training. See [paper khata or an app](@compare-paper).
- **Ledger apps** such as Khatabook and OkCredit: an everyday khata, reminders, bills, many languages.
- **Billing apps** such as Vyapar and myBillBook: GST invoices, stock and accounts.
- **A shared credit book:** regular customers who take goods on credit. See [ledger app or shared credit book](@compare-ledger-vs-shared).
