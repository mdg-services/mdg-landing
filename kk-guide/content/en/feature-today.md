## What the Today screen shows

Owners and managers see Today in the Pump app. Each block answers one question.

| Block on the screen | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| Credit given today | How much went out on credit today, and in how many fills? |
| Due on bills | How much is due on bills, on how many bills, and how many are past due? |
| This month | Credit given, money received, owed before and total owed, in four figures |
| Near or past the limit | Which transporters are close to their limit or over it? Each has a button to ask for payment on WhatsApp |
| All transporters | Who owes what, and how much of each limit is used? The list also filters by all, watch, frozen or OK |
| Fills to look at | Which fills in the last 7 days were marked? |

The fills to look at are marked because they look wrong: more than requested, over the limit, or no request. See [the daily check](@counter-daily-check) for how to go through the list each morning.

The WhatsApp button opens your own WhatsApp with a message ready. You press send. Khata Kavach sends nothing by itself.

## One transporter's page

Tap a transporter to see:

- **Owed against the limit**, with a bar showing how much of the limit is used.
- **How old the balance is**, in four bands: 0 to 15 days, 16 to 30, 31 to 60, and over 60. See [ageing explained](@paid-ageing).
- **Bills**, with the button to make a bill and each bill's status. See [bills](@feature-bills).
- **Record a payment**, and the whole ledger, line by line.

## The month adds up

The screens in our demo use made-up figures for a made-up pump. In the demo month, Owed before is Rs 5,71,400. Credit given is Rs 5,03,283 and Money received is Rs 3,97,004. Total owed is Rs 6,77,679, which is 5,71,400 plus 5,03,283 minus 3,97,004.
