## What the statement has

| Part | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Top | Your pump, the transporter, the statement date and the period it covers |
| Opening balance | What the transporter owed at the start of the period. For a second statement, the closing balance of the one before. |
| Each line | Date, bill or slip number, vehicle number, details (fuel and litres, or how paid and the UTR or cheque number), udhar given or money received, and the balance after the line |
| Closing balance | Opening balance + total udhar given − total money received |
| Foot | "Please check this statement and tell us within 7 days if anything looks wrong", and a signature line for you and for the transporter |

In the Excel sheet, yellow cells are for typing and grey cells work themselves out. It holds 50 lines; for a longer period, send a second statement that starts from the first one's closing balance. The rows in italics are a made-up example: delete them or type over every cell.

## How to use it

1. Fill the top: pump, transporter, period, opening balance.
2. Write one line for every fill and every payment in the period, in date order.
3. Check that the closing balance matches your own khata. If it does not, find the line before you send.
4. Send it from your own WhatsApp, once a month and whenever you ask for payment. Attach the bills it covers.
5. Ask the transporter to check it and to confirm it or tell you what looks wrong within 7 days. Keep the signed page or the reply with the statement.

## If the transporter disagrees

Go line by line, not total against total. Match each line with its bill or slip, and its vehicle number. A wrong line is not rubbed out: add a new line with the same amount as a minus figure and the reason, then the right line. See [a disputed fill](@example-dispute) for a made-up case and [disputes at the nozzle](@counter-disputes).

## Keeping the khata behind it

The statement is a copy of your khata for one transporter and one period. To keep the khata itself, use the [udhar khata format](@tool-khata-format), or see [how to keep an udhar khata](@credit-khata). To ask for what is still open, make a message with the [reminder maker](@tool-reminder-maker).

In Khata Kavach the transporter can open the same lines in his own app, and a numbered bill for a period goes from your own WhatsApp: see [numbered bills](@feature-bills).
