## Seven messages

These are for a petrol pump that gives diesel on credit to regular customers such as transporters and fleet owners.

Change the names, amounts and dates. The made-up figures follow one case: Bill 14, Rs 48,500, due 25 October.

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| 1. Three days before | Namaste Gupta ji. Bill 14 for Rs 48,500 is due on 25 October. The bill link is below. Please tell us when you pay. Thank you. |
| 2. On the due day | Namaste Gupta ji. Bill 14 for Rs 48,500 is due today. Please pay by UPI or at the pump, and tell us. Thank you. |
| 3. A week late | Namaste Gupta ji. Bill 14 for Rs 48,500 was due on 25 October and is still open. Can you tell us the date you will pay? We will note it. |
| 4. After a part payment | Namaste Gupta ji. We received Rs 20,000 on 2 November. Thank you. Rs 28,500 is still open on Bill 14. When can we expect the rest? |
| 5. After a missed promise | Namaste Gupta ji. You said you would pay Rs 28,500 on 8 November and we have not received it. Please call us today to settle a date. |
| 6. Last request | Namaste Gupta ji. Rs 28,500 on Bill 14 has been open since 25 October. If we do not receive it by 20 November, we will have to stop new credit on your account until it is cleared. We want to keep working together; please call us. |
| 7. After payment | Namaste Gupta ji. We received Rs 28,500. Bill 14 is now paid. Thank you. |

## Hinglish lines

For messages 3 and 6.

- **3.** Namaste Gupta ji, Bill 14 ke Rs 48,500 25 October ko due the, abhi baaki hain. Kis tareekh tak aa jayenge, bata dijiye. Hum note kar lenge.
- **6.** Namaste Gupta ji, Bill 14 ke Rs 28,500 25 October se baaki hain. 20 November tak nahi aaye to naya udhar rok denge. Hum saath kaam karte rehna chahte hain, ek baar phone kijiye.

## How to send

- Use your own WhatsApp, one transporter at a time. No groups, no broadcast.
- One message at a time. If the transporter gives a date, note it and wait for that date.
- Write the bill number, the amount and the date every time.
- Keep the chat as part of your record.
