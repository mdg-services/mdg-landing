## How the nozzle man adds a photo

1. Type the last 4 digits of the truck number and see the green light, "You can fill".
2. Type the rupees off the dispenser; the litres fill in at the day's rate.
3. Tap the photo button and take a picture of the number plate or the slip, or pick one from the phone. The photo goes with the fill.
4. Tap Done.

The fill is saved on the phone first, so it never waits for the picture. The photo follows after the fill is sent. In the list of today's fills, each one shows whether it has a photo and whether it has been sent.

The photo button is in the Pump app, in the phone's browser.

## Who sees the photo

| Who | Where they see it |
|---|---|
| The nozzle man | His own fills for today. |
| Owner and managers | On the fill's line in the transporter's ledger. |
| The transporter | On the fill's detail, with the fuel, litres, rupees, time and who filled. |

A transporter sees only the photos of his own fills.

## What it helps with

When a transporter or a driver asks "what exactly went in?", both sides can open the same line and look. The line shows the fuel, litres, rupees, time, who filled, and the truck number. With a photo, there is a picture beside the figures.

> **Note:** A photo makes a fill easier to question and answer. It does not stop a wrong fill. A wrong fill shows up on both phones, with the name of who filled.

A photo is kept with its fill for as long as the pump's account stays open. See [what to do when a fill is disputed](@counter-disputes).
