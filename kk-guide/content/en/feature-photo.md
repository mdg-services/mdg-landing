## How staff add a photo

1. Find the vehicle and tap "Enter sale" on the green screen.
2. Type the quantity and the rupees ("Type exactly what was given").
3. Tap "Photo" and take a picture, or pick one from the phone. The screen says "The photo goes with the sale".
4. Tap "Done".

The sale is saved on the phone first, so it never waits for the picture. The photo follows after the sale is sent. Under "My sales today", each sale shows whether it has a "Photo" and whether it is "Sent" or "Not sent".

The photo button is in the business app, in the phone's browser.

## Who sees the photo

| Who | Where they see it |
|---|---|
| Counter staff | Their own sales under "My sales today". |
| Owner and managers | On the sale's line in the customer's ledger. |
| The customer | On the purchase's detail, with the item, quantity, rate, time and "Given by". |

A customer sees only the photos of their own sales.

## What it helps with

When a customer or a driver asks "what exactly went out?", both sides can open the same line and look. The line shows the vehicle, item, quantity, rupees, time and who served. With a photo, there is a picture beside the figures.

> **Note:** A photo makes a sale easier to question and answer. It does not stop a wrong sale. A wrong sale shows up on both phones, with the name of who served.

A photo is kept with its sale for as long as the business's account stays open. See [what to do when a sale is disputed](@counter-disputes).
