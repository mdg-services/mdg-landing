## What the sheet has

| Tab | What you type | What it works out |
|---|---|---|
| Transporters | Name, phone, credit limit, credit days (for your reference), balance from the old book | Total udhar given, total received, balance now, credit left |
| Khata | Date, transporter (picked from the list), truck number, fuel and litres, udhar given or money received, how paid, UTR or cheque number, slip or bill number, who filled, note | The transporter's balance after every line |
| How to use | Nothing | Short steps and what the colours mean |

Yellow cells are for typing; grey cells work out by themselves. On the Transporters tab a row turns red when the balance is over the limit, and orange when less than a fifth of the limit is left. On the Khata tab the balance turns red on a line that took the transporter over. The colours follow the limit only, not late payments.

A box at the top of the Transporters tab counts Khata lines whose transporter is not on the list, so a misspelt name is caught instead of quietly dropping out of a balance. The sheet holds 200 transporters and 3,000 lines. The rows in italics are a made-up example: delete them, or type over every cell including the Note.

## How to use it

1. Add each transporter on the Transporters tab, with their limit and the balance from your old book. To pick a limit, use the [credit limit calculator](@tool-credit-limit).
2. Write every credit fill and every payment on the Khata tab the day it happens. Pick the transporter from the list.
3. Never type over an old line. Cancel it with the same amount as a minus figure in the same column and the reason in Note, then write the right one.
4. Each morning, look at the Transporters tab: red and orange rows first, and the name-check box.
5. Each month, filter the Khata tab by a transporter's name and send him the lines and the balance.

## The printable page

One page per transporter, A4 across: date, truck number, fuel and litres, udhar given, money received, how paid with the UTR or cheque number, slip or bill number, who filled, balance and the transporter's sign. Start from the balance brought forward, write each line when the fuel goes in, and carry the balance down. Strike a wrong line through and leave it out of the balance.

## What a sheet cannot do

A sheet or a register is your own record. The transporter sees it only when you send it, and nothing stops a fill over the limit unless someone checks. Khata Kavach keeps the same lines in a shared book: by default the transporter sends a request before each truck fills, the nozzle man sees a light for the limit, and both sides see each line as soon as the phone is online. See [a paper khata or an app](@compare-paper).
