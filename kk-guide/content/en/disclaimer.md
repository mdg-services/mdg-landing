These pages explain, in plain words, how businesses in India sell goods on credit to regular customers, keep the account and get paid, and where Khata Kavach fits. MDG Services writes and publishes them. MDG Services also makes Khata Kavach, so read every comparison knowing that.

## How we write them

- Facts about Khata Kavach come from the apps themselves. We describe only what the apps do today.
- Facts about another app come only from that company's own website, help pages or app store listing, and each page shows the date we last checked them. Where we could not find something on their pages, we say "we did not find it", not that the app cannot do it.
- Where another app does more than Khata Kavach, we say so.
- Where a page explains a law, we name the Act and section beside the rule so you can read the official text. It is the general position in India, not advice for your case.
- Examples use made-up businesses, customers and figures, and say so.
- We do not use other companies' logos or screenshots of their apps.

## If something is wrong

Write to the address at the foot of the page. We check every message and correct or remove the page.
