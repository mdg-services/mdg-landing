## Billing app or credit book?

[Vyapar](https://vyaparapp.in/) and [myBillBook](https://mybillbook.in/) are built for billing and accounting: GST invoices, e-invoices, e-way bills, stock and party ledgers. Khata Kavach does one job: diesel and petrol that a pump gives on credit to regular transporters, kept in one record that both sides see.

## Where the billing apps do more

- GST invoices, e-invoices, e-way bills, stock, accounting and desktop apps. Khata Kavach has none of these.
- Reach: both show 10M+ downloads on Google Play (19 October 2026). Khata Kavach is new.

## Where Khata Kavach works differently

By default, every credit fill starts with a request from the transporter's phone, one per truck. The nozzle man sees a light before fuel goes in. By default, past the limit a manager's PIN is needed (the owner can choose cash only), and the owner's Today screen lists these fills. A correction is a new line that both sides see.

## Which fits

- GST invoices, stock, accounts: a billing app.
- Regular transporters who fill diesel on credit: a shared credit book.
- Both jobs: GST invoices from your billing software; the credit at the nozzle from Khata Kavach. See how [a numbered bill differs from a GST tax invoice](@credit-bills).
