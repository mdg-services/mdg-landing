## One login, every supplier who uses it

You buy cement from one dealer, sand from another and steel from a third. Each keeps its own khata. In the [Customer app](@feature-customer-app) every supplier who has added you sits on one login, each with its own card, limit and lines. The app calls the seller "supplier".

| You want to know | Where the app shows it |
|---|---|
| What is left at this supplier | "Available credit", out of that supplier's limit |
| What you owe | "You owe" |
| Who took the material | Each purchase: vehicle, item, quantity, rupees, "Given by", and the photo if the counter added one |
| Anything unusual | A mark on the line, such as "More than requested", "No request" or "Over the limit" |
| What to pay | The "Bills" tab. If the supplier has added its UPI ID, "Pay ₹X by UPI" opens your own UPI app, and the money goes from your bank to the supplier |

For example, with made-up figures: Supplier A has a limit of Rs 1,00,000 and you owe Rs 62,000, so Rs 38,000 is left. Supplier B has a limit of Rs 50,000 and you owe Rs 18,500, so Rs 31,500 is left.

## Your supervisor's truck, on your say-so

For each vehicle you send a [request](@feature-requests): "Which vehicles need goods today?" Pick the item and the quantity in its own unit (bags, tonnes, kg, pieces), or rupees, or "As needed". "Same as last time" repeats the vehicles and quantities of the day before.

At the supplier's counter, staff see a green light for that vehicle when the account is in order. By default, no request shows red "No credit", and staff can serve for cash. "Cancel this request" takes a request back while it is open.

If the supplier lets a manager allow a sale without a request for you, your phone shows "No request" and "Manager allowed it".

## What to know first

- The supplier adds you. It works with suppliers who use Khata Kavach. See how it looks from [the dealer's side](@trade-cement).
- A vehicle number can be on one customer's list at a time. If a hired tractor is already on another customer's list, the app says so without naming them.
- The app has no site-wise split of purchases. You can give each vehicle a nickname of your own.
- A registered micro or small contractor supplying services to a larger firm: see [the MSME 45-day rule](@paid-msme) for who it may protect.
