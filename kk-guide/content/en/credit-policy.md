## The template

Copy this, fill every [blank], and give a copy to your staff and each customer. For a binding agreement, ask an advocate.

1. **Who gets credit.** Regular customers known for [months] months who paid on time. Each names one person responsible, a phone that gets answered, and the vehicles allowed to collect. See [who to give credit to](@credit-who).
2. **Limit.** A new customer starts at Rs [amount], and may go to Rs [amount] after [months] months of paying on time. The owner alone changes a limit. See [how to set a limit](@credit-limit).
3. **Credit period.** Pay within [days] days of the bill, or settle [monthly / every 15 days / when the limit is full].
4. **Who says yes at the counter.** Staff serve while the limit has room. A manager may allow a sale past it up to Rs [amount] and tells the owner the same day. Beyond that the owner decides. See [when a customer is over the limit](@counter-over-limit).
5. **What every sale records.** Date and time, vehicle, item, quantity in its own unit, rupees, who served, slip number or photo.
6. **Bills.** A numbered bill goes out [every N days / on day N of the month]. See [kachcha and pakka bills](@credit-bills).
7. **Late payment.** Reminder [days] days after the due day, a second on day [days]. Still unpaid on day [days]: credit stops, cash only, until the balance is cleared. See [when to stop credit](@paid-stop-credit).
8. **Interest.** [None / rate] on overdue amounts. Agree it with the customer in writing before the sale; where nothing is agreed, a court decides {{Interest Act 1978, s.3}}.
9. **Disputes.** Raise a doubt within [days] days of the bill. We check the slip, the photo and who served. A wrong line is fixed by a new line with the reason, never by rubbing out the old one.

Ask each customer to sign a copy or reply "Agreed" on WhatsApp.

## Where Khata Kavach fits

The owner sets each customer's limit and the "When do you collect?" term. The customer reads the terms and taps "I agree" before sending a request. By default, a manager's PIN lets a sale go past the limit, and the owner's Today screen flags the sale; the owner can set a customer to cash only instead. To ask a customer near or past the limit for payment, one tap opens your own WhatsApp with the message ready. Khata Kavach sends nothing itself.

Khata Kavach keeps the record. You set every rule, and it never lends or holds money.

General information, not legal advice. For your own case, speak to an advocate or a chartered accountant.
