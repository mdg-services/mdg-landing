## What paper does well

A paper khata, a register and an Excel sheet have run pumps for years. Paper needs no phone, no signal and no training. Excel adds totals and search.

## Side by side

| | Paper register | Excel sheet | Shared credit app (Khata Kavach) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost | A register and a pen | Software you may already have | Call us; the price depends on your business |
| Needs a phone or signal | No | A computer or phone | A phone at the pump; transporters use their own. The nozzle works with no signal once opened with internet |
| Who sees each line | Whoever holds the book | Whoever has the file | The pump and the transporter, the same line |
| Can a line change without the other side seeing | Yes, unless the customer holds a copy | Yes, unless the customer holds a copy | Once the line has been sent, no. A correction is a new line; the old line stays, struck out |
| How the transporter knows | When you tell him or show the book | When you send the file or a statement | In his own app, as soon as the pump's phone is online |
| Limit checked before the fuel goes in | By hand | By hand | Green, amber or red light at the nozzle |
| Transporter asks first | Only if you make it a rule | Only if you make it a rule | By default: a request, one per truck |
| Month-end bill | You add up and write it | You build it from the sheet | A numbered bill from the unbilled fills, made in a tap |

Paper and Excel rows describe general practice, not a named app's claim. The Khata Kavach column was checked on 19 October 2026. Tell us if anything has changed.

## When to stay, when to switch

Stay with paper if transporters are few, you see every fill, and no one else fills on credit. Look at an app when:

- drivers fill their trucks and you are not at the pump;
- month end takes days of adding up;
- a transporter says "we took 150 litres, not 200" and neither side can show the line.

## Moving from the book

Keep the old book. When you add each transporter in Khata Kavach, type what he owes from the paper book; it becomes his opening balance. Every fill after that is a line both sides see.
