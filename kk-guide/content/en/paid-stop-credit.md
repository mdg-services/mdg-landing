## Signals, and what to do

This page is for a petrol pump whose regular customers, transporters and fleet owners, take diesel on credit.

| Signal | What to do |
|---|---|
| Payments keep slipping past the agreed period | Send a [reminder](@paid-reminder-messages) the same day. Note the new date. A second slip in a row triggers your rule. |
| A promise is missed ("I will pay on Friday") | Ask for a call. If the second promise fails too, stop new credit. |
| The balance sits at the limit all the time | Do not raise the limit to keep him filling. Ask for part payment first. |
| A cheque is returned | Treat it as not paid (reverse the payment if you had recorded it) and stop new credit until the account is cleared. See [cheque bounce](@paid-cheque-bounce). |
| The transporter fills less from you but owes more | Ask what changed. Ask for payment before more credit. |

## Decide the rule first

Write one rule and use it for every transporter. For example: "New credit stops 30 days after the due day, until the dues are paid." You choose the number. Tell each transporter when you set his limit and days to pay. See [how to set a limit](@credit-limit).

## How to tell the transporter

Say it plainly and kindly. For example (made-up figures): "Namaste Gupta ji. Rs 48,500 has been open since 25 October. Until it is cleared we can fill your trucks for cash only. Once it is paid, we start again. Please call me if you want to talk."

- Use the same words for everyone.
- Say when it ends.
- Offer cash fills, so the relationship goes on.

## How Khata Kavach holds the line

- **Freeze.** The owner can freeze an account with "Freeze account". As soon as the nozzle phone is online, the transporter shows a red "No credit" light at the nozzle, the nozzle man sees "You can serve for cash", and the transporter's app says no credit will be given. Only the owner can freeze or unfreeze. Khata Kavach never freezes an account on its own.
- **A softer step.** Lower the limit, or set the account so that a full limit means "no credit, cash only" instead of "a manager can allow it". If a manager's PIN could allow this transporter a fill with no request, set "With no request" back to "No credit". See [over the limit at the nozzle](@counter-over-limit).
- **Find them.** The transporters list has "Watch" and "Frozen" filters.

## Restarting

When "Record a payment" brings the balance down, unfreeze the account. Restart with a lower limit than before, and watch the next few bills.
