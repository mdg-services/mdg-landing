## Full forecourt system or credit control?

GARRUDA, Petrosoft (YoungMinds) and Petro Genius are software for petrol pumps. Each lists credit limits and a customer app. GARRUDA's pages and listings add shift, nozzle and dip readings; GARRUDA and Petrosoft list Tally.

Khata Kavach does not run a pump: no nozzles, shifts, tanks, stock or Tally. It is made for credit that a business gives to regular customers, and it is not connected with any oil company.

## Where pump software does more

Shift, nozzle and dip readings (GARRUDA), Tally and a GST returns menu (Petrosoft), an owner's reports app (GARRUDA), and age-wise outstanding with party-wise or vehicle-wise statements (Petro Genius). Khata Kavach has no shifts, nozzle or dip readings, no Tally, no GST returns and no statements.

## Where Khata Kavach works differently

A pump, a cement store or a sand yard can use the same app, each item in its own unit. By default, every credit sale starts with the customer's request. Staff see green, amber or red. By default, past the limit a manager's PIN is needed and the owner sees it; the owner can choose cash only. Once a sale has reached the customer's phone, no line is edited or deleted: a correction is a new line. It never lends and never holds money.

## Which fits

- You need the whole pump run from one system: pump software.
- Your software is fine and credit is the gap: a credit book beside it. See [credit at a petrol pump](@trade-petrol-pump), [customer requests](@feature-requests) and [working with no signal](@feature-no-signal).
