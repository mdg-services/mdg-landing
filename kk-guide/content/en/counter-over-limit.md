## A rule sheet you can adopt

Decide these five points once, so the nozzle man does not decide in front of a waiting driver.

1. **Who may say yes.** Name one manager, or two. Never the nozzle man himself.
2. **How far over.** Pick a figure, for example up to Rs 5,000 over the limit. Past it, call the owner.
3. **What gets written down.** Who allowed it, how much over, and why.
4. **Cash first.** Offer to fill for cash. Or ask the transporter to pay something now, so the balance falls.
5. **Review the same day.** The owner reads every fill that went past the limit before the day ends.

For example, a transporter has Rs 2,000 of credit left and the fill comes to Rs 3,500. The fill is Rs 1,500 over, so the nozzle man calls the named manager.

## What the nozzle man does in each case

| Situation | What the nozzle man does |
|---|---|
| Slightly over | Call the named manager. The manager allows this one fill or says no. Write down who allowed it and how much over. |
| Far over | Offer cash. Or ask the transporter to pay part now. If he pays, a manager or owner records it and that much credit opens up again. Past your fixed figure, call the owner. |
| Account on hold | No credit. Fill for cash. The owner lifts a hold. |
| No request today | By default there is no credit. Fill for cash, or ask the driver to get the transporter to send a request, then refresh. |

## How Khata Kavach does it

The phone shows an amber light, "Call the manager", with the reason, and asks the manager to type his PIN to allow this fill. The other choices are to call the owner or to refresh.

After the PIN, the fill form opens. The fill is recorded as allowed by that manager, by name, and marked as over the limit. The owner sees it on the Today screen among the fills that look wrong, which lists marked fills from the last 7 days.

The owner chooses, for each transporter, what happens at the limit: a manager can allow it, or no credit, cash only. With cash only, the light is red, "No credit", and says "You can fill for cash."

Khata Kavach does not set your "how far over" figure, and the fill form has no box for the reason. Ask the manager to tell the owner.
