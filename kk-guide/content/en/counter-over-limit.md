## A rule sheet you can adopt

Decide these five points once, so staff do not decide in front of a waiting driver.

1. **Who may say yes.** Name one manager, or two. Never the person serving.
2. **How far over.** Pick a figure, for example up to Rs 5,000 over the limit. Past it, call the owner.
3. **What gets written down.** Who allowed it, how much over, and why.
4. **Cash first.** Offer to sell for cash. Or ask the customer to pay something now, so the balance falls.
5. **Review the same day.** The owner reads every sale that went past the limit before the day ends.

For example, a customer has Rs 2,000 of credit left and the load comes to Rs 3,500. The sale is Rs 1,500 over, so staff call the named manager.

## What staff do in each case

| Situation | What staff do |
|---|---|
| Slightly over | Call the named manager. The manager allows this one sale or says no. Write down who allowed it and how much over. |
| Far over | Offer cash. Or ask the customer to pay part now. If the customer pays, a manager or owner records it and that much credit opens up again. Past your fixed figure, call the owner. |
| Account on hold | No credit. Sell for cash. The owner lifts a hold. |
| No request today | By default there is no credit. Sell for cash, or ask the driver to get the customer to send a request, then refresh. |

## How Khata Kavach does it

The phone shows an amber light, "Call the manager", with the reason and "The manager must type his PIN to allow this sale." The buttons are "Manager PIN", "Call the owner" and "Refresh".

After the PIN, the sale form opens. The sale is recorded as "Allowed by" the manager's name and marked "Over the limit". The owner sees it under "Look at these" on the Today screen, which lists flagged sales from the last 7 days.

The owner chooses, for each customer, what happens at the limit: "a manager can allow it", or "no credit, cash only". With cash only, the light is red, "No credit", and says "You can serve for cash."

Khata Kavach does not set your "how far over" figure, and the sale form has no box for the reason. Ask the manager to tell the owner.
