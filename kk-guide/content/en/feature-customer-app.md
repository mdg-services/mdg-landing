## What the transporter can do

This page is for a pump owner, and for the transporter who buys diesel on credit. The app has a home screen, a place to send requests, the account lines, bills, the list of trucks and settings.

| Screen | What the transporter sees or does |
|---|---|
| Home | The credit left out of your limit, what he owes, the last payment and last fill, today's requests, and what is due on bills. A switch at the top changes Hindi to English |
| Send requests | "Send requests", then "Which trucks need fuel today?" For each truck, the fuel and "How much to fill?": litres, rupees or full tank. A request is valid today unless he picks another day, and can be cancelled while it is open |
| Account | Every line, newest first: fills, payments, struck-out lines, account corrections and the opening balance |
| A fill | The fuel, litres, rupees, time and truck number, who filled, who allowed it when a manager's PIN was used, and the photo if the pump added one |
| Bills | Unpaid or all, the bill's PDF, and a button to pay the amount by UPI while something is due, if your UPI ID is in your pump's details |
| Trucks | His trucks. Add numbers by pasting them; remove a sold truck. Old lines keep its number |
| Settings | Large text or normal, the phones signed in, adding another phone, and sign out |

Marked fills show their mark on the transporter's side too, such as more than requested or over the limit. See [how requests work](@feature-requests) and [what a photo adds](@feature-photo).

## How the transporter joins, and what stays out

You add the transporter. He cannot sign up alone. You send his invite link from your own WhatsApp. He reads the terms and agrees once: credit up to the limit, by default credit only when a request was sent, every fill and payment shows in the app, and the app keeps the record without taking or sending money. Until then he cannot send requests.

After paying a bill, the transporter tells you. Once you record the money, the bill shows paid, or part paid, and that much credit opens again. See [bills](@feature-bills).
