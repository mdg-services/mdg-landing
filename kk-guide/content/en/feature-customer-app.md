## What the customer can do

The app calls your business the supplier. The app has up to five tabs: Home, Requests, Bills, Vehicles and Settings. The account is opened from Home.

| Screen | What the customer sees or does |
|---|---|
| Home | "Available credit" out of your limit, "You owe", the last payment and last purchase, today's requests, and "Due on bills". A switch at the top changes Hindi to English |
| Send requests | By vehicle number: one request per vehicle, for the item and quantity in its own unit, or in rupees, or "As needed". "All N vehicles" and "Same as last time" save typing. By code or name: one item and how much, and "For whom / where" if you want. The app gives "Your pickup code" and "Send on WhatsApp", to pass to whoever collects. "Valid till" is today unless the customer picks another day. A request can be cancelled while it is open |
| Account | Every line, newest first: purchases, payments, "A wrong line was struck out", account corrections and the opening balance |
| A purchase | Item, quantity, rupees, time, the vehicle (or "Taken by" and a name, when the counter added who collected), "Given by" with the staff member's name, "Allowed by" when a manager's PIN was used, and the photo if the counter added one |
| Bills | "Unpaid" or "All", the bill's PDF, and "Pay ₹X by UPI" while something is due, if your UPI ID is in your business details |
| Vehicles | Shown only when a business works by vehicle number. Add numbers by pasting them; remove a sold vehicle. Old lines keep its number |
| Settings | "Large text" or normal, the phones signed in, "Add another phone" and sign out |

Marked sales show their mark on the customer's side too, such as "More than requested" or "Over the limit". See [how requests work](@feature-requests) and [what a photo adds](@feature-photo).

## How the customer joins, and what stays out

You add the customer. They cannot sign up alone. You send their link from your own WhatsApp. They read the terms and tap "I agree" once: credit up to the limit, by default credit only when a request was sent, every purchase and payment shows in the app, and the app keeps the record without taking or sending money. Until then they cannot send requests.

After paying a bill, the customer tells you. Once you record the money, the bill shows paid, or part paid, and that much credit opens again. See [bills](@feature-bills).
