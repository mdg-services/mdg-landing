For a shop or dealer whose regular customers ask for udhar at the counter.

## Three rules for saying no

1. **Talk about the account, not the person.** "The limit is Rs 50,000", not "I do not trust you".
2. **Offer something.** Cash today, part credit, or a date to ask again.
3. **Use the same words for everyone**, family included. Decide the rule beforehand: see [who to give credit to](@credit-who) and [how to set a limit](@credit-limit).

## What to say

| Situation | In English | In Hinglish |
|---|---|---|
| A new customer | "We start credit after a few cash purchases. Today it is cash; ask me again in [two weeks]." | "Naye grahak ko pehle kuch baar nakad par maal dete hain. Aaj nakad, [do hafte] baad phir poochhiye." |
| Over the limit | "Your limit was Rs [amount] and it is used up. Pay something today and I can give the rest on credit." | "Aapki limit Rs [amount] thi, poori ho gayi. Kuch abhi de dijiye, baaki udhar de dunga." |
| Old dues pending | "The old balance is still open. When it is cleared, credit starts again." | "Purana hisaab baaki hai. Clear hote hi udhar phir chalu." |
| A friend or relative | "The same rule for everyone, my family too. That is how credit keeps running." | "Sabke liye ek hi niyam, ghar walon ke liye bhi. Isi se udhar chal pata hai." |
| Staff, owner away | "The owner decides credit. I can serve you for cash now, and the owner will call you." | "Udhar malik tay karte hain. Abhi nakad par de sakta hoon, malik aapko phone karenge." |

Say only what is true: if the owner really decides, say so.

## When to say "yes, up to this much"

A limit often works better than a flat no, because the customer keeps buying: "Credit up to Rs 10,000, and the old balance first." Work out the figure with the [credit limit calculator](@tool-credit-limit). For a good customer a little over, decide in advance who may allow it: see [a customer over the limit](@counter-over-limit).

## Put the rule on the wall

A poster says it so your staff need not. The [udhar band hai poster page](@tool-udhar-poster) has six, from blunt to polite.

## Where Khata Kavach fits

At the counter, the Khata Kavach phone shows a green, amber or red light for the limit the owner set, so the no comes from the rule, not from the person serving. The owner sets every limit.
