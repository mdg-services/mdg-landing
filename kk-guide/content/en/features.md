## Two apps, one shared line

The business app is for the owner, managers and counter staff. The customer app is for the person who buys goods on credit. Each sale is one line, and both sides see it. Both apps open in the phone's browser today.

## Every feature, side by side

| Feature | What the owner gets | What the customer sees |
|---|---|---|
| [Customer requests](@feature-requests) | By default, nothing goes out on credit unless the customer asked that day, for a vehicle or with a pickup code. | "Send requests": pick vehicles, or one item and amount. |
| [Counter light and limit](@feature-counter-light) | A green, amber or red light before goods go out. By default, a manager's PIN to go past the limit. | "Available credit" against the limit. |
| [Photo with a sale](@feature-photo) | An optional photo on each sale. | The same photo on the sale's line. |
| [How a wrong entry is corrected](@feature-corrections) | A correction is a new line with a reason. | The old line struck out, and the new one. |
| [Works with no signal](@feature-no-signal) | The counter keeps selling. The sale is saved on the phone and sent later. | The sale, once the counter phone is online. |
| [Today screen](@feature-today) | Credit given today, then this month's credit given, money received and total owed, who is near the limit, sales to look at. | Home: what they owe, last payment, last purchase. |
| [Numbered bills with UPI](@feature-bills) | A bill on your letterhead in a tap, sent from your own WhatsApp. | A Bills tab, and, if you added a UPI ID, "Pay ₹X by UPI" to you directly. |
| [Customer app](@feature-customer-app) | Customers send their own requests and see their own account. | Hindi screens, large text, English one tap away. |

Every item has its own unit: litres, bags, tonnes, kg, pieces, boxes, cylinders, trips, metres, or an amount in rupees. One business can sell cement by the bag and sand by the tonne.

## How the counter knows the customer

The owner picks one way, once. Fuel stations start on "By vehicle number". Every other trade starts on "By code or name": the customer's request gets a 4-digit pickup code, the customer sends it on WhatsApp to whoever collects, and staff type that code.

Per customer, "With no request" can be "No credit" (the default), "Manager PIN" or "Trust him": any staff can give goods on his name, up to the limit.

## Who does what

- **Counter staff** type the customer's pickup code, or the last 4 digits of the vehicle, see the light, type what was given and tap Done. They never see a customer's phone number.
- **Managers** type their PIN to allow one sale past the limit. They also record money received and make bills.
- **The owner** sets each customer's limit, the staff, the items and the bill numbering.

## What Khata Kavach does not do

- It does not make a GST tax invoice. It makes a numbered bill.
- It does not lend, hold or collect money.
- It does not send messages by itself. One tap opens your own WhatsApp with the message ready.
- It does not track stock or record cash sales.
- It does not let a customer sign up alone: the business adds its customers. A business starts on its own, in the Business app.
