## Two apps, one shared line

The Pump app is for the owner, managers and nozzle men. The Transporter app is for the transporter who buys diesel on credit, and his staff. Each fill is one line, and both sides see it. Both apps open in the phone's browser today.

## Every feature, side by side

| Feature | What the owner gets | What the transporter sees |
|---|---|---|
| [Transporter requests](@feature-requests) | By default, nothing goes out on credit unless the transporter asked that day, one request per truck. | "Send requests": pick the trucks, then litres, rupees or a full tank. |
| [The light at the nozzle](@feature-counter-light) | A green, amber or red light before fuel goes in. By default, a manager's PIN to go past the limit. | The credit left against the limit. |
| [Photo with a fill](@feature-photo) | An optional photo on each fill: the number plate or the slip. | The same photo on the fill's line. |
| [How a wrong entry is corrected](@feature-corrections) | A correction is a new line with a reason. | The old line struck out, and the new one. |
| [Works with no signal](@feature-no-signal) | The nozzle keeps filling. The fill is saved on the phone and sent later. | The fill, once the pump's phone is online. |
| [Today screen](@feature-today) | Credit given today, then this month's credit given, money received and total owed, who is near the limit, fills to look at. | Home: what he owes, last payment, last fill. |
| [Numbered bills with UPI](@feature-bills) | A bill on your letterhead in a tap, sent from your own WhatsApp. | A bills section and, if you added a UPI ID, a button to pay the amount by UPI straight to you. |
| [Transporter app](@feature-customer-app) | Transporters send their own requests and see their own account. | Hindi screens, large text, English one tap away. |

## The four fuels, by the litre

Khata Kavach handles diesel, petrol, premium diesel and premium petrol, by the litre. Every morning from 6 a.m., whoever opens the Pump app first types each fuel's rate for the day, or taps "same as yesterday" and checks it. At the nozzle, the nozzle man types the rupees off the dispenser and the litres fill in at that rate. An owner or a manager can correct a day's rate.

## At the nozzle

The nozzle man types the last 4 digits of the truck number and sees the light and the credit left. He types the rupees, the litres fill in, and he taps Done. Both phones show the fill.

With no request the light is red, "No credit", and he can fill for cash. Per transporter, the owner can instead let a manager's PIN allow a fill without a request.

## Who does what

- **Nozzle men** type the last 4 digits, see the light, type the rupees (the litres fill in), and tap Done. They never see a transporter's phone number.
- **Managers** type their PIN to allow one fill past the limit. They also record money received and make bills.
- **The owner** sets each transporter's limit, the staff and the bill numbering.

## What Khata Kavach does not do

- It does not make a GST tax invoice. It makes a numbered bill.
- It does not lend, hold or collect money.
- It does not send messages by itself. One tap opens your own WhatsApp with the message ready.
- It does not track stock or record cash fills.
- It does not let a transporter sign up alone: the pump invites him with a link on WhatsApp. A pump starts on its own in the Pump app.
