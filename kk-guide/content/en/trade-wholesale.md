## The question a wholesaler has to answer

For example (made-up figures): a retailer arrives with a tempo, loads goods worth Rs 12,600 and says "put it on my account". Does the loading counter know how much credit that retailer has left? Does the retailer see the same line later? Khata Kavach answers both for businesses whose retailers collect in their own vehicles.

## How a collection works

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Limit | You set one rupee limit for each retailer, across all goods |
| Request | By default the retailer sends a request for the vehicle: "Which vehicles need goods today?" It is good till today unless another day is picked |
| Counter | Staff type the last 4 digits. Green shows credit left and "You can serve". Amber needs a manager's PIN. Red shows "No credit" and "You can serve for cash" |
| Sale | Staff type what was given, add the slip number and a photo if they want, and tap Done |
| Bill | You make a numbered bill for the period. The retailer sees it. If your UPI ID is in your business details, the retailer can pay by UPI straight to you |

See [how the light works](@feature-counter-light) and [how to set a limit](@credit-limit).

## Your goods, in their own units

Each item has one unit. Goods can be in boxes, bags or pieces, or "amount only", where staff type just the rupees. A wholesaler who sells mixed goods can keep one amount-only item called "Goods". A request and a sale each hold one item, so a mixed load goes as one sale per item, or as one amount-only sale.

## Where it fits, and where it does not

It fits a wholesaler whose retailers send their own vehicle and who wants them to ask first and see every line. It is not a distribution-management system: no beat plans, no route sheets and no stock. A [numbered bill](@feature-bills) is not a GST tax invoice, so [billing software](@compare-billing-apps) stays for those.

Khata Kavach keeps the account. It never lends and never holds money: you set the limit, the retailer pays you directly, and you record it.
