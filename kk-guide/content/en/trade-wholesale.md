## The question a wholesaler has to answer

For example (made-up figures): a retailer arrives with a tempo, loads goods worth Rs 12,600 and says "put it on my account". Does the loading counter know how much credit that retailer has left? Does the retailer see the same line later? Khata Kavach answers both, whoever collects: the retailer's tempo, a shop boy or your own delivery boy.

## How a collection works

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Limit | You set one rupee limit for each retailer, across all goods |
| Request | The retailer sends a request: the item, how much, and "For whom / where". The app gives it a 4-digit "Your pickup code", and "Send on WhatsApp" passes it to whoever collects |
| Counter | Staff type the code in "Code or customer name". "Code matches" and green show credit left and "You can serve". Amber needs a manager's PIN. Red shows "No credit" and "You can serve for cash" |
| Sale | Staff type what was given, add the slip number, "Collected by" and a photo if they want, and tap Done |
| Bill | You make a numbered bill for the period. The retailer sees it. If your UPI ID is in your business details, the retailer can pay by UPI straight to you |

See [how the light works](@feature-counter-light) and [how to set a limit](@credit-limit).

## A regular retailer, with no request

On each retailer's page, "With no request" is "No credit" (the default), "Manager PIN" or "Trust him". With "Trust him", any staff can give goods on his name with no request or code, up to his limit. New customers of a wholesaler start on "Trust him". The limit always applies.

## Your goods, in their own units

Each item has one unit. Goods can be in boxes, bags or pieces, or "amount only", where staff type just the rupees. A wholesaler who sells mixed goods can keep one amount-only item called "Goods". A request and a sale each hold one item, so a mixed load goes as one sale per item, or as one amount-only sale.

## Where it fits, and where it does not

It fits a wholesaler who wants retailers to ask first, or be trusted up to a limit, and to see every line, whoever collects the goods. It is not a distribution-management system: no beat plans, no route sheets and no stock. A [numbered bill](@feature-bills) is not a GST tax invoice, so [billing software](@compare-billing-apps) stays for those.

Khata Kavach keeps the account. It never lends and never holds money: you set the limit, the retailer pays you directly, and you record it.
