## The problem: the total reaches you at month end

Your drivers fill diesel on your account. If you see the total only when the bill arrives, a fill from three weeks ago is hard to check.

## What to ask your pump to show you

- Each fill: the day and the truck number.
- The litres and the rupees for each.
- Who filled it.
- A photo or a slip number.
- Your balance and your limit.

The Transporter app shows these in the same line the pump wrote, as soon as the pump's phone is online.

## Send a request for each truck

In the Transporter app:

1. Tap "Send requests". The screen asks: "Which trucks need fuel today?"
2. Pick all your trucks, or yesterday's trucks and litres in one tap, or search by truck number.
3. Say how much: the screen asks "How much to fill?" You can answer in litres, in rupees, or full tank.
4. Check how long the request stays valid. It is today unless you change it.
5. Send. Many trucks go in one batch.

No request, no credit by default: the light at the nozzle shows "No credit" and the nozzle man can fill for cash. Made a mistake? Cancel the request before the truck fills, or take back all of them right after sending. Once the fuel is filled, the request is used and cannot be cancelled.

## What you see afterwards

The home screen shows how much credit you have left and what you owe. Open your account to see every line, newest first. Open a line to see the litres, rupees, time, who filled, who allowed it if a manager used a PIN, and the photo. The bills tab lists each bill. A bill shows a pay-by-UPI button if the pump added a UPI ID. It opens your own UPI app, and the money goes from your bank to the pump. Tell the pump, who records it.

## How you start

The pump adds you with your name, mobile number and limit, and sends an invite link on WhatsApp. You give your name and email, type the emailed code, make a 6-digit PIN and agree to the terms. In Settings, People, you can add a manager with his own phone and PIN. The limit is the pump's decision. The app keeps the record and never lends or holds money. See [the Transporter app](@feature-customer-app) and [how requests work](@feature-requests). A registered micro or small transporter that supplies services to a larger firm may also have a payment-time rule on its side: see [the MSME 45-day rule](@paid-msme).
