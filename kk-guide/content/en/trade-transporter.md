## The problem: the total reaches you at month end

Your drivers take diesel on your account. If you see the total only when the bill arrives, a fill from three weeks ago is hard to check.

## What to ask your supplier to show you

- Each fill: the day and the vehicle number.
- The quantity and the rupees for each.
- Who served it.
- A photo or a slip number.
- Your balance and your limit.

The Customer app shows these in the same line the supplier wrote, as soon as the supplier's phone is online.

## Send a request for each truck

This is the fuel-station flow. Other trades work by a 4-digit pickup code instead.

In the Customer app:

1. Tap "Send requests". The screen asks: "Which vehicles need goods today?"
2. Pick "All N vehicles", or "Same as last time" (yesterday's vehicles and quantities), or search by vehicle number.
3. Say how much: in the item's unit, in "Rupees", or "As needed". For fuel, the third choice reads "Full tank".
4. Check "Valid till". It is today unless you change it.
5. Send. Up to 200 vehicles go in one batch.

No request, no credit by default: the counter shows "No credit" and staff can sell for cash. Made a mistake? Tap "Cancel this request", or "Take them all back" right after sending. Once the goods are given, the request is used and cannot be cancelled.

## What you see afterwards

The home screen shows "Available credit" and "You owe". Open "Account" from the home screen to see every line, newest first. Open a line to see the item, quantity, rate, time, "Given by", "Allowed by" if a manager used a PIN, and the photo. The "Bills" tab lists each bill. A bill shows "Pay ₹X by UPI" if the supplier added a UPI ID. It opens your own UPI app, and the money goes from your bank to the supplier. Tell the supplier, who records it.

## How you start

The supplier adds you with your name, mobile number and limit, and sends a link on WhatsApp. You read the terms and tap "I agree". The limit is the supplier's decision. The app keeps the record and never lends or holds money. See [the Customer app](@feature-customer-app) and [how requests work](@feature-requests). A registered micro or small transporter that supplies services to a larger firm may also have a payment-time rule on its side: see [the MSME 45-day rule](@paid-msme).
