This page is for a business that sells on credit to regular customers whose drivers, site supervisors or labourers collect the goods.

## Three people, one sale

The customer owes the money. The driver stands at your counter. Your staff serve one truck after another and cannot phone the customer for each.

| Who | Where it slips |
|---|---|
| Customer | May find out what each truck took only when your bill comes |
| Driver | Asks for a full tank when the customer meant Rs 5,000 |
| Your counter staff | Cannot tell what the customer meant for this pickup today |

## What each side needs

You need four things:

1. The customer's say-so for **that pickup, today**.
2. A cap: a quantity or a rupee amount.
3. A record the customer also sees: vehicle or code, item, quantity, rupees, who served, time, and a photo if you add one.
4. A rule for a vehicle that is not on the customer's list, or a code that does not match.

The customer needs one thing: to see what each pickup took, on their own phone, as soon as it is online.

## How a request does this

The customer sends it from their own phone. The owner chooses once how the counter matches it:

- **By vehicle number** (fuel stations start here): one request per vehicle, with the item and an amount. Staff type the last 4 digits.
- **By code or name** (other trades start here): one request, with the item, how much and a "For whom / where" note, such as "Raju, site 2". The app gives it a 4-digit "Your pickup code", which the customer sends on to whoever collects. Staff type it in "Code or customer name" and can add "Collected by".

Staff then see a light: green "You can serve" when the vehicle has a request or the code shows "Code matches"; red when there is no request ("No credit", then "You can serve for cash.") or the vehicle is not on the list. A wrong code is refused. The sale shows on the customer's phone with "Given by" the staff member's name. If staff type more than was requested, the line carries "More than requested".

For example (made-up): Sharma Builders asks for 50 bags of cement for "Raju, site 2" and gets code 4827. Raju gives the code at the counter, staff see "Code matches", and the line reads "Taken by Raju". See [customer requests](@feature-requests) and [the counter light](@feature-counter-light). If a quantity is questioned later, follow [a disputed credit sale](@counter-disputes).
