This page is for a business that sells on credit to regular customers whose drivers or site supervisors collect the goods.

## Three people, one sale

The customer owes the money. The driver stands at your counter. Your staff serve one truck after another and cannot phone the customer for each. Trouble here is often a mistake or a misunderstanding, not bad intent.

| Who | Where it slips |
|---|---|
| Customer | May find out what each truck took only when your bill comes |
| Driver | Asks for a full tank when the customer meant Rs 5,000 |
| Your counter staff | Cannot tell what the customer meant for this truck today |

## What each side needs

You need four things:

1. The customer's say-so for **that vehicle, today**.
2. A cap: a quantity or a rupee amount. In Khata Kavach the customer can also choose "As needed", which is held only by the credit limit.
3. A record the customer also sees: vehicle, item, quantity, rupees, who served, time, and a photo if you add one.
4. A rule for a vehicle that is not on the customer's list.

The customer needs one thing: to see what each vehicle took, on their own phone, as soon as it is online.

## How a request per vehicle does this

The customer opens "Send requests" and answers "Which vehicles need goods today?". For each vehicle they pick the item and an amount: in the item's unit, in rupees, or "As needed". The request is valid till today by default, and the customer can cancel it.

At the counter, staff type the last 4 digits of the vehicle and see a light.

| Situation | What staff see |
|---|---|
| Request for this vehicle, still valid | Green, "You can serve", with "To give" showing the request's quantity |
| No request (default setting) | Red, "No credit", then "You can serve for cash." |
| Vehicle not on the list | Red, with the reason in big words |

The sale then shows on the customer's phone with "Given by" the staff member's name, and the photo if the counter added one. If staff type more than was requested, the phone asks "Is it right? The owner will see it." and the line carries "More than requested". Counter staff never see a customer's phone number.

For example (a made-up customer, Sharma Roadlines, plate UP00 SH 1005): the customer asks for 200 L of diesel for today. Staff type 1005 and see green. If staff give 300 L, the line is marked "More than requested" and Sharma Roadlines sees the mark. See [customer requests](@feature-requests) and [the counter light](@feature-counter-light). If a quantity is questioned later, follow [a disputed credit sale](@counter-disputes).
