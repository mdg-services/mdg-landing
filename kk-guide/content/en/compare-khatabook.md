Khatabook is made by ADJ Utility Apps Pvt Ltd. Its Google Play listing describes credit and debit tracking, reminders, GST and non-GST bills, inventory and loans. Shown on Google Play on 19 October 2026: 4.5 stars, 5.92 lakh reviews and 100M+ downloads.

## Where Khatabook does more

Far more downloads, iPhone and desktop apps, GST and non-GST invoices, inventory, loans through partners and more languages. Khata Kavach is for petrol pumps only. It has no iPhone or desktop app, its bill is not a GST tax invoice, it does not track stock, it never lends, and it has Hindi and English.

## Where Khata Kavach works differently

By default, every credit fill starts with a request from the transporter's own app, one per truck. The nozzle man sees the light before fuel goes in. By default a manager's PIN is needed past the limit. The owner's Today screen lists the fills that look wrong from the last 7 days. A correction is a new line. See the [Transporter app](@feature-customer-app) and the [light at the nozzle](@feature-counter-light).

## Who should stay with Khatabook

Stay if you need GST invoices, stock, or an iPhone or desktop app. Look at Khata Kavach if you run a petrol pump, regular transporters fill diesel on credit and you want them to ask first.
