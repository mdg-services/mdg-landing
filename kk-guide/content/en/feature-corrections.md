## Who can correct what

Mistakes happen at any pump. Khata Kavach does not hide one. It keeps the wrong line, marks it, and adds the right one next to it.

| What went wrong | Who fixes it | How |
|---|---|---|
| The nozzle man typed a wrong fill, less than 2 minutes ago (the default window) | The nozzle man who made it | The fix option on his own fill: save the fix, or say the fill never happened |
| A wrong fill, any time after that | Manager or owner | Correct the fill, with a reason |
| A payment recorded wrongly | Manager or owner | The payment is cancelled; a bill it had paid opens again |
| The fill is already on a bill | Owner | Cancel the bill first, then correct the fill |
| The balance itself is wrong | Owner | Correct the balance, with a reason |
| A request sent by mistake | The transporter | Cancel it and send a new one |

The manager or owner gives a reason, then chooses to cancel the fill and enter the right one, or only to cancel it. A fill that is on a bill says the bill must be cancelled first.

## What both sides see

The old fill stays on the account, marked struck out. A new line records the correction, and the right fill follows. The pump and the transporter see the same lines. A fill that has not yet left the pump's phone is fixed or dropped on the phone, before the transporter has seen it.

For example (made-up figures): a transporter owes Rs 3,00,000. The pump types a payment of Rs 50,000 instead of Rs 5,000, so the balance shows Rs 2,50,000. A manager cancels it and the balance goes back to Rs 3,00,000. The manager then records Rs 5,000 and the balance is Rs 2,95,000. All three lines stay on both phones.

## Why this takes the stress off

Once a fill has reached the transporter's phone, a correction is a visible new line with a reason. The transporter does not have to wonder whether last week's figure was altered. The owner does not have to argue from memory. See [how a disputed fill is settled](@counter-disputes).
