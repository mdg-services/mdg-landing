## Five kinds of credit tools, side by side

The table says who writes the sale down, whether the customer sees it, and who carries the credit.

| Kind | Who records the sale | Does the customer see each sale | Who carries the credit | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper khata, slip book, Excel | The business | Only if you show or send it | The business | No phone, no signal, no training |
| Ledger apps: Khatabook, OkCredit | The business | By SMS or WhatsApp reminders; we did not find a customer app described on their pages | The business | Everyday khata, bills, many languages |
| Billing apps: Vyapar, myBillBook | The business | Bills and reminders on WhatsApp; myBillBook says it shares live ledgers | The business | GST invoices, stock, accounting |
| Pump software: GARRUDA, Petrosoft (YoungMinds), Petro Genius | Pump staff; GARRUDA starts from the customer's request for a vehicle | They list a customer app or portal | The pump | Pump back office; GARRUDA lists shifts, nozzle readings and Tally |
| Fuel cards and credit networks | The programme | Through its own app or portal | Depends on the programme | Cashless fuelling at the programme's own pumps |
| Khata Kavach | Counter staff, after the customer's request (by default) | Yes, in the customer's own app, as soon as the counter phone is online | The business. It never lends or holds money | Regular customers who take goods on credit |

## Where the other tools do more

- **Khatabook and OkCredit** have far larger download counts, shown on Google Play on 19 October 2026: 100M+ for Khatabook, 10M+ for OkCredit. Both have iPhone apps and many languages.
- **Vyapar and myBillBook** make GST invoices and e-way bills, and keep stock and accounts.
- **Pump software** covers the pump back office: GARRUDA lists shifts, nozzle readings and Tally.

Khata Kavach is new. Its bill is not a GST tax invoice, and it does not track stock.

## Where Khata Kavach sits

It has two apps. By default, the customer sends a request before credit is given: by vehicle number (staff type the last 4 digits) or with a 4-digit pickup code that the customer passes to whoever collects. Staff see a green, amber or red light. By default a manager's PIN is needed past the limit, or the owner can choose cash only. The owner's Today screen lists flagged sales from the last 7 days. A correction is a new line, so both sides see what changed.

The bill is a numbered bill, with a UPI QR once you add your UPI ID, not a GST tax invoice. For the price, call us. Both apps open in the phone's browser today. Android apps are coming to Google Play.
