## Where the other tools do more

- **Khatabook and OkCredit** have far larger download counts, shown on Google Play on 19 October 2026: 100M+ for Khatabook, 10M+ for OkCredit. Both have iPhone apps and many languages.
- **Vyapar and myBillBook** make GST invoices and e-way bills, and keep stock and accounts.
- **Pump software** covers the pump back office: GARRUDA lists shifts, nozzle readings and Tally.

Khata Kavach is new and is for petrol pumps only. Its bill is not a GST tax invoice, and it does not track stock.

## Where Khata Kavach sits

It has two apps: the Pump app and the Transporter app. By default, the transporter sends a request before each credit fill, one per truck, in litres, in rupees or for a full tank. The nozzle man types the last 4 digits of the truck number and sees a green, amber or red light. By default a manager's PIN is needed past the limit, or the owner can choose cash only. The owner's Today screen lists the fills that look wrong from the last 7 days. A correction is a new line, so both sides see what changed.

The bill is a numbered bill on the pump's letterhead, with a UPI QR once you add your UPI ID, not a GST tax invoice. For the price, call us. Both apps open in the phone's browser today. Android apps are coming to Google Play.
