For a business that sells goods on credit to regular business customers. This is the page the customer signs; your [credit policy](@credit-policy) is the one you keep.

## The form

> **Customer credit form**
>
> 1. **Parties.** Business: [name, address, phone]. Customer: [name, address], GSTIN or PAN [if any].
> 2. **Responsible person.** [Name], phone [number], who answers for the account.
> 3. **Who may collect goods.** [Names or vehicle numbers]. Changes are sent in writing by the responsible person.
> 4. **Credit limit.** The customer may owe at most Rs [amount] at any one time.
> 5. **Credit days.** Each bill is paid within [days] days of the bill date.
> 6. **Bills.** We send a numbered bill every [period] on WhatsApp. The customer tells us within [7] days if a line looks wrong.
> 7. **Past the limit.** Goods go out past the limit only with the owner's or a named manager's OK, or for cash.
> 8. **Late payment.** If a bill is [days] days past due, new credit pauses until the dues are paid. Cash sales continue.
> 9. **Interest.** [None / rate] on overdue amounts, from [day].
> 10. **Corrections.** A wrong line is corrected by a new line with the reason, never rubbed out.
>
> Customer: [name] · sign · date. Business: [name] · sign · date.

## Why each line is there

1. Names the two sides, so there is no doubt who owes.
2. Gives you one person to call.
3. Says who may collect, so staff know whom to serve.
4. One figure that staff and customer can both check.
5. Says when a bill is late.
6. Tells the customer how bills come, and gives a short window to object.
7. Says before the day who may say yes at the counter.
8. Makes the stop a rule, not an insult.
9. Interest stands on firmest ground when it is written here and agreed.
10. Keeps the history honest on both sides.

## Before you use it

- Take the limit and days from your [credit limit](@credit-limit) and policy, and agree them with the customer first.
- Sign two copies, one each.
- Whether this form makes a binding contract is for an advocate to say.

## Where Khata Kavach fits

In Khata Kavach you set each customer's limit in rupees and when you collect. The customer reads the terms and taps "I agree" before sending a request. If you want a signature on paper, keep the signed form too.

General information, not legal advice. For your own case, speak to an advocate or a chartered accountant.
