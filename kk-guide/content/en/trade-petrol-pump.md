## Six decisions for diesel on credit

| Decision | What to set |
|---|---|
| Who gets credit | Regular transporters you know. A new one starts with a small limit and gets more after paying on time. |
| How much | A limit in rupees, not litres, because the rate moves. See [how to set a credit limit](@credit-limit). |
| Before the fill | The transporter sends a request for each truck before the fill. No request, no credit, by default. |
| What each fill record shows | Truck number, litres, rupees, who filled, time, and a photo or slip number. |
| Billing | One bill on fixed days: every month, or every 15 days. |
| When to stop | No new credit when the limit is full or an old balance stays unpaid. Fix the number of days in advance. |

## Petrol, diesel and GST

Petrol and diesel are outside GST {{Finance Ministry reply, Rajya Sabha, 21 Jul 2026}}. State VAT and central excise apply instead. For the bill rules in your state, ask a chartered accountant. The Khata Kavach bill is a numbered bill, not a GST tax invoice.

## How it runs at the nozzle

1. By default, the transporter sends a request for each truck from the Transporter app: litres, rupees or a full tank.
2. At the nozzle, the nozzle man types the last 4 digits of the truck number and sees [a light](@feature-counter-light). Green says "You can fill" and shows how much credit is left. Amber says "Call the manager" and asks for a manager's PIN. Red says "No credit": fill for cash.
3. The nozzle man types the rupees off the dispenser; the litres fill in at the day's rate. He adds a photo if he wants, and taps Done.
4. Both sides see the line once the phone is online. The owner's Today screen shows credit given today.
5. Make a bill in a tap and send it from your own WhatsApp, with a UPI QR if you added your UPI ID. When money is received, type it in. That much credit opens up again.

For example (made-up): Sharma Fuels gives Gupta Transport a limit of Rs 2,25,000, and Rs 85,000 is owed. Gupta Transport sends a request for truck UP00 SH 1006. The nozzle man types 1006, sees green with Rs 1,40,000 of credit left, and types 250 litres and Rs 22,500 off the dispenser. Credit left falls to Rs 1,17,500.

Khata Kavach never lends and never holds money. The transporter pays you directly. It does not replace the pump's own accounts or connect to the dispenser. It does not connect to any oil company's system, and it is not connected with any oil company.
