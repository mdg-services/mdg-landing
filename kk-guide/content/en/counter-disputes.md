This page is for a petrol pump with regular credit customers. A quarrel over one fill costs time and trust, so settle it from the record, not from memory.

## Settle it in five steps

1. **Stay calm and pull the record.** Date, time, truck number, litres, rupees, who filled, the request it was made against, and the photo or slip number.
2. **Read it out together.** Go line by line with the customer. Ask what differs: the litres, the rupees, the truck or the day.
3. **Show the customer the same line.** In Khata Kavach the transporter sees it on his own phone as soon as the phone is online, with the nozzle man's name and the photo if he added one.
4. **If the record is wrong, correct it openly.** Write a new line that cancels the old one, keep the old one visible, and write the reason. Never overwrite.
5. **If the record is right, explain it and keep the line.** Note what was agreed so it is not raised again.

A made-up example: the line says 200 L of diesel at Rs 90 a litre, Rs 18,000. The customer says the truck took 180 L. The photo of the slip reads 180 L, so the nozzle man typed the requested figure by mistake. The correction cancels Rs 18,000 and adds 180 L, Rs 16,200. Owed falls by Rs 1,800.

## Stop the next one

- **Request first.** The transporter sends a request for the truck, with litres, rupees or a full tank, before it fills.
- **A photo with the fill.** It is optional, one per fill, and helps a quick answer.
- **A slip number.** Optional too, for those who still keep slips.
- **The transporter sees each line.** Once the phone is online, he can raise a doubt that day, not at month end.

## In Khata Kavach

A wrong fill shows up on both phones, with who filled and the photo if one was added. After a correction, the old line stays marked struck out and the new line sits below it.
