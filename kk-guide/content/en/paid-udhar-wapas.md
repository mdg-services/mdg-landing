## The seven steps

Dukan ka udhar kaise nikale? Udhar wapas kaise le? This page is for a shop or dealer with regular business customers, not for money lent to a friend.

1. **Check your own record.** What is owed, since when, and what the customer has seen.
2. **Send a clear bill.** Bill number, period, amount, how to pay. See [numbered bills](@feature-bills).
3. **Ask politely, with the exact amount and a date.** In person, or on your own WhatsApp to that customer alone. Ready words: [reminder messages](@paid-reminder-messages).
4. **Agree a plan if the customer cannot pay at once.** Part payments with dates, written down.
5. **Stop new credit until it is cleared.** See [when to stop credit](@paid-stop-credit).
6. **Send a written demand.** Amount, bills, dates, a last date to pay. Keep a copy.
7. **Legal steps last.** See [the steps](@paid-legal-steps). The usual time limit to sue is 3 years, from delivery or from the end of the agreed credit period {{Limitation Act 1963, Art. 14}} {{Limitation Act 1963, Art. 15}}. With many deliveries on one account, courts differ on the start: see [the time limit](@paid-time-limit).

## A made-up example

Made-up business, made-up figures. Gupta Transport owes Sharma Traders Rs 84,000 and cannot pay at once. They agree Rs 28,000 on the 5th of each of the next 3 months (3 x Rs 28,000 = Rs 84,000). New credit stays closed until the third payment.

## What not to do

- Shame the customer in public, on social media or in a group chat.
- Threaten the customer, or send someone to frighten them.
- Pass the customer's name around to other businesses.

Public shaming or threats can backfire. If in doubt, ask an advocate.
