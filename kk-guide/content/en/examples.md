> **Example:** a made-up business with made-up figures. It is not a real customer.

## What these examples are

Each page takes one made-up business and follows one real situation, from the customer's request to the bill. The screens are described in the words the real apps use, such as "No credit", "Manager allowed it" and "Part paid". The names, vehicles and rupees are made up. Every table adds up, so you can check the sums yourself.

## The four examples

- [A month of diesel credit](@example-pump-month): a fuel business and a transport customer with three trucks, known at the counter by vehicle number. It shows a request before every sale, a red light when no request was sent, a typo warning, a manager's PIN past a Rs 2,00,000 limit, one bill and a part payment.
- [A contractor's limit, bag by bag](@example-contractor-limit): a building material dealer and a contractor. One Rs 50,000 limit covers cement in bags, sand in tonnes and steel in kilos. Each load has a request and a 4-digit pickup code. A request is taken back, and a payment opens credit again.
- [A disputed sale](@example-dispute): a contractor says 40 bags went out, not 50. It shows what both phones show, how a manager corrects the line with a reason, and why the old line stays.
- [A bill and a part payment](@example-bill-payment): a bill sent on WhatsApp, the "Part paid" status, and what changes when money is recorded without choosing a bill.

| Idea | Where to see it |

| How the counter knows the customer | By vehicle number: diesel month. By pickup code: contractor limit, disputed sale |
| A manager's PIN past the limit | Diesel month |

## What the examples are not

- **Not real customers.** Sharma Traders, Sharma Roadlines and the plate UP00 SH 1005 are made up.
- **Not results.** No example claims a result for any business. The figures show the arithmetic, and nothing else.
- **Not a promise of how your business will look.** You set your own limits. Khata Kavach never lends and never holds money. The customer pays you directly, and you record it.

## Why there is no real case study yet

Khata Kavach is new, and we have no real case study to publish. When we do, it will come with the business's written permission and its own figures, and it will be marked apart from these made-up examples. Until then we show made-up examples and say so, rather than make up a customer and present it as real.

## How to read the tables

Each line is one thing that happened: a sale, a payment or a struck-out line. Money received opens up that much credit again. To see the screens behind the examples, read [what Khata Kavach does](@features).
