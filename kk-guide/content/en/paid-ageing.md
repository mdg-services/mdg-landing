## What ageing is

This page is for a business whose regular customers buy on credit.

Ageing is how long each part of a customer's balance has been owed. Count the days from the sale (or the bill) to today. Then match the bands to your credit period: if you gave 30 days, the first two bands are still inside the agreed time, and the 31 to 60 band is late.

## The four bands

| Band | Risk | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 15 days | Low | Nothing. Check that the bill went out. |
| 16 to 30 days | Watch | If your agreed days are over, one polite message with the amount and a date. See [reminder messages](@paid-reminder-messages). |
| 31 to 60 days | High | Call. Ask for a date or agree part payments. Hold new credit until a date is agreed. |
| Over 60 days | Highest | Stop new credit. Send a written demand. Check the [time limit](@paid-time-limit). |

## A made-up example

Made-up business, made-up figures. Gupta Transport owes Sharma Traders Rs 1,25,000.

| Band | Owed now | After Rs 20,000 paid against the oldest sales |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 15 days | Rs 40,000 | Rs 40,000 |
| 16 to 30 days | Rs 35,000 | Rs 35,000 |
| 31 to 60 days | Rs 30,000 | Rs 30,000 |
| Over 60 days | Rs 20,000 | Rs 0 |
| Total | Rs 1,25,000 | Rs 1,05,000 |

Rs 50,000 of the Rs 1,25,000 (30,000 + 20,000) is older than 30 days. The total alone does not show that. Sharma Traders asks for the over-60 money first, and the oldest band is gone once Rs 20,000 is counted against it.

## Why old money matters more

The older a sale, the harder it is to settle what was taken and what was paid. Old money also needs a look at the [time limit](@paid-time-limit). A customer with a large total that is all under 15 days old needs no chasing. A smaller total that is all over 60 days old does.

## In Khata Kavach

When a customer's balance sits in more than one band, the customer's page shows how old it is, in these same four bands. The Today screen lists customers who are near or past their limit. See [the owner's Today screen](@feature-today).
