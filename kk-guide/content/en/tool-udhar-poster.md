## Which poster for which rule

Put up only the one that is true at your pump.

| Your rule | Poster |
|---|---|
| No credit to anyone | 1 or 2 |
| Credit to known customers, up to a limit | 3 or 6 |
| Credit, with a slip for every fill | 4 |
| No new credit until old dues are paid | 5 |

## How to print and put them up

1. Print on A4 at "Actual size" or 100%, not "Fit". Black and white is fine.
2. For a larger sign, print at A3: the text stays sharp.
3. Fix it at eye level, on the customer's side of the office counter. A clear plastic sleeve keeps it clean.
4. Tell your nozzle men and cashier the same sentence in words: see [how to refuse udhar politely](@credit-say-no).

## Ten polite slogans to copy

For a poster, a WhatsApp status or the foot of your own bill book.

1. Credit only up to the agreed limit.
2. Every credit fill gets a slip.
3. Old account clear, then new credit.
4. For credit, please speak to the owner.
5. Every customer has an account here. Please check yours.
6. Pay on time and your credit stays open.
7. Credit is for regular customers with an account here.
8. Please ask for your credit limit at the pump office.
9. We give credit with care, so that we can keep giving it.
10. Today's fuel for today's payment, in cash or by UPI. Thank you.

## Where Khata Kavach fits

A poster tells customers your rule. Khata Kavach checks the limit at the nozzle: you set each transporter's limit in rupees, and the nozzle man's phone shows a green, amber or red light before fuel goes in. Khata Kavach never lends and never holds money. See [how to set a limit](@credit-limit) and [a customer over the limit](@counter-over-limit).
