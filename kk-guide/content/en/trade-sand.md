## Why sand and aggregate credit is hard to track

Your buyers are contractors, small transporters and tractor or truck owners. They take a load and settle later. The load is gone in minutes. What stays is a slip.

Each sale is against the buyer's request and its pickup code. One buyer can send many vehicles and drivers on one account.

## How a load is sold on credit

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Request | The buyer sends a request: the item and how much, in its own unit (tonnes, trips), or rupees, or "As needed". The app gives it a 4-digit "Your pickup code", and "Send on WhatsApp" passes it to the driver |
| Counter | Staff type the code in "Code or customer name". "Code matches" and a green light show credit left and what to give. Red shows "No credit" and the reason. Amber needs a manager's PIN |
| Sale | Staff type the quantity and the rupees from the weighbridge slip, add the slip number, "Collected by" and a photo of the loaded vehicle or the slip if they want, and tap Done |
| Both phones | The buyer sees the same line: item, quantity, rupees, who served, who took it, the photo |

For example (made-up figures): a buyer's limit is Rs 1,00,000 and the account shows Rs 62,000 owed. A request for 18 tonnes is served and the rupees typed are Rs 20,700, which works out to Rs 1,150 a tonne. Owed is now Rs 82,700, and Rs 17,300 of credit is left.

## Set the limit in rupees

The limit is one rupee figure per buyer. It is never turned into tonnes or trips, because the app keeps no rate list. See [how to set a credit limit](@credit-limit). By default, past the limit the light turns amber, and a manager's PIN lets one sale through. The owner's Today screen flags the sale. With a [request](@feature-requests) for each load, a driver with no request or code is, by default, served for cash or waits for the buyer to send one.

## What Khata Kavach does not do

It does not connect to the weighbridge, keep a rate list or track stock. A [photo on the sale](@feature-photo), when staff add one, shows what left if a load is questioned. It never lends and never holds money: the buyer pays you directly and you record it.
