## Words for the account

| Word | Hindi | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Udhar, udhari | उधार, उधारी | Fuel taken now and paid for later. Udhari is the amount owed because of it. |
| Khata | खाता | The account kept for one customer: what you filled on credit, what he paid, what is left. |
| Bahi khata | बही (हाथ का खाता) | The traditional hand-written account book that holds each customer's khata. |
| Ledger | हिसाब की बही | The full list of lines in an account. In Khata Kavach both sides see the same one. |
| Jama, kharch | जमा, ख़र्च | Jama is money received. Kharch is money spent. Jama-kharch records both. |
| Baki, bakaya | बाकी, बकाया | What is still unpaid. Bakaya usually means a larger or older balance. |
| Outstanding | बकाया रकम | The total a customer still owes you. |
| Opening balance | शुरू का बकाया | What the customer already owed on the day you started the new book. Khata Kavach has a box for it when you add a transporter. |
| Advance | एडवांस | Money paid before fuel is filled, or more than what was due. |

## Words for limit and time

| Word | Hindi | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Credit limit | उधार की सीमा | The most a customer may owe you at one time. You set it for each customer. See [how to set a limit](@credit-limit). |
| Credit period | उधार के दिन | The days you allow before payment is due, such as 30 days. |
| Due date | भुगतान की तारीख़ | The day payment falls due: the bill date plus the credit period. |
| Ageing | बकाया कितने दिन पुराना है (एजिंग) | How long a balance has been unpaid. Khata Kavach groups it as 0 to 15, 16 to 30, 31 to 60 and over 60 days. |
| Part payment | पार्ट पेमेंट | Paying less than what is due. The bill shows it as part paid and the rest stays due. |
| Statement | स्टेटमेंट | A list of every fill and payment in a period, with the balance at the end. |

## Words for bills, tax and payment

| Word | Hindi | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Bill | बिल | A numbered page or PDF listing what was filled and the total. |
| Kachcha bill | कच्चा बिल | In trade talk, usually a plain slip or estimate with no tax shown. Not a legal term. |
| Pakka bill | पक्का बिल | In trade talk, a full numbered bill with the seller's details; some traders mean one with tax shown. Not a legal term either. |
| GST tax invoice | GST टैक्स इनवॉइस | The invoice a GST-registered seller issues for taxable goods, with the tax shown. A Khata Kavach bill is not one. Petrol and diesel are outside GST {{Finance Ministry reply, Rajya Sabha, 21 Jul 2026}}. |
| Bill of supply | बिल ऑफ़ सप्लाई | A bill some sellers issue instead of a tax invoice when they do not charge GST. Ask your chartered accountant whether it applies to you. |
| TDS | TDS | Tax a buyer may deduct and pay to the government, so you receive that much less. Khata Kavach does not work it out; you type it in as "TDS deducted". |
| UTR | UTR | The reference number of a bank or UPI transfer. |
| UPI QR | UPI QR | A square code the customer scans with a UPI app to pay your UPI ID. The money goes straight to you. |

## Words for getting paid, and the law

| Word | Hindi | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Cheque bounce | चेक बाउंस | A cheque the bank returns unpaid. It can be an offence if the payee sends a written demand within 30 days of the bank's return memo and the drawer does not pay within 15 days {{NI Act 1881, s.138}}. Security cheques and disputed debts may fall outside. See [cheque bounce](@paid-cheque-bounce). |
| Legal notice | कानूनी नोटिस | A written demand for payment. In a cheque bounce case it is a required step {{NI Act 1881, s.138}}. |
| Acknowledgment of debt | देनदारी की लिखित स्वीकृति | A signed, written statement by the customer that the money is owed. Given before the time limit ends, it starts the period afresh {{Limitation Act 1963, s.18}}. Whether a confirmation in an app counts is not settled: it may help as evidence. |
| Limitation period | परिसीमा अवधि | The time within which you can sue. For goods sold on credit: 3 years from delivery when no credit period was agreed {{Limitation Act 1963, Art. 14}}, or from the end of an agreed credit period {{Limitation Act 1963, Art. 15}}. When many deliveries run on one account, courts differ on when the clock starts. See [the time limit](@paid-time-limit). |
| MSME, Udyam | MSME, उद्यम | Micro, small and medium businesses; Udyam is their registration. A payment rule protects registered micro and small suppliers: pay by the agreed date, at most 45 days {{MSMED Act 2006, s.15}}. Whether a trader qualifies is doubtful. See [the 45-day rule](@paid-msme). |
| Mediation | मीडिएशन (मध्यस्थता) | Talks with a neutral person to settle a dispute. Commercial disputes of Rs 3 lakh or more need mediation tried before a suit {{Commercial Courts Act 2015, s.12A}}. |
| Summary suit | संक्षिप्त वाद | A faster court case for a fixed sum backed by a written contract or bill {{CPC, Order XXXVII}}. Whether a khata counts as a written contract is not settled. |

For the steps in order, see [the legal steps in plain words](@paid-legal-steps).

General information, not legal advice. For your own case, speak to an advocate or a chartered accountant.
