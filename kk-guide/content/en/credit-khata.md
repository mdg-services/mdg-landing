## One page for each customer

An udhar khata (udhar ka hisab) only works if each customer has his own page and every line is written when the fuel goes in. A credit line needs the columns below.

## What every credit line shows

| Column | What to write |
|---|---|
| Date and time | When the truck filled |
| Truck | The number plate |
| Fuel and litres | Diesel or petrol, and how many litres |
| Rupees given | The value of the fill |
| Who filled | The nozzle man |
| Slip number or photo | Something you can point to later |
| Balance | The line above, plus this fill |

## What every payment line shows

The amount, how it was paid (cash, UPI, bank, cheque), the UTR (the bank's transaction number) or cheque number, the date, and which bill it clears. Then the new balance.

## Never overwrite

Strike a wrong line through and write the correct one below it, with a reason. The old line stays, so anyone reading the page can see what changed.

The page of a made-up customer, Sharma Roadlines. The 12 October line was written against the wrong truck, so it is struck out (shown as a minus) and written again.

At month end, copy the page and give the customer a statement.

## How a shared book changes it

On paper, the page stays with you. In Khata Kavach the transporter's phone shows each line as soon as it is online: the fill you entered, who filled, and the slip number and photo if the nozzle man added them. A correction appears as a struck-out line plus a new one, as above. The nozzle man types what was filled, and the owner's screen shows credit given, money received and what is still owed.
