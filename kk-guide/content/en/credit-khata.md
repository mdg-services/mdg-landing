## One page for each customer

An udhar khata (udhar ka hisab) only works if each customer has their own page and every line is written when the goods go out. A credit line needs the columns below.

## What every credit line shows

| Column | What to write |
|---|---|
| Date and time | When the goods left |
| Vehicle | The number plate that collected |
| Item and quantity | In its own unit: litres, bags, tonnes, pieces |
| Rupees given | The value of the sale |
| Who served | The staff member |
| Slip number or photo | Something you can point to later |
| Balance | The line above, plus this sale |

## What every payment line shows

The amount, how it was paid (cash, UPI, bank, cheque), the UTR (the bank's transaction number) or cheque number, the date, and which bill it clears. Then the new balance.

## Never overwrite

Strike a wrong line through and write the correct one below it, with a reason. The old line stays, so anyone reading the page can see what changed.

## An example page

> **Example:** a made-up business with made-up figures. It is not a real customer.

The page of a made-up customer, Sharma Roadlines. The 12 October line was written against the wrong vehicle, so it is struck out (shown as a minus) and written again.

| Date | Entry | Vehicle | Item | Given (Rs) | Received (Rs) | Balance (Rs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Oct | Sale, slip 114 | UP00 SH 1005 | Diesel 200 L | 18,000 | | 18,000 |
| 5 Oct | Sale, slip 121 | UP00 SH 1005 | Diesel 150 L | 13,500 | | 31,500 |
| 9 Oct | Payment, UPI | | | | 20,000 | 11,500 |
| 12 Oct | Sale, slip 130 | UP00 SH 1006 | Diesel 100 L | 9,000 | | 20,500 |

| 12 Oct | Sale, slip 130 | UP00 SH 1005 | Diesel 100 L | 9,000 | | 20,500 |

At month end, copy the page and give the customer a statement.

## How a shared book changes it

On paper, the page stays with you. In Khata Kavach the customer's phone shows each line as soon as it is online: the sale you entered, who served, and the slip number and photo if the counter added them. A correction appears as a struck-out line plus a new one, as above. The counter types what was given, and the owner's screen shows credit given, money received and what is still owed.
