## The two clocks

This page is for a petrol pump that sells diesel on credit to regular customers such as transporters, not for money lent to a friend or relative.

The time limit is the last day to file a suit for the price of diesel sold on credit.

| Your case | The 3 years start | Source |
|---|---|---|
| No credit period was agreed | On the day the diesel was delivered (filled) | {{Limitation Act 1963, Art. 14}} |
| A credit period was agreed, say 30 days | On the day that period ends | {{Limitation Act 1963, Art. 15}} |

## What starts the 3 years afresh

- **A signed acknowledgment.** The transporter writes that the money is owed and signs it before the 3 years end. A fresh 3 years run from the day of signing {{Limitation Act 1963, s.18}}.
- **A part payment, with a note.** A fresh 3 years run from the payment only if the payer also writes or signs an acknowledgment of that payment {{Limitation Act 1963, s.19}}. A payment on its own does not do it.

## A made-up timeline

Made-up dates and figures. Diesel worth Rs 1,04,000 is filled in Gupta Transport's trucks at Sharma Fuels on 10 January 2026 with 30 days' credit.

| Date | What happens | Last day to file, about |
|---|---|---|
| 10 Jan 2026 | Diesel filled, 30 days' credit agreed | Not counting yet |
| 9 Feb 2026 | Credit period ends, nothing paid | 9 Feb 2029 {{Limitation Act 1963, Art. 15}} |
| 1 Jun 2027 | The transporter pays Rs 20,000 by UPI and writes nothing about it | Still 9 Feb 2029 {{Limitation Act 1963, s.19}} |
| 15 Mar 2028 | The transporter signs a note confirming Rs 84,000 is owed (Rs 1,04,000 less Rs 20,000) | 15 Mar 2031 {{Limitation Act 1963, s.18}} |

## What is not settled

- **Many fills on one account.** Courts differ on when the clock starts: at each delivery, at the last delivery, or when payment fell due. Count from your oldest unpaid fill to be careful; an advocate can count the exact last day.
- **A confirmation in an app or chat.** Whether it counts as a signed written acknowledgment is not settled. It may help as evidence. For the time limit, get a signature on paper.

## What to do

Do not let dues age. Check [how old your udhar is](@paid-ageing) every month, and ask transporters with an old balance to sign a confirmation. Keep the [evidence](@paid-proof). Legal steps take time, so start well before the date: see [the steps](@paid-legal-steps).

Khata Kavach shows how old each transporter's balance is (0 to 15, 16 to 30, 31 to 60, over 60 days) and dates every fill, bill and payment. It does not collect dues.
