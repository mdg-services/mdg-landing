## What an entry can and cannot do

This page is for a petrol pump and its regular customers, transporters and fleet owners, who take diesel on credit.

A khata entry is your own record. Books of account and records kept on a phone or computer can be used as evidence. But a line you wrote yourself does not settle who owes what. The transporter can say his truck never took that diesel.

A record kept on a phone or server normally goes to court with a certificate attached {{BSA 2023, s.63}}. Whether a given record is accepted is for the court to decide.

## What helps, and what to keep

| Keep this | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| A bill the transporter received, and the WhatsApp message it went in | Shows the amount and period were sent to him |
| A signed fill slip, from the driver or the transporter's person | Shows who took the diesel |
| A dated balance confirmation, signed by the transporter on paper | Shows he accepts the amount. Signed before the time limit ends, it starts the 3 years afresh {{Limitation Act 1963, s.18}} |
| Payment records with UTR or cheque number | Show what was paid, and against which bill |
| Photos of the slip and the vehicle's number plate | Show what was given, which truck took it, and when |

The 3 years are explained in [the time limit to recover dues](@paid-time-limit).

## What is not settled

- **A tap or a chat.** Whether a transporter's confirmation in an app or on WhatsApp counts as "in writing and signed" is not settled. It may help as evidence. For the time limit, a signed paper is the careful choice.
- **A khata alone.** Whether it counts as a written contract for a faster suit is not settled. See [the legal steps](@paid-legal-steps).
- **The certificate.** Who signs it, and what it holds: ask an advocate.

## Keep the record clean

Every credit fill needs the date, time, transporter, vehicle number, fuel, litres, rupees, who served and a slip number. Never overwrite: strike out the wrong line and add a new one with the reason. See [how to keep a khata](@credit-khata) and [how to settle a disputed fill](@counter-disputes).

In Khata Kavach, each fill is one line, with a [photo](@feature-photo) if the nozzle man added one. As soon as the phone is online, the transporter sees the same line in his own app. A correction is a new line, so both sides can see what changed.

General information, not legal advice. For your own case, speak to an advocate or a chartered accountant.
