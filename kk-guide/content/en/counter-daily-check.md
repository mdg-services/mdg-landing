## The six daily checks

Aim for a short routine at the same time every day. Write down two or three names to call.

| What to look at | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Credit given today | Shows how much went out, and in how many fills | If it is far above a usual day, open the fills and see why |
| Money received | Shows whether money comes in as fast as credit goes out | Record any payment you received, with how it was paid |
| Near or past the limit | These customers have little room left | Ask for payment, or decide the limit afresh |
| Fills allowed past the limit, or with no request | Shows who allowed it and why | Ask that manager. Check the reason makes sense |
| Fills that look wrong | Litres above the request, rupees that do not match the litres, a late entry | Call the nozzle man. If the line is wrong, correct it with a reason |
| Who to call today | Turns the list into action | Call those you picked, in person or on WhatsApp |

For example (made-up): a customer's limit is Rs 10,00,000 and Rs 8,40,000 is owed. 80 percent of the limit is Rs 8,00,000, so this customer is near the limit.

## How Khata Kavach's Today screen shows these

The owner and managers open Today in the Pump app.

- **Credit given today**, with the number of fills.
- **This month**: credit given, money received, and what is still owed. Money received is shown for the month, not for the day.
- **Due on bills**, with how many are past due.
- **Near or past the limit**: customers at 80 percent of the limit or more. Each has a button that opens your own WhatsApp with a message asking for payment, ready. You press send.
- **Fills that look wrong**: fills marked in the last 7 days, such as over the limit, no request, more than requested, or arrived late. A fill that a manager allowed with a PIN shows that manager's name.

Tap a customer to see how old their balance is, in four bands. See [ageing explained](@paid-ageing). To work through each screen, read [the owner's Today screen](@feature-today). A marked fill that turns out wrong is settled as in [a disputed credit fill](@counter-disputes).
