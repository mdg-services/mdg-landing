## The six daily checks

Aim for a short routine at the same time every day. Write down two or three names to call.

| What to look at | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Credit given today | Shows how much went out, and in how many sales | If it is far above a usual day, open the sales and see why |
| Money received | Shows whether money comes in as fast as credit goes out | Record any payment you received, with how it was paid |
| Near or past the limit | These customers have little room left | Ask for payment, or decide the limit afresh |
| Sales allowed past the limit, or with no request | Shows who allowed it and why | Ask that manager. Check the reason makes sense |
| Sales that look wrong | A quantity above the request, an odd rate, a late entry | Call the counter. If the line is wrong, correct it with a reason |
| Who to call today | Turns the list into action | Call those you picked, in person or on WhatsApp |

For example (made-up): a customer's limit is Rs 10,00,000 and Rs 8,40,000 is owed. 80 percent of the limit is Rs 8,00,000, so this customer is near the limit.

## How Khata Kavach's Today screen shows these

The owner and managers open "Today" in the Business app.

- **Credit given today**, with the number of sales.
- **This month**: Credit given, Money received, Owed before and Total owed. Money received is shown for the month, not for the day.
- **Due on bills**, with how many are past due.
- **Near or past the limit**: customers at 80 percent of the limit or more. Each has "Ask for payment on WhatsApp", which opens your own WhatsApp with the message ready. You press send.
- **Look at these**: sales marked in the last 7 days, such as "Over the limit", "No request", "More than requested", "Odd rate" and "Arrived late". A sale that a manager allowed with a PIN is recorded "Allowed by" that manager's name.

Tap a customer to see how old their balance is, in four bands. See [ageing explained](@paid-ageing). To work through each screen, read [the owner's Today screen](@feature-today). A marked sale that turns out wrong is settled as in [a disputed credit sale](@counter-disputes).
