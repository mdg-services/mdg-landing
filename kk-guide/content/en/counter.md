## Three questions before goods go out

You set the rules. Your counter staff apply them in seconds, with a customer waiting, and cannot phone you before every sale.

| Question staff ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Is this customer allowed credit today? | An account may be on hold, or the vehicle or code may not belong to a credit customer. |
| How much is left? | The limit is the most you agreed to be owed. Past it, the risk is yours. |
| Did the customer ask for it? | Nothing should leave on credit that the customer did not ask for, except a customer you trust. |

If any answer is no, staff sell for cash or call the person who may decide. See [what to do when a customer is over the limit](@counter-over-limit).

## What every credit sale must record

One line per sale, written the same way every time:

1. Who took it: the vehicle number, or the pickup code and who collected.
2. The item and the quantity, in the item's own unit (litres, bags, tonnes).
3. The rupees.
4. Who served.
5. The date and time.
6. A slip number or a photo of the load.
7. If a manager allowed it, who.

A line with no vehicle or code, or no quantity, settles nothing. For an argument about one sale, see [a disputed sale](@counter-disputes).

## What memory, a register and slips record

Each of these works while you stand at the counter for every sale. They strain when staff serve customers you do not see.

- **Memory.** The person who knows a customer's balance is not always the person serving.
- **A register.** One copy, kept by the seller. It shows what was written, not what the customer already owes at that moment.
- **Paper slips.** They need no phone and no signal. They show no running total. See [paper slips and coupons](@counter-slips).

## Decide once, let the screen apply it, let both see it

Khata Kavach follows that order. The customer sends a request from their own phone. The owner chooses once how the counter knows the customer: by vehicle number (a request per vehicle; staff type the last 4 digits) or by code or name (the app gives the request a 4-digit pickup code, which the customer passes to whoever collects; staff type the code). Staff then see one light: green "You can serve", amber "Call the manager", or red "No credit". By default, past the limit a manager types a PIN for that one sale. Staff type what was given and tap "Done". Once the phone is online, both sides see the same line, with a photo if added. The owner's Today screen shows credit given today, the month's totals, and flagged sales such as "Over the limit".

| Person | Decides | In Khata Kavach |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Each customer's limit, what happens at the limit or with no request, freezing an account | These are the owner's settings. Managers and staff cannot change them |
| Manager | One sale past the limit, where the owner allows it | Types a PIN on the counter phone; one PIN allows one sale |
| Counter staff | Serve on green, serve for cash on red | Find the vehicle or code, see the light, type what was given |

See [the counter light and credit limit](@feature-counter-light) and [how to set a credit limit](@credit-limit).
