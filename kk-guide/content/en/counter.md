## Three questions before the truck fills

You set the rules. Your nozzle men apply them in seconds, with a driver waiting, and cannot phone you before every fill.

| Question the nozzle man asks | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Is this truck allowed credit today? | An account may be on hold, or the truck may not belong to a credit customer. |
| How much is left? | The limit is the most you agreed to be owed. Past it, the risk is yours. |
| Did the transporter ask for it? | Nothing should go on credit that the transporter did not ask for, unless you have chosen to allow it for him. |

If any answer is no, the nozzle man fills for cash or calls the person who may decide. See [what to do when a customer is over the limit](@counter-over-limit).

## What every credit fill must record

One line per fill, written the same way every time:

1. The truck: its number plate.
2. The fuel and the litres.
3. The rupees.
4. Who filled.
5. The date and time.
6. A slip number or a photo, of the number plate or the slip.
7. If a manager allowed it, who.

A line with no truck number, or no litres, settles nothing. For an argument about one fill, see [a disputed fill](@counter-disputes).

## What memory, a register and slips record

Each of these works while you stand at the pump for every fill. They strain when nozzle men fill for customers you do not see.

- **Memory.** The person who knows a customer's balance is not always the person at the nozzle.
- **A register.** One copy, kept by the pump. It shows what was written, not what the customer already owes at that moment.
- **Paper slips.** They need no phone and no signal. They show no running total. See [paper slips and coupons](@counter-slips).

## Decide once, let the screen apply it, let both see it

Khata Kavach follows that order. The transporter sends a request from his own phone, one per truck: litres, rupees, or a full tank. At the nozzle, the nozzle man types the last 4 digits of the truck number and sees one light: green "You can fill", amber "Call the manager", or red "No credit". By default, past the limit a manager types a PIN for that one fill. The nozzle man types the litres and rupees off the dispenser and taps Done. Once the phone is online, both sides see the same line, with a photo if added. The owner's Today screen shows credit given today, money received, what is still owed, who is near the limit, and fills that look wrong.

| Person | Decides | In Khata Kavach |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Each transporter's limit, what happens at the limit or with no request, freezing an account | These are the owner's settings. Managers and nozzle men cannot change them |
| Manager | One fill past the limit, where the owner allows it | Types a PIN on the nozzle man's phone; one PIN allows one fill |
| Nozzle man | Fill on green, fill for cash on red | Types the truck's last 4 digits, sees the light, types the litres and rupees |

See [the light at the nozzle and the credit limit](@feature-counter-light) and [how to set a credit limit](@credit-limit).
