## The one test: does a vehicle come to collect?

Khata Kavach is a shared credit book between a business that sells on credit and its regular customers. It fits when the customer's own vehicle collects the goods. Every sale and every request is tied to a vehicle number. This page is for a business and its regular customers, not for money lent to a friend.

There are two apps. The Business app is for the owner, managers and counter staff. The Customer app is for the regular customer, who by default sends a request for each vehicle before goods go out. Both apps open in the phone's browser today. Android apps are coming to Google Play.

## Who it fits

| Trade | What is sold, in which unit | Who collects | Fits? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fuel stations | Diesel and petrol, in litres | The driver of the customer's truck | Yes: [diesel on credit](@trade-petrol-pump) |
| Transporters (the customer's side) | Diesel or material bought on credit, in the supplier's units | Their own drivers | Yes: the supplier adds them. See [the transporter's view](@trade-transporter) |
| Cement and building material | Cement in bags, steel in kg, bricks in pieces | A contractor's tractor or truck, or a site supervisor | Yes: [cement on credit](@trade-cement) |
| Sand and aggregate | Tonnes | The contractor's truck | Yes: [sand and aggregate](@trade-sand) |
| Wholesalers | Any unit, or amount only | The buyer's vehicle | Yes, when a vehicle collects: [wholesale credit](@trade-wholesale) |
| Shops that deliver to homes | Anything | No vehicle comes | No |

Every item has its own unit: litres, bags, tonnes, kg, pieces, or just rupees. One business can sell cement in bags and sand in tonnes.

## Who it does not fit

- **Shops that deliver with no vehicle.** Every sale is tied to a vehicle number, so it is not made for them.
- **Businesses that need GST tax invoices, stock or accounts from the same app.** Khata Kavach makes a numbered bill, not a GST tax invoice. It does not track stock or file GST. Use billing software for that: see [how billing apps compare](@compare-billing-apps).
- **Anyone who wants someone else to carry the credit.** You set each customer's limit and you carry the credit. Khata Kavach never lends and never holds money. The customer pays you directly and you record it.
- **Cash-only counters.** It keeps credit sales and money received, not cash sales.

## What is the same in every trade listed here

By default the customer sends a request from their own phone. At the counter, staff see a green, amber or red light. Both sides see every sale as one line once the phone is online, and a correction is a new line. The owner's Today screen shows credit given today, money received this month and who is near the limit.

A business starts Khata Kavach on its own, in the Business app, in about a minute. It then adds its own customers. Hindi is the main language in both apps, with English one tap away.
