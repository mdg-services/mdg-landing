## The one test: do regular customers fill on credit?

Khata Kavach is a shared credit book between a petrol pump and its regular customers. It fits when the customer has a credit account and sends his truck, with his own driver, to your nozzle. This page is for a pump and its regular customers, not for money lent to a friend.

There are two apps. The Pump app is for the owner, managers and nozzle men. The Transporter app is for the regular customer, who by default sends a request before each truck fills. Both apps open in the phone's browser today. Android apps are coming to Google Play.

## How the nozzle knows the truck

The nozzle man types the last 4 digits of the truck number and sees the light. He types the litres and the rupees off the dispenser. There is no rate list in the app: nothing is priced or converted. A truck number can be active with one transporter at a time.

## Who it fits

| Who | What they do | Fits? |
|---|---|---|
| Petrol pumps, of any oil company | Give diesel and petrol on credit to regular customers, by the litre | Yes: [diesel on credit](@trade-petrol-pump) |
| Transporters (the customer's side) | Buy diesel on credit for their trucks, and send a request for each truck | Yes: the pump adds them. See [the transporter's view](@trade-transporter) |
| Fleet owners, bus and tractor owners | Fill their vehicles on credit at the pump | Yes, the same way as a transporter |
| Contractors with machines | Their machines fill at the pump on the contractor's credit | Yes, by the vehicle number, the same way |

It covers the four fuels the pump sells: diesel, petrol, premium diesel and premium petrol.

## Who it does not fit

- **Walk-in customers who fill for cash.** It is for regular customers with a credit account.
- **A pump that needs GST tax invoices, stock or accounts from the same app.** Khata Kavach makes a numbered bill, not a GST tax invoice. It does not track stock, nozzle readings or shifts, and does not file GST. Use pump or billing software for that: see [how billing apps compare](@compare-billing-apps).
- **Anyone who wants someone else to carry the credit.** You set each transporter's limit and you carry the credit. Khata Kavach never lends and never holds money. The transporter pays you directly and you record it.
- **Businesses that are not petrol pumps.** Khata Kavach is built for pumps and their customers only.

## What is the same at every pump

By default the transporter sends a request from his own phone. At the nozzle, the nozzle man sees a green, amber or red light. Both sides see every fill as one line once the phone is online, and a correction is a new line. The owner's Today screen shows credit given today, money received and who is near the limit.

A pump starts Khata Kavach on its own, in the Pump app with "Set up your pump", in about a minute. It then adds its own transporters. Hindi is the main language in both apps, with English one tap away.
