## The rule of thumb

Ask: how much does this customer buy from you in one credit period? That is a sensible limit.

**Limit = monthly purchase × credit days ÷ 30**

It is a rule of thumb, not a law. Your own margin, the customer's other dues and how reliably they have paid you can move it up or down. A new customer starts lower, say at half, and rises after on-time payments.

## Three worked limits

> **Example:** a made-up business with made-up figures. It is not a real customer.

| Customer | Monthly purchase | Credit days | Working | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A transporter buying diesel | Rs 4,50,000 | 15 | 4,50,000 × 15 ÷ 30 | Rs 2,25,000 |
| A contractor buying cement | Rs 3,00,000 | 30 | 3,00,000 × 30 ÷ 30 | Rs 3,00,000 |
| A retailer buying for the shop | Rs 1,20,000 | 7 | 1,20,000 × 7 ÷ 30 | Rs 28,000 |

If the retailer is new, start at half: Rs 14,000.

## Credit days and the limit move together

Seven days of credit means a small limit. Thirty days means a large one, for the same buying. Pick the period first, then the limit follows. Fixing 7, 15 or 30 days also tells you when a balance is late: see [when to stop credit](@paid-stop-credit).

## Set it in rupees

Prices move, so a limit in litres or bags drifts. With made-up prices: a limit of 2,500 litres is Rs 2,25,000 when diesel is Rs 90 a litre, but Rs 2,37,500 at Rs 95. A limit of 750 bags is Rs 3,00,000 when cement is Rs 400 a bag, but Rs 3,15,000 at Rs 420. The money you have out grew while the limit looked the same. A limit in rupees stays the same.

## A good customer a little over

Decide in advance who may allow it: a named manager, never the person serving. Write down how much over and why. See [what staff do when a customer is over the limit](@counter-over-limit). To decide who gets a limit at all, see [who to give credit to](@credit-who).

Khata Kavach checks the rupee limit you set at the counter, with a green, amber or red light. By default, a manager's PIN is needed to go past it. The owner changes a customer's limit; managers and counter staff cannot.
