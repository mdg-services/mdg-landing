## The two words

This page is for a petrol pump whose regular customers, transporters, take diesel on credit.

| Word | In Hindi | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Outstanding | बकाया | Everything the transporter still owes you today, whether or not it is due yet |
| Overdue | देर से बाकी रकम | The part of it whose due date has passed |
| Due date | भुगतान की तारीख़ | The fill date or bill date plus the credit days you agreed |

Overdue is always part of outstanding, never more.

## A made-up example

Made-up pump, made-up figures. Sharma Fuels gives Gupta Transport 15 days' credit for diesel. Today is 30 October.

| Fill | Amount | Due date | Today |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 October | Rs 30,000 | 23 October | Overdue by 7 days |
| 18 October | Rs 25,000 | 2 November | Not due yet |
| 25 October | Rs 25,000 | 9 November | Not due yet |
| Total | Rs 80,000 outstanding | | Rs 30,000 overdue |

If Gupta Transport now pays Rs 30,000, counted against the oldest fill, outstanding falls to Rs 50,000 and nothing is overdue.

## Why watch overdue, not just outstanding

Two made-up transporters:

| | Outstanding | Overdue | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transporter A | Rs 80,000 | Rs 0 | Every rupee is inside the credit days. No chasing. |
| Transporter B | Rs 30,000 | Rs 30,000 | Every rupee is late. Call B first. |

A owes more and has no problem. B owes less and every rupee is late. Outstanding shows how much credit you have out, which you compare with the [credit limit](@credit-limit). Overdue shows where to act.

## What to do about each

- **Outstanding:** keep it under the transporter's limit, and look at the total across transporters with the [debtor days calculator](@tool-debtor-days).
- **Overdue:** send a [reminder](@paid-reminder-messages) the same day. See how old it is in [ageing bands](@paid-ageing). Decide in advance [when to stop credit](@paid-stop-credit).
