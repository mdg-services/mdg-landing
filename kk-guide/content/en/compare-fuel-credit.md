## Three ways to sell diesel on credit

1. **Your own credit.** You choose who, and how much. You carry the credit. Khata Kavach keeps the record both sides see.
2. **An oil company's fleet card.** The transporter holds a card and the programme sets the terms.
3. **A fuel credit network.** A platform that helps transporters get fuel credit. Supermileage's site describes "a technology-driven fuel credit and fleet management platform"; who carries the credit is not stated on its pages.

## Where the programmes do more

IndianOil and HPCL cards work across that company's own pumps. IndianOil lists reward points, crew accident cover up to Rs 5 lakh and reports; BPCL lists ad hoc balance limits and a pump locator.

## Where your own credit differs

The decision is yours, for a transporter you know. The transporter pays you directly by cash, UPI, bank or cheque, and you record it. The risk of non-payment stays with you, so a limit and a record that both sides see matter. See [how to set a credit limit](@credit-limit).

## Which fits

- The transporter buys fuel at many pumps: look at a card programme or a network, and check where it works.
- The pump sells to transporters it knows: its own credit. See [credit at a petrol pump](@trade-petrol-pump) and [what transporters see](@trade-transporter).
