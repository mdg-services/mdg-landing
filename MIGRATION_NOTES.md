# Moving mdgservices.in from Vite to Next.js

Branch `nextjs-migration`. Nothing here is committed or deployed.
Next.js 16.3.8 (exact), App Router, React 19, Tailwind 3, Vercel.

## What moved where

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| `index.html` (title, meta, JSON-LD, Bing tag, fonts link) | `app/(en)/layout.tsx`, `app/hi/layout.tsx`, `src/seo/*`, `src/fonts/*` |
| `src/main.tsx`, `App.tsx`, react-router | `app/` routes; `src/views/*` (home, register, grievance) |
| `src/i18n/lang.tsx` (one provider, device/storage guess) | `lang.ts` (paths, cookie), `server.ts` (`getT`), `client.tsx` (`useT`); the URL decides |
| `middleware.ts` (guides snapshots, film) | `proxy.ts` + `src/routing/*` (Next 16 calls middleware "proxy") |
| `api/*.ts` | `app/api/<name>/route.ts` + `src/lib/api.ts`; `server/` untouched |
| `vite.config.ts` (dev API, film plugin, guide plugin) | `scripts/film-build.ts` (runs before dev and build into `public/film/`), Next itself |
| `scripts/guide-pages.ts` (HTML snapshots per day) | `src/guides/` (`content.ts` rules, `model.ts`, `GuidePage.tsx`, `route.tsx`); `scripts/guide-cli.ts` kept (`check`, `schedule`) |
| `public/robots.txt`, snapshot sitemap and llms.txt | `app/robots.ts`, `app/sitemap.ts`, `app/llms.txt/route.ts` (hourly, only pages live today) |
| `public/404.html` | `app/global-not-found.tsx` (site design, real 404) |
| `motion` library | CSS (`lib/reveal.tsx` + `RevealObserver`, hero entrances, call sheet, terms dialog); package removed |
| `VITE_ASSIST_API_BASE` | `NEXT_PUBLIC_ASSIST_API_BASE` |

`vercel.json`: `"framework": "nextjs"`, film redirects, cron, security and film cache headers kept.
The SPA rewrites and the film rewrites are gone (the proxy and `next.config.ts` do that job, so `next start` behaves like Vercel).

## What behaves differently

- **Language.** `/` English, `/hi` Hindi, twins for register, grievance(s) and guides. The switch is a plain link to the twin; the link to an English page is `…?lang=en`, which the proxy turns into the clean address plus the `mdg_lang` cookie, so choosing English works with no script and in a new tab (a bare `/` would send a Hindi phone straight back to `/hi`). The click also writes the cookie at once and `mdg.lang` in localStorage, which the film pages read. Order of precedence: `?lang=`, then the cookie, then `Accept-Language`. For `Accept-Language` the rule is the first language the site can serve (English or Hindi), best q first, as the old device check did: `mr-IN,hi;q=0.8` gets Hindi. The brief said "first entry"; the old behaviour was kept so a Marathi or Punjabi phone that lists Hindi next does not lose Hindi. Change `firstAcceptedLanguage` in `src/routing/site.ts` if the literal reading is wanted.
- The enrolment and grievance pages got the EN / हिंदी switch in their slim header (needed now that the URL decides). Their meta descriptions are their own, not the home one.
- **Navbar** has a "Guides" item (`nav.guides`), both languages.
- **Guides** use the site's header, footer, fonts and cards. No snapshot folders: the proxy returns the 404 page for a path whose day has not come (India time) and 308s a missing trailing slash; pages, sitemap and llms.txt refresh hourly and list what is live at that moment, and the daily cron (06:15 India time) also throws away their saved copies just before it tells IndexNow, so the crawl it starts sees today's list. New shortcode `{{penalty-note}}`, worded as in the static pages on main (its clause numbers are ordinary clause chips).
- **Crisp guides.** `checkContent` now carries main's length rule (800 words for a hub or topic page, 650 others, 750 tools, 1,100 glossary; Hindi 1.6 times), so a page that is too long fails the build. The hub shows its topic cards straight after the hero and the explanation below them; a card is never shown twice (related pages skip what is already a card); a page with four or more sections gets a one-line "On this page" row of jump links; "MDG 2024" never breaks across lines in the heading; table words stay whole and a table that scrolls shows a soft edge on the side that has more; on a phone the floating "Call us" is just the handset and the assistant's invitation bubble is not shown on guide pages; breadcrumb links have a thumb-sized target; the guides' small Hindi text is a size up.
- **Animation.** Same fade-ups, in CSS. The hover lift is the CSS `translate` property; the scroll bar eases instead of springing. The hero's top label, its paragraph and its buttons rise without fading in: a fade made the browser count the paragraph as the page's main content only after its delay (the label alone moved the homepage score from 85 to 95; the paragraph is what real phones measured, about 1.1 s later than first paint). The four headline figures are in the server HTML with their real values and only count up from 0 when they turn out to be below the fold.
- **Fonts.** Self-hosted by `next/font`, all `display: "optional"` (the brief said `swap`; see below). On the English page the two Devanagari faces (185 kB) are switched on after the page has loaded, and not at all on a 2G or save-data connection; until then the कवच marks and "हिंदी" use the device's own font. On the Hindi page Noto is preloaded and Tiro comes after load.
  - **Trade-off to approve:** `optional` means a face is used only if it arrives in the first ~100 ms; otherwise that page view keeps the size-matched fallback and the face is cached for the next view. `swap` made the late face re-wrap text already read, and real phones counted 0.02 to 0.05 of layout shift on most Hindi pages (Lighthouse: 0.017 to 0.046), against the brief's target of 0. With `optional` it is 0 everywhere. The cost: on a slow first visit, mostly the Hindi page (its Noto file is 121 kB), text is drawn in the phone's own fonts (on Android the system Devanagari font is Noto Sans Devanagari already) until the second page. To go back, change `display` to `"swap"` in `src/fonts/en.ts` and `hi.ts`.
- Canonical of the home page is `https://mdgservices.in` (Next drops the lone slash; a bare origin is the same address as `https://mdgservices.in/`), and the sitemap lists it the same way. `/register/` redirects to `/register` in the proxy; `/hi/`, `/hi/register/`, `/hi/grievance/` and `/hi/grievances/` redirect (308) through `redirects()` in `next.config.ts`, because putting the Hindi pages in the proxy's matcher would run a function in front of every Hindi page view.
- The home page's meta description has the Latin spelling "(Dealer's Kavach)" back, as the old `index.html` had it (the old SPA's script replaced it at run time; crawlers that do not run scripts saw the raw one).
- `/privacy` is `public/privacy.html` as on main (VRUOOM PRIVATE LIMITED, 15 October 2026) with a canonical and a description added.
- Accessibility: the form pages have a `<main>`; the "Call us" buttons, the Google Play links and the assistant launcher are named by their visible words first. The favicon is 96 px (6 kB, was 256 px, 32 kB) and the three small brand images are cached for a week.
- The build uses **webpack** (`next build --webpack`, `next dev --webpack`). `server/` and `guide/*.ts` import each other as `./x.js` for files that are `.ts`; I was not allowed to edit them and Turbopack cannot map that. `zod` and `nodemailer` are `serverExternalPackages` so the API keeps zod's English error texts (bundled, they came out as "Invalid input").
- `next dev` 16.3 writes `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` into the project; `agentRules: false` turns that off.
- API routes: status codes, bodies, CORS and OPTIONS answers checked against the live site with invalid input only. Next adds a harmless `Vary: rsc…` header.

## Checking things

- `GUIDE_FAKE_NOW=2026-11-01T00:00:00Z` makes proxy, pages, sitemap and llms.txt behave as on that day. `NEXT_DIST_DIR=.next-fake` builds a second copy beside the normal one (it adds `.next-fake` globs to `tsconfig.json`; remove them before committing).
- `npm run guide -- check` runs before every build; a broken, missing or too-long page fails it.
- The daily cron can be tried without sending anything: serve a build with `CRON_SECRET=x GUIDE_FAKE_NOW=2026-12-31T00:00:00Z` (a day after the last release, so the IndexNow list is empty) and call `/api/indexnow` with `authorization: Bearer x`; the sitemap, llms.txt, hubs and every guide page then show `x-nextjs-cache: MISS` once and are rebuilt for that date.

## Not finished, or worth knowing

- Not deployed and not tried on Vercel. Lighthouse numbers are from `next start` on this machine (HTTP/1.1, gzip, simulated slow 4G); Vercel serves HTTP/2 and brotli.
- **Hindi pages score 90 to 92, English 96 to 97** (guides target 95): the Hindi page preloads Noto Sans Devanagari (121 kB) and Inter, and Lighthouse charges those bytes to the page's main-content time (3.5 s against 2.8 s). On a throttled real phone the two are the same (first paint and main content both about 0.8 s). Cutting the font needs a subset built from the Hindi text, which would replace `next/font/google` and break for any new word until rebuilt; the tool to do it here (`hb-subset`) cannot read or write woff2. An owner decision: accept 90 for Hindi, or fund the subsetting step.
- The Devanagari faces on English pages (185 kB, after load) are skipped for 2G and save-data visitors. For everyone else they still load; two tiny subsets for the three or four words would need the same subsetting step.
- Space Mono Bold (9.5 kB) is preloaded on every English page though only two pills far down the home page use it. Left as is: with `display: optional` a face that is not preloaded would rarely show on first view, and those two pills would fall back.
- Guide titles: 52 of the 79 titles in the old content are over 70 characters in a search result. They come from `guide/content` (`metaTitle`), which belongs to the content owner, and match the live site.
- The full disclaimer under every guide page, and the notice strip above it, stay as the brief required; shortening the foot to three lines with a link is the owner's call. The shared footer's small print is the home page's and was not touched.
- Film pages link to `/register?lang=hi` and `/?call=1&lang=hi`, which redirect to the `/hi` page and keep the language choice in the cookie. Linking straight to `/hi/register` would save one hop and lose that.
- `guide/README.md` and the comments in `guide/inventory.ts` and `guide/schedule.ts` still name `scripts/guide-pages.ts`, `middleware.ts` and snapshot folders (main's `guide/README.md` has the crisp-page rules and the new URLs). Those files belong to the content work, so I left them; they need one line each.
- No guide page on this branch's old content uses `{{penalty-note}}`; main's content does, and it renders as a quiet box with clause chips.
