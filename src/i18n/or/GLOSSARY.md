## Notes for a native reviewer
- The danda "।" (U+0964) is the sentence mark and is kept on purpose; it sits in the Devanagari Unicode block but is the standard Odia full stop.
- ପାବତି is the everyday word for a receipt/acknowledgement; swap if you prefer ସ୍ୱୀକୃତି.
- ପ୍ରିମିୟମ ସେବା ordinals (ପ୍ରଥମ…ଷଷ୍ଠ) are slightly formal but are how Odia speakers count a list aloud.


## The website and the guides (added for the site)

Register: plain written Odia a dealer reads on a phone, polite ଆପଣ throughout, short sentences, no heavy Sanskrit words where an everyday one exists. Never "ସାର୍". Sentence end is the danda "।". Digits stay Latin (0-9), money stays "₹3,000". Hindi site copy is the model for tone and for what stays in Latin letters; Odia SOV order is close to Hindi, so the Hindi sentence splits (the pieces that sit around an inline brand mark) mostly carry over.

Stay in Latin letters, exactly as in the Hindi file: SDMS, DSR, OMC, IRAS, AAC, QRC, MDG, MDG 2024, GST, PESO, BS VI, E20, kg/m³, 15°C, MS, HSD, URLs, e-mail addresses, the toll-free number, clause tags like {{5.1.9}} and every {name} placeholder.

Brand: "Dealer's कवच" / "खाता कवच" are written in Devanagari by the page itself where it is the logo mark. Where the Hindi file writes the name inside a sentence in Devanagari ("डीलर कवच", "खाता कवच"), write ଡିଲର କବଚ / ଖାତା କବଚ in Odia. MDG Services, VRUOOM PRIVATE LIMITED, Google Play, App Store stay in Latin.
